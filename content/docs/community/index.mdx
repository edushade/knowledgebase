Learners talk to your academy in one place, **Community**: each programme's chat and
its Q&A, direct messages, and — separately — **Support** for help from your team.
Reviews are where they tell the next buyer what they thought.

## Where it is switched on

| Switch | Where | What it governs |
|---|---|---|
| **Chat is on** | The programme's **Community** tab | Its channels, including Q&A |
| **Start new programmes with chat on** | <Path>Engage > Community > Chat Settings</Path> | Chat for new programmes |
| **Learners can message each other** | The same page | Direct messages between classmates |
| **Enable Rating** | The course's settings step | Reviews and the star rating |

## In the admin menu

Under **Engage**, **Community** holds **Conversations**, **Questions**, **Support**,
**Reported Messages** and **Chat Settings**. The admin dashboard's **Community** card
counts questions waiting, open support requests and reported messages.

## If you only do one thing

Answer the first ten questions in a new course's Q&A within a day.

A Q&A where early questions were answered becomes one where questions get asked — and
every answered question is one support message you do not receive, forever.
