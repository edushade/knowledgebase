## Two switches control all of it

| Switch | On the course's settings step | What it governs |
|---|---|---|
| **Enable Discussion** | Yes | Discussions, comments and reactions |
| **Enable Rating** | Yes | Reviews and the star rating |

Both are per programme, so a formal diploma and an open community course need not behave
the same.

## What is not here

**There is no private messaging between learners.** No direct messages, no chat. The
learner menu has no Message item, and discussions and comments are how learners talk on
your platform.

## If you only do one thing

Answer the first ten questions in a new course's discussion within a day.

A discussion area where early questions were answered becomes one where questions get
asked — and every answered question is one support message you do not receive, forever.
