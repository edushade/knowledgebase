Learning is better together. This section is where your learners ask questions, help
each other, and share how they feel about your courses. It also gives you honest
feedback you can act on — star ratings, written reviews, and quick emoji reactions.

There are four ways your community can take part:

| Tool | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Discussions** | Topic threads attached to a course, quiz, event, cohort, or learning path. |
| **Comments** | Quick replies on a lesson, course, assignment, discussion, and more. |
| **Reviews & ratings** | A star rating plus optional written feedback on what you offer. |
| **Reactions** | A one-tap emoji response — Like, Love, Support, and more. |

<Callout type="info">
Learners can only take part in the community for content they are enrolled in. If a
learner is not enrolled in a course, they will not see its discussions, comments, or
reviews, and they cannot post there. Admins are not limited this way.
</Callout>
