---
title: "Reactions"
description: "Reactions on comments and discussions have been retired — what learners use instead to say an answer helped."
---

Learners used to react to comments and discussion replies with one tap — like, love and
so on. Reactions went with comments and course discussions when those were replaced by
each programme's chat and Q&A.

<WhoCan roles="Learner, Instructor, Admin" />

## What to use instead

| You want to know… | Use |
|---|---|
| Which answer actually helped | The **Accepted answer** on a question in Q&A — see [Questions and answers](/docs/knowledgebase/community/discussions) |
| What learners thought of the course | [Reviews and ratings](/docs/knowledgebase/community/reviews-and-ratings) |
| Whether the course is working | [Progress analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/progress) |

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can learners react to a chat message?">
No. They can reply, and report a message that should not be there.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Our activity log mentions reactions.">
That is the name of the group your older entries are filed under, **Reviews and
reactions**.
</Accordion>
