---
title: "Comments"
description: "Comments under lessons have been replaced by each programme's Q&A and lesson questions — where to send learners now."
---

Learners used to leave comments under a lesson. That has been replaced: a question about
a lesson now goes in **Questions on this lesson**, which posts it to the programme's
**Q&A** channel, and conversation happens in the programme's chat.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor, Learner" />

## What replaced comments

| You want learners to… | Use |
|---|---|
| Ask about one lesson | **Questions on this lesson**, under every lesson — see [Questions and answers](/docs/knowledgebase/community/discussions) |
| Talk about the course | The programme's chat channels — see [Programme chat](/docs/knowledgebase/community/programme-chat) |
| Tell you something is broken | **Report a problem** — see [Reported problems](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/reported-problems) |
| Say what they thought of the course | A review — see [Reviews and ratings](/docs/knowledgebase/community/reviews-and-ratings) |

A question asked against a lesson is better than a comment was: it can be answered, the
answer can be marked, and the next learner finds it on the same lesson.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Where did our old comments go?">
Lesson comments and course discussions were retired together. Questions are now asked in
each programme's Q&A channel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner cannot ask about a lesson.">
The lesson questions card appears where the programme has chat switched on and a Q&A
channel that shows its questions under each lesson.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I remove a learner's message?">
Moderators can delete a message in chat or Q&A. See
[Programme chat](/docs/knowledgebase/community/programme-chat).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
