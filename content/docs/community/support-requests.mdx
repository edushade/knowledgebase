Every learner has a **Support** page in their menu. They ask for help there, and your
answer reaches them on that page and by email.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## What the learner sends

**New request** asks for a **Subject**, what it is about — **General question**, **A
course or lesson**, **Something isn't working**, **Payments and fees**, **My account**
or **Something else** — the programme if there is one, a message, and up to ten files.

The **Message us** button on a learner's order page opens the same Support page.

## Answering

<Path>Engage > Community > Support</Path> is the inbox. Cards at the top count what is
**Open**, **Waiting on learner**, **Unassigned**, **Assigned to you** and **Unread**.

Open a request to reply with **Send reply**. Each request has a **Status**, who it is
**Assigned to**, and a **Priority** — **Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Urgent**.

When you are waiting on the learner, they see the request as **Replied**. They can close
a request themselves once they have what they need.

## Good to know

- Each programme's **Community** tab, and the admin dashboard's **Community** card, say
  how many support requests are open.
- A support request is private between the learner and your team — unlike a question in
  [Q&A](/docs/knowledgebase/community/discussions), which other learners can read.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says they never got an answer.">
Look for the request in the inbox and check its status. Your reply also goes by email,
so check their address if they say nothing arrived.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should course questions go here?">
Better in the programme's Q&A, where the next learner can find the answer. Support is
for things only your team can sort out — payments, accounts, something broken.
</Accordion>
