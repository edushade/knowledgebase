Every programme can have its own chat: announcements from you, a place for learners to
talk, and a Q&A channel for questions. Learners find all of it on one screen,
**Community**, in their menu.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Switching it on

Open a course, learning path or batch and go to its **Community** tab.

**Turn on chat** adds three channels: **Announcements**, a **General** chat and a
**Q&A** channel. From then on, the tab has four sections.

### Chat

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chat is on** | Off hides the chat from this programme's learners. |
| **Separate men and women** | Brothers and sisters get their own channels. Learners who have not said which they are are asked once, and can read announcements until they choose. |
| **Staff chat** | **Open staff chat** — the same conversations, from your side, with who is online. |

### Channels

The channels learners see, in order. Each row shows its type and who can see it, and
whether it is locked or slowed. **Open** goes to it; the **⋯** menu has **Edit**,
**Lock**, **Slow mode** and **Remove**.

- **Lock** — only moderators can post. Learners can still read.
- **Slow mode** — one post every 10 seconds, 30 seconds, minute or 5 minutes, for a
  channel that is moving too fast.

Announcements cannot be locked or slowed: only your team posts there anyway.

**Add a channel** opens a short dialog:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Type** | **Chat** — learners and staff talk. **Announcements** — only your team posts. **Q&A** — questions and their answers. |
| **Name** | One name, which you can translate into each of your site's languages. |
| **Topic, optional** | A line under the name. |
| **Who can see it** | **Everyone**, **Men and women, one channel each**, **Men only**, **Women only**, or **A segment** — one of your audiences. |

A Q&A channel has its own settings — see
[Questions and answers](/docs/knowledgebase/community/discussions).

### Waiting on staff

Said in sentences: how many questions are waiting for an answer, and how many support
requests are open, each with a link to them.

### Moderation

Who moderates this chat — staff on the programme whose role includes **Moderate chat** —
with links to change it. Then reported messages waiting for review, learners who are
muted (**Mute a learner** for an hour, a day, a week or 30 days, and **Lift**), and the
**Moderation log**, which names the channel and the learner each action was about.

## Settings for the whole academy

<Path>Engage > Community > Chat Settings</Path>:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chat is on in N programmes** | **Turn on for the rest** switches chat on everywhere at once. |
| **Start new programmes with chat on** | New programmes begin with chat. |
| **Separate men and women** | The academy-wide default. |
| **Staff can read** | **Both sides**, or **Their own side only**. |
| **Learners can message each other** | Direct messages between classmates. Learners can always message staff. |
| **Daily email of unread messages** | A once-a-day summary for anyone with something unread. |

## Direct messages

Learners can always write to the teachers and staff of their programmes. They can write
to classmates only when **Learners can message each other** is on — and only within
their own side when men and women are separate.

Your team's conversations are under <Path>Engage > Community > Conversations</Path>,
split into **Programmes** and **Direct**.

## Keeping it civil

Learners can report a message — spam, harassment, something inappropriate, off topic.
Reports land in <Path>Engage > Community > Reported Messages</Path>, where a moderator
reads it, goes to the conversation, deletes it, or notes what they did.

A muted learner can read but not post until the mute ends. Keep a light hand: a chat
that is heavily moderated stops being used.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner cannot see Community in their menu.">
It appears once they are in a programme with chat switched on, or have a conversation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner can read but not post.">
The channel is an announcements channel or is locked, they are muted, or an instalment
is unpaid — the chat tells them which.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who counts as a moderator?">
Staff on the programme whose role includes **Moderate chat**. See
[People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can learners react to messages with emoji?">
No. Learners can reply, edit or delete their own messages, and report someone else's.
</Accordion>
