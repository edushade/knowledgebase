description: "Where learners ask about a course and get an answer — the Q&A channel every programme has, the lesson questions card, and marking the answer that helped."
---

Learners ask their questions in the programme's **Q&A** channel. A teacher or a
classmate answers, and the reply that helped is marked, so the next learner with the
same question finds the answer already there.

Q&A replaced the old course discussions. The **Discussion** menu, the programme's
**Discussions** tab and the **Enable Discussion** switch are gone; questions now live in
[Community](/docs/knowledgebase/community/programme-chat).

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor, Learner" />

## Where it lives

Every programme with chat switched on has a **Q&A** channel — turning chat on for a
programme adds one alongside **Announcements** and a **General** chat. Learners reach it
three ways:

- **Community**, in their menu, under the programme.
- The **Q&A** button at the top of the course page.
- **Questions on this lesson**, a card under each lesson, with **Ask about this lesson**.

A programme with no Q&A channel says so on its **Community** tab, with **Add a Q&A
channel**.

## Asking a question

**Ask a question** opens a short form:

| Field | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The question in one line. |
| **Details** | Anything else, with attachments. |
| **Which lesson is it about? (optional)** | Puts the question on that lesson's card. |
| **Ask privately** | Only the learner and the teachers see it. Offered where you allow it. |

Learners can search, and filter by **All**, **Unanswered**, **Mine** and by lesson.

## Marking the answer

Once a reply answers the question, **Mark as answer** puts it at the top as the
**Accepted answer**, with who marked it. By default the person who asked or a teacher
can mark it; you can limit it to staff.

## Setting up a Q&A channel

On a channel's **Edit** dialog, under **Questions**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show its questions on the course page and under each lesson** | On by default. |
| **Learners may ask privately** | On by default. |
| **Only staff mark the answer** | Off by default. |

## Answering them

<Path>Community > Questions</Path>, under Engage in the admin menu, lists what learners
asked across your programmes, most recent activity first, with **Waiting for an
answer** and **Answered** as filters. Open one to reply or mark the answer that helped.

Each programme's **Community** tab also says how many questions are waiting, and the
admin dashboard's **Community** card counts them across the academy.

## Keeping it alive

A Q&A channel with no replies is worse than none — it advertises that nobody is
listening.

1. **Answer within a day, every day, for the first month.** A place where questions get
   answered becomes a place where questions get asked.
2. **Seed it.** Post the three questions every batch asks, with their answers, before
   the batch starts.
3. **Let learners answer each other**, and mark their answers. The best Q&A channels are
   mostly learners.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Learners cannot find the Q&A.">
Chat is off for that programme, or the programme has no Q&A channel. Both are on the
programme's **Community** tab.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where did our old discussions go?">
Course discussions were retired. Questions are now asked in each programme's Q&A
channel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I close a question?">
Yes. A locked question can still be read, but takes no more replies. A whole channel
can be locked too.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Someone posted the answers to a quiz.">
Delete it, and say in your course instructions that it is not allowed. The second part
prevents the next one.
</Accordion>
