Your website is where people meet your academy before they are learners. It is built
from **blocks**: ready-made sections you fill with your own words and arrange.

Over a hundred of them exist, and the most useful ones show your real catalogue —
courses, batches, prices, curricula — so your site is never out of date with your
academy.

<Path>Website</Path> opens its own dashboard — how many pages and posts you have, what
was edited last, and what visitors have sent you.

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/website/website-dashboard.png"
  alt="The Website dashboard, with counts for pages, posts, form responses and active forms."
  caption="New page and New post are here, and View site opens the real thing in a new tab."
/>

The **Visitors** panel stays empty until you connect a tracking service — see
[Analytics & Tracking](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/control-panel). Nothing else on this
screen needs setting up.

## The four launch mistakes

| Mistake | Cost |
|---|---|
| **Search indexing left off** | Invisible. Everything else you do for search is wasted. |
| **A typed course list instead of a Courses block** | Wrong the day you add a course. |
| **No custom domain before selling** | Every link already shared points at the old address. |
| **Never buying something yourself** | You find the missing price, the broken verification email and the empty checkout the way your first customer does. |
