Your website is made of pages, and a page is made of **blocks**. A block is a section —
a hero, a course list, a pricing table, a FAQ — that you drop in, fill with your own
words, and arrange.

<Path>Configure > Website</Path>.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## What a block is

Each block is a ready-made section with its own fields. You do not lay out columns or
choose spacing; you pick the block that does the job and fill it in.

There are over a hundred, covering the sections a course business actually needs:

| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Opening** | Hero, split hero, poster hero, masthead |
| **Selling** | Pricing, plans, inclusions, outcomes, "is this for you?" |
| **Proof** | Testimonials, reviews, ratings, stats, results, partners, logos |
| **Content** | Features, FAQ, accordion, timeline, steps, gallery, team |
| **Catalogue** | Courses, learning paths, bundles, categories, products, events, posts |
| **Course pages** | Curriculum, instructor, course stats, cohort schedule, countdown |
| **Enrolment** | Checkout, enrolment panel, cohort selector, "enrolment closed" |
| **Framing** | Header, footer, announcement bar, call to action, mobile sticky bar |
| **Building** | Section, row, column, grid, spacer, divider, text, image, video, button |

## Blocks that pull in live data

The ones worth knowing about do not hold content at all — they show what is already in
your academy and stay current by themselves:

| Block | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Courses**, **Learning Paths**, **Bundles**, **Products**, **Events** | Your published catalogue |
| **Categories**, **Category programs** | Courses in a category |
| **Curriculum** | A course's real outline |
| **Course stats**, **Course ratings**, **Instructor** | That course's live figures |
| **Cohort schedule**, **Cohort countdown**, **Cohort selector** | A batch's dates and seats |
| **Enrolment panel**, **Checkout** | The real purchase flow |
| **Posts** | Your [blog](/docs/knowledgebase/website/blog) |

<Callout type="info">
**Use these rather than typing your course list into a text block.** A list you typed is
wrong the day you add a course; a Courses block never is.
</Callout>

## Pages

A page has:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Content** | The blocks. |
| **Metadata** | Title, description, slug — what search engines read. See [SEO](/docs/knowledgebase/website/seo). |
| **Settings** | How the page behaves. |
| **Preview** | What it will look like. |

Every piece of text in a block can be written in each of your site's languages. See
[Languages](/docs/knowledgebase/website/languages).

## System pages

Some pages are built in because the platform needs them: the course page, the checkout
page, the catalogue, sign-in.

You can **edit their metadata**, and you can **replace a system page** with one of your
own — either a new page or an existing one.

<Callout type="warn">
Replace a system page only when you know what it has to do. A replaced checkout page
that does not contain a Checkout block cannot take money.
</Callout>

## Legal pages

**Create legal pages** generates terms, privacy and cookie pages for you. It will tell
you when they are live, and it asks before replacing pages you already have.

Point your privacy settings at them afterwards — see
[Privacy and consent](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/privacy-and-consent).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="My course list is out of date.">
It is a text block somebody typed. Replace it with a Courses block, which reads your
catalogue.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A block shows nothing.">
Blocks that pull in live data show nothing when there is nothing to show — no published
courses in that category, no batches on that course.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I build a page from scratch?">
Yes, from the layout blocks — section, row, column, text, image, button. The ready-made
blocks exist because they are faster and they look right on a phone.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I edit the checkout page?">
Yes. It is a page with a Checkout block, and the block's labels, methods and order card
are yours. See [Checkout](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/checkout).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I replaced a system page and something broke.">
Replace it back with the original, then work out what the page needed. System pages do
a job the platform depends on.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
