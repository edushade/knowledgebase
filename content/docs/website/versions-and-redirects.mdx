A redirect sends somebody arriving at one address to another. Every time a page's
address changes, you need one.

<Path>Configure > Website</Path>, under redirects.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Why they matter

A page's address is in more places than you think:

- Links other sites made to you.
- Search engine results, for months afterwards.
- Links your learners shared in group chats.
- Printed material, where it never changes.
- Your own old blog posts.

Change the address without a redirect and all of those land on nothing. With one, they
land where you meant.

<Callout type="warn">
**The traffic you lose this way does not come back.** A course page that ranked well,
moved without a redirect, starts again from nothing.
</Callout>

## Adding one

Each redirect is an old address and a new one. Add them as you change pages — not in a
clean-up later, by which time nobody remembers what the old addresses were.

**Bulk Import Redirects** takes a list, which is what you need when moving a site onto
Edushade from somewhere else. That is the moment redirects matter most: everything the
old site ranked for arrives at an address that no longer exists.

## When you need one

| Change | Redirect needed |
|---|---|
| A page's slug changes | **Yes** |
| A page is deleted | **Yes** — to the nearest sensible page |
| A course is renamed and its slug follows | **Yes** |
| The blog moves | **Yes** |
| Moving from another website | **Yes**, in bulk |
| A page's content changes | No |
| A menu label changes | No |

## Better than a redirect: not moving

The cheapest redirect is the one you did not need. When you create a page, give it an
address you will be happy with in three years — short, readable, and about the thing
rather than the campaign.

`/courses/spoken-english` will still be right. `/courses/spoken-english-january-2026`
will not.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="We moved a page and traffic disappeared.">
Add the redirect now. Some of it comes back once search engines re-crawl.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long should a redirect stay?">
Permanently. It costs nothing and something somewhere still points at the old address.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I redirect to another site?">
Point a redirect wherever it needs to go.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We are moving our site to Edushade. What do we need?">
A list of your old addresses and their new equivalents, imported in bulk before you
switch the domain over.
</Accordion>
