A blog is how people who have never heard of you arrive. For a course business it is
usually the cheapest traffic there is.

<Path>Configure > Website</Path>, under posts and blog settings.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Switching it on

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable Blog** | Turns the blog on. |
| **Blog Page URL** | Where it lives — `/blog`. |
| **Posts Per Page** | How many before paging. |
| **URL Structure** | **Path-based** (`/blog/page/2`) or **query-based** (`/blog?page=2`). |

## A post

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Post Content** | The writing. |
| **Post Metadata** | Title, description, slug. See [SEO](/docs/knowledgebase/website/seo). |
| **Categories** and **Tags** | How posts are grouped. |
| **Author** | Who wrote it. |
| **Published Date** | When it went out. |
| **Post Preview** | What it looks like before publishing. |

## How posts are displayed

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Post Layout** | **List View**, **Masonry**, **Infinite scroll** |
| **Show Author**, **Show Categories**, **Show Tags** | What appears with each post |
| **Related Posts to Display** | How many at the end |
| **Enable Social Sharing** | Share buttons |
| **Enable RSS Feed** | A feed of your posts |

Related posts matter more than they look: somebody who read one thing is the most likely
person to read a second.

## Comments

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Comment System** | **Native Comments**, **Disqus**, **Facebook Comments** or **Disabled** |
| **Require Comment Approval** | Nothing appears until you approve it |
| **Spam protection** | Guards against automated comments |

If you enable comments, turn on approval. A blog with spam comments looks abandoned.

## What to write

The posts that bring a course business traffic are the questions people ask **before**
they buy:

- "How long does it take to get IELTS 7?"
- "What is the difference between a certificate and a diploma?"
- "Can I learn web development without a degree?"

Each of those is something somebody types into a search engine, and each ends naturally
at one of your courses.

What does not work: announcements about your academy. Nobody searches for those.

## Showing posts elsewhere

A **Posts** block puts your latest writing on any page — your homepage, a course page,
a category page. See [Pages and blocks](/docs/knowledgebase/website/pages-and-blocks).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The blog page is empty.">
**Enable Blog** is off, no posts are published, or the blog page URL points somewhere
else.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can several people write posts?">
Yes, with the right role. Each post carries its author. See
[People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can posts be in more than one language?">
Each field can be written in each of your site's languages. See
[Languages](/docs/knowledgebase/website/languages).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I schedule a post?">
Set its published date in the future.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do posts help us appear in search?">
More than any other single thing you can do, if they answer questions people actually
ask. See [SEO](/docs/knowledgebase/website/seo).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
