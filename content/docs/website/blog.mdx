A blog is a great way to share news, tips, and stories, and to bring visitors to your
site. You can write posts, give each one a featured image and author, organize them with
tags and categories, and show related posts to keep readers reading.

## What a post can have

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title and content** | The headline and the body of the post. |
| **Languages** | Write the post in more than one language so each reader sees it in theirs. |
| **Featured image** | The main picture shown at the top and in listings. |
| **Authors** | Who wrote the post. |
| **Tags** | Short keywords for grouping related posts. |
| **Categories** | Bigger groupings that can nest — a category can sit inside another. |
| **Related posts** | Other posts to suggest at the end. |

Featured images come from your [Files & Media](/docs/knowledgebase/files) library.

## How to write a post

1. Go to **Admin → Website → Blog** and click **New Post**.
2. Add a **title** and write the **content**.
3. Choose a **featured image**.
4. Set the **author(s)**.
5. Add **tags** and pick a **category** (you can nest categories for structure).
6. Choose any **related posts** to suggest.
7. Add other languages if you run a multi-language site.
8. **Publish** when ready — see [Publishing](/docs/knowledgebase/website/publishing).

## Categories and tags

- **Categories** are your main groupings, and they can nest — for example "Study Tips"
  could contain "Exam Prep". Use them as the backbone of your blog.
- **Tags** are lighter, free-form keywords. A post can carry several tags.

## Good to know

- You can set where your blog lives and how many posts show per page — see
  [Navigation & menus](/docs/knowledgebase/website/navigation-menus).
- Strong titles and descriptions help your posts appear in search — see
  [Search visibility (SEO)](/docs/knowledgebase/website/seo).
