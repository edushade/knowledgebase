A website has its own state, separate from any page's. It decides whether the world can
see your academy at all.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The four site states

| State | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| **Live (Public)** | Everybody. |
| **Coming Soon** | Visitors see a holding page. |
| **Maintenance Mode** | Visitors see a maintenance notice. |
| **Private (Sign-in required)** | Only people who sign in. |

**Private** is how a corporate academy runs: no public catalogue, learners sign in and
see what they are enrolled in. **Coming Soon** is for the weeks before you launch, when
you want the address to work and the content not to show.

## Your own domain

**Add Custom Domain**, then point the domain at your site. **Domain Status** tells you
where the setup has reached.

Do this before you start selling. Every link shared, every search result, every
certificate someone shows an employer carries the address they first saw — and moving
later loses some of that.

## Before you go live

<Steps>

<Step>

### Check it on a phone

Most of your visitors are on one. Open your homepage, your best course page, and
checkout on a real phone.

</Step>

<Step>

### Walk through buying something

Register a fresh account and buy a course, start to finish. This finds the missing
price, the broken verification email and the empty checkout page that no amount of
previewing will.

</Step>

<Step>

### Check the metadata

Site title, tagline, meta description, social share image. Share a link to your own
homepage in a chat and look at what appears. See [SEO](/docs/knowledgebase/website/seo).

</Step>

<Step>

### Check the legal pages exist

Terms, privacy and cookies — and that your privacy settings point at them. See
[Privacy and consent](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/privacy-and-consent).

</Step>

<Step>

### Turn indexing on

**Allow search engines to index this site** must be on, or nothing you do for search
matters. It is the single most common launch mistake.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Site settings worth knowing

| Setting | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Site Title**, **Tagline** | What your site is called. |
| **Homepage** | Which page is the front page. |
| **Header Block**, **Footer Block**, **Announcement Bar Block** | The framing shown on every page. |
| **Support Email**, **Phone Number**, **Physical Address** | Your contact details. |
| **Social Share Image** | What appears when a link is shared. |
| **Currency**, number format, date format, timezone | Regional settings. |
| **Lazy Load Images**, **Minify CSS/JS** | Performance. Leave both on. |

Number formats include **১,২৩৪.৫৬ (Bangla)** alongside the European and English ones —
worth setting for a Bangla-first academy.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Our site is live but not in Google.">
Check **Allow search engines to index this site** is on, then see
[SEO](/docs/knowledgebase/website/seo). Indexing takes time even once it is right.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I keep the site private while building it?">
Yes — **Coming Soon** or **Private**. Both keep the address working.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A change is not showing.">
Check the page itself is published, not just saved, and reload. Some changes are cached
briefly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I run the site under our existing domain?">
Yes, pointed here. Set it up before you start selling.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does Maintenance Mode do to learners?">
Visitors see the maintenance notice. Use it for a planned change, and say when you will
be back.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
