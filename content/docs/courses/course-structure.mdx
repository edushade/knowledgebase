A course is a list, and the shape of that list is most of what a learner feels about the
course. This page is about the shape; [the course builder](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/curriculum-builder)
is about the buttons.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Section** | A container with a title. "Module 1", "Week 3". Holds everything else. |
| **Lesson** | The teaching — video, audio, reading, PDF, live class or embedded content. |
| **Quiz** | A test. Marked automatically or by you. |
| **Assignment** | Work the learner submits for marking. |
| **File** | A download in its own right — a handout, a dataset. |
| **Breakpoint** | A dated term. Cohort-based courses only. |
| **Stage** | The equivalent of a section in a learning path, holding whole courses. |

## The shape that works

Sections at the top level, everything else inside them:

```
Section — Getting started
  Lesson — Welcome (video, 4 min)
  Lesson — How this course works (reading)
  Quiz  — Quick check

Section — Core ideas
  Lesson — The three principles (video, 18 min)
  File  — Principles worksheet
  Assignment — Apply the principles
```

Three habits make the difference:

1. **Name sections by what the learner will be able to do**, not "Module 2".
2. **Keep sections roughly even** — five items each reads better than two sections of
   two and one of fourteen.
3. **End a section with something that proves it landed** — a quiz or a short
   assignment. It also gives you something to grade.

## Terms sit above sections

On a cohort-based course, a **breakpoint** is a dated term and sections sit inside it:

```
Breakpoint — Semester 1 (Jan 15 – Apr 30)
  Section — Foundations
  Section — Practice
Breakpoint — Semester 2 (May 15 – Aug 30)
  Section — Advanced work
```

Anything *outside* a term still belongs to the course but is not counted in any term's
result. See [Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints).

## What the shape decides

The outline is not only presentation. Four things read it:

- **Progress.** The share of published items a learner has finished. See
  [Progress tracking](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/progress-tracking).
- **Grading.** Which items carry marks, and how they are weighted. See
  [Weighted grading](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/weighted-grading).
- **Drip.** What opens when. See [Drip scheduling](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling).
- **Prerequisites.** What must be finished before what. See
  [Prerequisites](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/prerequisites).

Move a quiz out of a section and all four change.

## What the learner sees

The outline, in your order, with sections they can fold shut and a tick against
everything they have finished. Anything unpublished is not there at all. Anything held
by a drip schedule shows as locked, with the date it opens — but anything held by an
[eligibility rule](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/eligibility-rules) simply does
not appear, with no explanation given.

An item marked **Free Preview** can be opened by anyone, enrolled or not.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can a lesson sit outside a section?">
Yes, at the top level. It works, and for a very short course it is fine. For anything
longer, learners lose their place in a flat list.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many items should a course have?">
However many it takes — but keep each one small. A 40-minute video that a learner cannot
pause between ideas is one item; split into four, it is four ticks and four places to
come back to.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does reordering the course disturb learners already in it?">
Their finished items stay finished. What changes is what they see next, and anything
tied to position — drip offsets counted from the course's start, for instance. Reorder
freely early on; be careful once a batch is running.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can two courses share the same section?">
No. A section belongs to one course. To reuse the material, use the
[Content Bank](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/content-bank), or put the whole course into a
[learning path](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/learning-paths).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
