---
title: "Self-Paced or Cohort-Based"
description: "The one choice you make before anything else on a course — whether learners move at their own speed or together in a batch."
---

Every course is one of two types, and you pick which before the form even opens. The
choice decides whether a learner starts the moment they pay, or waits for a batch to
begin.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The two types

| | **Self Paced** | **Cohort-Based** |
|---|---|---|
| When a learner starts | The moment they enrol | When their batch starts |
| Who they learn with | Alone | A group on the same schedule |
| Dates | None on the course itself | Each batch has its own start and end |
| Seat limits | One limit for the whole course | A limit per batch |
| Price | One price for the course | Priced per batch |
| Terms and semesters | Not available | Available |
| Steps in the form | 3 | 4 — an extra step for the batches |

## Which one fits

**Choose Self Paced when** the course is always open, people buy it one at a time, and
there is no reason for two learners to be on the same page in the same week. A recorded
skills course, an onboarding course, a library of reference material.

**Choose Cohort-Based when** the teaching happens on a calendar. A diploma with
semesters, an exam-prep batch that sits the exam together, a class with live sessions,
anything where "when does it start?" has a real answer.

<Callout type="warn">
The type is fixed once the course is created — the edit form does not offer to change
it. If you get it wrong, rebuild the course under the right type while it is still
small, and delete the first one.
</Callout>

## What cohort-based gives you that self-paced does not

A cohort-based course holds one or more **batches**. A batch is the thing learners
actually enrol into: it has its own dates, its own seat count, its own price, its own
staff and its own learner list. The course is the syllabus; the batch is the class.

That unlocks a few things self-paced courses cannot do:

- **Terms and semesters.** A batch can be divided into terms, each with its own dates,
  so a learner's result is reported per semester as well as overall. See
  [Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints).
- **Partial access.** You can sell or grant one semester at a time instead of the whole
  programme. See [Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access).
- **Transfers.** A learner can be moved from one batch to another, carrying or resetting
  their marks. See [Transfers](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/transfers).
- **Progression.** You can decide who moves up to the next semester and who repeats.
  See [Progression](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/progression).
- **An enrolment window.** A batch can close to new learners on a date, or allow late
  joiners for a while after it starts. See
  [Enrolment windows](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/enrollment-windows).

## What the learner sees

A self-paced course shows **Enrol** and opens straight away.

A cohort-based course shows the batches on offer, each with its start date and how many
seats are left. The learner picks one. If the batch has not started yet, they can see
the course page and their place in it, but the content opens when the batch does.

## Good to know

- A **learning path** can hold both kinds of course at once, so a self-paced primer can
  sit in front of a scheduled batch. See
  [Learning paths](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/learning-paths).
- Batches are not in the main menu. You reach them from the course itself — the fourth
  step of the course form, and the **Cohorts** area of a cohort-based course.
- A cohort-based course with no batches cannot be enrolled in by anyone. Make the first
  batch before you publish.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can one course have both self-paced and scheduled learners?">
No. The type belongs to the course, not to the learner. If you need both, make two
courses, or put a self-paced course and a cohort-based course into one
[learning path](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/learning-paths).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need a batch for every intake?">
Yes — one batch per intake. The January class and the June class are two batches of the
same course, each with its own dates, seats, price and learner list. That is what lets
you report on them separately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a self-paced course have live sessions?">
Yes. A lesson can be a **Live** lesson with a scheduled time and a meeting link, in any
course type. What a self-paced course cannot do is make everyone reach that lesson in
the same week. See [Lesson types](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/lesson-types).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I picked the wrong type and the course already has learners in it.">
Rebuilding is still the only way to change the type, so the question becomes what
happens to the people already in it. Enrol them into the new course yourself in bulk,
and keep the old course alive, unpublished, until everyone has finished. See
[Admin and bulk enrollment](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/admin-and-bulk-enrollment).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
