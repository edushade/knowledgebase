---
title: "Terms and Semesters"
description: "Cut a batch into terms so results, access and enrolment can all work one semester at a time."
---

A term — the screens call it a **breakpoint** — is a dated slice of a batch. Everything
between its start and end dates belongs to that term: the lessons taught in it, the
marks earned in it, and the result reported for it.

You need terms when your programme has semesters. You do not need them for a short
course that is simply taught from start to finish.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## What a term gives you

| Without terms | With terms |
|---|---|
| One result, at the end | A result per term, and an overall result |
| Learners buy the whole programme | Learners can buy one semester at a time |
| Everyone finishes together | You decide each term who moves up and who repeats |
| One enrolment window | Each term can open and close on its own dates |

Those four are why terms exist. If none of them is something you need, leave the batch
undivided.

## Two ways to create them

### Generated with the batch

When you make a batch, **Breakpoint Interval** cuts its dates up for you — weekly,
bi-weekly, monthly, or **Custom Intervals** where you give the number and the dates are
split evenly. The **Breakpoints Preview** panel shows what you are about to get before
you save. Most semester programmes use **Custom Intervals** with the number of
semesters. See [Cohorts and batches](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/cohorts).

### Added by hand in the builder

In the course builder's **Curriculum** tab, add a **Breakpoint** item and set its dates
yourself. Use this when the terms are not evenly spaced — a short foundation term
followed by three long ones.

Either way, you then drag content into the term it belongs to.

## What you set on a term

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What learners see — "Semester 1", "Term 2". Required. |
| **Description** | Optional. |
| **Start Date & Time** | When the term begins teaching. Required. |
| **End Date & Time** | When it ends. Required. |
| **Enrollment opens** / **Enrollment closes** | When this term may be *sold*, which is not the same as when it is taught. Leave both empty and the term follows its batch. |
| **Transcript available from** | A date before which the term's result stays hidden from the learner. Leave it empty and the term's end date is used. |
| **Status** | **Draft** keeps the term out of sight; **Published** makes it live. |

<Callout type="info">
**The enrolment window is optional on purpose.** Left empty, a term is on sale until its
batch's end date — or, if the batch does not allow late entry, only until that term
starts teaching. Fill it in when one semester needs to sell on different dates from the
rest.
</Callout>

## How terms change the rest of the system

**Results.** Each term produces its own result, alongside the overall one. See
[GPA and results](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/gpa-and-results) and
[Terms and periods](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/terms-and-periods).

**Access.** A learner can be given one term rather than the whole batch. That is what
[partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access) means, and it is
how a semester-by-semester programme is sold.

**Progression.** At the end of a term you decide, per learner, whether they go up to the
next one or repeat. See [Progression](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/progression).

**Certificates.** A term-level transcript can be released on its own date, before the
programme has finished.

## Good to know

- **Content outside a term still belongs to the batch.** A lesson that sits above the
  first breakpoint, or between two of them, is part of the course but is not counted in
  any term's result. Keep everything that should be marked inside a term.
- **Terms are a cohort-based idea.** A self-paced course has no dates to cut up.
- **Changing a batch's dates does not redraw its terms.** The terms keep the dates they
  were given. If the batch moves, go through the terms and move them too.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="I changed the batch dates and the terms are now wrong.">
Terms hold their own dates and do not follow the batch. Open each one and correct it. If
there are many and the shift is uniform, it is usually faster to delete them and
generate the set again from the batch's **Breakpoint Interval** — but only before
learners have marks in them, because deleting a term takes its structure with it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner buy just Semester 2?">
Yes, if Semester 2 is a term with its own price. That is the main reason to use terms.
See [Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access) and
[Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner's marks are missing from their semester result.">
The usual cause is content sitting outside any term. A quiz that is not inside a
breakpoint is not part of a semester, so it never reaches that semester's result — even
though it shows on the overall one. Move it inside the term it belongs to.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a term and a section?">
A **section** groups content for tidiness — "Module 3". A **term** is a dated period
that results, access and enrolment are all calculated against. A section has no dates
and changes nothing about marking.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I add a term to a batch that is already running?">
Yes. Add the breakpoint, set its dates, and move the content that belongs to it inside.
Marks already recorded against that content are then counted in the new term.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
