The settings step is the last of the three, reached from **Settings** at the top right of
the course builder or by clicking the **3**. It holds two cards: **Basic Settings**
describes the course, **Settings Behavior** decides what it does.

## Basic Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Difficulty Level** | Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced. Shown on the course page and usable as a filter in your catalogue. |
| **Language** | The language the course is taught in. Shown on the course page and in the catalogue filters. This is not the same as the languages you *write* the course in. |
| **Maximum Enrollments** | How many learners may be in the course at once. **0 means unlimited.** |
| **Duration** | How long the course takes, as hours, minutes and seconds. Shown to learners as a rough guide; it does not time anything. |
| **Course Instructions** | A note shown to learners taking the course — house rules, what to bring, how it is marked. |
| **Completion Message** | What a learner reads when they finish. Leave it empty for the standard message. |
| **Search Keywords** | Other names people might search for — an abbreviation, the name in another language. Keywords are never shown to learners; they only widen what finds the course. |

## Settings Behavior

### Who can get in

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enrollment Open** | Off closes the course to new learners. People already in it keep their access. Use this to retire a course without deleting it. |

### What the course awards

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable Certificate** | Whether this course awards a certificate at all. Off means no certificate, whatever the rules say. |
| **Enable Transcript** | Whether learners can see and download their own result for this course. Off hides it from the learner's panel — you can still view and print it yourself. |
| **Certificate Code** | A short code for the course, used in certificate numbers. Leave it empty and the course's slug is used instead. See [Certificate numbers](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/issuing-certificates). |
| **Completion certificate type** | Which certificate this course grants when a learner finishes. The pass rules live on the type, not here. |
| **Enrollment certificate type (instant)** | A certificate granted the moment someone enrols — useful for a proof-of-admission document. |
| **Enrollment design** | Which design that enrolment certificate is printed on. **Use default design** falls back to your academy's default. |

Certificate types are made once and reused across courses — see
[Certificate types](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-types).

### When results are released (self-paced only)

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Certificate available from** | A date before which a passing learner still cannot download their certificate. Leave it empty to release it the moment they pass. |
| **Transcript available from** | The same, for the result or transcript. Leave it empty to release it immediately. |

On a cohort-based course, release is governed by the batch's own dates instead.

### How content behaves

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Customize video playback for this program** | Off means this course follows your academy-wide video settings. On lets you decide, for this course only, where YouTube and Vimeo videos play and whether learners can skip ahead or change speed. See [Video playback](/docs/knowledgebase/files/video-playback). |

### What learners may do

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable Discussion** | Lets learners discuss the course content. See [Discussions](/docs/knowledgebase/community/discussions). |
| **Enable Rating** | Lets learners leave a star rating and review. See [Reviews and ratings](/docs/knowledgebase/community/reviews-and-ratings). |

### What the course page promises

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Learning Outcomes** | What a learner will be able to do afterwards. Up to 30 lines, each added with **Add outcome**. |
| **Requirements** | What someone needs before they enrol. Up to 30 lines, each added with **Add requirement**. |

Both appear on the public course page. They are promises, not rules — nothing is
enforced. To actually stop the wrong people enrolling, use
[eligibility rules](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/eligibility-rules).

## Publishing

The status button at the top right carries the course's state:

| Status | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Only you and your team. Nothing is live. |
| **Published** | Live in your catalogue and open to enrolment. |
| **Archived** | Retired. Out of the catalogue; existing learners keep what they have. |

<Callout type="info">
Published is not the same as for sale. A published course with no price cannot be
bought — you can still enrol people into it yourself. See
[Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
</Callout>

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Enrollment Open and Archived?">
**Enrollment Open** off keeps the course in your catalogue but refuses new learners —
good for "this intake is full". **Archived** takes it out of the catalogue altogether —
good for "we do not teach this any more". In both cases the people already enrolled keep
their access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I turned on Enable Certificate but nobody is getting one.">
The switch allows a certificate; it does not decide who earns one. A learner also has to
meet the rules on the certificate type — usually passing everything that counts. See
[Certificate requirements](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-requirements),
which walks through why a certificate has not appeared.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Language and the flag buttons on step 1?">
**Language** says what language the teaching is in, and is a label on the course page.
The flag buttons let you write the title and description in several languages at once,
so a learner reading your site in Bangla sees the Bangla title. They are unrelated.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Maximum Enrollments is set but people are still getting in.">
On a cohort-based course the limit that bites is the batch's seat count, not this one.
Check the batch. See [Cohorts and batches](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/cohorts).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where did Pass Mark go?">
There is no pass mark on the course settings step. What counts as a pass is decided by
the grading scheme and the course's grading setup. See
[Grading schemes](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/grading-schemes).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
