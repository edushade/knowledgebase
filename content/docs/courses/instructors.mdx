Instructors are the people who teach. You can assign them to any course, learning path,
or cohort, give each one a role, and feature your best teachers so learners can discover
them.

## Instructor roles

When you assign someone, you choose a role:

| Role | Typical use |
|---|---|
| **Instructor** | The main teacher leading the course. |
| **Co-instructor** | A second teacher sharing the lead. |
| **Assistant** | A helper supporting the class — answering questions, grading, and so on. |

You can assign more than one person, and mix roles — for example, one instructor and two
assistants.

## How to assign an instructor

1. Open the course, learning path, or cohort you want.
2. Go to its **Instructors** area.
3. Click **Add Instructor** and choose the person.
4. Pick their **role**: instructor, co-instructor, or assistant.
5. Optionally turn on **Featured** to highlight them publicly.
6. Click **Save**.

## Featuring instructors

Mark an instructor as **featured** to highlight them. Your platform also has a public
list of instructors, so learners can browse and discover the people who teach. Featuring
your strongest teachers helps attract enrollments.

## What the learner sees

Learners see the assigned instructors on the course, path, or cohort page. Featured
instructors appear in your platform's public instructor list, where learners can browse
teachers and the courses they offer.

## Tips

- Assign at least one instructor to every offering so learners know who teaches it.
- Use assistants for large classes so questions and grading get covered.
- Feature your most experienced or popular teachers to build trust with new learners.
