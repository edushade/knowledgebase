title: "Instructors and Staff"
description: "Who teaches a programme, who helps run it, and what each of them can see."
---

Two different lists decide who is attached to a course, batch, learning path or event.
**Instructors** are the people whose names learners see. **Staff** are everyone else who
helps run it.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Instructors

Instructors are set on the programme itself — step 1 of a course or learning path, and
in the batch form for a cohort. **At least one is required**, and the form will not save
without one.

Learners see instructors on the course page and in the catalogue. On an **event** the
same list is shown as **Speakers**.

## Staff

Staff are added from the programme's **Staff** screen, and are not shown to learners.
Each person is given one relation:

| Role | Who it is for |
|---|---|
| **Coordinator** | Runs the batch day to day — schedules, chases, keeps it on track. |
| **Support** | Answers learners' questions. |
| **Assistant** | Helps with marking and content. |
| **Mentor** | Attached to learners rather than to the material. |

These labels describe the *job*, not the permissions. What a person can actually do
comes from their role — see
[People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles). Adding someone
as an Assistant does not by itself let them mark anything.

## Where staff are set

| Programme | Where |
|---|---|
| Course | The course's **Staff** screen |
| Batch | The batch's **Staff** screen |
| Learning path | The path's **Staff** screen |
| Event | The event's **Staff** screen |

A batch can have different staff from its course. That is the point: the June batch's
coordinator need not be the September one's.

## My Programmes

Anyone marked as an instructor gets **My Programmes** at the top of the Teach menu. It
lists only the programmes they are attached to — as instructor, speaker or staff — with
**Your role**, what they are **Part of**, and how many **Learners** are in it.

For someone who teaches two batches out of your forty courses, this is the only list
they need. An admin who teaches nothing sees it empty, which is why it only appears for
people marked as instructors.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The Instructors box is empty and I cannot save the course.">
Nobody on your team is marked as an instructor yet. Instructor is a property of the
person, set on their account — not something you can grant from inside the course form.
See [People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An instructor says they cannot see their course.">
Check two things. First, are they actually on that programme's instructor or staff list?
Second, does their role carry permission to open courses at all? Being named on a course
and being allowed to open courses are separate, and both are needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can an instructor only see their own learners?">
Yes — that is what **My Programmes** does, and the screens behind it are limited to the
programmes they are on. Note that a few academy-wide screens, such as the instructor
comparison in Analytics, are refused to someone limited to their own programmes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between an instructor and a Mentor?">
An instructor teaches the programme and learners see their name. A mentor is attached to
help learners and is not shown publicly. Use instructor for the person delivering the
course.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
