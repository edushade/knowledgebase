When your content is ready, a set of settings controls how it appears in your catalog and
how learners experience it. The same settings apply to both courses and learning paths.
This page is your checklist for going live.

## The settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Difficulty** | Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced. |
| **Language** | The main language of the course or path. |
| **Seat limit** | The maximum number of learners. Leave blank for unlimited. When set, your platform shows "seats remaining". |
| **Instructions** | Guidance shown to learners about how to take the course. |
| **Completion message** | The message learners see when they finish. |
| **Passing grade** | The score a learner needs to pass. |
| **Certificate** | Turn the completion certificate on or off. |
| **Password protection** | Require a password to open the course. |
| **Email notifications** | Turn course-related emails to learners on or off. |
| **Discussion** | Turn learner discussion on or off. |
| **Ratings** | Let learners rate the course on or off. |
| **Enrollment open or closed** | Control whether new learners can join right now. |
| **SEO slug and meta** | The web address and search preview text for the course page. |
| **Tags** | Keywords that help learners find it. |
| **Learning outcomes** | What learners will be able to do after finishing. |
| **Requirements** | What a learner should know or have before starting. |

## Statuses and the catalog

A course or path is always in one of these states:

- **Draft** — only you and your team can see it.
- **Published** — live in your catalog for everyone.
- **Private** — hidden from your catalog. New learners cannot find it, and you enroll
  learners into it directly.
- **Archived** — retired and no longer offered.

<Callout type="info">
Use **private** for an exclusive or invite-only course. It stays off your public catalog,
and you add learners yourself. See
[Admin and bulk enrollment](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/admin-and-bulk-enrollment).
</Callout>

## How to publish

1. Open your course or learning path.
2. Open its **Settings** and fill in the fields above.
3. Set the **seat limit**, **passing grade**, and turn on a **certificate** if you award
   one.
4. Choose whether **enrollment** is open.
5. Fill in the **SEO slug and meta** so the page reads well in your catalog and in search.
6. Change the status to **Published**.

## Managing many at once

From the courses and learning paths list, you can manage in bulk:

- **Duplicate** a course or path to start a similar one quickly.
- **Bulk delete** several at once.
- **Export to a spreadsheet (CSV)** to review your catalog outside the platform.
- **Import** courses or paths from a prepared file.

## What the learner sees

In your catalog, learners see published courses with their thumbnail, title, difficulty,
language, and tags. If you set a seat limit, they see "seats remaining". When enrollment
is closed, they cannot join. If a course is password protected, they must enter the
password to open it. Private courses do not appear at all — learners reach them only when
you enroll them.

## Tips

- Fill in the **SEO slug and meta** before publishing so your course page looks polished
  in search and previews.
- Set a **passing grade** and turn on a **certificate** together when you want learners to
  earn recognition.
- Use **duplicate** to spin up the next term's version, then edit the dates and details.
- Close **enrollment** when a cohort is full or a course is being updated.
