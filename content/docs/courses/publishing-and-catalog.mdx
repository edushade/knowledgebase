title: "Publishing and the Catalog"
description: "What publishing actually does, why a published course can still be invisible, and the four switches that decide whether anyone can buy it."
---

"Published" sounds like one thing and is really four. A course appears in your catalogue,
accepts new learners, is buyable, and shows its content — and each of those is a separate
switch. Most "my course is live but nothing is happening" questions are one of the four
being off.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The four switches

| Switch | Where it lives | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| **Status: Published** | The status button on the course settings step | Whether the course exists publicly at all |
| **Enrollment Open** | Course settings | Whether new learners may join |
| **A price** | [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing) | Whether anyone can buy it |
| **Item Published switches** | Each row in the course builder | Whether the content inside is visible |

All four have to line up. A published course with no price cannot be bought — you can
still enrol people into it yourself. A published course whose every item is unpublished
opens to an empty outline.

## Publishing a course

Open the course, go to the settings step, and use the status button at the top right:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Only your team can see it. Nothing is public. |
| **Published** | In the catalogue, open to enrolment. |
| **Archived** | Out of the catalogue. Existing learners keep their access. |

## Retiring a course

Three ways, in increasing severity:

1. **Enrollment Open off** — stays in the catalogue, refuses new learners. For "this
   intake is full".
2. **Archived** — out of the catalogue. For "we do not teach this any more".
3. **Deleted** — gone, along with its content, its marks and its progress. Rarely the
   right answer.

In the first two, people already enrolled keep everything.

## The catalogue

Your catalogue is the public list on your website. A published course appears there with
its thumbnail, title, category, difficulty, language and price.

What learners can do with that list — the filters, the sorting, the search — is part of
your website. See [Search and discovery](/docs/knowledgebase/search) and
[Pages and blocks](/docs/knowledgebase/website/pages-and-blocks).

## Keeping part of a course out of public view

**Hide from Website**, on an item's **⋯** menu in the builder, keeps that item out of the
outline shown on the public course page while leaving it fully available to enrolled
learners. Use it when the course page's outline would give away the answers, or is
simply too long to be useful as a sales pitch.

The opposite is **Free Preview**, on the same menu, which lets anyone open an item
without enrolling. One strong lesson on free preview does more than a paragraph of
description.

## Before you publish

- Every item that should be live has its **Published** switch on.
- The thumbnail and description read well at catalogue size.
- **Learning Outcomes** and **Requirements** are filled in — they are the two things
  buyers actually read.
- A price exists, or you have a reason there is none.
- You have opened the course as a learner would, not from the builder.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The course is published but not in my catalogue.">
Check the status really saved — the button reverts to **Draft** if a required field is
missing, and step 1 will show which. Then check whether your website's course list is
filtered to particular categories. See
[Pages and blocks](/docs/knowledgebase/website/pages-and-blocks).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Learners can see the course but cannot buy it.">
It has no price. Published and for sale are different things. See
[Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I publish a course before the content is finished?">
Yes, and it is the usual way to run a cohort. Publish the course, publish week one, and
publish the rest as you go — or use
[drip scheduling](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling) to release
material on dates you set now.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to learners if I archive a course?">
Nothing changes for them. They keep their access, their progress and their certificate.
Archiving only removes the course from the catalogue and stops new enrolments.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A cohort-based course is published but has no batches.">
Then nobody can enrol, because learners enrol into a batch and there is none. Make one
first. See [Cohorts and batches](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/cohorts).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
