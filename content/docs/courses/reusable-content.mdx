---
title: "Reusable Content"
description: "Build a section, lesson, or assignment once and attach it to as many courses as you like."
---

Some content belongs in more than one course — an onboarding lesson, a standard
assignment, or a whole section you reuse each term. Instead of rebuilding it every time,
you can create it once on its own and attach it wherever you need it.

## What you can reuse

You can create these on their own and attach them to a course later:

- **Sections** — a ready-made group of content.
- **Lessons** — a single lesson of any type.
- **Assignments** — a task learners submit.

Once created, the same item can be attached to many courses, so your content stays
consistent and you save time.

## How to create reusable content

1. Go to **Admin → Courses** and open the area for the item you want — **Lessons**,
   **Sections**, or **Assignments**.
2. Click **New** and build the item just like you would inside a course.
3. Click **Save**. The item now exists on its own, ready to attach.

## How to attach it to a course

1. Open the course you want and go to the **Curriculum** (content) area.
2. Click **Add** and choose to add an existing lesson, section, or assignment.
3. Pick the item you created earlier.
4. Click **Save**. It now appears in the course outline.

## Tips

- Build a small library of standard lessons (welcome, how-to-study, course rules) and
  reuse them across every course.
- Reusable assignments keep your grading consistent when you run the same task in
  different courses.
- To copy an entire course outline as a fresh starting point, use **clone the
  curriculum** from [Course structure](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/course-structure)
  instead.
