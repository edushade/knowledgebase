---
title: "Creating a Course"
description: "Build a course from an empty form to a published page — the three steps, what each field is for, and what to do when something will not save."
---

A course is your main teaching unit. Making one takes three steps: describe it, fill it
with content, then set the rules it runs by. You can stop after any step and come back —
nothing is live until you publish it.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

**Before you start** — you need at least one [category](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/categories-and-tags)
and one person marked as an instructor. The form will not save without both.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Decide whether it is self-paced or cohort-based

Open <Path>Courses</Path> and click **Add Course**, at the top right. The first thing
you are asked is the course type.

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/courses/choose-course-type.png"
  alt="A dialog headed Choose Course Type with two cards, Self Paced and Cohort-Based, and a Continue button"
  caption="This choice adds a step to the form later — cohort-based courses get a fourth step for their batches."
/>

**Self Paced** opens to a learner the moment they enrol and lets them move at their own
speed. **Cohort-Based** puts learners into a batch with a start date, moving through the
course together.

Choose one and click **Continue**. If you are not sure, read
[Self-paced or cohort-based](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/course-types) first — this is
the one choice that is awkward to change later.

</Step>

<Step>

### Describe the course

Step 1 of the form is everything a learner reads before they decide to enrol.

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/courses/create-course-step-1.png"
  alt="Step 1 of the Create Course form showing Course Title, Slug, Description, Category on the left and Thumbnail, Preview Media and Instructors on the right"
  caption="The small flag buttons beside Course Title and Description switch the language you are typing in."
/>

| Field | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Course Title** | The name learners see. Required. |
| **Slug** | The short name used in the course's web address. It fills itself in from the title; change it only if you want a tidier address. |
| **Description** | A few sentences on what the course teaches. Formatting, links and images are allowed. |
| **Category** | Which part of your catalogue it belongs to. Required, and you can pick more than one. |
| **Thumbnail** | The cover image in your catalogue and at the top of the course page. |
| **Preview Media** | An image or short video shown on the course page *instead of* the thumbnail — a taster someone can watch before enrolling. |
| **Instructors** | Who teaches it. Required — at least one. |

The flag buttons beside **Course Title** and **Description** let you write the same
field in each language your academy has switched on. A small amber dot on a flag means
that language is still empty; a dashed outline means the language has text saved but is
not switched on for your site. See [Languages](/docs/knowledgebase/website/languages).

</Step>

<Step>

### Add the content

Click the **2** at the top, or **Save Draft** and then **2**. This is the course builder,
where the course actually gets made.

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/courses/course-builder.png"
  alt="The course builder showing four tabs — Curriculum, Grading, Drip Schedule, Prerequisites — with buttons to add a Section, Lesson, Quiz, Assignment or File, and a list of course items each with a Published toggle"
  caption="Four tabs, one job each. Curriculum is where you build; the other three decide how it is marked, when it opens, and who may take it."
/>

On the **Curriculum** tab, add a **Section** first and then put **Lesson**, **Quiz**,
**Assignment** and **File** items inside it. Drag anything by the handle on its left to
reorder it. Each item has its own **Published** switch, so you can leave half-finished
material out of sight while the rest goes live.

The other three tabs can wait until the content exists:

- **Grading** — how marks are combined into a result. See [Course grading setup](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/course-grading-setup).
- **Drip Schedule** — release content over time instead of all at once. See [Drip scheduling](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling).
- **Prerequisites** — who is allowed to take the course. See [Eligibility rules](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/eligibility-rules).

Full detail: [The course builder](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/curriculum-builder) and
[Lesson types](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/lesson-types).

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the rules, then publish

Click **Settings** at the top right, or the **3**. This step holds everything that is not
content: how long the course is, whether enrolment is open, whether it awards a
certificate, and more.

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/courses/course-settings.png"
  alt="The course settings step with a Basic Settings card on the left and a Settings Behavior card on the right holding Enrollment Open, Enable Certificate and Enable Transcript switches"
  caption="Two cards: Basic Settings describes the course, Settings Behavior decides what it does."
/>

The settings worth getting right on day one are **Maximum Enrollments** (0 means
unlimited), **Enrollment Open**, and **Enable Certificate**. Everything else has a
sensible default. Each field is explained in
[Course settings](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/course-settings).

When you are ready, use the status button at the top right — it reads **Draft** until you
change it — and choose **Published**. A published course appears in your catalogue and
can be enrolled in.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

A published course is visible but not necessarily buyable. Two things are separate from
each other:

- **Publishing** puts the course in your catalogue.
- **Pricing** decides what it costs, and is set in
  [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing). A course with no price cannot
  be bought; you can still enrol people into it yourself.

## If something goes wrong

**"At least one instructor is required."** Step 1 will not save without someone in the
**Instructors** box. If the list looks empty, nobody on your team is marked as an
instructor yet — see [People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).

**The Category box has nothing in it.** Categories are made once, in
<Path>Taxonomy > Categories</Path>, and then reused. See
[Categories and tags](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/categories-and-tags).

**You cannot find the Publish button.** It is the status button at the top right of the
settings step, not a separate button. If it still says **Draft** after you choose
**Published**, the course is missing something required — step 1 will show which field.

**You picked the wrong course type.** The type is set when the course is created and the
form does not offer to change it afterwards. Build the course again under the right type
while it is still small.
