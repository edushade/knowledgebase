---
title: "Cohorts and Batches"
description: "A batch is the class learners actually join — its dates, seats, price, staff and schedule. Here is how to make one and what every field does."
---

On a cohort-based course, learners do not enrol into the course. They enrol into a
**batch** of it. The course is the syllabus; the batch is the class that runs from
June to September with forty seats and its own teacher.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Where batches live

Batches are not in the main menu. They belong to their course, and you reach them from
step **2** of a cohort-based course.

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/courses/cohorts-step.png"
  alt="The Edit Cohort Configuration step showing two batch cards side by side, each with its dates, seats taken and a Build Curriculum button, next to a Create New Cohort card"
  caption="Each card is one batch. Build Curriculum opens that batch's own content — batches of the same course do not have to teach the same thing."
/>

Each card shows the batch's length, how many of its seats are taken, its start and end
dates, and whether it is published. The buttons do three different jobs:

- **Build Curriculum** opens that batch's content.
- The **pencil** edits the batch's details — dates, seats, staff.
- The **bin** deletes the batch and everything attached to it.

## Making a batch

Click **Create Cohort**. The dialog opens with two ways to start:

- **Start from Scratch** — an empty form.
- **Re-use from Existing** — copy an existing batch. You are then asked whether to copy
  its content too: **Yes, include all content** or **No, create empty cohort**. Use this
  for the next intake of a course you already run.

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/courses/create-cohort.png"
  alt="The Create New Cohort dialog with Start from Scratch and Re-use from Existing tabs, and fields for Cohort Title, Cohort Code, Description, Max Enrollment and Status"
  caption="Breakpoints Preview on the right fills itself in once you have set the dates and the interval."
/>

### What the batch is called

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Cohort Title** | What learners see — "Spring 2026 Cohort", "Batch 12". Required. |
| **Cohort Code** | A short code used in certificate numbers. Leave it empty and it is worked out from the title, so "Cohort 14" becomes "C14". |
| **Description** | Optional. Shown to learners choosing between batches. |
| **Thumbnail** | A cover image for this batch. Without one, the course's thumbnail is used. |

### Who may join, and how many

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Max Enrollment** | Seats in this batch. **0 means unlimited.** This is the limit that counts — the course's own Maximum Enrollments does not apply to a batch. |
| **Status** | **Published** makes the batch joinable. A draft batch is invisible to learners. |
| **Enrollment Open** | Off refuses new learners without hiding the batch. |
| **Instructors** | Who teaches this batch. Required. A batch can have different teachers from the course. |

### When it runs

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/courses/create-cohort-schedule.png"
  alt="The lower half of the Create New Cohort dialog with Start Date and End Date, Certificate available from, Breakpoint Interval, Live Class Days, Allow Late Enrollment and live class times"
  caption="Breakpoint Interval is the one to think about — it decides how the batch is cut into terms, and it is awkward to change later."
/>

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start Date & Time** | When the batch begins. Required. |
| **End Date & Time** | When it finishes. Required. |
| **Certificate available from** | A date before which a passing learner still cannot download their certificate. Leave it empty and the batch's end date is used. |
| **Allow Late Enrollment** | On lets people join after the batch has started. Off closes it the moment teaching begins. See [Enrolment windows](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/enrollment-windows). |

### How the batch is divided

**Breakpoint Interval** cuts the batch into terms between its start and end dates:

| Choice | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Weekly** | One term per week. |
| **Bi-weekly** | One term per fortnight. |
| **Monthly** | One term per month. |
| **Custom Intervals** | You say how many — **Number of Breakpoints**, from 1 to 52 — and the dates are split evenly. This is what a semester programme uses. |
| **No Breakpoints** | One undivided batch. |

The **Breakpoints Preview** panel on the right fills in as you type, so you can see the
dates you are about to create before you commit to them. Full detail:
[Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints).

### Live classes

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Live Class Days** | Which days of the week this batch meets. Pick as many as you need. |
| **Live Class Start Time** / **Live Class End Time** | The usual class hours. |

These describe the batch's normal rhythm. An individual live session still carries its
own date and meeting link — see [Lesson types](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/lesson-types).

## Each batch has its own content

**Build Curriculum** on a batch card opens content that belongs to that batch alone.
Two batches of the same course can teach a different syllabus, which is what lets you
revise a programme between intakes without disturbing the batch already running.

## Managing a running batch

Open a batch and you get its own screens:

- **Learners** — who is in it, and their state.
- **Staff** — teachers, speakers and assistants on this batch. See [Instructors and staff](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/instructors).

The rest of the management screens — grades, progress, progression, reports — are
reached from the course and can be filtered to one batch.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I change a batch's dates after it has started?">
Yes, by editing the batch. Be careful if the batch has terms: moving the start or end
date does not silently redraw them, and anything already scheduled against the old dates
keeps the old dates. See
[Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints) for what has to
be regenerated.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner joined the wrong batch.">
Move them rather than re-enrolling them. A transfer carries their record across and lets
you decide whether their existing marks come with them. See
[Transfers](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/transfers).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why can nobody enrol, even though the batch is published?">
Work down this list: **Enrollment Open** is off; the seats are full; the batch has
already started and **Allow Late Enrollment** is off; or the batch has no price set and
nobody has been enrolled by hand. See
[Enrolment windows](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/enrollment-windows).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I price the course or the batch?">
The batch. Each one carries its own price, so an early batch can be cheaper than a later
one. See [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does Re-use from Existing copy?">
The batch's settings always. Its content only if you answer **Yes, include all content**.
It does not copy learners, marks or prices.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
