Every assignment produces a queue. This is how to work it without losing track of who
is still waiting.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Finding what is waiting

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| A course's **Assignments** screen | Each assignment, and its submissions. |
| An assignment's **Submissions** | Who has handed in, and what state each one is in. |
| <Path>Grading > Assignment Submissions</Path> | Everything waiting, across every programme. |

Work from the assignment's own submissions list while a batch is handing in, and from
the academy-wide list when you are clearing a backlog across several courses.

## Marking one

Open a submission and you get **Grade Submission** — "Review and grade the learner's
assignment submission":

| Field | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Score** | The learner. Out of the assignment's total marks. |
| **Feedback** | The learner. |
| **Comments (Internal)** | Only you and your colleagues — never the learner. |

Save, and the submission is graded. The learner's score appears and the programme's
result picks it up.

### What internal comments are for

Not a second opinion of the learner — a record for the next person who looks at this
mark:

- "Second marker agreed 62."
- "Submitted two days late; extension agreed by email."
- "Same structure as last year's submission from another learner — checked, different work."

Six months later, when a mark is queried, that line is the difference between an answer
and a guess.

### Feedback that is worth the time

The learner reads one thing and acts on one thing. Three sentences beats a page:

1. What worked.
2. The one change that would most improve it.
3. What to do next.

"Good work, 7/10" teaches nothing. Neither does a paragraph nobody finishes.

## Marking a batch fairly

- **Mark question by question** where the assignment has parts — your standard stays
  steady across thirty papers.
- **Write the common feedback once** and adapt it. Most of a batch makes the same two
  or three mistakes.
- **Hold the scores** until you have marked everyone, where the assignment's programme
  allows it. Learners comparing marks mid-marking helps nobody.

## After marking

Assignment marks feed the programme's result, with the weight you gave them. After a
batch of marking, run **Calculate All Grades** so the subject and final results catch
up. See [Learner grades](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/learner-grades).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner's assignment shows no mark.">
Nobody has marked it. A submission is not a grade — it waits in the queue until someone
opens it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner submitted the wrong file.">
Reset their submission so they can hand in again, or raise the maximum attempts for the
assignment. See [Attempts and retakes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/attempts-and-retakes).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I mark anonymously?">
No. The learner's name is on the submission.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner see my internal comments?">
No. **Feedback** is theirs; **Comments (Internal)** is yours. They are separate boxes
on the same screen, so check which one you are typing in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An unmarked assignment is dragging results down.">
That is **Treat Ungraded Items as Zero** being on. Off, unmarked work is left out until
it is marked. See
[Course grading setup](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/course-grading-setup).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
