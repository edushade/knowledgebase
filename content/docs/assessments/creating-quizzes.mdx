---
title: "Creating a Quiz"
description: "Building a quiz in three steps — details, questions, settings — and attaching it to the course that should use it."
---

A quiz is built once and can be used in several courses. It has its own list at
<Path>Quizzes</Path>, and its own three-step builder.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

**Before you start** — decide whether this quiz counts. A practice quiz and a final exam
are built the same way but settled very differently at step 3.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Describe the quiz

From <Path>Quizzes</Path>, choose **Create Quiz**. Step 1 takes:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Quiz Title** | Required. Up to 200 characters, and writable in each of your languages. |
| **Description** | What it covers. |
| **Quiz Type** | Your own classification — "Weekly test", "Final exam". See below. |
| **Categories** | For finding it again in a long list. |
| **Difficulty Level** | Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced. |
| **Thumbnail** | Optional cover image. |
| **Instructions** | Shown before a learner starts: rules, materials allowed, what to do if something breaks. |

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/assessments/create-quiz-step-1.png"
  alt="Step 1 of the quiz builder, with title, categories, description and instructions on the left and type and difficulty on the right."
  caption="The small flags beside Title and Description switch between your languages. A dot on a flag means that language is still empty."
/>

**Continue** at the end of step 1 creates the quiz — it appears in your list straight
away as a draft, so nothing is lost if you stop here. **Save Draft** does the same
without moving on. Nothing is visible to learners until you publish at the end.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add the questions

Step 2 is the question builder. **Add Question** opens the type picker.

Six types are available, and all six mark themselves — a quiz needs no marking queue. If
you need written work, that is an
[assignment](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/assignments) rather than a quiz question.
See [Question types](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/question-types).

Each question takes a title, a score and its own options or pairs. Drag to reorder.
**Preview** shows the quiz as a learner will meet it — worth doing before you publish.

You can also pull questions from the
[Content Bank](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/content-bank) rather than writing them here.

</Step>

<Step>

### Settle how it is sat

Step 3 is the settings: the time limit, how many attempts, the pass mark, whether
answers are shown afterwards, and when scores appear.

This is where a practice quiz and an exam part company. Full detail:
[Quiz settings](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-settings).

Then **Publish**.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Putting it in a course

A published quiz is not yet in anything. Open the course builder, use **+ Quiz**, and
pick it. The same quiz can go into several courses — see
[Reusable content](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/reusable-content) for what that means
when you edit it later.

Once it is in a course you can also set, from the item's **⋯** menu:

- **Completion Rule** — whether sitting it is enough, or it must be passed.
- **Drip Schedule** — when it opens and closes.
- **Access Rules** — who may take it.

## Quiz types

A **quiz type** is your own label — "Class test", "Mock exam", "Final". Manage them from
**Manage Quiz Types**.

They are worth setting up early: the
[assessment report](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/assessment-report) and the
analytics both filter by type, so "how did everyone do on the mock exams?" is a filter
rather than a spreadsheet.

## What else a quiz gives you

From a quiz's own screens:

| Screen | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Questions** | The paper itself. |
| **Participants** | Who has sat it. |
| **Non-participants** | Who has not — the list you actually act on. |
| **Reports** | Problems learners reported with its questions. |
| **Reschedules** | Extra time or a second window for named learners. |
| **Leaderboard** | Ranking by score. |
| **Preview** | The learner's view. |

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can the system write questions for me?">
No. There is no question generation available.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I published the quiz but learners cannot find it.">
A published quiz still has to be added to a course. Until it is, it exists only in your
quiz list.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I copy a quiz?">
Yes — **Duplicate Quiz** from the quiz list. You get an independent copy to edit without
disturbing the original.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change a quiz learners have already taken?">
You can, and you should think twice. Editing a question does not re-mark attempts
already sat, so you end up with two groups marked by different papers. See
[Grading quizzes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/grading-quizzes).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I edit several quizzes at once?">
Yes. Select them in the quiz list and bulk-edit their settings. You are shown what is
about to change, across how many quizzes, before it is applied.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
