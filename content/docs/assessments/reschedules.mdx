---
title: "Reschedules"
description: "Giving named learners their own window on a quiz, without moving the deadline for everyone else."
---

Somebody was ill. Somebody's connection failed in the last ten minutes. Somebody has an
agreed arrangement. A reschedule gives those learners their own window on a quiz and
leaves the deadline standing for the rest.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Giving one

Open the quiz and go to its **Reschedules** screen, then **Re-schedule quiz for
selected users**:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Program** | Which course this applies to. A shared quiz is rescheduled per course. |
| **Unlock at** | When these learners may start. Required. |
| **Lock at** | When their window closes. Optional — leave it empty for no closing time. |

Then choose the learners. Existing reschedules are listed so you can edit or remove
them.

Removing a reschedule puts those learners back on the quiz's normal schedule.

## When to reschedule, and when not to

| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| A learner missed the window | **Reschedule** |
| A learner needs longer *during* the attempt | Not available — the time limit is quiz-wide. Reschedule them for a fresh attempt instead. |
| A learner used up their attempts | Give another attempt — see [Attempts and retakes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/attempts-and-retakes) |
| A whole group missed it | Change the quiz's availability, or set a [makeup](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/makeup-assessments) |
| The whole batch is behind | Move the course's drip schedule, not the quiz |

## Why this rather than reopening the quiz

Reopening a quiz for one learner opens it for everyone, including the ones who have
already finished and would be glad of another go. A reschedule is narrow, and it leaves
a record of who was given extra time and when — which is the thing you will want when
somebody asks why.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="I rescheduled a learner and they still cannot open it.">
Check the **Program** on the reschedule. A quiz used in two courses needs the
reschedule on the course the learner is actually taking it in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a reschedule give a fresh attempt?">
No. It changes *when* they can sit it, not how many times. If they have used their
attempts, give them another one as well.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I reschedule a whole batch?">
You can select many learners at once. For a genuine whole-batch change, move the quiz's
availability instead — it is one change rather than a list.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a rescheduled attempt count differently?">
No. It is an ordinary attempt, marked by the programme's usual rules.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
