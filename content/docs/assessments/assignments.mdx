An assignment is work handed in rather than answered: an essay, a file, a project. Every
one is marked by a person, so an assignment is a promise of your time as much as the
learner's.

Assignments are added from the course builder with **+ Assignment**, or from an item's
**⋯** menu.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## What you set

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Assignment Title** | Required. Up to 100 characters. |
| **Description** | What it is about. |
| **Instructions** | The brief — what to produce, in what form, how it will be judged. |
| **Total Marks** | What it is out of. At least 1. |
| **Passing Marks** | The minimum to pass. |
| **Maximum attempts** | How many times a learner may submit. |
| **Time limit** | Optional. |
| **Attachments** | Materials that come with the brief — a template, a dataset, a rubric. |
| **Status** | **Draft** or **Published**. |

### Merit list

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Show Merit List To Learner** | Whether learners see the ranking at all. | On |
| **Show Other Merit List** | Whether they see other learners' positions, or only their own. | On |

See [Merit lists](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/merit-lists).

## The scheduling trap

**An assignment has no due date of its own.** There is no deadline field on the
assignment form.

The dates come from the [drip schedule](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling)
on the assignment item in the course: **unlock** is when it opens, **lock** is the
deadline.

<Callout type="warn">
Setting up an assignment and expecting a deadline to appear is the most common mistake
here. If you did not set a lock date on the item, the assignment has no deadline —
whatever the instructions say.
</Callout>

To extend the deadline for one learner, use a user-level override on that item rather
than moving the date for everyone.

## When does it count as done?

By default, a learner who submits has completed the assignment — whether or not it has
been marked, and whatever mark it gets.

Change that from the item's **⋯** menu, **Completion Rule**:

- **Attempt only** — submitting is enough. The default behaviour.
- **Attempted with a Pass** — it must reach the passing marks.

See [Completion rules](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/completion-rules).

## Marking

Submissions arrive in the course's **Assignments** screen. Open one and you get **Grade
Submission**:

| Field | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Score** | The learner. |
| **Feedback** | The learner. |
| **Comments (Internal)** | Only you and your colleagues. |

Internal comments are where you record "second marker agreed", "late by two days,
allowed" — the context that matters next term when somebody queries the mark.

See [Assignment submissions](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/assignment-submissions).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Where do I set the due date?">
On the assignment's item in the course, under **Drip Schedule** — the **lock** date is
the deadline. There is no due date on the assignment itself.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner submitted after the deadline.">
If a lock date was set, they could not have — so either there was none, or they have an
override. Check the item's drip schedule and its user-level overrides.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can learners submit more than once?">
Up to the maximum attempts you set. Which submission counts follows the programme's
replacement policy, the same as a quiz — see
[Retakes and replacement](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/retakes-and-replacement).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can two instructors mark the same assignment?">
Both can open and mark it, and the last save stands. Use **Comments (Internal)** to
record who marked it and what was agreed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does an unmarked assignment pull a learner's result down?">
Only if **Treat Ungraded Items as Zero** is on for the programme. Off, it is left out
until it is marked. See
[Course grading setup](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/course-grading-setup).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
