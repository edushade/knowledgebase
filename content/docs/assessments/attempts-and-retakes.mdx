title: "Attempts and Retakes"
description: "What an attempt is, the five states it can be in, how many a learner gets, and how to clear one."
---

Every time a learner starts a quiz, that is an attempt. How many they may have is set
on the quiz; which one counts is set on the programme. The two are separate and both
have to line up.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The five states

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **In progress** | The learner is sitting it now. |
| **Submitted** | They have handed it in. Every quiz question marks itself, so this normally becomes **Graded** in the same moment. |
| **Graded** | Marked, with a score. |
| **Expired** | The time ran out and it was submitted for them. |
| **Abandoned** | Started and never finished or submitted. |

**Abandoned** is the one worth watching. A learner who opened a quiz, lost their
connection and never came back has used an attempt and has nothing to show for it — and
they will tell you it was not their fault. It usually was not.

## How many attempts

**Maximum Attempts** on the quiz, at least 1. A learner who has used them all cannot
start another.

The learner sees exactly where they stand the moment they finish:

  caption="Attempt 1 of 2 — 1 attempt remaining. Try Again appears only while they have one left."

The result breaks down into **Correct**, **Incorrect**, **Partial** and **Skipped**, and
then by question type — so a learner who failed can see it was the dropdown questions,
not the whole subject. **Download result** gives them a copy, and **Review Quiz** walks
them back through the paper where the quiz allows it.

## Which attempt counts

Set on the **programme**, not the quiz — **Allow Grade Replacement** and its policy:

| Policy | The mark is |
|---|---|
| **Replace** | The most recent attempt. Also what you get with replacement off. |
| **Average** | The average of the attempts that have been marked. |
| **Best** | The highest. |

See [Retakes and replacement](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/retakes-and-replacement).

<Callout type="info">
Three attempts under **Best** is practice: a learner can only improve. Three attempts
under **Replace** is three chances to do worse than the first. Pick the pair, not just
the number.
</Callout>

## Giving a learner another go

Two ways, and they are not the same:

- **Raise Maximum Attempts** — for everyone. Right when the quiz was too short, or
  something went wrong for the whole batch.
- **Reset the attempt** — for one learner. Clears that attempt and its mark, so they can
  sit it again.

**Reset this attempt** is on the attempt itself. It asks first, and it cannot be undone.

## Deleting an attempt

Deleting an attempt removes it and its mark. The learner's progress and grade are
reworked to match — so under a **Best** policy, deleting their best attempt promotes
their next best automatically.

That is worth knowing before you delete anything: the number will move, and it should.

## Shared quizzes

A quiz used in several courses keeps attempts per enrolment, so marks do not leak
between courses. But resetting a learner's attempt on a shared quiz affects it
everywhere they have taken it — the confirmation names the programmes involved. Read it.

## After clearing anything

Resetting or deleting an attempt changes the marks below the programme's result. Run
**Calculate All Grades** so the subject, semester and final figures agree. See
[Learner grades](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/learner-grades).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner's attempt shows 0% right after submitting.">
It has not been marked. Under a **Replace** policy the most recent attempt is the mark,
and an unmarked attempt has nothing in it. Once it is marked, the real score appears.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner says they were kicked out mid-quiz.">
Look at the attempt's state. **Expired** means the time limit ran out and it was
submitted for them. **Abandoned** means it was never submitted at all — a closed tab, a
lost connection.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner resume an attempt they left?">
Only where the quiz allows pausing. Otherwise the clock kept running while they were
away.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I deleted an attempt and the learner's grade changed on its own.">
That is correct. Progress and grade are reworked after a deletion, so under **Best**
the next-highest attempt takes over.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I give extra time rather than another attempt?">
You cannot extend a running attempt. Reschedule the learner so they can sit it again in
their own window — see [Reschedules](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/reschedules).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
