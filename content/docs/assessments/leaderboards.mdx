title: "Leaderboards"

A leaderboard ranks the learners in a programme. Unlike a
[merit list](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/merit-lists), which covers one assessment,
a leaderboard looks at the whole course or learning path.

It is a tab on the programme: **Leaderboard**.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Five ways to rank

| Ranked by | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Overall Grade** | Who is doing best overall. |
| **By Quiz** | Who is strongest on one quiz. |
| **By Assignment** | Who is strongest on one assignment. |
| **By Progress** | Who has got furthest through the content. |
| **By Section** | Who is strongest in one part of the course. |

**By Progress** is the one people overlook. Ranking by how much has been *done* rather
than how well surfaces the learners who are quietly working, and the ones who stopped in
week two.

## What it shows

Rank, student, and the figures that fit the ranking — quizzes completed, assignments
completed, overall grade, section scores. It can be filtered by gender where you hold
that, for institutions that report separately.

Each quiz also has its own leaderboard, reached from the quiz itself.

## Before you show one to learners

A leaderboard is motivating for the people near the top and demoralising for everyone
else. Two things make it kinder:

- **Rank by progress, not grade.** Effort is something every learner can move.
- **Use the merit list settings** to let a learner see their own position without the
  whole list. See [Merit lists](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/merit-lists).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Why is a learner missing from the leaderboard?">
They have nothing to rank yet — no completed assessments, or no released results.
Learners with partial access are ranked on what they hold.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the leaderboard use released results only?">
Yes. A result that is held does not appear, which is why a leaderboard can look thin
immediately after an exam.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I rank across two courses?">
No. A leaderboard covers one programme. For a track made of several courses, use a
[learning path](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/learning-paths) — its leaderboard covers the
whole path.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a leaderboard for streaks or points?">
No. Rankings come from grades and progress. See
[Learning streaks](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/learning-streaks).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
