title: "Merit Lists"
description: "A ranked list of results for one exam or assignment — what it shows, and the two switches that decide who sees it."
---

A merit list ranks everyone who sat one assessment, best first. It is the familiar
results sheet: rank, name, score, out of, pass or fail, grade.

Every quiz and every assignment can have one.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor, Learner" />

## What is on it

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Position, best first. |
| **Learner** | Who they are. |
| **Score** | What they got. |
| **Total Marks** | What it was out of. |
| **Result Status** | **Passed** or **Not passed**. |
| **Grade** | Their band, where the programme has a grading scheme. |

## Who sees it

Two settings, on each quiz and each assignment:

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Show Merit List To Learner** | Whether learners see a merit list at all. | On |
| **Show Other Merit List** | Whether they see other learners' rows, or only their own. | On |

The pairing matters:

- **Both on** — a public ranking. Everyone sees everyone.
- **First on, second off** — each learner sees their own position and nothing else. They
  know they came 7th without knowing who came 6th.
- **First off** — no merit list for learners. You still have it.

<Callout type="info">
Turning the second one off is the setting most academies actually want. A learner knowing
their own rank is motivating; a published list of who came last is not.
</Callout>

## Results have to be released first

A merit list shows released results only. A learner looking before release sees *"No
results have been released yet."*

That is the quiz's score-release setting — see
[Quiz settings](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-settings). Hold the scores until
marking is finished, then release everyone at once, and the merit list appears complete
rather than filling in as you mark.

## Where to find it

- **You** — from the quiz or the assignment.
- **A learner** — from their exam or assignment, as **Merit List**.

## Merit list or leaderboard?

Easy to confuse:

| | **Merit list** | **Leaderboard** |
|---|---|---|
| Covers | One assessment | A whole programme |
| Ranks by | Score on that assessment | Points across everything |
| Shows | Rank, score, pass, grade | Rank, name, points |

See [Leaderboards](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/leaderboards).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says the merit list is empty.">
Results have not been released. Check the quiz's score-release settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I hide particular learners from it?">
Not individually. The choice is all rows, their own row only, or no merit list.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Two learners have the same score. Who ranks higher?">
They share the position in the ordering. The list does not invent a tie-break.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the merit list follow the course's grading scheme?">
The **Grade** column does — it uses the programme's scheme, so the band and the pass
line are the ones you set. See
[Grading schemes](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/grading-schemes).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
