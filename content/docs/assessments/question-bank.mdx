The question bank is the **Question** collection of the
[Content Bank](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/content-bank). A question kept there belongs
to your academy rather than to one quiz, and can be pulled into any paper.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Why bother

Three reasons, in order of how much time they save:

1. **Next year's paper.** A bank of 200 questions makes a new exam an afternoon of
   selection rather than a week of writing.
2. **Several versions of the same exam.** Different questions on the same material is
   the most effective integrity measure available to you — see
   [Keeping an exam honest](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/proctoring).
3. **Questions that have been proved.** A question whose
   [analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-analytics) show it works is worth
   keeping; one everybody gets right is not.

## What the bank holds

Each question carries its text, its **Type**, its **Marks**, whether it is **Required**,
and whether it is **Auto Grade**d. Two filters make a long list usable:

- **Question Type** — narrow to one kind.
- **Auto Gradable** — the questions that mark themselves, or the ones that need a person.

The second filter is the practical one when you are building a paper to a time budget:
a quiz of twenty auto-marked questions costs you nothing to mark, and one with five
essays costs you an evening.

## The copy rule

**A banked question pulled into a quiz becomes that quiz's own copy.**

- Editing the bank afterwards does **not** change quizzes that already took it.
- Editing the quiz's copy does **not** change the bank.

That is deliberate: a question a cohort has already answered must not change under them.
It also means a mistake fixed in the bank has to be fixed again in any live quiz that
took it.

## Keeping it usable

A bank nobody can search is a bank nobody uses:

- **Write the question so it reads alone.** "Which of these is true?" tells you nothing
  in a list of 900.
- **Archive rather than delete.** An archived question is out of the way but still
  there when you need to reconstruct an old paper.
- **Review after each sitting.** The quiz analytics tell you which questions earned
  their place.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I import questions from a spreadsheet?">
No. Questions are written in the bank or in a quiz. Courses and learning paths have an
import; questions do not. See
[Importing and exporting](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/importing-and-exporting).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I fixed a question in the bank but the quiz is still wrong.">
The quiz holds its own copy. Fix it there too — and if learners have already sat it,
correct their marks as well. See
[Grading quizzes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/grading-quizzes).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a quiz draw random questions from the bank?">
No. You choose which questions go into a quiz. To vary a paper, build more than one
version from the bank.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who can see the bank?">
Anyone whose role lets them see course content. It is shared across your academy.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
