Assessments are how you check what your learners have understood. This section covers
**quizzes**, **assignments**, and **exams** — from a quick five-question check to a timed,
proctored final. Many question types grade themselves the moment a learner submits, so
you can give instant feedback without lifting a finger. Others you grade by hand with your
own comments and scores.

## What you can build

| Assessment | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Quiz** | A set of questions of many types, with timers, attempts, and instant or manual grading. | Knowledge checks, practice tests, graded exams. |
| **Assignment** | A task where learners submit text and/or files, which you score by hand. | Essays, projects, file submissions, take-home work. |
| **Exam** | A quiz placed inside course content as a graded assessment that feeds the final grade. | Midterms, finals, end-of-term tests. |

## What you can do

- Choose from a wide range of **question types** — multiple choice, true/false, matching,
  fill-in-the-blank, numeric, essay, file upload, and many more.
- Set **time limits**, **attempt limits**, and **retake rules**.
- **Shuffle** questions and answer options so no two attempts look alike.
- Turn on **proctoring** — full-screen lock, webcam, tab-switch alerts, and more.
- Show a **leaderboard** ranking learners by their best score.
- Grade by hand with **feedback and score overrides** where a question needs a human.
- Group a main test and a makeup into one slot so a learner can take **either one**.
- See **detailed analytics** at the quiz, question, and answer level.
