---
title: "Quiz Settings"
description: "Every setting on a quiz, grouped by what it protects — time, attempts, order, scoring, and who sees what."
---

A quiz's settings decide how it is sat and when its score appears. They are grouped on
the quiz's **Settings** step.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Timing

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Time limit** | Switch on, then set the minutes. | Off |
| **Allow Pause** | Lets a learner stop the clock and come back. | Off |

Leave **Allow Pause** off for anything that counts. On, the time limit stops meaning
very much.

## Attempts and participants

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Maximum Attempts** | How many times a learner may sit it. At least 1. | 1 |
| **Maximum Participants** | A cap on how many people may take it. Switch on, then set the number. | Off |

Which attempt actually counts is not set here — it comes from the programme's
[grade replacement policy](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/retakes-and-replacement).
A quiz allowing three attempts under a **Best** policy is practice; the same quiz under
**Replace** is three chances to do worse.

## Order and completeness

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Shuffle questions** | Each learner gets the questions in a different order. | Off |
| **Shuffle answer options** | Options are scrambled within each question. | Off |
| **Require All Questions** | A learner cannot submit with anything blank. | Off |

Shuffling is the most practical thing you can do about learners sitting a quiz side by
side. It does not stop anyone determined, but it stops "what did you put for 3?".

## Scoring

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Pass Score Enabled** | Whether this quiz has a pass mark at all. | On |
| **Pass Score** | The minimum percentage required to pass. | 70 |
| **Show answer after activity** | Whether learners see the correct answers afterwards. | Off |

## When the score appears

This is the group people get wrong, so it is worth being exact:

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Show each learner's score as soon as they submit** | The learner sees their score immediately. | On |
| **Release scores on** | A date and time that releases everyone's score at once. | None |

Switch the first **off** and a learner's score waits — for the release date if you set
one, or for someone to mark it if you did not.

<Callout type="info">
Release everyone at once for an exam. It stops the first learner out of the room
telling the queue how they did, and it gives you time to check the paper before anyone
sees a mark.
</Callout>

## Access

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Password protected** | A password learners must type to start. | Off |

A password suits an invigilated sitting, where you give it out in the room. It is not
much use for a quiz taken at home.

## Merit list

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Show Merit List To Learner** | Whether learners see the ranking at all. | On |
| **Show Other Merit List** | Whether they see other learners' positions, or only their own. | On |

See [Merit lists](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/merit-lists).

## The rest

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Completion Message** | What a learner reads when they finish. |
| **Instructions** | Shown before they start — rules, materials allowed, what to do if something breaks. |
| **Certificate** | Whether this quiz can award one on its own. |

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner's attempt ran out of time and submitted itself.">
That is what a time limit does. Whether the attempt counts depends on the programme's
replacement policy — see
[Attempts and retakes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/attempts-and-retakes).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Learners say they cannot see their score.">
**Show each learner's score as soon as they submit** is off. They are waiting for the
release date, or for someone to mark the written questions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I give one learner extra time?">
Not on the quiz's time limit. Give them a reschedule instead — see
[Reschedules](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/reschedules).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Required on a question and Require All Questions?">
**Required** is per question. **Require All Questions** applies to the whole quiz at
once. Use the quiz-wide one for an exam where nothing may be skipped.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I show the answers afterwards?">
For practice, yes — that is where the learning happens. For anything that counts, no:
the first learners through will hand the answers to the rest.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
