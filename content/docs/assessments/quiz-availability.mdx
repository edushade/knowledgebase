---
title: "Quiz Availability"
description: "When a quiz opens and closes, and how to give one learner a different window."
---

A quiz's availability is not a setting on the quiz. It belongs to the quiz *in a
course* — which is what lets the same quiz be a practice test in one course and a timed
exam in another.

It is set from the quiz item's **⋯** menu in the course builder, under **Drip
Schedule**.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Opening and closing

| You set | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Unlock** | When the quiz becomes available. |
| **Lock** | When it closes again. **No Lock** leaves it open. |

The unlock can be a fixed date, a period after the learner enrolled, a period after the
previous item opened, or on completion of something else. See
[Drip scheduling](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling) for all six
strategies.

<Callout type="info">
**Lock is what makes an exam window.** Without one, a quiz that "ran on Tuesday" is
still open on Friday — and a learner who sits it late is indistinguishable from one who
sat it on the day.
</Callout>

## Availability is not the time limit

Two different clocks, and people confuse them constantly:

| | **Availability window** | **Time limit** |
|---|---|---|
| Set on | The quiz item in the course | The quiz's settings |
| Means | The quiz exists between these times | A learner has this many minutes once they start |
| Example | Open 6pm–8pm on Tuesday | 45 minutes |

A quiz can have both: a window of two hours in which to start, and 45 minutes once you
do.

## One learner, a different window

**Re-schedule quiz for selected users**, from the quiz's **Reschedules** screen, gives
named learners their own window:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Program** | Which course this reschedule applies to. |
| **Unlock at** | Required. |
| **Lock at** | Optional. |

Use it for the learner who was ill, the one with an agreed extension, the one whose
connection failed halfway through. It leaves the deadline intact for everyone else, and
leaves a record of who was given extra.

See [Reschedules](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/reschedules).

## What the learner sees

A quiz that has not opened shows as **locked**, with the date it will open. A quiz that
has locked is no longer available to start. In both cases they are told — availability
is one of the few access rules that explains itself.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner started before the lock time and was still cut off.">
Check whether it was the lock or the time limit. A lock closes the quiz; a time limit
ends that learner's attempt. Both can submit an attempt automatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The same quiz needs different dates in two courses.">
That is how it already works — availability belongs to the quiz item in each course, so
set them separately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I open a quiz for just one learner?">
Yes, with a reschedule. Leave the quiz locked for everyone and give that learner their
own unlock time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I locked a quiz by accident and learners lost attempts in progress.">
Attempts already submitted are safe. Any in progress end. Reopen it and give the
affected learners a reschedule or another attempt.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
