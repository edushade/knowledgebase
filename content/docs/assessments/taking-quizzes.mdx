description: "What a learner actually goes through, so you can answer their questions without taking the quiz yourself."
---

This is the learner's side. Knowing it saves you from guessing when somebody says "it
submitted on its own" or "it says I already did it".

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## Starting

A learner opens the quiz and is asked to confirm: *"Are you sure you want to start this
quiz? Once started, the quiz must be completed."* That warning is real — on a timed
quiz, the clock starts here.

If the quiz is password protected they are asked for the password before anything else.

If they have sat it before, they see their history first: how many attempts they have
used out of the maximum, their highest score, their average, and each attempt listed.

## While they are in it

| What they get | Notes |
|---|---|
| **One question at a time** | With **Previous** and **Next**. |
| **A timer** | Time left, where there is a limit. Some questions can carry their own. |
| **Quiz Navigation** | Every question, filterable by **Answered**, **Unanswered** and **Marked for Review**. |
| **Flag question** | Marks a question to come back to before submitting. |
| **Report a problem** | Flags a broken or wrong question to you. See [Reported problems](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/reported-problems). |

The navigation panel is the thing most learners miss. It is how they find the three
questions they skipped.

## Submitting

Submitting asks them to confirm, and tells them how many answers are still blank —
*"3 answers left"*, *"Answer all questions to get full score"*. Where **Require All
Questions** is on, they cannot submit until everything is answered.

**When time runs out** the quiz submits itself. The learner is told to keep the page
open while it does. If they are offline at that moment, they are told it will be
submitted as soon as they are back online — so the honest answer to "it submitted
itself" is yes, that is what a time limit does.

## Afterwards

What they see next depends on the quiz's settings:

| Setting | What they see |
|---|---|
| Score shown immediately | Their score, the result, and a message pitched to how they did. |
| Score held | *"Under review"* until you release it. |
| Answers shown after | The correct answers and any explanations you wrote. |
| Answers not shown | Their score only. |

An attempt still in progress reports *"Quiz not submitted yet — pass/fail and final
scores are shown only after you submit"*, which is the honest answer to a learner asking
why their half-finished attempt shows nothing.

Where a merit list is allowed, they can see where they came — see
[Merit lists](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/merit-lists).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says the quiz submitted itself.">
The time ran out. The attempt is marked **expired**, and it counts as an attempt. If the
cause was a failure on your side rather than theirs, reset the attempt or give them
another. See [Attempts and retakes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/attempts-and-retakes).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner lost their connection mid-quiz.">
Their answers so far are held and the quiz submits when they are back online, if the
time ran out while they were away. If they come back in time they can carry on.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner says they cannot start.">
Check in order: has it opened yet, has it locked, have they used their attempts, is
there a password they do not have. See
[Quiz availability](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-availability).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner go back to a question?">
Yes, with Previous and the navigation panel, until they submit. **Flag question** is
how they keep track of the ones to return to.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does a learner see no score after submitting?">
Either the quiz has written questions waiting to be marked, or scores are held until a
release time. See [Quiz settings](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-settings).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
