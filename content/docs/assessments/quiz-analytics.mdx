Analytics turn quiz results into insight. You can see how a quiz performed overall, which
questions tripped learners up, and even which answer options were most chosen. You can also
manage your quizzes in bulk and recover ones deleted by mistake.

## Levels of detail

You can look at results at three levels:

| Level | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Quiz level** | Overall performance — average scores, pass rate, attempts, and how learners did as a group. |
| **Question level** | How each question performed — how many got it right or wrong. |
| **Answer-option level** | For choice questions, which options learners picked — including popular wrong answers. |

<Callout type="info">
A question almost everyone gets wrong may be too hard, badly worded, or have an incorrect
answer key. Cross-check it against [question reports](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/question-reports).
</Callout>

## Tracking who hasn't attempted

Analytics can show you the **non-participants** — learners who have not yet taken the quiz.
Use this to follow up before a deadline, send a reminder, or plan a makeup.

## Managing quizzes in bulk

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Duplicate** | Make a copy of a quiz to reuse as a starting point. |
| **Bulk export** | Download quiz data as a spreadsheet (CSV) file. |
| **Bulk import** | Upload questions in bulk from a spreadsheet (CSV) file. |
| **Recover deleted** | Restore a quiz that was deleted by mistake. |

These tools save time when you run many quizzes or want to build a question library.

## Putting analytics to use

- **Improve weak questions** — rewrite or replace questions that consistently confuse
  learners.
- **Set a fair pass mark** — use the overall results to check your pass mark isn't too harsh
  or too easy. See [Quiz settings](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-settings).
- **Follow up with learners** — use the non-participant list to nudge those who haven't taken
  it.
- **Review the merit list** — see top performers alongside the
  [leaderboard](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/leaderboards).
