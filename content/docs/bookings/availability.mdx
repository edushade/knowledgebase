Availability is when you are bookable. Without it, a meeting plan exists and offers no
slots at all.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Setting your hours

Hours are set per day of the week — Monday to Sunday — with a start and an end time.
A day with no hours is a day nobody can book.

**Copy to all days** takes the hours you have set and applies them everywhere, which is
the fast way to "9 to 5, weekdays" and then remove the two days you do not work.

## Timezone

Your availability is in a timezone, and invitees see slots in theirs. Set it correctly:
an hour's difference is the sort of mistake that produces a missed meeting and a
conversation about whose fault it was.

## What narrows the slots further

Availability says when you *could* be booked. Four other things cut it down:

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Buffer before** / **after** | Holds time either side of each meeting. |
| **Minimum notice** | Removes slots too close to now. |
| **Max meetings per day** | Stops offering once you have hit it. |
| **Interval** | How slots are spaced through the window. |

All four are on the [meeting plan](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/meeting-plans).

## Your real calendar

Connect a calendar and "bookings respect your real availability and land in your day" —
the time you are already busy stops being offered.

<Callout type="warn">
**Without a connected calendar, availability is a guess.** Your hours say you are free
at 2pm; your actual calendar says you are in a meeting. Somebody books it. See
[Integrations](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/integrations).
</Callout>

## Setting hours you can keep

- **Leave gaps.** Availability with no buffers produces eight meetings back to back.
- **Protect a block.** Keep part of each day unbookable for the work meetings are about.
- **Do not offer evenings you resent.** An hour you regret is an hour you will cancel.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="My plan shows no available slots.">
No hours set, or everything is being removed by notice period, buffers or a daily cap.
Widen the hours first, then relax the rules one at a time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Somebody booked a time I was busy.">
No calendar connected. Your availability does not know about anything outside the
platform.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can different plans have different hours?">
Availability belongs to the plan, so a 30-minute consultation and an hour-long tutorial
can be offered at different times.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Invitees see the wrong times.">
Your timezone, or theirs. Slots are shown in the invitee's timezone, so check what yours
is set to.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I block a holiday?">
Holidays appear on calendars but do not remove booking slots. Take the day out of your
hours, or block it on your connected calendar. See
[Holidays](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/holidays).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
