A meeting plan is a bookable thing: "30-minute consultation", "one-hour tutorial". You
set it up once and people book slots against it.

<Path>Teach > Live Sessions > Meeting Plans</Path>.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

Each row carries the plan's name and duration, its **Status**, its **Categories** and
its **Timezone**. The icons beside it edit, delete, unpublish, or open the public
booking page so you can see what an invitee sees.

**Integrations** on this screen is where the calendar and conferencing connections live
— see [Integrations](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/integrations).

## What a plan holds

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Meeting Plan Name** | "The name invitees see and the address they book at." |
| **URL Slug** | The address people book at. |
| **Description** / **Summary** | What the meeting is for. |
| **Meeting Plan Type** | **One to One** or **One to Many**. |
| **Duration (minutes)** | How long it runs. |
| **Host** | Who takes it. |
| **Max Participants Limit** | For a group meeting. |
| **Group Meeting Spots** | How many places. |
| **Guests Allowed** | Whether an invitee may bring others. |
| **Thumbnail** / **Cover** | Its images. |
| **Intro Video URL** | A short video on the booking page. |
| **Categories** | For your own organisation. |

## Instant or by request

**Booking Mode** is the decision that shapes everything else:

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Instant Booking** | "Customers can pick a time from your available slots and book instantly." |
| **Request-Based Booking** | "Customers request a time, and you must approve it before it's confirmed." |

Instant suits office hours and anything routine. Request-based suits meetings you may
need to decline, or where the time has to be negotiated — and it means a queue you have
to work.

## Where the meeting happens

**Medium Type** decides the venue:

| Medium | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Zoom**, **Google Meet**, **Microsoft Teams** | Need the matching provider connected. |
| **Live** / **Other** | A link you supply. |
| **Offline** | A **Physical Address**. |

"Video options need the matching provider connected" — see
[Integrations](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/integrations). Connected, every booking gets
its join link automatically; not connected, you paste one in each time.

An **Access Password** and **Joining Instructions** can be added for either.

## Scheduling rules

Every one already has a sensible default. Change only what this plan needs.

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Buffer before** | "Free time held before each meeting." |
| **Buffer after** | "Free time held after each meeting." |
| **Minimum notice (minutes)** | "How close to the start time invitees can still book." |
| **Max meetings per day** | A cap. Leave empty for no limit. |
| **Pending booking expiry** | "How long an unpaid booking holds its slot." |
| **Interval** | How slots are spaced. |

<Callout type="info">
**Buffers are what stop a day from becoming unworkable.** Fifteen minutes after each
meeting turns eight back-to-back calls into a day you can survive.
</Callout>

## Cancellation and rescheduling

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Cancellation** | Don't allow, or allow up to 1, 2, 4, 6, 12 or 24 hours before. |
| **Rescheduling** | The same. |

Allowing changes up to 24 hours before is usually the right balance: it gives people a
way out that is not simply not turning up.

## Payment

A meeting plan can be charged for — see
[Booking payments](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/payments). **Pending booking expiry**
decides how long an unpaid booking holds its slot before releasing it.

## Who hears about bookings

**Team members to notify** — "covers new requests, confirmed and paid bookings, and
invitee cancellations or reschedules". Name the people who actually need to know,
including the host.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Nobody can book anything.">
The plan needs availability. A plan with no hours set has no slots to offer. See
[Availability](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/availability).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Bookings have no join link.">
No conferencing provider is connected, or the medium is set to one you supply yourself.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="People book with five minutes' notice.">
Raise **Minimum notice**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can one plan have several hosts?">
A plan has a host. For several people offering the same meeting, make a plan each.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between One to One and One to Many?">
One to One is a private meeting. One to Many is a group session with a number of spots —
a group tutorial rather than a webinar. For a webinar, use an
[event](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/events).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
