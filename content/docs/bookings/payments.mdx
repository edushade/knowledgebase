---
title: "Charging for Meetings"
description: "Charge for a session — either before a student can book it, or after the booking is confirmed."
---

Meetings can be free or paid. If you charge, you choose **when** the payment is collected:
before the booking is locked in, or after it is confirmed. Paid bookings use the same
checkout your platform already uses for everything else.

## Free meetings

If a meeting plan has no price, booking is instant. The student picks a time, confirms, and
the booking is **confirmed** right away — the meeting link and calendar invite are created
automatically.

## Paid meetings

When a meeting plan has a price, you decide the timing:

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Collect payment before booking** | The student must pay to lock in the time. Until they pay, the booking is held as **Pending**. Once payment goes through, it becomes **Confirmed**. |
| **Collect payment after confirmation** | The booking is confirmed first, and payment is collected afterward. Useful when you invoice or settle later. |

## Pending bookings that aren't paid

When payment is required first, the slot is held as **Pending** while the student completes
checkout. If they do not pay within the allowed time, the booking **expires automatically**
and the time opens back up for others. This keeps your calendar from filling with bookings
that were never paid for.

## How to add a price

1. Open the meeting plan in **Admin → Bookings → Meeting Plans**.
2. Add a **price** for the meeting.
3. Choose when to collect it — **before booking** or **after confirmation**.
4. Click **Save**.

## What the student sees

- For **pay before booking**: after choosing a time, the student is taken to checkout. The
  time is held for them while they pay. Once paid, they see a confirmation and receive the
  calendar invite. If they do not finish in time, they are told the hold expired and can try
  again.
- For **pay after confirmation**: the student is booked right away and pays afterward.

<Callout type="info">
Paid meetings use your platform's normal payment options. To set those up, see
[Payment methods](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/payment-methods).
</Callout>

## Good to know

- A **Pending** booking is waiting for payment. A **Requested** booking is waiting for your
  approval. They are different — see
  [Group & request bookings](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/group-and-request-bookings) and
  [Managing bookings](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/managing-bookings).
- Expired pending bookings free up the slot automatically — you do not need to clean them up.
