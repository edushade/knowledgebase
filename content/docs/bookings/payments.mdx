title: "Booking Payments"
description: "Charging for a meeting — setting a price, and what happens to a slot nobody has paid for."
---

A meeting plan can be free or paid. Charging for consultations, tutorials and
assessments is often the most direct money an academy makes.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Setting a price

In [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing), with the entity type
**Meeting Plan**. The same options apply as anywhere else — one-time, instalment or
free.

For most meetings, one-time and lifetime access is the right shape: they are buying a
slot, not a subscription.

## The unpaid slot

**Pending booking expiry**, on the meeting plan, decides "how long an unpaid booking
holds its slot".

<Callout type="warn">
**Set this deliberately.** Too short and somebody fetching their phone loses the slot;
too long and one unpaid booking blocks an hour nobody else can take. Fifteen to thirty
minutes suits most academies.
</Callout>

When it expires, the slot is released and offered again.

## What you can see

The booking list shows paid and unpaid bookings, and the payment appears in
[Transactions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/transactions) like any other.

**Team members to notify** on the plan covers "confirmed and paid bookings", so whoever
takes the meeting knows it is paid before they join.

## Paid and request-based together

A plan can be both paid and request-based. The order is: they request, you approve, they
pay, it is confirmed.

It is the right shape for anything you may decline — an assessment interview, a
consultation that needs the right person. It is also more steps, and some people fall
out at each one, so use it only where approval genuinely matters.

## Refunds

A refunded booking follows the same path as any other refund, including the decision
about access. See [Refunds](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/refunds).

For a meeting that has already happened, the question is your policy rather than the
platform's.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A booking is confirmed but not paid.">
Check the plan's **Payment Setting** and that the price exists. A plan with no price is
free, whatever you intended.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Somebody paid and has no booking.">
Find the transaction. If it succeeded, the booking should exist — if it does not, that
is one to look at directly. See
[Transactions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/transactions).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I charge different prices for the same plan?">
A restricted price with named users gives a rate only they see. See
[Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner pay in instalments for a meeting?">
Technically yes. For a single session it rarely makes sense.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
