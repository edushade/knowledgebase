An event's **Participants** screen is its list of people — the one you work before the
event and read afterwards.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## What the list shows

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Learner** / **Invitee** | Who they are. |
| **Registered At** | When they signed up. |
| **Joined At** | When they actually arrived — blank if they did not. |
| **Status** | Where they stand. |

**Registered At** against **Joined At** is your attendance rate, and for a free webinar
it is usually far lower than people expect. Knowing the real figure is what lets you
plan the next one.

## Adding people

| Action | For |
|---|---|
| **Register** | One person, added by you. |
| **Import Participants** | A CSV of people you already hold. |

The import is the useful one. An event promoted on social media or through a partner
produces a list somewhere else; bring it in rather than typing it.

Upload the CSV in the shape the screen asks for, and check the result before the event
rather than on the day.

## Removing somebody

**Remove participant** — "this will remove the participant from this event" — and it
asks first.

## Before the event

1. **Check the numbers** against your limit.
2. **Send a reminder.** Build a campaign to the registrants — see
   [Campaigns](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns). A reminder on the morning
   is worth more than any other thing you do.
3. **Check the joining details work.** Open the link yourself.

## After the event

- **Export the list.** It is a list of people interested in what you teach, which is the
  most valuable thing a free event produces.
- **Look at who registered and did not come.** They were interested enough to sign up;
  a recording and a follow-up often converts them.
- **Follow up the people who did come**, while they remember it.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="My import failed.">
The file does not match the shape the screen asks for. Check the headings, and try a
handful of rows first.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Somebody registered twice.">
Remove the duplicate registration. If they have two accounts, that is a different
problem — see
[Duplicate accounts](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/merging-accounts).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Joined At is empty for everyone.">
Either nobody joined, or the session happened somewhere the platform could not observe —
an offline event, or a link outside your connected provider.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I register somebody who has no account?">
Registration attaches to a person. For a list of outsiders, import them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I message just the registrants?">
Export the list and build an audience from it, then send a campaign. See
[Audiences](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/audiences).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
