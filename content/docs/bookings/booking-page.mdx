Every meeting plan has a public booking page at its own address — "the name invitees see
and the address they book at".

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## What they see

| Part | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| The name and description | The meeting plan |
| The cover image and intro video | The meeting plan |
| The length | **Duration** |
| Available times | Your [availability](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/availability), in their timezone |
| The price | Its [pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/payments), where you charge |

## What they do

They pick a time and give their details. Anything you ask for is "information submitted
by the attendee during booking" and is kept with the booking.

Then, depending on the plan:

- **Instant booking** — confirmed immediately, with their joining details.
- **Request-based** — their request goes to you, and they wait for approval.
- **Paid** — they pay. The slot is held until **Pending booking expiry** passes.

## Making the page work

The page is short, so each piece carries weight:

1. **Say what the meeting is for**, in the description. "30-minute consultation" tells
   nobody whether it is for them.
2. **Use the intro video.** Thirty seconds of the person they will be meeting does more
   than any paragraph.
3. **Ask for as little as possible.** Every extra field loses people.
4. **Offer real times.** A page whose next slot is in nine days is a page people leave.

## Sharing it

The page has its own address — put it in your email signature, on your course pages, in
a [campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns). It works for anybody, whether
or not they have an account on your platform.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The page shows no times.">
No availability, or every slot is removed by buffers, notice period or a daily cap. See
[Availability](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/availability).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An invitee booked and got no joining details.">
For a request-based plan, nothing is confirmed until you approve it. For instant, check
a conferencing provider is connected — see
[Integrations](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/integrations).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change what the page looks like?">
What it shows comes from the meeting plan — name, description, images, intro video.
There is no separate design.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do invitees need an account?">
Not to book. They give their details on the page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I stop a plan being bookable for a while?">
Set it inactive, or clear its availability.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
