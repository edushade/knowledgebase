---

Your bookings dashboard is the single place to see and act on every meeting. From here you
can find a booking, confirm a request, reschedule, or cancel — and your students can manage
their own bookings too, within the deadlines you set.

## The bookings dashboard

Go to **Admin → Bookings → Meeting Schedules** to see all your bookings. You can:

- **Search** for a booking by student name or meeting.
- **Filter** by status, by meeting plan, or by date.
- **Open** any booking to see its full details — who booked, when, the location, any guests,
  and notes.

## Booking statuses

Every booking has a status that tells you exactly where it stands.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Booked and on the calendar. Attendees have the invite and the link. |
| **Pending** | Held while it waits for payment. Becomes Confirmed once paid. |
| **Requested** | A student proposed a time and it is waiting for your approval. |
| **Rescheduled** | The meeting was moved to a new date or time. |
| **Cancelled** | The meeting was called off by you or the student. |
| **Expired** | A pending booking that was not paid in time, so the slot was freed. |

## Confirm a request

For request bookings, open the **Requested** booking and click **Confirm** to lock it in. The
meeting link and calendar invites are then created and everyone is notified. See
[Group & request bookings](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/group-and-request-bookings).

## Reschedule a meeting

Both you and the student can move a meeting to a new time, as long as it is before the
reschedule deadline you set.

1. Open the booking and click **Reschedule**.
2. Pick a new open time.
3. Confirm. The calendar event updates and everyone is notified of the new time.

## Cancel a meeting

Both you and the student can cancel, up to the cancellation deadline.

1. Open the booking and click **Cancel**.
2. Confirm. The calendar event is removed and everyone is notified.

<Callout type="info">
The reschedule and cancellation deadlines come from the meeting plan's rules. Set them in
[Availability & booking rules](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/availability).
</Callout>

## Extra guests

A student can invite **additional guests** to a meeting beyond themselves, when the plan
allows it. Each guest is added with their own details and receives the calendar invite and
meeting link along with the main attendee.

## Time zones

Time zones are handled for everyone automatically:

- The student sees and books times in **their** time zone.
- You see them in **your** time zone.
- The calendar invite shows each person the correct local time.

This means no one has to do time-zone math, and meetings start when everyone expects.

## Unpaid bookings expire on their own

When a booking is waiting for payment and the student does not pay in time, it expires
automatically and the slot opens back up. You do not need to clean these up by hand. See
[Charging for meetings](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/payments).

## What the student sees

Students can view their own bookings and, within the deadlines, reschedule or cancel them.
Every confirmation, change, and cancellation sends them an updated notice and calendar invite,
so they always have the latest details.
