## The list

Each row carries:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Meeting Plan** | Which plan was booked, and how long it runs. |
| **Medium** | How it happens — **Zoom**, **Meet**, **Phone Call** or **In Person**. |
| **Invitee** | Their name, email, phone, and whatever they wrote when booking. |
| **Date & Time** | When. Blank until a request has been given a time. |
| **Status** | See below. |
| **Timezone** | Theirs, so you are not doing the arithmetic in your head. |

Filter by **Status**, **Meeting Plan**, **Team Member** or **Date**, and search by name.
**Date** picks one day — **Yesterday**, **Today**, **Tomorrow** or any day on the
calendar — counted on your academy's clock. See
[Date filters and your time zone](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/date-filters).

<Callout type="info">
**What the invitee wrote is the most useful column on the screen** and the easiest to
scroll past. Somebody who books a slot and writes down what they want to ask has told
you how to prepare. Read it before the call, not during it.
</Callout>

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Requested** | They asked for a slot. Nothing is confirmed until you act. |
| **Awaiting payment** | Booked but not paid for. It holds the slot until it expires. |
| **Scheduled** | Confirmed, with a time. |
| **Rescheduled** | Moved since it was first booked. |
| **Expired** | It was never paid for or never confirmed in time. |
| **Canceled** | Called off, by either side. |

**Requested** and **Awaiting payment** are the two that need you. Everything else is
either settled or over.

## What you can do with one

The **⋯** menu on each row:

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| **View Details** | Always. |
| **Set Schedule** | Only on a request, or a booking awaiting payment that has no time yet. This is how you confirm one. |
| **Reschedule** | Any booking that is not canceled or expired. |
| **Cancel Booking** | Same. It asks first, and cannot be undone. |

### Confirming a request

A request-based plan produces bookings that are not confirmed until you act, and the
menu carries a badge for them — because a request sitting unanswered is somebody
waiting.

**Set Schedule** lets you review the times they asked for, or pick a different one.

<Callout type="warn">
Work the request queue daily. An unanswered request is worse than a declined one: the
person does not know whether to wait or go elsewhere.
</Callout>

### Rescheduling and cancelling

Both tell the invitee. Whether *they* can do either themselves is set on the plan — not
at all, or up to 1, 2, 4, 6, 12 or 24 hours before. See
[Meeting plans](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/meeting-plans).

Allowing invitees to cancel is usually better than not: a cancelled slot is one you can
offer again, and a no-show is not.

## Who is told what

**Team members to notify** on the plan covers "new requests, confirmed and paid
bookings, and invitee cancellations or reschedules". Invitees get their own
notifications — **Booking Confirmed**, **Meeting Reminder**, **Meeting Rescheduled**,
**Booking Cancelled** — which you control in
[Notification settings](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/notification-preferences).

**The reminder is the setting that reduces no-shows.** Make sure it is on.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A booking has no meeting link.">
No conferencing provider connected, or a plan with a medium you supply yourself. See
[Integrations](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/integrations).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An invitee says they never got confirmation.">
Check the [delivery logs](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/delivery-and-analytics). The
usual answer is a sending service rather than the booking.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I book on somebody's behalf?">
Use the booking page yourself with their details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Too many people are not turning up.">
Turn on the meeting reminder, raise the minimum notice, and allow cancellation — a
cancelled slot is recoverable, a no-show is not.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An unpaid booking is holding a slot.">
It holds it for as long as **Pending booking expiry** allows, then releases it.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
