---
title: "Group & Request Bookings"
description: "Let several students share one time slot, or let students propose their own times for you to approve."
---

Most meetings are one student, one time slot. But two extra styles cover other needs: a
**group meeting** where several students share one slot, and a **request booking** where a
student suggests a time and you approve it.

## Group meetings

A group meeting lets more than one student book the **same** time slot, up to a limit you
set. This is ideal for group office hours, a study session, or a small workshop.

### How to set it up

1. Open the meeting plan in **Admin → Bookings → Meeting Plans**.
2. Turn on group booking and set the **capacity** — the maximum number of students per slot.
3. Click **Save**.

### How it works

- The slot stays open until it reaches its capacity. Each student books their own spot.
- When the slot is full, it no longer appears for new students.
- Everyone in the group gets the same meeting link or location and the same calendar invite.

### What the student sees

The student books a spot in the slot just like a normal meeting. They may see how many spots
remain. Once the group is full, that time disappears from the booking page.

## Request bookings

A request booking flips the usual flow: instead of picking from fixed open times, the student
**proposes** a time (and can add a note about what they need), and you **approve** it before
it becomes final. This suits meetings where the timing is flexible or needs your judgment.

### How it works

1. The student opens the booking page and suggests a time, with an optional note.
2. The booking arrives as **Requested** — it is not yet on the calendar.
3. You review it in your dashboard and either **confirm** it or let it go.
4. When you confirm, it becomes **Confirmed**: the meeting link and calendar invites are
   created and everyone is notified.

### What the student sees

After proposing a time, the student is told their request is awaiting approval. When you
confirm, they receive a confirmation and a calendar invite. If a request is not approved, it
simply does not become a meeting.

<Callout type="info">
A **Requested** booking is waiting for your approval. A **Pending** booking is waiting for
payment. Keep the two in mind when reviewing your dashboard — see
[Managing bookings](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/managing-bookings).
</Callout>

## Good to know

- Group meetings and request bookings are set per meeting plan, so you can mix styles across
  the meetings you offer.
- You can also let students bring **extra guests** along to a booking — covered in
  [Managing bookings](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/managing-bookings).
