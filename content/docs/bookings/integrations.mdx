Two connections make bookings work properly. Without them, bookings still happen — they
just happen in a calendar that does not know about your other meetings, with a link you
paste in by hand.

<Path>Settings > Integrations</Path>, under **Calendar** and **Conferencing**.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Calendar

"Link a calendar so bookings respect your real availability and land in your day."

| Provider |
|---|
| **Google Calendar** |
| **Outlook Calendar** |

Two things change once it is connected:

1. **Your real commitments stop being offered.** Time you are already busy is not
   bookable.
2. **Bookings appear in your calendar**, where you will actually see them.

<Callout type="warn">
**Without a connected calendar, availability is a guess.** Your hours say 2pm is free;
your actual diary says otherwise; somebody books it. This is the single most common
cause of a double-booked host.
</Callout>

## Conferencing

"Connect a video provider so every booking gets a join link automatically."

| Provider |
|---|
| **Zoom** |
| **Google Meet** |
| **Microsoft Teams** |

Connected, a booking creates its own meeting and the invitee gets the link with their
confirmation. Not connected, the plan's medium has to be a link you supply, and you
supply it every time.

## Connecting

Each provider takes its own app credentials — a client ID, a secret, and an **OAuth
redirect URL** that you copy from here into the provider's console.

<Callout type="info">
**Credentials are stored securely.** Leave a field blank to keep the existing value. And
"new connections go through this app — accounts already connected keep working", so
changing the credentials does not break the people already connected.
</Callout>

## Disconnecting

Disconnecting asks first. Meetings already created keep their links; new bookings go
back to needing a link from you.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Bookings have no join link.">
No conferencing provider connected, or the plan's medium is one you supply. Check the
plan's **Medium Type** — "video options need the matching provider connected".
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Somebody booked a slot I was busy in.">
No calendar connected, or the busy time was in a calendar other than the connected one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The redirect URL is rejected.">
It must match exactly what the provider's console has, including the protocol and any
trailing slash. Copy it from here rather than typing it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can different hosts connect their own calendars?">
Each person connects their own account, so each host's availability reflects their own
diary.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use a provider that is not listed?">
Set the plan's medium to one you supply, and paste the link into each booking yourself.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
