title: "Bookings & Appointments"
description: "Let instructors and advisors offer bookable meetings, and let students pick a time and book online — with automatic meeting links and calendar invites."
---

Bookings is your appointment scheduling tool. Instructors, advisors, and mentors
publish the kinds of meetings they offer — a tutoring session, an advising call, an
office-hours slot — and students pick an open time and book it themselves. It works the
same way popular scheduling tools do: you set your availability once, share a link, and
let people book the times that suit them.

## What you can do

- **Offer bookable meetings.** Create a meeting plan for each kind of session you run —
  a 30-minute consultation, a one-hour class, and so on.
- **Set your availability.** Choose the days and times you are open, plus rules like how
  much notice you need and gaps between meetings.
- **Share a booking page.** Each meeting plan has its own web address you can share. The
  page shows open times in the student's own time zone.
- **Collect payment (optional).** Charge for a session before or after it is booked.
- **Meet anywhere.** Use Google Meet, Zoom, an in-person address, or a phone call. Online
  links are created for you automatically.
- **Stay organized.** Calendar invites go out on their own, and reminders, reschedules,
  and cancellations keep everyone in sync.

## How a booking works

1. You create a **meeting plan** and set your **availability**.
2. (Optional) You connect Google Calendar, Google Meet, or Zoom so links and invites are
   made for you.
3. A student opens your **booking page** and sees open times in their own time zone.
4. The student picks a time and fills in a short form.
5. The booking lands in one of three states:
   - **Confirmed** — booked and on the calendar (free meetings).
   - **Pending** — held while it waits for payment.
   - **Requested** — waiting for you to approve it.
6. Once confirmed, the meeting link and calendar invites are created automatically and
   everyone is notified.
