Two different shapes live here, and picking the right one saves a lot of work:

| | **Meeting plan** | **Event** |
|---|---|---|
| Who picks the time | The attendee | You |
| Shape | A slot somebody books | One session many people register for |
| Good for | Consultations, tutorials, office hours | Webinars, seminars, open days |

There is a third thing that is neither: a **Live lesson** inside a course, which is a
scheduled class for the people already enrolled. See
[Lesson types](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/lesson-types).
