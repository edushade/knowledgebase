Everything that governs files sits in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under
**Media**.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Max upload file size (MB)** | The limit for your team. |
| **Max upload file size for learners (MB)** | A separate limit for learners, who upload assignments. |
| **Allowed file types** | A comma-separated list of what is accepted. |
| **Video speed controls** | Whether learners can change playback speed. |
| **Video quality controls** | Whether they can change quality. |
| **Video downloads** | Whether videos can be downloaded. |
| **Restrict paid content download** | Disables download and print for paid course, learning path and cohort content. |
| **Auth Page Image** | The picture beside your sign-in form. |

## Restrict paid content download

<Callout type="info">
**This is the setting an academy selling courses actually wants.** On, learners cannot
download or print the content of anything paid — it stays inside your platform.
</Callout>

It covers paid courses, learning paths and cohorts. Free content is unaffected, which is
usually right: a free guide is meant to travel.

It is not absolute. Anything a learner can see, a learner can photograph. What it stops
is the easy route — the one where a whole course ends up in a shared folder because
downloading was a button.

## Video quality and speed

**Video quality controls** matters more than it looks in markets with uneven
connections. Letting a learner drop to a lower quality is the difference between
watching and giving up.

**Video speed controls** here is academy-wide; the managed player has its own setting
per programme. See [Video playback](/docs/knowledgebase/files/video-playback).

## Choosing the size limits

| Limit | A reasonable starting point |
|---|---|
| Your team | High enough for a lesson video. |
| Learners | Enough for a document or a photo of handwritten work, and no more. |

A learner limit that is too generous fills your storage with phone video that nobody
will ever watch.

## Allowed file types

List what you actually need. A short list prevents accidents — somebody uploading an
installer, or a format nothing can open.

Remember learners' assignments when you set it: a list without `pdf` and `docx` will
stop submissions.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner's upload is refused.">
The learner size limit, or the file type. Both are separate from your team's.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Learners are sharing our course videos.">
Switch on **Restrict paid content download**. It stops the easy route, which is most of
it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I stop screenshots?">
No. Anything on screen can be captured.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How much storage do we have?">
Your plan's allowance. The practical control is the yearly tidy — sort by **Largest
First** and deal with the top twenty. See
[Organizing files](/docs/knowledgebase/files/organizing-files).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can one course allow downloads when others do not?">
The download restriction is academy-wide and applies to paid content. To let learners
keep something, attach it as a downloadable file rather than putting it in a paid
lesson.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
