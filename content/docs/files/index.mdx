Files & Media is the central library for everything you upload — documents, images,
audio, video, and PDFs. Upload a file once, keep it organized in folders, and attach it
wherever you need it: courses, lessons, assignments, notices, website pages, and
products.

## What you can do here

- **Upload** documents, images, audio, video, and PDFs — including large files.
- **Organize** your files in folders: browse, rename, move, search, and delete.
- **Preview** common file types right in your platform, without downloading.
- **Attach** files to courses, lessons, assignments, notices, website pages, and products.
- **Collect learner uploads**, such as assignment submissions and profile photos.

Think of it as one tidy storage room behind your whole platform. Anything you teach
with, sell, or publish can pull from the same shared library.

## Where files get used

Once a file is in your library, you can attach it across your platform:

- [Courses & Content](/docs/knowledgebase/courses) — add handouts, slides, and lesson materials.
- [Assignments](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/assignments) — collect and review learner submissions.
- [Website & Pages](/docs/knowledgebase/website) — use images and downloads on your public pages.
