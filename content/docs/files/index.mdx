## Three decisions worth making now

| Decision | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Restrict paid content download** | <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> → Media | Stops the easy route to your paid courses ending up in a shared folder. |
| **The learner upload limit** | The same place | Too generous and your storage fills with phone video. |
| **A folder structure** | The file manager | Twenty minutes now, hours later. |
