---
title: "Learner Uploads"
description: "What learners can send you, the separate limits that govern it, and where their files end up."
---

Learners upload two things: their assignment submissions, and their own profile picture.
Both are governed by limits set separately from your team's.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The learner limits

In <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Media**:

| Setting | What it governs |
|---|---|
| **Max upload file size for learners (MB)** | How big a submission can be. |
| **Allowed file types** | What they may send. |

<Callout type="warn">
**Check the allowed types before an assignment is due.** A list without `pdf` and
`docx` stops every submission, and you will find out at 11pm the night of the deadline.
</Callout>

Set the size limit to what a real submission needs. Too generous and you collect phone
video; too tight and a scanned handwritten paper is refused.

## Assignment submissions

A learner submits against an [assignment](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/assignments),
and the file arrives with their submission. You open it from the marking screen — see
[Assignment submissions](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/assignment-submissions).

Submissions are attached to the learner and the assignment, not to your shared file
library, so they do not clutter it.

## Avatars

Learners can upload a profile picture, unless the field is locked. See **Locked Profile
Fields** in [the Control Panel](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/control-panel).

## Telling learners what to send

Most submission problems are avoidable, in the assignment's instructions:

- **The format.** "A single PDF."
- **The size.** "Under 10 MB."
- **The naming.** "Your name and the assignment number."
- **What to do if it fails.** Who to contact, and by when.

Those four lines save more time than any setting on this page.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner cannot upload their assignment.">
The size limit or the file type. Both are separate from your team's limits — check the
learner ones.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner submitted the wrong file.">
Reset their submission so they can send another, or raise the assignment's attempts.
See [Attempts and retakes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/attempts-and-retakes).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do submitted files live?">
With the submission, reachable from the marking screen. They are not in your shared
library.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can learners upload to a lesson?">
No. Learner uploads are assignment submissions and avatars.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I stop learners uploading at all?">
Do not set assignments that need a file, or restrict the allowed types. There is no
separate switch.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
