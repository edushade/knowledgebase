title: "Search History"

---

A learner's recent searches are kept and shown in the search box before they type
anything, so going back to something they looked at yesterday is one tap.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## What they see

Open the search box with nothing typed:

- **Their recent searches**, most recent first.
- **What is [trending](/docs/knowledgebase/search/trending-and-popular)** on your site.

Tapping a past search runs it again.

## Clearing it

A learner can remove one item or **Clear All**. Clearing everything asks for
confirmation first, since it cannot be undone.

The history belongs to the learner. They control it.

## Why it is there

A learner comparing three courses looks at one, leaves, and comes back the next evening.
Without history they start again; with it, their three searches are waiting.

It is a small thing that matters most for the people deciding whether to buy.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner's history disappeared.">
They cleared it, or they are signed in on a different account or device.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see what one learner searched for?">
Their history is theirs. What you see is the aggregate — what is
[trending](/docs/knowledgebase/search/trending-and-popular) across your site.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner switch history off?">
They can clear it whenever they like. There is no separate switch.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is search history covered by our privacy settings?">
It is activity data, kept under your retention settings. See
[Privacy and consent](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/privacy-and-consent).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
