Search is how most visitors reach a course. Everything about it is driven by what you
have already written into your courses, so there is very little to configure and quite a
lot to get right.

## The one field to fill in

**Search Keywords**, on every course's settings step.

It holds "other names people might search for, such as an abbreviation or its name in
another language", and **it is never shown to learners**. A course called "Business
English" with `ইংরেজি, spoken english, English course` in its keywords is found by
three times as many people, at no cost.

Most academies never fill it in.

## A ten-minute monthly habit

1. Look at what is [trending](/docs/knowledgebase/search/trending-and-popular).
2. Search those terms yourself.
3. Anything that returns nothing but should: add the term to that course's keywords.
4. Anything you do not teach, but several people searched for: a course idea, asked for
   by name.
