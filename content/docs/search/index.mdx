Search is the fastest way for people to find what they're looking for on your site. One
search box covers your whole catalog — courses, learning paths, blog posts, pages,
products, bundles, cohorts, events, and meeting plans. As people type, they get instant
suggestions, and they can narrow results with simple filters.

Search also helps you. It quietly keeps track of what people look for most, so you can
see which topics are popular and what learners wish you offered.

## What search can do

- **Search everything in one place** — titles, descriptions, and tags across all your content.
- **Suggest as you type** — instant matches appear before anyone finishes typing.
- **Narrow results** — filter by content type, status, and language, each with a count.
- **Surface what's hot** — trending search terms and the content people click most.
- **Remember recent searches** — each learner sees their own recent searches and can clear them.
