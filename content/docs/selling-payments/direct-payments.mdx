title: "Direct Payments"

Many learners would rather send money the way they always do: **Send Money** on bKash
to your number, a bank transfer, or cash at your office. Direct payments let them do
that at checkout. They send the money to you, tell you the transaction ID, and the
course opens once someone on your team has found the payment on your own statement.

Direct payments work on their own — an academy with no online payment at all — or
beside online payment, so learners can choose.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## How it works

1. At checkout the learner chooses **Send the money yourself**, and one of your
   accounts — for example **Send Money by bKash**.
2. They are shown the amount, your number and the name on the account, with copy
   buttons and the steps for that kind of account.
3. They tell you the **Transaction ID** from their SMS or receipt, the day they sent it,
   and — if you ask — the number they sent from and a screenshot.
4. The payment waits in **Pending Verification**. Your team is alerted by email and in
   the app.
5. Someone checks your statement, then presses **Found it, give access** or **Couldn't
   find it**. The learner is told either way.

Nothing opens for the learner until your team confirms the payment.

## Setting it up

In <Path>Settings > Integrations > Payments</Path>, the **Direct payments** section:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Take direct payments** | The switch. Learners see it at checkout as **Send the money yourself**. |
| **Ways to pay you** | Your accounts. **Set up** opens the page where you add them. |
| **Payments waiting to be checked** | How many are waiting, and **Check them**. |

The set-up page has four parts:

| Part | What you decide |
|---|---|
| **Where learners send money** | Each bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Upay number, bank account, or your office. Each one becomes its own choice at checkout, with its own steps. |
| **What learners tell you** | The **Transaction ID** is always asked. You choose whether to ask for the **Number they sent from**, and whether a **Screenshot of the payment** is **Optional**, **Required** or not asked. |
| **Checking payments** | Your **Office hours**, shown to learners while they wait. Staff who can change orders are the ones who confirm payments. |
| **A note for learners (optional)** | Anything else, in your own words, up to 1,000 characters. Shown under the payment details. |

The full walkthrough is
[Set up direct payments](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/set-up-direct-payments).

### Each account

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Pay by** | **bKash**, **Nagad**, **Rocket**, **Upay**, **Bank account** or **Cash at your office**. |
| **Account type** | For a mobile wallet: **Personal**, **Merchant** or **Agent**. It decides what learners are told to tap — **Send Money**, **Make Payment** or **Cash Out**. |
| **Number** and **Name on the account** | Learners check the name in their app before they confirm. |
| **Tell learners to expect an answer within** | **1 hour**, **4 hours** or **1 working day**. Learners are told this when they send. |
| **Largest amount (optional)** | Above this, learners do not see this account — useful for a personal wallet with a daily limit. |
| **Offer at checkout** | Switch an account off to stop taking payments into it for now. |

As you fill in an account, **Learners will see these steps** shows exactly what the
learner will be told.

## When you take both online and direct payments

The **At checkout** section appears once both are switched on:

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Show first** | **Online payment** or **Direct payments**. The other is listed underneath. |
| **Offer direct payments** | **On every order**, **Only after an online payment fails**, or **Only on orders above an amount** (set the **Smallest order**). Online payment is always offered. |
| **Courses that take direct payments** | **All courses and programmes**, or **Only the ones I choose**. |

## What the learner sees

- **At checkout**, two cards: **Pay online** and **Send the money yourself**, each of
  your accounts listed with how soon you check.
- **Where to send it** — the amount, your number or bank details, copy buttons, and the
  steps. On an Android phone, bKash and Nagad have a button that opens the app.
- **Tell us about your payment** — the transaction ID (they can paste the whole SMS and
  the ID is picked out of it), **Today**, **Yesterday** or **Pick a date**, and the
  screenshot if you ask for one.
- **"Thank you. We're checking your payment"** — with your answer time, your office
  hours, and **Fix it** if they typed something wrong.
- **On the course page**, until you decide: *"We're checking your payment"* in place of
  the buy button, so nobody pays twice.
- **If you could not confirm it**: your reason, and **Send again**.

Their order shows the same, in [Payment history](/docs/knowledgebase/learner/payment-history).

## Good to know

- **Discount codes do not apply to direct payments.** A code works when paying online.
  A learner who chooses to send the money themselves sends the full price, less any
  scholarship you have given them.
- **Instalment plans are paid online.** Direct payments are offered for a full price.
- **The same transaction ID cannot be used for two orders.** A learner who tries is told
  it has already been sent.
- **A learner cannot report twice for the same course.** While one payment is waiting,
  they can fix it but not send another.
- **Your team is told once per payment**, by email and in the app.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Do I still need an online payment service?">
No. Direct payments work on their own. An online service lets learners start straight
away without anyone checking, so many academies use both.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner typed the wrong transaction ID.">
They can correct it with **Fix it** until your team has looked at it. Your team can also
correct it on the payment's **Edit** page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who can confirm payments?">
Staff whose role lets them change orders. Change who that is in
[People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does the learner read when we cannot find the payment?">
A sentence for the reason you chose — for example *"The amount we received doesn't
match the price."* — and your note. See
[Check a direct payment](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/check-a-direct-payment).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I stop taking payments into one number for a while?">
Yes. Edit the account and switch off **Offer at checkout**. Payments already reported to
it stay in **Pending Verification**.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
