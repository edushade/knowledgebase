A scholarship reduces what one learner pays. Unlike a
[coupon](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/coupons-and-offers), which anyone with the
code can use, a scholarship is granted to a person.

Scholarships live in <Path>Billing > Offers & Promotions > Scholarships</Path>.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Setting one up

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What it is called. |
| **Description** | A note. |
| **Scholarship Type** | **Percentage**, **Fixed** or **Up to Limit**. |
| **Scholarship Percentage** | How much, where the type is a percentage. |
| **Entity Type** and the programme | What it applies to. A cohort-based course needs the **batch**. |
| **User (Learner)** | Who it is for. |
| **Start Date** / **End Date** | When it is valid. |
| **Application Form** | A form applicants fill in, where you want one. |
| **Show Scholarship Percentage** | Whether the learner is told how much they were awarded. |
| **Status** | Active or not. |

**Up to Limit** covers the cost up to a ceiling, with the learner paying anything above
it. It is the right shape for "we will cover up to ৳10,000 of any programme".

## Applications

Where a scholarship has an **Application Form**, learners apply and their applications
arrive in <Path>Billing > Offers & Promotions > Scholarship Applications</Path> — see
[Scholarship applications](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/scholarship-applications).

Without a form, you grant it directly.

## Revoking one

A scholarship can be revoked, and the important part is what happens to the learner's
access. **Revoke Policies**:

| Policy | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Remain existing access** | They keep what they have. The scholarship stops applying to anything new. |
| **Revoke access** | Their access is withdrawn. |
| **Limit access** | Their access is reduced. |

<Callout type="warn">
Decide the policy before you revoke, not after. **Revoke access** takes a learner out of
a programme they may be halfway through, and there is no undo that gives the time back.
</Callout>

## What the learner sees

A scholarship reduces the price at checkout. The transaction records **Scholarship
Applied**, so the gap between the price and what they paid is explained on the record.

With **Show Scholarship Percentage** off, they see the lower price without being told
what proportion was waived — which some institutions prefer.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A scholarship is not reducing the price.">
Check it is active, in date, for that learner, and for the exact thing they are buying —
on a cohort-based course it must name the batch, not the course.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can one learner hold two scholarships?">
Each applies to what it names. For two scholarships on the same purchase, grant one for
the right total instead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Scholarship or coupon?">
Scholarship when it is about *this learner* and you may need to justify it. Coupon when
it is about *this campaign* and anybody with the code may use it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I give a full scholarship?">
Yes — a percentage of 100, or a fixed amount equal to the price. The learner still goes
through checkout, at nothing to pay.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a scholarship appear in revenue reports?">
The transaction shows what was actually paid, with the scholarship recorded as the
reason for the difference. Your revenue is the real figure.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
