---
title: "Scholarships"
description: "Offer discounted or free access through an application program tied to a course, path, or cohort."
---

A **scholarship** gives learners a discount — or free access — through an application
process. Unlike a coupon code, a scholarship has learners **apply**, and you **approve**
who gets it. Each scholarship is tied to a specific course, learning path, or cohort.

## The two types

| Type | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fixed percentage** | Every approved applicant gets the same discount (for example, 50% off). |
| **Up to a limit** | You set the exact percentage for each approved applicant, up to a maximum you've chosen. |

In both cases:

- **100%** means free, immediate access.
- A **partial** percentage means the learner buys at a reduced price.

## What you can attach

- An **application form** — questions the learner answers when applying.
- **Revoke rules** — conditions that automatically disqualify a learner later, such as
  failing a quiz or going inactive.
- **Active dates** — the window during which the scholarship accepts applications.

## How it works, step by step

1. **You create the scholarship** in **Admin → Scholarships → New Scholarship**: pick the
   course/path/cohort, choose the type and percentage, optionally add a **form** and
   **revoke rules**, and set the **active dates**.
2. **A learner applies** — they browse the public scholarships and submit an application
   (filling in your form if you added one).
3. **You review each application** and either:
   - **Approve** it. For the "up to a limit" type, you set the exact percentage for that
     learner.
   - **Reject** it, with a reason the learner can see.
4. **Later, you can revoke** an approved scholarship — manually, or automatically through
   the revoke rules you set.

## What the learner sees

- A list of **public scholarships** they can apply to.
- The result of their application — approved (with the discount applied) or rejected
  (with your reason).
- If approved at **100%**, immediate free access. If approved partially, a **reduced
  price** at checkout.

## Good to know

- Scholarships are reviewed and approved one application at a time, so you stay in control
  of who receives them.
- You can export scholarship data to a spreadsheet from the billing area.
