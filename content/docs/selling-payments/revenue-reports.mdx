The billing overview is the money view of your academy. It has two halves: what you
have earned, and what is stuck.

## What you have earned

Three cards sit across the top of <Path>Billing > Overview</Path>: **Total Revenue**,
**Payments to Check**, and **Refund Requests**. Total Revenue carries this
month against last month, and a separate figure for each currency you take.

The other two are there because they need you, not because they inform you.

Deeper in the billing screens:

| Figure | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Revenue Trend** | The direction. |
| **Revenue by Entity Type** | Courses against bundles against products, over time. |
| **Average Price** | What a sale is typically worth. |
| **Most Popular** / **Most Popular Model** | What sells, and how people pay for it. |
| **Recent Transactions** | The latest activity. |

**Most Popular Model** is quietly useful. If nearly everyone chooses instalments, your
headline price is above what your market pays comfortably — which is a pricing decision,
not a billing one.

## What is stuck

**Outstanding & at-risk** is the part to work weekly — *"what learners owe today, and
payments in the last 30 days that did not go through"*. Every tile is a number you can
click straight through to the list behind it, and each one is money that has not landed
or a learner who cannot get in.

| Tile | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Instalments overdue** | What is late, how many learners, and how much of it is late fees. It also says how many are past the grace period — those learners have lost access. |
| **Instalments due in 7 days** | What is about to fall due. The one tile that lets you act before anything goes wrong. |
| **Payments waiting to be checked** | Someone paid; nobody confirmed it. They have no access, and the tile says how long they have been waiting. |
| **Subscription renewals overdue** | A renewal that has not been paid. |
| **Lapsed subscriptions** | Renewal unpaid for more than 90 days. |
| **Failed payments** | Attempts that did not go through. |
| **Checkouts not completed** | They got to payment and stopped. |

A period selector in the corner sets how far back the panel looks — **Last 30 days** by
default.

<Callout type="warn">
**Clear payments waiting to be checked first, every week.** It is the only one of these
where a paying customer is sitting locked out of something they have already bought, and
it is the one that produces angry messages.
</Callout>

**Instalments overdue** is the second. The tile tells you how many learners are past the
grace period, and past the grace period means access paused — so that number is both
money and a support queue.

## Reading revenue honestly

**Manual enrolments are not revenue.** Enrolling somebody yourself creates no
transaction. If cash changed hands, record it — see
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments) — or your
figures are wrong by whatever you take offline.

**Refunds come off.** Check the refund rate beside revenue, not separately. See
[Refunds](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/refunds).

**Revenue is not delivery.** Money arriving is not learners finishing. Read this beside
[Programme analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/programmes): a programme earning
well with poor completion is borrowing against its reputation.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Revenue looks far lower than what we actually took.">
Cash and bank transfers taken outside the platform and never recorded. Every one of
them is missing here.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Revenue is flat but enrolments grew.">
More free enrolments, more discounts, or more manual enrolments. Compare the enrolment
figure with the transaction count.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export the figures?">
Yes — the transaction lists export, which is what most accounting handovers need.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does this include instalments not yet paid?">
Revenue is money received. What is still owed is on the outstanding and overdue lists.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
