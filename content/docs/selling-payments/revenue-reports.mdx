The billing dashboard is your money overview. It shows how much you're earning, where it's
coming from, and what needs your attention — all in one place.

## What the dashboard shows

| View | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Overall stats** | Headline numbers — total revenue and key totals at a glance. |
| **Revenue trend** | How your earnings change over time, so you can see growth or dips. |
| **Revenue by item type** | How much comes from courses, books, bundles, and so on. |
| **Revenue by program** | How much each course or program earns. |
| **Pending tasks** | Things waiting on you, such as refund requests to review. |

To open it, go to **Admin → Billing → Dashboard**.

## Exporting to a spreadsheet

Nearly everything in this section can be exported to a spreadsheet (CSV) for your own
records or accounting:

- Prices
- Transactions
- Refunds
- Offers (coupons)
- Bundles
- Installment plans
- Scholarships

Look for an **Export** button on each of these screens.

## Good to know

- The dashboard reflects real activity across your platform, so the numbers update as
  buyers purchase, subscribe, and request refunds.
- Pair the **revenue trend** with the **revenue by program** view to see which offerings
  are pulling their weight.
