---
title: "Subscriptions"
description: "Sell recurring memberships that renew each cycle and grant ongoing access."
---

A **subscription** is a recurring plan. The buyer pays on a regular cycle — monthly or
yearly, for example — and keeps access for as long as they stay subscribed. It's a good
fit for a membership site, an "all-access pass," or any offering you want to bill again
and again.

## What a subscription plan can offer

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Billing tiers** | Different options on the same plan, each with its own price and access length — for example, a monthly tier and an annual tier. |
| **Free trial** | An optional period before billing starts, so buyers can try before they pay. |
| **What it unlocks** | Either **all content** on your platform, or a **specific set of items** you choose. |
| **Access length per tier** | How long each cycle's access lasts before it renews. |

## Creating a subscription plan

1. Go to **Admin → Subscriptions → New Plan**.
2. Give the plan a **name** and **description**.
3. Add one or more **billing tiers** (for example, monthly and annual), each with its
   price and cycle length.
4. (Optional) Turn on a **free trial** and set its length.
5. Choose what the plan unlocks: **all content** or a **specific set of items**.
6. **Publish** the plan.

## How renewal and cancelling work

- Access **renews automatically** each cycle as long as the subscription is active.
- If a buyer **cancels**, access continues until the **end of the current period**, then
  stops. They aren't cut off mid-cycle.

## What the buyer sees

- The plan's tiers and prices, plus the free trial if you offer one.
- Ongoing access to whatever the plan unlocks.
- The option to cancel; after cancelling, they keep access until the period ends.

## What you see

In **Admin → Subscriptions** you can see **subscriber counts** and **revenue** for your
plans.

## Good to know

- A subscription is **one item per buyer** — a buyer holds one active plan, billed on its
  cycle, not a cart of many items.
- For a course someone pays for once, use a **one-time** price instead. See
  [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
