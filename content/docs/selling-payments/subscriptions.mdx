If you are looking for "set a monthly price and it renews itself", read this page before
planning around it.

<Callout type="warn">
**Subscription is not one of the payment modes.** A price can be **One-time Purchase**,
**Installment** or **Free**. There is no "subscription" option on the pricing form, and
the Subscriptions screens are not in the Billing menu.
</Callout>

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## How to sell recurring access today

Use **limited access** on a one-time price:

1. Set the mode to **One-time Purchase**.
2. Set **Access duration** to **Limited** — 1 month, 6 months, 1 year.
3. The learner buys. When the duration runs out, their access lapses.
4. They buy again to continue.

It is a renewal the learner initiates rather than one you take automatically. For an
academy where most people pay by mobile wallet rather than a stored card, that is closer
to how money actually moves anyway.

See [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).

## Managing lapsing access

Lapsed access is visible, so it can be worked:

- The enrolment list shows **Expires**, **Access** and **Access ended**.
- The billing overview surfaces **Lapsed subscriptions** and **Subscription renewals
  overdue**.
- An [audience](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/audiences) can be built from the
  learners whose access is about to run out, and a
  [campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns) sent to them.

A reminder a week before access ends recovers far more than one sent after.

## Paying over time is a different thing

If what you want is "the fee split across six months", that is an
[instalment plan](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments), which is fully
supported — down payment, schedule, grace period, late fees and all.

Instalments pay off a fixed total. A subscription would pay for continuing access. Most
academies asking for a subscription actually want instalments.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I charge a card automatically every month?">
Not from the pricing form. Limited access, bought again, is the supported shape.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are the Subscribers and Failed Renewals screens?">
Groundwork for recurring billing that is not finished and is not in the menu. Do not
build a commercial plan on them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I stop someone's access?">
Let the duration lapse, or end their enrolment. See
[Admin and bulk enrollment](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/admin-and-bulk-enrollment).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I offer a free trial?">
Not as a trial that converts by itself. The closest thing is a free price with limited
access — a month free, then they buy.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a learner lose their progress when access lapses?">
No. Their record is intact. They lose access to the content, and get it back when they
buy again.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
