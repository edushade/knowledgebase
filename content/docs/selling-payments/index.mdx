Three facts shape everything in this section. Knowing them first saves a lot of
backtracking:

- **Published is not for sale.** A course with no price cannot be bought.
- **One item per checkout.** No basket. To sell several together, make a
  [bundle](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/bundles).
- **A coupon has to be typed in.** Nothing discounts itself.

## The weekly habit

Four lists on the billing overview, in this order:

1. **Offline payments to verify** — people who have paid and cannot get in.
2. **Instalments overdue** — money owed, grouped by how late.
3. **Failed payments** and **Checkouts not completed** — sales that nearly happened.
4. **Pending refund requests** — decisions waiting on you.

Twenty minutes a week on those four is worth more than any report on this page.
