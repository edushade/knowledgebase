---
title: "Selling & Payments"
description: "Turn your courses and content into a storefront — set prices, take payments, offer discounts, and track your revenue."
---

This is where you sell what you've built. List your courses, books, and other items in
a storefront, set prices, accept payments, and keep an eye on how much you're earning.
Everything connects back to your courses, so once a learner pays, they get access
automatically.

You can sell a lot more than just courses. You can also offer bundles, recurring
memberships, pay-over-time plans, discount codes, and scholarships.

<Callout type="info">
  **One item per purchase.** Buyers buy one thing at a time. There is no shopping cart
  for combining many items into a single order. If you want to sell several things
  together, group them into a [bundle](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/bundles).
</Callout>

## What you can sell

| Item | Bought how often |
|---|---|
| **Courses, learning paths, cohorts, events** | Once per buyer (no duplicates) |
| **Bundles** (several items in one package) | Once per buyer |
| **Subscriptions** (recurring memberships) | Ongoing, renews each cycle |
| **Books, generic products, bookings** | Can be bought again and again |
