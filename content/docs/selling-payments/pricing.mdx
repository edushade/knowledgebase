A price connects a thing you sell to an amount. Until something has one, it cannot be
bought — however published it is.

Prices live in <Path>Billing > Pricing Management > Product Pricing</Path>.

The cards above the list are worth a glance each time you open it. **Most Popular Model**
tells you how your learners actually prefer to pay, and **Average Price** across your
catalogue is the number most academies have never worked out.

## What you can price

| Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Course** | A self-paced course. |
| **Cohort** | A batch. This is what learners buy on a cohort-based course — not the course. |
| **Learning Path** | A whole programme. |
| **Bundle** | Several things as one purchase. |
| **Event** | A seat at an event. |
| **Product** | Something that is not a course. |
| **Meeting Plan** | A bookable session. |

<Callout type="warn">
**Pricing the course instead of the batch is the commonest mistake on a cohort-based
course.** Learners enrol into a batch, so the batch needs the price. A priced course
with unpriced batches sells nothing.
</Callout>

## The three payment modes

| Mode | What it means |
|---|---|
| **One-time Purchase** | The customer pays once and gets access. |
| **Installment** | They pay over time. See [Installments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments). |
| **Free** | No charge. They still need an account, and they still enrol. |

## The amounts

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | What they actually pay. |
| **Regular amount** | The original price before discount, optional. Shown struck through, so the saving is visible. |
| **Currency** | Bangladeshi Taka (৳) or US Dollar ($). |

**Regular amount** is how you show a discount without a coupon. Set the amount to 2,000
and the regular amount to 3,000 and the course reads "৳2,000, was ৳3,000".

## How long access lasts

| Choice | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Lifetime** | Unlimited access forever. |
| **Limited** | Access expires after a set duration — a number and a unit: days, weeks, months or years. |

Limited access is how a subscription-like arrangement is actually built here: a year's
access, bought again when it runs out.

When access expires the enrolment lapses, and the learner's content closes. They keep
their record.

## When the price is live

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start date** | When the price becomes active. |
| **End date** | When it becomes inactive. |

That is how an early-bird price works: an early price that ends, and a standard price
that starts the same day.

## Selling part of a programme

A price can name particular **content scopes** — semesters — rather than the whole
programme. That is what partial access is sold through: Semester 1 at one price, the
whole diploma at another.

A semester-scoped price is governed by that semester's own enrolment window rather than
the batch's late-entry rule, which is what lets a running batch still sell Semester 3.
See [Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access) and
[Enrolment windows](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/enrollment-windows).

## A price only some people can see

**Restrict price visibility** with **Allowed users** means only the people you name can
see and buy at that price. Use it for a corporate rate, an alumni rate, or a negotiated
price you do not want published.

A restricted price and a public price can exist side by side on the same thing — the
named people see theirs, everyone else sees the public one.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The course is published but there is no Enrol button.">
It has no price. Publishing puts it in the catalogue; a price makes it buyable.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A free course — do learners still have to register?">
Yes. Free means no payment, not no account. They sign up and are enrolled immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change the price of something people have already bought?">
Yes. Existing purchases are unaffected — they bought at the price that was live then.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I sell in a currency other than Taka and Dollars?">
No. Those two are what is available.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a subscription that renews by itself?">
Not from the pricing form. The three modes are one-time, instalment and free. A
recurring arrangement is built as **limited** access that the learner buys again. See
[Subscriptions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/subscriptions).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
