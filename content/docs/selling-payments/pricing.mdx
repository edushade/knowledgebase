Pricing is how you decide what an item costs and what a buyer gets for it. Every item
you sell needs at least one price. You can also give an item **more than one price** —
for example, a one-time price and a pay-over-time plan side by side.

## The four price models

Each price uses exactly one of these models:

| Model | What it means |
|---|---|
| **One-time** | Pay once, get access. The most common choice for a course or book. |
| **Free** | No payment needed — the buyer gets access right away. |
| **Subscription** | A recurring charge that renews each cycle. See [Subscriptions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/subscriptions). |
| **Installment** | Split the cost into a down payment plus monthly payments. See [Installments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments). |

## Extra options on a price

Beyond the model, each price can carry these settings:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Access window** | How long access lasts (for example, 6 months). If you leave this empty, access doesn't expire — it's effectively lifetime. |
| **Sale price** | Show a lower price next to the regular one. Buyers see both, with the regular price struck through. |
| **Schedule** | Set a start and end date so a price is only active during a chosen period. |
| **Allowed buyers** | Restrict a price to specific people — a private price meant for one learner. |
| **Currency** | The currency for this price. Your platform is Bangladesh-first, so Taka (৳) is common, but you choose. |

## Setting a price

1. Open the item in **Admin → Products** (or the course/path/event you want to sell).
2. Go to its **Pricing** area and click **Add Price**.
3. Choose a **model**: one-time, free, subscription, or installment.
4. Enter the **amount** and pick the **currency**.
5. (Optional) Add a **sale price** to run a discount.
6. (Optional) Set an **access window** so access expires after a set time.
7. (Optional) Set a **schedule** if the price should only be active for a period.
8. (Optional) Limit it to **allowed buyers** for a private price.
9. **Save**.

## What the buyer sees

- The **price** (and the regular price struck through if there's a sale).
- For a recurring or pay-over-time price, the **billing terms** — how much and how often.
- After paying, access begins. If you set an **access window**, they'll have it for that
  period; otherwise it doesn't expire.

## Good to know

- An item can have several prices at once — for example, a standard price plus a private
  price for a sponsored learner.
- A **free** price (or a 100% discount from a coupon or scholarship) skips payment and
  grants access immediately. See [Checkout](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/checkout).
- You can export your prices to a spreadsheet from the billing area.
