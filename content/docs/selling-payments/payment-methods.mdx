Two different things are often called "payment method", and keeping them apart makes
everything else clearer.

| | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Payment method** | What the buyer uses. | bKash, Nagad, a card, cash |
| **Payment gateway** | The service that processes it. | SSLCommerz, bKash, Stripe |

A learner pays with **bKash** through **SSLCommerz**. Both are recorded on the
transaction, and when you reconcile against a statement it is the gateway's own
reference you match on.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The gateways you can connect

Set up under **Online payment** in <Path>Settings > Integrations > Payments</Path>.

| Gateway | What it needs |
|---|---|
| **SSLCommerz** | Store ID, store password, store currency. |
| **EPS** | Merchant ID, store ID, user name, password, hash key — all five. |
| **bKash** | App key, app secret, username, password. |
| **Stripe** | Secret key, and a webhook secret. |
| **PayPal** | Client ID, client secret, webhook ID. |

Each value is copied from that provider's own dashboard, under whatever they call their
developer or API settings. The field names here match theirs, so they are findable.

For an academy selling in Bangladesh, **SSLCommerz** is usually the one to start with —
it covers bKash, Nagad, Rocket and cards through one connection.

Each connection also carries:

- **Label** — a name of your own, where you have more than one.
- **Priority** — which is offered first.
- **Enabled** — on or off without deleting the configuration.
- **Live** — whether it is processing real money or test payments.

<Callout type="warn">
**Check the live switch before you announce a course.** A gateway left in test mode
takes payments that are not real, and learners are enrolled against money that never
arrived.
</Callout>

## Taking money outside a payment service

Not every payment goes through an online service, and in Bangladesh most academies take
some by bKash Send Money, bank transfer or cash. Three routes:

1. **Let the learner tell you** — they send the money to your own account and report
   it at checkout. See [Direct payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/direct-payments).
2. **Record it yourself** — [Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).
3. **Confirm what somebody else recorded** — the **Pending Verification** list on
   [Transactions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/transactions).

All three produce a real transaction, so your revenue figures include the cash.

### Online and direct, side by side

<Path>Settings > Integrations > Payments</Path> has both:

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Online payment** | The services above. Learners pay on a secure page and get access at once. |
| **Direct payments** | Your own bKash, Nagad, Rocket and Upay numbers, bank accounts and office. Your staff confirm each payment. |
| **At checkout** | Appears when both are on: which comes first, when direct payments are offered, and for which courses. |

At checkout the learner sees **Pay online** and **Send the money yourself**. Discount
codes work only when paying online.

## What the buyer sees

The methods shown at checkout are part of your checkout page — their names, their order
and their brand marks are yours to set. See
[Checkout](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/checkout).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner paid but the transaction says failed.">
Check with the gateway using its own reference. A payment that succeeded there and
failed here usually means the gateway could not tell us — the money is real, and the
right fix is to record and verify it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use more than one gateway?">
Yes. Connect several, set their priority, and enable the ones you want offered.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Our gateway is not on the list.">
Those five are what is available.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need a gateway at all?">
Not to run an academy. With direct payments learners pay into your own accounts and
your team confirms each one. You do need one to sell without anyone checking each sale.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which currencies can I charge in?">
Bangladeshi Taka and US Dollars. See
[Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
