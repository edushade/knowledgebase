An instalment plan — the screens call it an **EMI plan** — lets a learner pay a
programme off over months instead of all at once. For an expensive diploma it is often
the difference between selling and not selling.

Plans live in <Path>Billing > Pricing Management > EMI Plans</Path>.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/selling-payments/emi-plans.png"

Two columns on that list save you from the commonest mistake:

- **Entities Using** — how many courses, batches or products currently offer this plan.
- **Customers Using** — how many learners are part-way through paying on it.

<Callout type="warn">
**A plan with customers on it is not safe to edit.** They agreed to the terms that were
there when they bought. Changing the duration or the interest underneath them changes
what they owe. Make a new plan instead, and switch the price over to it — people already
paying stay on the old one.
</Callout>

The **Active EMIs** tab beside the list is the other side of the same thing: not the
plans, but the learners currently paying on one.

## Building a plan

A plan is a reusable arrangement. Build a few and attach them to prices.

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What learners see — "6 months, 20% down". |
| **Duration in months** | 1 to 60. |
| **Down payment** | What they pay up front, as a **percentage** or a **fixed** amount. |
| **Minimum down payment** | A floor, so a percentage of a cheap course is still worth taking. |
| **Processing fee** | Your charge for the arrangement, **percentage** or **fixed**. |
| **Interest type** | **None**, **Flat**, **Per Installment** or **Reducing**. |
| **Interest rate** | Up to 100%. |
| **Grace period (days)** | How long a late payment is tolerated before access is affected. |
| **Late fee** | **Percentage** or **fixed**. |
| **Status** | Active or inactive. |

<Callout type="info">
**Set a grace period you can live with.** It is the number that decides when a learner
loses access, and a learner locked out two days after a missed payment will be on the
phone the same afternoon.
</Callout>

## Offering a plan on a price

On the price: switch on **Enable EMI payment**, choose which **installment plans** may
be used, and set a **minimum amount** — the purchase total below which instalments are
not offered.

A learner buying above that minimum sees the plans at checkout and picks one.

Academy-wide rules — which entity types may offer instalments at all, and the global
minimum — are in **Global EMI Settings**.

## When the instalments are due

| Choice | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Same date every month** | The 5th, every month. |
| **Every set number of days** | 30 days from the last payment. |

## How a learner's standing is reported

Every learner on a plan carries a state, and it is on the enrolment list:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On track** | Nothing owing. |
| **Due date passed (in grace)** | Late, still inside the grace period. Access continues. |
| **Past grace — access paused** | Late beyond the grace period. **Their content is closed.** |
| **Defaulted** | Written off. |
| **Paid off** | Finished. |

**Past grace — access paused** is the one that generates support messages, and it is
worth recognising: the learner's enrolment looks live, but nothing opens. See
[How access works together](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-access-works-together).

## Chasing what is owed

The billing overview carries the lists:

- **Instalments overdue**, grouped by how late — **due in the next 7 days**,
  **1–7 days late**, **8–30 days late**, **over 30 days late**.
- **Outstanding and at-risk**, with the next due date per learner.

The grouping is the useful part. A learner 1–7 days late usually just forgot; one over
30 days late is a different conversation.

## Recording a payment taken outside the platform

**Pay Installment** records a payment against a plan — for a learner who paid you in
cash or by bank transfer. The amount is worked out for you. See
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner cannot see instalments at checkout.">
Four things, in order: **Enable EMI payment** on the price; at least one plan selected;
the purchase total above the **minimum amount**; and that entity type allowed to offer
instalments in **Global EMI Settings**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner paid but is still locked out.">
The payment may not be recorded against the instalment. Check their plan and record the
payment if it came in outside the platform.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change a plan a learner is already on?">
Editing a plan changes what it offers from then on. For a learner already on it, treat
their arrangement as agreed and handle exceptions on their own schedule.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Flat, Per Installment and Reducing interest?">
**Flat** charges on the original amount for the whole term. **Per Installment** charges
per payment. **Reducing** charges on what is still owed, so it falls as they pay.
Reducing is the cheapest for the learner and the most familiar to anyone who has had a
bank loan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a paused learner lose their progress?">
No. Their record is intact; access is closed. When the payment arrives, they carry on.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
