---
title: "Manual Payments"
description: "Recording money taken outside the platform — cash, a bank transfer, a counter payment — so the learner gets access and the figures stay honest."
---

A learner pays in cash at your office, or by bank transfer, or to an agent. The money is
real; the platform knows nothing about it. A manual payment records it, enrols them, and
keeps your revenue figures true.

<Path>Billing > Transactions > Manual Entry</Path>.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Why not just enrol them

[Enrolling someone yourself](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/admin-and-bulk-enrollment)
gives them access and creates **no transaction**. Do that for everyone who pays in cash
and your revenue reports are wrong by however much cash you take — which, for many
academies in Bangladesh, is most of it.

Record the payment instead. They get the same access, and the money appears where money
belongs.

## Recording one

<Steps>

<Step>

### Choose the customer

Search and pick the learner. They need an account first — see
[Adding people](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/adding-people).

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose what they bought

Pick the **entity type** and the thing itself. The selector shows its title, its price
and the seats left, so you can see what you are about to sell.

For a cohort-based course, pick the **batch**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the amount

**Base price** fills in from the price you picked, and can be changed. Then:

- **Apply discount** — a **percentage**, a **fixed** amount, or an **offer code**.
- **Processing fee** — anything you charge on top.
- **Other charges**, with a label of your own — an admission fee, a materials charge.

A percentage discount cannot exceed 100%, and a fixed one cannot exceed the base price.

</Step>

<Step>

### Record how they paid

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Gateway** | Which service, or offline. Required. |
| **Payment method** | Cash, bank transfer, bKash, Nagad, Rocket. Required. |
| **Payment date & time** | When they actually paid, not when you are typing. Required. |
| **Payment reference number** | Their bank reference or transaction number. |
| **Transaction/Gateway ID** | If the gateway gave one. |

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the status

| Status | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Confirmed. The learner is enrolled. |
| **Pending** | Recorded but not confirmed — it joins **Pending Verification**. |
| **Rejected** | Not accepted. |

Record a payment you have not actually seen as **Pending**, and verify it when the money
lands.

</Step>

<Step>

### Leave a note

**Internal notes** and **Admin reference** are private — the customer never sees them.
Write down who took the money and where. It is the difference between a clean month-end
and an argument.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Verifying what somebody else recorded

**Pending Verification**, on the transactions screen, is the queue of payments recorded
but not confirmed. Work it daily: every row is a learner who has paid and cannot get in.

Rows marked **Reported by learner** came from the learner themselves, at checkout — see
[Letting learners tell you they paid](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/payment-methods#letting-learners-tell-you-they-paid).
Open one to see the transaction number they typed and their receipt photo, if they added
one. Rejecting one of these needs a reason: the learner is sent it, so write it for them
("We couldn't find this transaction number in our bKash statement").

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The learner is not in the customer list.">
They have no account. Create one first — a manual payment attaches to a person, not to
a name.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I recorded the payment but they have no access.">
Check the status. **Pending** records the money without enrolling them; **Verified**
enrols them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I record a payment for an instalment plan?">
Yes — **Pay Installment** on the learner's plan. The amount is worked out for you. See
[Installments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I record a partial payment?">
Set the amount to what was actually paid. For a genuine arrangement to pay over time,
use an instalment plan rather than a series of manual entries.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do manual payments count towards revenue?">
Yes, once verified. That is the point of recording them.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
