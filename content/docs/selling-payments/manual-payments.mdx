---
title: "Manual Payments"
description: "Recording money taken outside the platform — cash, a bank transfer, a counter payment — so the learner gets access and the figures stay honest."
---

A learner pays in cash at your office, or by bank transfer, or to an agent. The money is
real; the platform knows nothing about it. A manual payment records it, enrols them, and
keeps your revenue figures true.

<Path>Billing > Transactions > Manual Entry</Path> opens **Manual Payment Entry**.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

<Callout type="info">
**Would the learner rather tell you themselves?** With
[direct payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/direct-payments) switched on,
learners report the payment at checkout and you only confirm it. Manual entry is for the
payments nobody reported.
</Callout>

## Why not just enrol them

[Enrolling someone yourself](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/admin-and-bulk-enrollment)
gives them access and creates **no transaction**. Do that for everyone who pays in cash
and your revenue reports are wrong by however much cash you take — which, for many
academies in Bangladesh, is most of it.

Record the payment instead. They get the same access, and the money appears where money
belongs.

## Recording one

The form is in sections, each with a line on the left saying what it is for.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Learner

Search by name or email and pick the learner. They need an account first — see
[Adding people](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/adding-people).

</Step>

<Step>

### What they paid for

Pick the **Kind of item** — Course, Learning path, Cohort, Bundle, Event, Product or
Meeting plan — then the **Item and price**. Each price shows its amount and the places
left, so you can see what you are about to sell.

For a cohort-based course, pick the batch.

</Step>

<Step>

### Price

The price fills in from the one you picked. Then, if they apply:

- **Discount** — **A percentage off**, **A fixed amount off**, or **An offer code**
  (press **Check code** to apply it).
- **Processing fee** — anything you charge on top.
- **Other charges**, with **What they are for** — an admission fee, a materials charge.

**Total paid** at the bottom is what the learner paid.

</Step>

<Step>

### Payment

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Paid by** | Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Bank transfer. |
| **Paid on** | When they actually paid, not when you are typing. |
| **Transaction ID** | The bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank ID, if there is one. |
| **Payment reference** | A receipt or slip number — anything else you file it under. |

</Step>

<Step>

### Status

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Received** | The money is in your account. The learner gets access now. |
| **Waiting to be checked** | Not confirmed yet. It joins **Pending Verification**, and the learner gets access once someone marks it received. |

Record a payment you have not actually seen as **Waiting to be checked**, and confirm it
when the money lands.

**Internal notes** are private — the learner never sees them. **Your reference** is for
where the payment came up, such as a ticket number or a phone call. Write down who took
the money and where. It is the difference between a clean month-end and an argument.

Press **Record payment**.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Correcting a payment

Open the payment from <Path>Billing > Transactions</Path> and press **Edit**. Payments
sent to you directly and payments your staff recorded can be edited; online payments
cannot. The learner and the kind of item stay as they were; the rest can be corrected.
Press **Save changes**.

On a payment that is not yet received, the status also offers **Not received** — the
money did not arrive or the ID does not match. The learner gets no access, and any offer
code on it can be used again.

On a payment the learner reported themselves, the form opens with **Reported by the
learner** at the top. Their transaction ID appears as **TrxID the learner sent**, which
you can correct if they mistyped it. To give access or turn it down, use **Check this
payment** — it shows what they sent and what they will read. See
[Check a direct payment](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/check-a-direct-payment).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The learner is not in the list.">
They have no account. Create one first — a manual payment attaches to a person, not to
a name.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I recorded the payment but they have no access.">
Check the status. **Waiting to be checked** records the money without enrolling them;
**Received** enrols them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="It says enrollment is closed.">
The batch or course is not taking enrolments. You can still record the payment with
**Create anyway** if you mean to let this learner in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I record a payment for an instalment plan?">
Yes — **Pay Installment** on the learner's plan. The amount is worked out for you. See
[Installments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I record a partial payment?">
Set the amount to what was actually paid. For a genuine arrangement to pay over time,
use an instalment plan rather than a series of manual entries.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do manual payments count towards revenue?">
Yes, once received. That is the point of recording them.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
