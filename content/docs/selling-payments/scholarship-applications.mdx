Where a [scholarship](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/scholarships) has an
application form, the applications arrive in
<Path>Billing > Offers & Promotions > Scholarship Applications</Path>.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## What an application carries

The applicant, the scholarship they applied for, the programme, the dates, their status
— and what they wrote on your form.

The form is yours: whatever you ask on it is what you see here. Ask for what you will
actually use to decide, and nothing else — every extra field loses applicants who were
eligible.

## Deciding

| Decision | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | The scholarship applies to that learner. |
| **Reject** | It does not. |
| **Revoke** | Withdraws one already granted. |

Each decision takes a **note**. Write one every time:

- **Approving** — on what basis, so the next person applying in the same circumstances
  is treated the same.
- **Rejecting** — what was missing, so the learner can be told something useful.
- **Revoking** — what changed.

<Callout type="info">
Scholarship decisions are the ones you will be asked to justify, sometimes months later
and sometimes by someone upset. The note is the whole defence.
</Callout>

## Statuses

**Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Revoked**. Pending is the working queue; the
rest are the record.

## Revoking

Revoking asks what should happen to the learner's access — **remain existing access**,
**revoke access**, or **limit access**. Decide that before you revoke. See
[Scholarships](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/scholarships).

## Running it fairly

- **Decide what qualifies before you open applications.** A rule written afterwards is a
  rule that bends.
- **Work the queue on a schedule**, and say what it is. Applicants waiting silently
  assume no.
- **Tell people either way.** A rejection with a reason is survivable; silence is not.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Nobody is applying.">
Check the scholarship is active and in date, and that learners can find the form.
Usually the form exists and nothing links to it — put it on the course page or send it.
See [Forms and leads](/docs/knowledgebase/website/forms-and-leads).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An approved learner still pays full price.">
Check the scholarship names the exact thing they are buying — on a cohort-based course,
the batch rather than the course — and that it is in date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I approve several at once?">
Decisions are made one at a time, with their own note. That is slower, and it is what
makes each one defensible.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner apply twice?">
They can submit again. Use the notes to record what happened the first time.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
