A product is something you sell that is not a programme. Today that means a **digital
file** — a book, a workbook, a guide — with a preview and a full version.

Products live in <Path>Commerce > Products</Path>.

<Callout type="warn">
**Products are digital only.** There is no stock, no inventory, no shipping, and no
physical fulfilment. A product is a file a buyer downloads.
</Callout>

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## What a product holds

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What buyers see. |
| **Slug** | Its web address. |
| **Short description** | The line in listings. |
| **Description** | The full text on its page. |
| **Authors** | Who wrote it. |
| **Categories** and **Tags** | How it is found. |
| **Language** | What it is in. |
| **Image** and **Product Gallery** | Its cover, and more pictures. |
| **Preview PDF** | The sample anyone may read. |
| **The file buyers receive** | The full PDF, released on purchase. |
| **Enable Ratings** | Whether buyers may review it. |
| **Meta Title** and **Meta Description** | What search engines show. |
| **Status** | Draft or published. |

The preview is worth real effort. For a book, the first chapter sells the rest better
than any description.

## Taxonomies

<Path>Commerce > Products > Taxonomies</Path> lets you create your own ways of
classifying products — a taxonomy like "Fabric Type" with terms like "Organic Cotton",
each with its own slug.

Use it for the attributes buyers actually filter on in your catalogue. Categories and
tags cover the general case; a taxonomy is for a dimension specific to what you sell.

## Pricing a product

In [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing), with the entity type
**Product**. The same options apply — one-time, instalment or free, with lifetime or
limited access.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I sell a physical book?">
Not as a fulfilled order. There is no inventory or shipping. You could sell a product
and handle delivery yourself, but nothing in the platform tracks it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a product be in a bundle?">
Yes. A book alongside the course it accompanies is a good bundle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What stops a buyer sharing the file?">
Nothing technical beyond their own account. Treat a sold PDF as something that can
travel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a product be given away free?">
Yes — a free price. It still needs an account, so you get a record of who took it,
which is the point of a free guide.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do products appear in search and the catalogue?">
Yes, with their categories, tags and taxonomies as filters. See
[Search and discovery](/docs/knowledgebase/search).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
