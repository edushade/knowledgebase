Checkout is a **page on your website**, built from a Checkout block — which means the
wording, the steps and the payment methods shown are yours to set, not fixed by the
platform.

<Callout type="warn">
**One item per checkout.** There is no basket and no multi-item order. A buyer purchases
one thing at a time. To sell several together, make them one
[bundle](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/bundles).
</Callout>

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## The two steps

### Step 1 — who they are

Name, phone and email. A buyer who is already signed in sees their own details with a
"not you?" link instead of an empty form.

Where your academy checks age, this step asks for a **date of birth**, and a learner
below the age you allow is asked for a **parent's email address** so consent can be
collected. See [Privacy and consent](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/privacy-and-consent).

### Step 2 — how they pay

The payment methods you have switched on, each with its own name and brand mark —
**bKash**, **Nagad**, and a catch-all for other cards and mobile wallets. A note under
the methods is yours to write.

Where you also take [direct payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/direct-payments),
this step has two cards:

| Card | What the learner reads |
|---|---|
| **Pay online** | Your online methods, marked **Starts right away** for a course, learning path or bundle. |
| **Send the money yourself** | Each of your own accounts — for example **Send Money by bKash** or **Bank transfer** — with how soon you check, and the badge **Opens once we've checked**. |

Choosing an account changes the button to **Show me where to send it**, and the learner
is walked through sending the money and telling you the transaction ID. Which card comes
first is your choice.

See [Payment methods](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/payment-methods).

## The order summary

Beside the steps sits the order: what they are buying, the fee, and the total. A coupon
code is entered here — see
[Coupons and offers](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/coupons-and-offers).
A code works only for paying online: choosing **Send the money yourself** removes it and
hides the code box, and the learner sends the full price.

Where instalments are offered on that price, the plans appear for the buyer to choose
from.

## What you can change

Because checkout is a block, nearly everything on it is editable in the website builder:

| What | Examples |
|---|---|
| Headings and labels | "Almost there", the name and phone labels |
| Error messages | What a bad phone number says |
| The methods shown | Which are offered, their names, their brand marks |
| The note under the methods | "All payments are secure" |
| The order card | Its heading, the line under the item, the back link |

Every piece of text can be written in each of your site's languages.

See [Pages and blocks](/docs/knowledgebase/website/pages-and-blocks).

## After payment

The enrolment is created as soon as the payment succeeds, and the programme appears
under **My Course** on the learner's dashboard.

If the payment fails, nothing is enrolled and the transaction records the failure. Those
rows are worth working — see
[Transactions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/transactions). Where you take direct
payments, the page that says *"Your payment didn't go through"* also offers **Or send the
money yourself**, so a learner whose card was refused can still pay you.

A learner who reported a direct payment opens the course once your team confirms it.
Until then the course page shows *"We're checking your payment"* in place of the buy
button, so nobody pays twice.

## What checkout does not do

- **No basket.** One item per purchase.
- **No order numbers, inventory, shipping or tax calculation.**
- **No buying for somebody else.** There is no gift purchase and no "send this to a
  payer" flow. Take the payment and enrol the recipient yourself.
- **No automatic discounts.** A coupon has to be typed in.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner wants to buy two courses at once.">
Two purchases, or one bundle. There is no basket.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a parent pay for a learner?">
Not through checkout. Take the payment yourself and record it — see
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments). The parent's
email asked for at checkout is about consent for a young learner, not about who pays.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A buyer got as far as paying and stopped.">
They are on the **Checkouts not completed** list. One message recovers a useful share.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The checkout page does not match our branding.">
It is a block on your site. Edit it in the website builder.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner says the coupon box is not there.">
Check the block's configuration on your checkout page.
</Accordion>
