<Path>Billing > Transactions</Path> is the record of money. Most of it you will never
read. Four slices of it need working every week.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The lists

| List | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **All Transactions** | Everything. For looking one payment up. |
| **Pending Verification** | Offline payments waiting for you to confirm. |
| **Manual Entry** | Recording a payment taken outside the platform. |
| **Refunds** | Refund requests and what happened to them. |

## What a transaction carries

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Transaction ID** | Your reference. |
| **Customer** | Who paid. |
| **Entity** and **Entity Type** | What they bought. |
| **Amount**, **Original Amount**, **Currency** | What it cost, and what it cost before any discount. |
| **Payment Method** | bKash, Nagad, card, cash, bank transfer. |
| **Payment Gateway** | Which service processed it — not the same as the method. |
| **Payment Status** | Pending, Success, Failed, Refunded, Cancelled, Processing. |
| **Offer Applied** / **Scholarship Applied** | Why the amount is lower than the price. |
| **Internal Notes** | Private — the customer never sees these. |

<Callout type="info">
**Method and gateway are different things.** A learner pays with **bKash** (the method)
through **SSLCommerz** (the gateway). When you are reconciling against a gateway
statement, the gateway's own transaction reference is the one to match on — it is on
the transaction, and can be copied straight from the screen.
</Callout>

## The four things to work weekly

The billing overview surfaces these as **Quick Actions**, and each is a list of real
problems:

### Offline payments to verify

Someone said they paid by bank transfer. Until you confirm it, they have no access.
This is the one to clear daily — a learner who paid and is waiting is the unhappiest
customer you have.

### Failed payments

Payments that did not go through. Some are a card problem the learner has already
solved; some are a learner who tried, failed and gave up. The second group is money
sitting on the floor.

### Checkouts not completed

They got as far as paying and stopped. A single message recovers a surprising share.

### Paid, no access yet

Money in, no enrolment. Rare, and always worth looking at: it means the payment
succeeded and the thing it paid for did not happen.

## Finding one payment

Search by customer, by transaction ID, or by the gateway's reference. All three work,
and the gateway reference is the one an outside enquiry will quote.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says they paid and has no access.">
Find the transaction. **Pending** means it has not completed — if it was an offline
payment, verify it. **Success** with no enrolment is the "paid, no access yet" case and
needs looking at directly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The amount does not match the price.">
Check **Offer Applied** and **Scholarship Applied**, and compare **Amount** against
**Original Amount**. A discount, a coupon or a scholarship explains almost all of them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete a transaction?">
No, and you should not want to. Money records are a trail. Refund it or cancel it —
both leave the history intact.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I reconcile with the gateway's statement?">
Match on the gateway's own transaction reference, not on the platform's transaction ID.
The gateway reference is what the statement shows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a manual enrolment appear here?">
No. Enrolling someone yourself creates no transaction. If money changed hands outside
the platform, record it as a manual payment. See
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
