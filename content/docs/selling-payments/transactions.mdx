<Path>Billing > Transactions</Path> is the record of money. Most of it you will never
read. A few slices of it need working every week.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The lists

Under **Transactions** in the Billing menu:

| List | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **All Transactions** | Everything. For looking one payment up. |
| **Pending Verification** | Payments learners sent you directly, and ones your staff recorded as waiting. The number beside **Billing** in the menu is how many are waiting. |
| **Manual Entry** | Recording a payment taken outside the site. |
| **Refunds** | Refund requests and what happened to them. |

## The columns

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Where the payment stands (below). A payment a learner told you about adds **Reported by learner**. |
| **Amount** | What was paid, and how much of it was refunded. |
| **Customer** | Who paid. Links to their profile. |
| **Item** | What they bought, with its kind underneath. |
| **Date** | When. |
| **Transaction ID** | The bKash, bank or payment-service ID, with a copy button, and your own order number under it. |
| **Paid through** | **Directly to you** for money sent to your own accounts, **No payment needed** for a free enrolment, or the name of the online payment service. |
| **Method** | bKash, Nagad, card, cash, bank transfer. |

Click a row to open the payment's own page. The **⋯** at the end of a row has **View**,
**Edit** (for direct payments), **Download receipt** (once paid) and **Refund** (once
paid, if your role allows it).

### What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Paid** | The money arrived and the learner has access. |
| **Confirmed** | A free enrolment. |
| **Waiting to be checked** | Money sent to you directly that nobody has confirmed yet. Click it to check it. |
| **Pending** | An online payment that has not finished. |
| **Not accepted** | A direct payment your team could not confirm. |
| **Failed** | An online payment that did not go through. |
| **Refund requested**, **Partly refunded**, **Refunded** | Where a refund stands. |
| **Cancelled** | Stopped before it was paid. |

## Filtering

| Filter | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **Paid**, **Pending or waiting to be checked**, **Failed or not accepted**, **Refunded**, **Cancelled**. |
| **Item type** | Course, Learning Path, Cohort, Bundle, Event, Product, Meeting Plan — then the item itself. |
| **Paid through** | **Directly to you**, or one online payment service. |
| **Currency** | Shown when you have sold in more than one. |
| **Needs follow-up** | **Checkouts not completed**, **Failed and not paid since**, **Paid, no access yet**. |
| **Date** | The admin's date filter, counted in your academy's time zone. See [Date filters and your time zone](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/date-filters). |

**Manual Entry**, above the list, records a payment taken outside the site.

## The payment's page

Each payment opens as one page of cards. At the top: the amount, its status, who paid
and what for, and one sentence saying where it stands — *"Paid on 21 Nov. Rahim has
access."* or *"Waiting to be checked. Rahim reported sending it on 21 Nov, 10:42 am."*
Beside it, **Download receipt** once it is paid, and **Edit** for a direct payment.

| Card | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Check this payment** | For a direct payment waiting to be checked: what the learner sent and the decision, as on Pending Verification. |
| **Transaction IDs** | Every ID you can search for on your statement or in the payment service's panel, each with a copy button, and your **Order number** last. |
| **What the learner reported** | What they sent, and — if you turned it down — why, and what they read. |
| **Payment** | Amount, **Paid through**, method, fees, what you receive, and the date. |
| **Learner** | Who paid, how much they have paid in total, and **All their payments**. |
| **What they bought** | The item, its price, any discount or scholarship, and the access it gives. |
| **Instalments** | For an instalment plan: what is paid and what is still due. |
| **Internal notes** | Only your staff see these. |
| **Activity** | What happened to this payment, oldest first. |
| **Related payments** | Other instalments of this purchase, or other payments by this learner. |

<Callout type="info">
**Reconcile on the Transaction IDs card.** A learner pays with **bKash** through an
online payment service, and the statement shows that service's own reference — not
your order number. The card lists each reference by the name the statement uses, so
you search for the right one.
</Callout>

## The things to work weekly

The billing overview's **Pending Tasks** and the **Needs follow-up** filter bring up
these:

### Payments waiting to be checked

Someone sent you money and told you. Until you confirm it, they have no access. Clear it
daily — a learner who paid and is waiting is the unhappiest customer you have. See
[Check a direct payment](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/check-a-direct-payment).

### Failed payments

Payments that did not go through. Some are a card problem the learner has already
solved; some are a learner who tried, failed and gave up. **Failed and not paid since**
shows the second group.

### Checkouts not completed

They got as far as paying and stopped. A single message recovers a surprising share.

### Paid, no access yet

Money in, no enrolment. Rare, and always worth looking at: it means the payment
succeeded and the thing it paid for did not happen.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says they paid and has no access.">
Find the payment. **Waiting to be checked** means they sent it to you directly — check
it. **Pending** means an online payment that has not finished. **Paid** with no
enrolment is the **Paid, no access yet** case and needs looking at directly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The amount does not match the price.">
Open the payment and read **What they bought**: a discount, an offer code or a
scholarship explains almost all of them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete a transaction?">
No, and you should not want to. Money records are a trail. Refund it or cancel it —
both leave the history intact.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I reconcile with the payment service's statement?">
Use the **Transaction IDs** card on the payment's page. It names each reference the way
the statement does.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is there no Edit on an online payment?">
An online payment is recorded by the payment service itself. Only payments sent to you
directly, or recorded by your staff, can be edited.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a manual enrolment appear here?">
No. Enrolling someone yourself creates no transaction. If money changed hands outside
the platform, record it as a manual payment. See
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
