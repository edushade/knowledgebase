---
title: "Quiet Hours"
description: "Not messaging people at three in the morning — and the one setting that keeps urgent messages flowing."
---

Quiet hours stop notifications going out overnight. A learner woken at 3am by a
marketing SMS does not become a better customer.

<Path>Notifications > Settings</Path>, under **Quiet Hours**.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable Quiet Hours** | Turns the whole thing on. |
| **Start Time** / **End Time** | The window — for example 22:00 to 08:00. |
| Timezone | Which clock the window is measured against. |
| **Apply to Marketing Only** | Holds marketing, lets everything else through. |

## Turn on "Marketing Only"

<Callout type="warn">
Without it, quiet hours hold **everything** — including a password reset at 11pm from a
learner who cannot get in, and a payment confirmation for somebody who has just paid.
</Callout>

With **Apply to Marketing Only** on, the split is the one you want: offers and
promotions wait until morning; verification, payments and deadlines go now.

## Choosing the window

For a Bangladesh academy, 22:00 to 08:00 on **Asia/Dhaka** is a reasonable default.

Think about when your learners actually study. An academy whose learners are working
adults studying at 11pm has a different evening from one teaching schoolchildren.

## Learners in other timezones

If you teach across countries, a single window cannot be night everywhere. A learner's
own timezone is on their profile — see
[Profiles and devices](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/profiles-and-devices) — and it
is worth getting right for anybody studying from abroad.

## What quiet hours do not do

- **They do not hold the in-app inbox.** That is not a disturbance; it waits for the
  learner to sign in.
- **They do not stop a learner acting at night.** This is about what you send, not what
  they do.
- **They do not reschedule.** A held message goes when the window ends, not at a time
  you choose.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner did not get a password reset at night.">
Quiet hours without **Apply to Marketing Only**. Turn it on — a password reset is never
a marketing message.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I set different hours per channel?">
One window, for all channels. The marketing-only switch is the finer control.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I set different hours per audience?">
No. It is academy-wide.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do quiet hours apply to a scheduled campaign?">
A marketing campaign scheduled inside the window waits for it to end, with marketing-only
on. Schedule campaigns for when people will read them anyway.
</Accordion>
