Most of what your academy sends is not a campaign — it is the platform telling somebody
something happened. A payment succeeded, an assignment is due, a certificate was issued.

<Path>Notifications > Settings</Path> is where all of that is governed.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The channel switches

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable Email Notifications** | Email on or off academy-wide. |
| **Enable SMS Notifications** | SMS on or off. |
| **Enable Push Notifications** | Push on or off. |
| **Enable In-App Inbox** | The bell inside your platform. |

Each channel also has its defaults — for email, a **Default From Name**, **Default From
Email** and a **reply-to address**; for push, a default icon and badge.

<Callout type="warn">
**Set the From name and reply-to address before you send anything.** An email from "no
reply" that a learner cannot answer is a support request they will send some other way.
</Callout>

## What can be notified about

Notification types are grouped by what they are about. The groups you will actually
configure:

| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Account Updates** | Verification, password reset, sign-in from a new device, profile updated. |
| **Course Updates** | Course started, new content added, lesson completed, milestone reached. |
| **Assignment Updates** | Deadline approaching, graded, overdue. |
| **Quizzes** | Completed, deadline approaching, attempts exhausted. |
| **Enrolment** | Expiring soon, expired, extended, revoked, bulk enrolment created. |
| **Cohorts** | Starting soon, started, ended, enrolment opened. |
| **Payment & Billing** | Payment successful, payment failed, invoice generated, instalment due, instalment overdue. |
| **Certificates** | Issued, expiring soon, expired, revoked. |
| **Class & Meeting Alerts** | Scheduled, reminder, started, cancelled, rescheduled, recording available. |
| **Discussions** | Reply received, mentioned, instructor replied, answer accepted. |
| **Learning Paths** | Started, course unlocked, completed. |

For each, you choose which channels carry it.

## The ones worth getting right

| Notification | Why |
|---|---|
| **Account Verification** | Nobody gets in without it. |
| **Password Reset** | Nobody gets back in without it. |
| **Payment Successful / Failed** | Money. The failed one especially — it is a sale you can still save. |
| **Instalment Due Soon / Overdue** | Money you are owed. |
| **Enrollment Expiring Soon** | A renewal you will otherwise lose. |
| **Assignment Deadline Approaching** | The single most effective nudge in any academy. |
| **Certificate Issued** | The moment a learner tells other people about you. |

## Rate limits and marketing

Separate limits for marketing, per user:

| Setting | What it caps |
|---|---|
| **Marketing Emails per Day** / **per Week** | How often one person is emailed. |
| **Marketing SMS per Day** / **per Week** | The same for SMS. |
| **Marketing Push per Day** / **per Week** | And push. |
| **Exclude Transactional from Limits** | Keeps payment and verification messages flowing whatever the marketing cap. |

**Turn on Exclude Transactional.** Otherwise a marketing cap can block a verification
email, and the learner cannot get in at all.

## Quiet hours

**Enable Quiet Hours**, with a start time, an end time and a timezone, stops messages
going out overnight. **Apply to Marketing Only** keeps urgent messages flowing while
holding the rest.

See [Quiet hours](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/quiet-hours).

## What learners control

From their own settings, learners choose what they receive and on which channel.
Marketing can be switched off; transactional messages they need cannot.

That is why categorising a message honestly matters — see
[Message templates](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/message-templates).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Learners are not getting payment confirmations.">
Check the notification type is enabled on a channel, that the channel is on, and that a
sending service is connected. See [Channels](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/channels).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We are sending too much.">
Lower the marketing rate limits and turn on quiet hours. Both act per learner, so one
enthusiastic campaign cannot flood anybody.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner says they opted out but still gets emails.">
They opted out of marketing. Transactional messages — payments, verification,
deadlines — still reach them, and should.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change the wording of these?">
Yes, through [templates](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/message-templates).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="SMS is costing too much.">
Narrow which notification types use it. Verification and payment are worth it; a lesson
completed is not.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
