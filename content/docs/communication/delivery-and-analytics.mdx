This page is your view into how your messaging is doing and how to stay compliant. You can
see what reached people, what they opened, and what they clicked — and you have the tools
to honor opt-outs and keep proper consent records.

## Delivery and engagement at a glance

You get results at two levels:

| Level | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Per campaign** | Results for one specific [campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns). |
| **Platform-wide** | The big picture across all your sending. |

The key numbers you can track:

- **Delivered** — how many messages reached people.
- **Opened** — how many were opened.
- **Clicked** — how many people clicked a link inside.
- **Bounced** — how many could not be delivered.

You can also see:

- A **channel breakdown** — compare email vs SMS vs push vs chat apps.
- A **timeline** — how activity built up over time.
- An **export** — download the figures to a spreadsheet for your own records.

## Delivery logs

Beyond the totals, you can look at a record of individual messages to see what happened to
each one — whether it was sent, delivered, opened, or clicked. This is useful when a learner
says they did not receive something and you want to check.

## Compliance tools

Staying compliant keeps your messages trusted and delivered. Your platform includes:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **One-click unsubscribe** | A link in your messages that lets a recipient opt out instantly, with no sign-in needed. |
| **Suppression list** | A list of people who should never be messaged — opt-outs are added here so they are not contacted again by mistake. |
| **Consent & retention controls** | Settings to record that people agreed to be contacted, and to control how long message records are kept. |

<Callout type="info">
When someone unsubscribes, they are added to the suppression list automatically. From then
on, your campaigns skip them — you do not have to remember to remove them yourself.
</Callout>

## How to view your results

1. Go to **Admin → Communication → Analytics** for the platform-wide view.
2. Or open a single campaign and view its **Results** tab for that campaign's numbers.
3. Use **Export** to download a spreadsheet.

## What the learner sees

- A clear unsubscribe option in marketing messages.
- Once they opt out, the messaging stops — they will not keep getting the same campaigns.

## Good to know

- Watch **bounces**: a rising bounce rate often means out-of-date addresses or numbers, and
  hurts how often your messages get delivered.
- Compare the **channel breakdown** to learn where your audience actually pays attention, then
  focus your effort there.
- Keeping the suppression list and consent records in good shape protects your sender
  reputation and keeps you compliant.
