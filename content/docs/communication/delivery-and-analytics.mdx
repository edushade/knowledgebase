A message sent is not a message received. Three screens under
<Path>Notifications > Monitoring</Path> tell you which.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The three views

| Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **System Notifications** | Messages the platform sent by itself — verification, enrolment, payment. |
| **Campaign Notifications** | Messages from campaigns you sent. |
| **Delivery Logs** | Every attempt, with what happened. |

A campaign also has its own analytics, for just that send.

## Reading a delivery log

| Field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Recipient** | Who it was for. |
| **Channel** | Email, SMS, push, in-app. |
| **Provider** | Which service carried it. |
| **Status** | **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Not delivered**. |
| **Attempts** | How many times it was tried. |
| **Error** | Why it failed. |
| **Sent At** | When. |

**Sent** and **Delivered** are different. Sent means handed to the service; delivered
means it arrived. The gap between the two is where problems live.

## Telling the two kinds of problem apart

| What you see | Whose problem |
|---|---|
| Nothing sent at all | **Yours.** No service connected, or it is switched off. |
| Sent, not delivered, many recipients | **Yours.** Rate limits, or the service rejecting you. |
| Sent, not delivered, one recipient | **Theirs.** A bad address, a full mailbox, a dead number. |
| Delivered but they say otherwise | **Theirs.** Spam folder. |
| Nothing sent to one person | **Their preferences.** They switched that channel off. |

<Callout type="info">
Check this before replying to "I never got the email". It takes a minute and it answers
the question properly — "it was delivered at 14:12, please check spam" rather than a
guess.
</Callout>

## What to watch

**Not delivered, rising.** Something changed — a service's limits, your sending
reputation, a credential. Catch it in a week, not a term.

**Attempts above one.** Messages are being retried. Occasional is normal; routine means
a service that is struggling.

**A channel at zero.** Nothing is going out on it. Usually a service switched off, and
usually discovered far too late.

## Before an intake

Four things, every time:

1. Send a test on each channel you use.
2. Check nothing is at zero.
3. Check the rate limits against what you are about to send.
4. Register a test account and go through it — verification, welcome, the lot.

An academy that skips this discovers on registration day that no verification emails are
going out, and loses the intake.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says they never got the verification email.">
Find it in the delivery logs. **Delivered** means spam folder. **Not delivered** with an
error means the address. Nothing at all means your sending service.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A campaign shows fewer sent than the audience size.">
Learners who switched that channel off are not sent to. The gap is usually preferences,
not failure.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Everything stopped at once.">
A service's credentials or limits. Run its connection test — see
[Channels](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/channels).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I resend to the ones that failed?">
Build an audience of those recipients and send again. Fix the cause first, or they will
fail the same way.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long are logs kept?">
Long enough for the question in front of you, not forever. Export anything you need to
keep.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
