---
title: "Notification Inbox"
description: "The in-app feed where each learner sees their personal messages and unread count."
---

The notification inbox is each learner's personal feed inside your platform. It collects
the messages meant for them — reminders, updates, and announcements sent over the
platform — so they have one place to catch up, even if they missed the email or text.

The inbox is different from the [notice board](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/announcements).
The notice board shows shared notices for a group; the inbox shows messages addressed to
that one person.

## What the learner sees

- A bell or inbox icon with an **unread count** showing how many new messages they have.
- A list of messages, newest first. Each message has:
  - A **title**.
  - A short **body**.
  - An optional **action link** — for example "Resume your course" or "View your receipt".
- The ability to **mark one message as read**, or **mark all as read** at once.

## How it fills up

The inbox is filled automatically as things happen on your platform and as you send
[campaigns](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns). A learner does not add anything
themselves — it is their record of messages sent to them.

Each learner controls how much arrives, on which channels, in their
[notification preferences](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/notification-preferences).

## What is kept and what is not

Most messages stay in the inbox so a learner can read them later. Some sensitive,
one-time messages are **not** kept in the inbox — for example a sign-in verification code.
These are delivered once and then gone, which keeps private codes from sitting around.

<Callout type="info">
The inbox is part of every signed-in person's experience — learners, instructors, and
admins all have one.
</Callout>

## Good to know

- The unread count clears as the learner reads messages or chooses **mark all as read**.
- An action link is the fastest way to send someone straight to the right page — use it in
  your [message templates](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/message-templates) where it helps.
