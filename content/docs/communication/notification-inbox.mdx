Every learner has a notification inbox: a bell with the things your academy has told
them. It is the in-app channel, and it is the only one that cannot bounce, go to spam,
or cost money.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## What a learner sees

Their notifications, newest first, with unread ones marked. They can mark one read or
open the full list — **View All Notifications**. When they are through, it says *"You're
all caught up!"*

Each notification can carry a link, so "your assignment was graded" takes them to the
grade rather than making them look for it.

## What lands there

Whatever you have enabled on the in-app channel — payments, deadlines, grades,
certificates, discussion replies, enrolment changes. See
[Notification settings](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/notification-preferences).

Refunds are a good example of how much detail arrives: a learner is told when their
request is received, when it is approved, and when it completes, each with the amount
and the item.

## Inbox, noticeboard, campaign

Three different things, and learners meet all three:

| | **Inbox** | **Noticeboard** | **Campaign** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it carries | Things that happened to them | Announcements to a group | Something you chose to send |
| Who it is about | One learner | Everyone, or an audience | An audience |
| Travels outside the platform | No | No | Yes |

## Why it is worth keeping on

**It cannot fail to deliver.** No address to be wrong, no spam folder, no cost per
message. If a learner signs in, they see it.

What it cannot do is reach somebody who does not sign in — which is the whole case for
email and SMS alongside it.

## The push prompt

Where push notifications are enabled, learners are asked whether to allow them. Ask at a
moment that makes sense — after they enrol, not the second they arrive — and some of
them will say yes.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner's inbox is empty.">
Either nothing has happened to them yet, or the in-app channel is off for those
notification types.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send a message straight to one learner's inbox?">
Not as a one-off. The inbox carries notifications and the noticeboard carries
announcements. For one person, a campaign to an audience of one, or an email.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can learners switch the inbox off?">
They control what they receive and where, in their own settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long do notifications stay?">
They build up a history, with the unread ones marked. Nothing needs clearing.
</Accordion>
