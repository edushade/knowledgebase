A campaign is how you send the same message to a group of people at once. Pick a message,
choose who gets it, choose the channels, and send. Campaigns are great for welcome series,
course launches, reminders, and promotions.

Campaigns reach people over [channels](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/channels) like
email, SMS, push, and chat apps. If you only want a notice inside your platform, use the
[notice board](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/announcements) instead.

## What goes into a campaign

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | The group of people who will receive it — see [Audiences](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/audiences). |
| **Message template** | The message design to send — see [Message templates](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/message-templates). |
| **Channels** | The ways to deliver it — email, SMS, push, or a chat app. |
| **Timing** | Send now, schedule for later, or repeat on a regular basis. |

## How to send a campaign

1. Go to **Admin → Communication → Campaigns**.
2. Click **New Campaign**.
3. Choose the **audience**.
4. Choose the **message template**.
5. Pick the **channels** to send on.
6. Choose the **timing** — now, scheduled, or recurring.
7. Review, then click **Send** (or **Schedule**).

## Timing options

- **One-off** — send a single message right away.
- **Scheduled** — pick a future date and time; the campaign sends itself then.
- **Recurring** — send on a regular schedule, such as a weekly digest.

## The life of a campaign

A campaign moves through clear stages, and you stay in control at each step:

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved while you set it up. Nothing is sent yet. |
| **Send** | The campaign starts going out to the audience. |
| **Pause** | Sending stops temporarily. |
| **Resume** | Paused sending picks up again. |
| **Cancel** | The campaign is stopped for good. |
| **Duplicate** | Make a copy to reuse the setup for a new send. |
| **Rerun** | Run a finished campaign again. |

<Callout type="info">
**Duplicate** is the fastest way to repeat a successful campaign — copy it, tweak the
audience or wording, and send.
</Callout>

## See how it performed

Every campaign comes with its own results so you can learn what worked:

- **Delivered** — how many messages reached people.
- **Opened** — how many were opened.
- **Clicked** — how many people clicked a link inside.
- **Bounced** — how many could not be delivered.
- A **channel breakdown**, so you can compare email vs SMS vs push.
- A **timeline** showing activity over time.

For the full picture across all your sending, see
[Delivery & analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/delivery-and-analytics).

## What the learner sees

- The message arrives on the channels they have agreed to receive — and only those.
- It respects their [preferences](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/notification-preferences)
  and [quiet hours](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/quiet-hours).
- Marketing messages always include a way to unsubscribe.

## Good to know

- Send a [test](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/message-templates) of your template to
  yourself before launching to a big audience.
- A smaller, well-chosen [audience](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/audiences) usually
  beats sending to everyone — people open messages that are relevant to them.
- Scheduling lets you write a campaign during work hours but send it at the best time for
  your learners.
