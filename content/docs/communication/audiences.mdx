An audience — the screens call it a **segment** — is a group of people defined by
conditions. "Learners in Dhaka who have not signed in for 30 days" is an audience.

<Path>Notifications > Segments</Path>.

## Built-in audiences

Five come with your academy, marked **Built-in**, and they keep themselves up to date —
nobody has to maintain the list.

| Audience | Who is in it |
|---|---|
| **All learners** | Everyone with a learner account. |
| **Instructors** | Everyone who teaches. |
| **Learners with an active enrollment** | Enrolled in at least one course, batch or learning path right now. |
| **Learners active in the last 30 days** | Opened any of their courses in the last 30 days. |
| **Learners not enrolled in anything** | Registered, never enrolled. |

Those last two are the quietly valuable ones. **Learners not enrolled in anything** is
everybody who signed up and never bought — the cheapest audience you will ever have.
**Learners active in the last 30 days** inverted gives you the people slipping away.

For anything else, build your own.

## Building one

An audience is conditions on fields, grouped under **Advanced Options**:

| Category | Fields |
|---|---|
| **User Profile** | Name, Email, Phone, Gender, Language, Timezone, Status, Created At |
| **Classification** | Is Learner, Is Instructor, Is Guest, Source |
| **Contact Info** | Email Verified, Consent Given, User Status |
| **Enrollment** | Enrolled in Course / Learning Path / Cohort, Total Enrollments |
| **Activity** | First Seen, Last Seen |
| **Preferences** | Country, Continent |

Operators follow the field: text offers *equals*, *contains*, *is one of*; numbers offer
*greater than*, *between*; dates offer *before*, *in the last*, *in the next*.

<Callout type="info">
These are the same fields
[eligibility rules](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/eligibility-rules) are built
from. An audience you have worked out is a rule you can reason about, and the reverse.
</Callout>

## Always preview

**Preview results** shows who is currently in the audience. Use it every time.

Two failures it catches instantly:

- **Nobody matches.** Usually a condition on a field most learners never filled in, or
  conditions that have to be true at once when you meant either.
- **Everybody matches.** Usually a condition that does nothing.

A campaign to nobody is a wasted afternoon. A campaign to everybody is worse.

## Static and dynamic

| Type | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Dynamic** | Recalculated when it is used. Somebody who becomes inactive joins it automatically. |
| **Static** | A fixed list, as it was. |

Dynamic for anything you will send repeatedly. Static when you need the same people
twice — a before-and-after message to exactly one group.

## Audiences worth keeping

| Name | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Never signed in | Registered, never seen |
| Gone quiet | Last seen more than 30 days ago |
| Enrolled, not started | Has enrolments, no progress |
| About to lapse | Access ending in the next 7 days |
| Finished last term | Completed a programme recently |

Build them once as dynamic audiences and they stay useful for years.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Save is refused.">
Add at least one condition with a field actually selected. An empty condition row counts
as incomplete.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Preview shows nobody.">
Your conditions all have to be true at once. Check each one on its own — a condition on
Gender, for instance, excludes everyone who never filled it in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I build an audience from grades or progress?">
Not from these fields. For "everyone behind in this course", work from the
[progress analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/progress) and use a programme-based
audience.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does an audience respect notification preferences?">
Yes. A learner who has switched a channel off does not receive on it, whatever audience
they are in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export an audience?">
Use the people or enrolment list with the same filters, which exports.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
