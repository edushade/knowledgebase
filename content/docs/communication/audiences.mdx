---
title: "Audiences"
description: "Build groups of people to send to — fixed manual lists or rule-based groups that update on their own."
---

An audience is a group of people you want to message. Build an audience once, then reuse it
in any [campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns). Audiences let you reach the
right people instead of messaging everyone every time.

## Two kinds of audience

| Type | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Static** | A manual list of people you pick yourself. | A fixed group that does not change, like a specific event's attendees. |
| **Dynamic** | A group defined by rules, which updates itself as people match or stop matching. | An ever-changing group, like "learners who haven't finished Module 1". |

A static audience stays exactly as you set it. A dynamic audience refreshes on its own — new
people who match the rules join automatically, and people who no longer match drop out.

## What dynamic rules can use

You build a dynamic audience by setting conditions on what you know about each learner. You
can filter on:

- **Learner details** — the basic information on their profile.
- **Enrollment** — what they are enrolled in.
- **Progress** — how far they have gotten.
- **Completion** — what they have finished.
- **Quiz scores** — how they performed on quizzes.
- **Subscription or billing status** — for example, active or lapsed.
- **Engagement** — how active they have been recently.

For example: "learners enrolled in *Photography Basics* who are less than 50% through and
have not signed in for two weeks."

## Preview and size

Before you send, you can:

- **Preview** the audience to see who is in it right now.
- See an **estimated size** — how many people it currently includes.

This helps you confirm you are reaching the right group before launching a campaign.

## How to create an audience

1. Go to **Admin → Communication → Audiences**.
2. Click **New Audience**.
3. Choose **Static** (pick people) or **Dynamic** (set rules).
4. For dynamic, add your conditions.
5. Click **Preview** to check who matches and the estimated size.
6. Click **Save**.

## Good to know

- Dynamic audiences are powerful for ongoing campaigns — set the rule once and the right
  people always receive it.
- Use **Preview** before every important send, so there are no surprises about who gets it.
- For comparison, the [notice board](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/announcements) has
  its own built-in targeting (by role, course, cohort, and more) — audiences here are for
  email, SMS, push, and chat campaigns.
