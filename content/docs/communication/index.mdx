Three different things reach your learners, and knowing which is which saves a lot of
confusion:

| | What it is | Travels outside the platform |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** | The platform telling somebody what happened to them | Yes, where you enable it |
| **Campaigns** | A message you wrote, to a group | Yes |
| **Noticeboard** | An announcement on the board | **No — in-app only** |

That last row is the one that catches people out. A notice reaches nobody who does not
sign in.

## Do these three first

1. **Connect an email service and test it.** Nothing else here works without one, and
   discovering that during an intake costs you the intake.
2. **Turn on "Exclude Transactional from Limits".** Otherwise a marketing cap can block
   a verification email and lock a learner out.
3. **Write eight templates** — welcome, payment confirmation, payment reminder, course
   starting, assignment due, results published, certificate earned, access ending. That
   is most of your year's communication.
