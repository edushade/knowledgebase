This is where you talk to your learners and your team. You have two main ways to reach
people: a **notice board** inside your platform, and **notifications** that go out over
email, SMS, push, and chat apps. Together they cover everything from a quick class
update to a full marketing announcement.

## The two ways to reach people

| Tool | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Notice board** | Notices that appear inside your platform when a learner signs in. | Class updates, holiday notices, schedule changes — things people see when they log in. |
| **Notifications** | Personal messages delivered by email, SMS, push, and chat apps. | Welcome emails, reminders, receipts, and bulk announcements that reach people outside the app. |

The notice board lives only inside your platform. Anything that needs to land in an
inbox, a phone, or a chat app is handled by the notifications side.
