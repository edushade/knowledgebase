A notice is an announcement on your academy's noticeboard. Learners see it when they
sign in.

<Path>Engage > Noticeboard</Path>.

<Callout type="warn">
**Notices are in-app only.** Posting one does not email, text or push anything. A
learner who does not sign in never sees it. To actually reach people, send a
[campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns).
</Callout>

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Writing one

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What learners see in the list. |
| **Notice Body** | The notice itself. |
| **Thumbnail** | An image for it. |
| **Attachments** | Files learners can download — a timetable, a form. |
| **Priority** | How it is labelled. See below. |
| **Who sees this** | Everyone, or particular audiences. |
| **Pinned** | Keeps it at the top. |
| **Published At** | When it appears. |
| **Expires at** | When it drops off. |
| **Status** | Draft, Scheduled, Published, Expired or Archived. |

The list can be filtered by **Status**, by **Priority**, and by **Sent to learners in** —
which narrows it to the notices sent to one programme. On an academy posting weekly
notices to several batches, that last filter is the only way to find anything.

## Priority

Priority changes the label learners read:

| Priority | They see |
|---|---|
| **Urgent** | "Urgent" |
| **High** | "Important" |
| **Normal** | "Notice" |
| **Low** | "Update" |

Use Urgent rarely. A noticeboard where everything is urgent has no urgent notices.

## Who sees it

**Who sees this** targets the notice using the same
[audiences](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/audiences) campaigns use. A notice for one
batch goes to that batch; a general one goes to everybody.

Targeting matters more than it seems. A noticeboard full of notices that do not apply to
you is a noticeboard you stop reading.

## Expiry and pinning

**Expires at** takes a notice down on its own. Set it on anything with a date in it — an
exam notice about last Tuesday makes the whole board look unmaintained.

**Pinned** keeps something at the top. One pinned notice is a notice; four are a wall.

## What the learner sees

A **Notice Board** in their dashboard, newest first, with unread ones marked. They can
mark one read, or all. Attachments are downloadable, and each notice shows who posted
it.

## Noticeboard, notification or campaign?

| You want to… | Use |
|---|---|
| Put something on the board for people who sign in | **Notice** |
| Reach people wherever they are | [Campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns) |
| Tell someone something happened to them | A notification — the platform does this itself |

For anything that matters — a cancelled class, a changed deadline — do both: a notice
for the board and a campaign to reach them.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Does posting a notice email anyone?">
No. In-app only. Send a campaign as well.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I schedule a notice?">
Yes — set **Published At** in the future and it appears then.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are recurring notices available?">
Do not rely on the recurring option. Post each one when it is needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see who read a notice?">
Learners mark notices as read for themselves; there is no per-notice readership report.
If you need to know it was received, send a campaign and read the
[delivery logs](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/delivery-and-analytics).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can instructors post notices?">
If their role allows it. See
[People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
