**Exam** collects the assessments a learner is scheduled to sit.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## Three tabs

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming Exams** | Scheduled, not yet sat. |
| **Previous Exams** | Sat. |
| **Published Results** | Released results. |

## What a card shows

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Starts At** / **Ends At** | The window. |
| **Time Limit** | Minutes, or **No Limit**. |
| **Total Questions**, **Total Marks** | What they are facing. |
| **Submitted At**, **Time Spent** | After they sit it. |
| **Result**, **Score**, **Accuracy Rate** | Once released. |
| **Passed** / **Failed** | The outcome. |
| **Take Exam** | The button, when it is open. |
| **Merit List** | The ranking, where you allow it. |
| **Exam Review** | Looking back at what they answered. |

## The four states that generate questions

| What they see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Locked** | Not open yet. |
| **Closed** | The window has passed. |
| **Missed** | They did not sit it, with **Deadline was …**. |
| **Under Review** | Submitted, result not released. |

**Under Review** is the one you will be asked about most. It means one of two things:
written questions are waiting to be marked, or the scores are held until a release time.
From the learner's side both look identical.

## Taking it

Starting asks them to confirm — *"once started, the quiz must be completed"* — and the
clock begins there. Full detail:
[Taking a quiz](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/taking-quizzes).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says the exam is locked.">
It has not opened. Its window is on the quiz item's drip schedule — see
[Quiz availability](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-availability).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They missed it and want another chance.">
Give them a [reschedule](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/reschedules) — their own window,
without moving the deadline for everyone.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="It says Under Review and they want their mark.">
Either marking is outstanding or the release time has not come. Both are on your side.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is a quiz not in their Exam area?">
The Exam area collects scheduled assessments. A practice quiz inside a lesson is part of
the course, not an exam.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can they see the questions afterwards?">
Through **Exam Review**, where you have allowed answers to be shown. See
[Quiz settings](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-settings).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
