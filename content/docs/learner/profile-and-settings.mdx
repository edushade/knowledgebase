A learner's **Settings** holds everything about their own account.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## What is in there

| Section | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Account Edit** | Name, bio, date of birth, gender, location. |
| **Change Password** | Set a new one. |
| **Change Email** | With verification. |
| **Change Phone Number** | With verification. |
| **Two-factor authentication** | Turn it on, where offered. |
| **Devices** | Where they are signed in, and signing other devices out. |
| **Activity Logs** | What their account has done. |
| **Notifications** | What they receive, and on which channel. |
| **Your Data** | Their own data. |
| **Account Deletion** | Closing the account, where you allow it. |

## What you have locked

**Locked Profile Fields**, in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **User
Settings**, lists fields a learner cannot change.

<Callout type="warn">
**Lock the name if you issue certificates.** A certificate prints the name on the
account — a learner who can edit it can edit the name on their certificate the evening
before they download it.
</Callout>

Date of birth is worth locking too, wherever age rules apply.

## Profile completion

Where you require it, a learner is asked to fill in particular fields before going on.
Set in the same place, under **Profile Completion**.

Ask for what you will use. Every required field is a wall between somebody who has paid
and the course they paid for.

## Devices

The devices list is how a learner signs out a phone they no longer have, without
contacting you. It is also how you spot a shared account — see
[Security](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/security).

## Their data

**Your Data** gives learners their own record. What they can do there follows your
privacy settings — see
[Privacy and consent](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/privacy-and-consent).

## Resetting their own progress

Where you have enabled it, a learner can reset their progress on a course. It is **off
by default**, and worth leaving off for any programme that awards a certificate — a
reset cannot be undone.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner cannot change their name.">
It is a locked profile field. Change it for them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They changed their email and cannot sign in.">
The new address needs verifying. If they cannot receive it, verify it from their record.
See [Verification](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/verification).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They reset their progress by accident.">
It cannot be undone. Switch the learner-reset setting off if that is a risk.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They want to delete their account.">
**Account Deletion**, where you allow it. What happens to their data follows your
privacy settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They say they are getting too many emails.">
Their notification settings let them choose per channel. Marketing can be switched off;
transactional messages cannot.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
