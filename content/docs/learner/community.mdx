**Community**, in the learner's menu, is one screen for everything they say to your
academy and their classmates. **Support**, just below it, is where they ask your team
for help.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## Finding it

**Community** appears in the menu once the learner is in a programme with chat switched
on, with a count of unread messages. It also has a card on their dashboard.

Inside a programme there are shortcuts:

- **Chat** and **Q&A** buttons at the top of the course or learning path page, with
  their own unread counts.
- A **Chat** button in the lesson toolbar.
- **Questions on this lesson**, a card under each lesson, with **Ask about this
  lesson**.

## The screen

Down the left side, a narrow rail: **Direct messages** first, then one button for each
programme, showing the programme's picture — or its initials if it has none.

Picking a programme on a computer opens a channel straight away: the first one with
unread messages, or the last one they read. On a phone they see the list of channels
first and tap one.

A programme's channels are grouped under **Announcements**, **Discussions** and
**Q&A**:

| Channel | What a learner can do |
|---|---|
| **Announcements** | Read. *"Only our team posts here."* |
| **Discussions** | Talk with classmates and teachers. Reply, edit or delete their own messages, and report someone else's. |
| **Q&A** | Ask a question, reply, and mark the answer that helped. See [Questions and answers](/docs/knowledgebase/community/discussions). |

When they cannot post, the chat says why — the channel is locked, a moderator has paused
them for a while, or an instalment is unpaid, with **See your payments**.

### Programmes that separate men and women

The learner is asked once — **I'm a brother** or **I'm a sister** — and joins that side's
channels. Until they choose, they can read the announcements only. After that, only your
team can change it.

## Direct messages

**New message** lets a learner write to a teacher or a member of staff on one of their
programmes — and to a classmate, if your academy allows learners to message each
other.

## Their own settings

The gear at the bottom of the rail:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Email me a daily summary** | Once a day, if someone mentioned them or sent a message they have not read. |
| **Play a sound for new messages** | On to begin with, with **Play a test sound**. |
| **Show browser notifications** | Off until they turn it on. |
| **Hide my online status** | They appear offline, and nobody sees when they were last here. Others still see when they are typing. |

## Support

**Support** is in every learner's menu. **New request** sends your team a question with
a subject, what it is about, and files if needed. Replies arrive on the same page and by
email, and the menu shows a count of unread replies. See
[Support requests](/docs/knowledgebase/community/support-requests).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner cannot see Community.">
They are not in a programme with chat switched on yet. **Support** is always there.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner cannot message a classmate.">
Learners can message each other only where your academy allows it, and only within their
own side where men and women are separate. They can always message staff.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner leave a comment under a lesson?">
No. Questions about a lesson go in **Questions on this lesson**, which posts to the
programme's Q&A.
</Accordion>
