academy starts here.

## The menu

| Item | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | This screen. |
| **My Course** | Their programmes. |
| **Assignments** | Work to submit. |
| **Exam** | Scheduled assessments and results. |
| **Saved** | Lessons and bookmarks they kept. |
| **My Calendar** | Classes, deadlines and events. |
| **Events** | Events they can attend. |
| **Notice Board** | Your announcements. |
| **Notification** | Things that happened to them. |
| **Payment History** | Their orders. |

A language switcher sits at the foot of that menu, so a learner can read your academy in
their own language without asking anyone. See
[Languages](/docs/knowledgebase/website/languages).

## The figures

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Performance** | Their average score across all quizzes and assignments. |
| **Total Ongoing Courses** | How many are in progress, out of their total. |
| **Courses Completed** | How many they have finished. |
| **Time Spent** | Minutes of study. |
| **Live Class Participation** | Classes attended, out of the total. |
| **Quizzes Completed** | Out of the total. |
| **Assignments Completed** | Out of the total. |

Each carries a tooltip explaining itself, so a learner asking "what does Performance
mean?" has the answer on screen.

## The panels below

| Panel | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Continue learning** | Picks up exactly where they stopped — "you left off at…". The most-clicked thing on the screen. |
| **Upcoming Classes** | Live sessions coming up, with the joining details. |
| **Day Streak** | Consecutive days of study, and their best run. Only if [streaks](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/learning-streaks) are switched on. |
| **My Courses** | Every programme, with progress and score. |
| **Learning Stats** | Where their time went — recorded classes, live classes, quizzes. |
| **Leaderboard** | Where they stand. |
| **Pending Quizzes** | Quizzes not yet sat. |
| **Pending Assignments** | Work not yet handed in. |
| **Areas for Improvement** | The subjects they are scoring worst in. |

<Callout type="info">
**Areas for Improvement is the one worth pointing learners at.** Most people do not know
which subject is dragging their result down — this panel names it, and a learner who
knows what to fix usually fixes it.
</Callout>

## What a new learner sees

Not much — which is the problem worth solving. A learner who has enrolled and not
started sees zeros and an empty calendar.

Three things that help:

1. **Drip the first lesson open immediately.** A course where nothing is available on
   day one loses people on day one.
2. **Put something in the noticeboard** before the batch starts, so the board is not
   empty.
3. **Send a welcome** with a link straight into the first lesson. See
   [Campaigns](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says Performance looks wrong.">
It is the average across every quiz and assignment, including ones they have not sat if
the programme counts unmarked work as zero. See
[Course grading setup](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/course-grading-setup).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Time Spent looks far too low.">
Time is recorded while they are in the content. Someone who downloads and reads offline
registers almost nothing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Live Class Participation says zero.">
They have not joined a live session, or the sessions are not recorded as attended —
check how the class is being run.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change what the dashboard shows?">
**Learner Home** in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> decides what they land on.
The figures themselves are fixed.
</Accordion>
