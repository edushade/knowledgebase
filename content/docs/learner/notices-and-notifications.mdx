---
title: "Notices and Notifications"
description: "The two things in a learner's menu that look the same and are not."
---

A learner has **Notice Board** and **Notification** side by side in their menu. They
carry different things, and knowing which is which saves a conversation.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## The difference

| | **Notice Board** | **Notification** |
|---|---|---|
| What it holds | Announcements you posted | Things that happened to them |
| Who it is for | Everyone, or a group | One learner |
| Who wrote it | You | The platform |

"Class cancelled tomorrow" is a notice. "Your assignment was graded" is a notification.

## The notice board

Notices, newest first, with unread ones marked. Each shows its priority as a label —
**Urgent**, **Important**, **Notice** or **Update** — who posted it, and any files
attached.

They can **Mark as read**, or open **View All Notices**.

<Callout type="warn">
**Notices are in-app only.** A learner who does not sign in never sees one. For anything
that matters, post the notice *and* send a
[campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns).
</Callout>

## Notifications

Things that happened to them, newest first, with unread marked. When they are through:
*"You're all caught up!"*

Most carry a link — "your assignment was graded" goes to the grade rather than making
them look for it.

What arrives is what you have enabled on the in-app channel. See
[Notification settings](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/notification-preferences).

## Why the bell is the reliable one

The in-app channel cannot bounce, cannot go to spam, and costs nothing. If a learner
signs in, they see it.

What it cannot do is reach somebody who does not sign in — which is the whole case for
email and SMS alongside it.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says they never saw the announcement.">
If it was a notice, they did not sign in. Notices do not leave the platform.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Their notification list is empty.">
Nothing has happened to them yet, or those notification types are off on the in-app
channel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They are getting too many notifications.">
They can choose what they receive, per channel, in their own settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send a notice to one learner?">
Notices go to everyone or to an audience. For one person, a campaign to an audience of
one, or an email.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A notice about last week is still showing.">
It has no expiry date. Set **Expires at** on anything with a date in it.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
