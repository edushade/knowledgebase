**My Course** is the learner's own catalogue: the programmes they are enrolled in.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## How it is grouped

| Group | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Diploma Program** | Their learning paths. |
| **Courses** | Everything else they are enrolled in. |
| **More Courses for You** | Recommendations from your catalogue. |

Each group says so honestly when it is empty — "You don't have any courses enrolled
yet" — rather than showing nothing at all.

**More Courses for You** is quietly commercial: it is where a learner who finished one
course finds the next one.

## Opening a programme

A programme shows its description, its duration, and its outline. The learner works
through the outline, and their place is remembered.

What they can see of the outline is decided by every access rule you have set. A course
that looks half-empty to them usually is — see
[How access works together](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-access-works-together).

## What each item shows

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Open, with a tick | Finished. |
| Open, no tick | Available, not finished. |
| Locked, with a date | Held by a [drip schedule](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling). |
| Locked, with a requirement | Waiting on a [prerequisite](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/prerequisites). |
| Not there at all | Unpublished, outside their access, or excluded by a rule. |

The last row is the one that generates support messages, because the learner has nothing
to describe.

## When a course has no content

A learner opening a course with nothing published sees "No Curriculum Available". That
is the message somebody means when they say "the course is empty" — it is worth knowing
it is a real message and not their imagination.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says a course is missing.">
Check their enrolment exists and is active, then check their scopes — a learner with
[partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access) sees only what
they hold.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They say the course is empty.">
Either nothing is published, or everything is outside their access. Open it as them —
see [Support login](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/support-login).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is their diploma separate from their courses?">
Learning paths are grouped as diploma programmes because they are one programme rather
than a collection.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I control what appears under recommendations?">
It comes from your published catalogue. What you publish is what can be recommended.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
