**My Calendar** collects everything with a date on it for that learner.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## What appears

| Item | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Live classes** | Live lessons in their courses. |
| **Deadlines** | Lock dates on assignments and quizzes. |
| **Exams** | Scheduled assessments. |
| **Events** | Events they registered for. |
| **Bookings** | Meetings they booked. |
| **Holidays** | Your academy's calendar. |

It is per learner, so it shows what applies to them rather than everything happening in
your academy.

## What it depends on

The calendar can only show dates that exist. Two things to get right on your side:

1. **Set lock dates on assignments and quizzes.** A deadline that is only mentioned in
   the instructions is not a date, and does not appear here. See
   [Assignments](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/assignments).
2. **Give live lessons a real start time.** A live lesson without one cannot be shown.

<Callout type="info">
A learner whose calendar is empty does not conclude they have nothing to do — they
conclude the calendar does not work, and stop looking at it.
</Callout>

## Timezones

Times appear in the learner's own timezone, which is on their profile. Somebody studying
from another country sees their own clock — provided their timezone is set. See
[Profiles and devices](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/profiles-and-devices).

## Holidays

Holidays show, so learners know the academy is closed. They do not move anything — a
deadline on a holiday stays on the holiday until you move it. See
[Holidays](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/holidays).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner's calendar is empty.">
Nothing with a date applies to them. Check that your assignments have lock dates and
your live lessons have start times.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A deadline shows at the wrong time.">
Their timezone. It is on their profile, and a learner who never set it sees your
academy's.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can learners add their own entries?">
It shows what your academy has scheduled for them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can they export it to their own calendar?">
Bookings land in a connected calendar on your side — see
[Calendar and conferencing](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/integrations). The learner's
view is inside the platform.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
