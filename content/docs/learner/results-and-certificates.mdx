**My Certificates** — *"every certificate you've earned across your programs"* — is
where a learner's certificates live.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## What they see

The list can be searched **by certificate number or program**, and filtered by
programme and by type:

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Certificate of Enrollment** | Granted on enrolling. |
| **Certificate of Completion** | Earned by finishing. |

Each entry shows **Issued `{date}`** once it is theirs. One that is earned but not yet
released reads **Available on `{date}`** or **Available soon** — which is the honest
answer to "where is my certificate?" and it is already on their screen.

When there is nothing: *"Certificates you earn from your programs will appear here."*

## Their result

A learner's result for a programme appears where you have allowed it. **Enable
Transcript** on the course decides whether they see it at all — off, they see nothing
and you can still print theirs.

Four things can hold a result back, in order: the programme's transcript switch, a
release date, an unpublished term, and an individual result still being held. See
[Transcripts and results](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/transcripts).

## Why a certificate is not there

Six gates, and the learner can see none of them. Work down
[Certificate requirements](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-requirements) —
the usual answers for a learner who has passed are:

1. The release date has not arrived.
2. They hold part of the programme, not all of it.
3. Marking is outstanding somewhere, so the overall basis is still waiting.
4. A requirement is unmet — commonly a feedback form they have not submitted.

That fourth one is worth checking first, because it is the only one the learner can fix
themselves.

## What a learner can do with it

View it, and download it. There is no public page where somebody else can verify it —
see [Verifying certificates](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/verifying-certificates).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner passed and has no certificate.">
Go down the six gates. The release date and partial access are the two most common.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Their certificate has the wrong name.">
It prints the name on their account. Correct the account and reissue — then lock the
name field so it cannot change again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They cannot see their transcript.">
**Enable Transcript** is off for that programme. They see nothing; you still can.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They want their certificate verified by an employer.">
Give the employer the certificate number and confirm it yourself. There is no public
verification page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They have an enrolment certificate but wanted a completion one.">
Two different types with different rules. The enrolment one is granted on enrolling.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
