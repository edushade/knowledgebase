A lesson opens in place, with the course outline beside it. The learner works through,
and their position is remembered.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## What they get, by type

| Type | How it opens |
|---|---|
| **Video** | In the player, with their position remembered. |
| **Audio** | The same. |
| **Text** | A formatted reading. |
| **PDF** | In a reader, with full screen available. |
| **Live** | The scheduled time, and a way to join. |
| **Embed** / **iFrame** | The outside content, in place. |

What a learner can do in a video — skip ahead, change speed, use the keyboard — is set
by you. See [Video playback](/docs/knowledgebase/files/video-playback).

## Three things they can do from a lesson

### Save it

**Save** keeps the lesson in their [Saved](/docs/knowledgebase/learner/saved) list. In a
video, audio or PDF they can also bookmark the exact moment or page.

### Report a problem

Under every lesson there is **Report a problem**: a reason, and a sentence of their own.
It lands in your
[Reported Problems](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/reported-problems) queue.

### Ask about it

Where the programme has chat switched on, the lesson toolbar has a **Chat** button, and
under the lesson **Questions on this lesson** shows what others asked, with **Ask about
this lesson**. See [Community](/docs/knowledgebase/learner/community).

## When something counts as finished

A tick appears when the item's
[completion rule](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/completion-rules) is met: opened,
watched to a point, time spent, sat, or passed. That tick is what moves their progress.

## What they do not get

- **No downloading**, where **Restrict paid content download** is on for paid content.
- **No skipping ahead**, where you have locked seeking on the managed player.
- **No access to what comes next**, where a drip schedule or prerequisite holds it.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner watched a video and it is not ticked.">
Check the lesson's **Duration** against the real video. Progress is measured against
the figure you typed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They lost their place in a video.">
Position is remembered per learner. A different device, or a reset, starts again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A PDF will not open on their phone.">
A PDF **lesson** opens in the reader. A PDF **file item** downloads, which depends on
them having something to open it with. For a reading, use a PDF lesson.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They cannot skip to the part they need.">
Seeking is locked on the managed player. See
[Video playback](/docs/knowledgebase/files/video-playback) — it is worth asking whether
it is earning its cost.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner says the video will not play.">
If it is a YouTube video, their bot check is the usual cause and it is outside your
control. Ask them to report it, and see
[Reported problems](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/reported-problems).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
