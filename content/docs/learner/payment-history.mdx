**Payment History** is the learner's list of orders.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## What they see

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Order ID** | Their reference. |
| **Transaction ID** | The payment's reference. |
| **Item**, **Type** | What they bought. |
| **Total Items**, **Total Amount** | The order. |
| **Payment Method** | How they paid. |
| **Date** | When. |
| **Status** | Where it stands. |
| **View Details** | The full record. |

Where there is nothing: "No orders found".

## Requesting a refund

**Request Refund** on an order opens a form: the transaction amount, the **Refund
Type** — **Full Refund** or **Partial Refund** — and a reason.

Once sent, the row reads **Refund submitted**, then **Refunded** when it completes.
Where a refund is not available it says **No refund available** rather than offering a
button that does nothing.

The request arrives in your refunds queue, where you approve or decline it with a
reason, and decide separately what happens to their access. See
[Refunds](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/refunds).

## Instalments

A learner paying in instalments sees what is still owed and what is due next. Where a
payment is outstanding they may see **Advance Required** — which is also the reason
their content has closed, if they are past the grace period.

See [Installments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments).

## What they are told along the way

Refund notifications reach them at each step: the request received, the refund approved
with the amount, and the refund completed. Those are worth leaving on — a refund in
silence produces a support message every time.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner cannot find a payment they made.">
Payments taken outside the platform only appear if they were recorded. See
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They told you they paid by bKash, and it says waiting.">
A payment they reported at checkout waits here until someone checks it in **Pending
Verification**. See
[Letting learners tell you they paid](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/payment-methods#letting-learners-tell-you-they-paid).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They requested a refund and heard nothing.">
It is in your pending refunds. Every day it sits there is a day they assume the answer
is no.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Their access stopped and they say they paid.">
Check their instalment state. **Past grace — access paused** looks like an active
enrolment and opens nothing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can they get an invoice?">
**View Details** holds the record of the transaction.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a refund be requested for a free enrolment?">
There is nothing to refund, and the row says so.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
