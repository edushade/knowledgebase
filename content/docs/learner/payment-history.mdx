**Payment History**, in the learner's menu, is their list of orders. Each one opens a
page of its own with everything about that purchase.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## The list

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Transaction ID** | The order's reference. |
| **Date** | When. |
| **Item**, **Type** | What they bought. |
| **Amount** | What it cost. |
| **Payment Method** | How they paid. For money they sent you directly, the way they sent it — **bKash**, **Nagad**, **Rocket**, **Upay**, **Bank transfer** or **Cash at our office**. |
| **Status** | Where it stands. |
| **Actions** | Open the order, or ask for a refund. |

On a phone the same orders show as cards.

### What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Paid** | Done. |
| **Confirmed** | A free enrolment. Nothing was paid. |
| **Being checked** | They sent the money to you directly and told you. Your team has not confirmed it yet. |
| **Not accepted** | Your team could not confirm the payment they reported. The reason is shown on the row. |
| **Pending**, **Failed**, **Cancelled** | An online payment that has not finished, did not go through, or was stopped. |
| **Refund requested**, **Partly refunded**, **Refunded** | Where a refund stands. |

## The order page

Opening an order shows, from the top:

1. **What they bought** — its picture, its type and its title, linked to the course when
   they have access. Where the price they bought is no longer on sale, the page says so
   and adds that their access and any instalments carry on as before.
2. **One status block** — a single sentence saying where the order stands, and the one
   thing to do next, if there is one.
3. **Payment details** — **Amount**, **Payment method**, **Paid on**, and the **Order
   number** with a copy button. For a payment they sent you directly there is also
   **What you sent**: where it went, the number it came from, the transaction ID and the
   day.
4. Refunds and the instalment plan, where they apply.

Beside it sits the total, **Print receipt** once the payment has succeeded, and
**Questions about this order?** with **Message us**, which opens their
[Support](/docs/knowledgebase/learner/community#support) page.

## A payment they sent you directly

When a learner pays into your own bKash, bank account or office and tells you the
transaction ID, the order waits for your team. See
[Direct payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/direct-payments).

| What happened | The status block says | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting for your team | *"Thanks. We're checking your payment."* and how soon you usually check | **Fix it**, while nobody has checked it yet |
| Your team confirmed it | *"We've confirmed your payment"* — *"You have access now."* | Open the course |
| Your team could not confirm it | *"We couldn't accept the payment you reported"*, with your reason | **Send again** |

**Fix it** lets them correct the **Transaction ID**, the number they sent from, the day,
or the screenshot — the usual mistake is a mistyped transaction ID. The amount and the
account they paid into cannot be changed. Once your team has looked at the payment, it
can no longer be fixed.

**Send again** takes them back to the course page to report the payment again with the
right details. The page tells them they do not need to pay twice.

The reason they read is the one your team chose, in the learner's own words — for
example *"The amount we received doesn't match the price."* — followed by any note your
team wrote.

## Requesting a refund

**Request Refund** on an order opens a form: the transaction amount, the **Refund
Type** — **Full Refund** or **Partial Refund** — and a reason.

Once sent, the status reads **Refund requested**, then **Refunded** when it completes.
Where a refund is not available it says **No refund available** rather than offering a
button that does nothing.

The request arrives in your refunds queue, where you approve or decline it with a
reason, and decide separately what happens to their access. See
[Refunds](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/refunds).

## Instalments

A learner paying in instalments sees what is still owed and what is due next. Where a
payment is outstanding they may see **Advance Required** — which is also the reason
their content has closed, if they are past the grace period.

See [Installments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments).

## What they are told along the way

Refund notifications reach them at each step: the request received, the refund approved
with the amount, and the refund completed. A payment they reported is confirmed, or
turned down with its reason, by email and in their notifications. Those are worth
leaving on — a decision in silence produces a support message every time.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner cannot find a payment they made.">
Payments taken outside the platform only appear if they were recorded — by you, or by
the learner reporting it. See
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They sent the money by bKash, and it says Being checked.">
It is waiting in **Pending Verification** for your team. Check it against your bKash
statement and confirm it. See
[Checking a direct payment](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/check-a-direct-payment).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They typed the wrong transaction ID.">
They can correct it themselves with **Fix it** on the order page, as long as your team
has not checked it yet.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="They requested a refund and heard nothing.">
It is in your pending refunds. Every day it sits there is a day they assume the answer
is no.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Their access stopped and they say they paid.">
Check their instalment state. **Past grace — access paused** looks like an active
enrolment and opens nothing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can they get a receipt?">
Yes, once the payment has succeeded — **Print receipt** on the order page prints it with
your academy's name. A payment still being checked has no receipt yet.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
