sees, in the order they meet it.

It is written for you, not for them. When somebody says "I cannot find my certificate",
this is where you check what they are looking at.

## What learners cannot do

Worth knowing before you promise any of it:

| Not available |
|---|
| Message another learner — there is no chat or direct messaging |
| Buy two things in one checkout |
| Pay for somebody else |
| Download their own transcript, unless you enable it per programme |
| Verify a certificate through a public page |

## The fastest way to answer a learner's question

See it as they do. [Support login](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/support-login)
shows you their account, read-only, with every access rule applied. It settles in
seconds what a screenshot argues about for a day.
