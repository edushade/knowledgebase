**Saved** is a learner's own shortlist: *"lessons you saved and moments you bookmarked,
to come back to."*

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## Two different things

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Saved items** | A whole lesson, quiz, assignment or file, kept for later. |
| **Bookmarks** | A specific moment in a video or audio, or a page in a PDF. |

Both live under **Saved**, with tabs for **All**, **Saved items** and **Bookmarks**.

## Saving something

**Save** on a lesson, quiz, assignment or file keeps it.

Inside a video or audio, **Bookmark this moment** marks the exact second. In a PDF,
**Bookmark this page** marks the page. Going back later jumps straight there.

## Why it is worth telling learners about

Bookmarking a moment is the single most useful study feature in the platform, and almost
nobody discovers it on their own.

A learner revising for an exam who can jump straight to the four minutes that mattered
in each of eight videos is in a different position from one scrubbing through four
hours.

Say so in your course instructions, and the learners who use it will tell you it was
the best thing you told them.

## What they see when it is empty

*"Nothing saved yet. Use Save on a lesson, quiz, assignment or file to keep it here. In
a video, audio or PDF, add a bookmark to come back to that exact moment or page."*

Which is the explanation — it just arrives after they have already not used it.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Does saving affect progress?">
No. It is a personal shortlist and changes nothing about completion or grades.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner bookmark a text lesson?">
Bookmarks mark a moment or a page — video, audio and PDF. A text lesson can be saved as
a whole.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do bookmarks survive a reset?">
A reset clears progress. Anything that depends on their place in the content may not
survive it — see
[Resetting progress](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/resetting-progress).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see what learners save?">
No. It is their own list.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
