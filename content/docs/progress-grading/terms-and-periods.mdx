description: "How a term's result is worked out, what belongs to it, and the one mistake that produces a strange semester mark."
---

When a batch is divided into [terms](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints),
each term produces a result of its own beside the overall one. A learner's record then
reads "Semester 1: 78%, Semester 2: 81%, Overall: 79%".

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## What belongs to a term

**A term's result counts only what sits inside it.** Its subjects, its assessments, its
weights. Nothing else.

That gives three cases worth knowing:

| Where the content sits | Counts towards the term? | Counts towards the overall result? |
|---|---|---|
| Inside the term | Yes | Yes |
| Outside every term | No | Yes |
| Inside a *different* term | No — it counts there | Yes |

<Callout type="warn">
**Content outside every term is the usual cause of a strange semester result.** A quiz
sitting above the first breakpoint, or in the gap between two of them, shows on the
overall result and on no semester. Keep anything that should be marked inside the term
it belongs to.
</Callout>

## A subject in two terms

If the same course sits under Semester 1 and Semester 2, each semester counts only the
part under it. The learner is not marked twice, but neither semester sees the whole
subject, and both results look wrong.

Put a subject in one term. If it genuinely spans two, split it into two subjects.

## The overall result is not an average of the terms

Semester results do not add up to the final result, and they are not meant to. The final
result is calculated from the programme's subjects and their weights across the whole
thing. A term with two subjects and a term with eight do not count equally.

## Releasing a term's result

Each term carries its own **Transcript available from** date. Leave it empty and the
term's own end date is used. That is what lets Semester 1 results go out in April while
Semester 2 is still running.

A term also has a status: an unpublished term keeps its results back entirely.

## Terms and progression

At the end of a term you decide, per learner, whether they go up or repeat. The rules
read the term's result and the pass line from the grading scheme. See
[Progression](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/progression).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A semester shows no result but the learner has marks.">
Either the content they were marked on is not inside that term, or the term's results
are being withheld — by its date, by being unpublished, or by an assessment whose
result has not been released.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A semester reads Pending with a subject count beside it.">
That is deliberate. The count tells the learner how many subjects the semester holds
while its results are still held back — more honest than a blank or a zero.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I moved a quiz into a term. Do old marks follow it?">
Yes — marks already recorded against that quiz are counted in the term once the quiz is
inside it. Run **Calculate All Grades** so the semester result is rebuilt.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a self-paced course have terms?">
No. Terms are dated periods inside a batch, and a self-paced course has no dates to
divide.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
