title: "The Assessment Report"
description: "Who has done what — completion across every quiz and assignment in a programme, per learner and over time."

The assessment report answers a different question from a transcript. A transcript says
how well learners did; this says **who has done what**. It lives on the **Assessment
Report** tab of a course or learning path.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The three views

### Completion Report

Learner completion across the assessments you have filtered to, summarised four ways:

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Total Learners** | Everyone in scope. |
| **Completed all** | Learners who have finished every filtered assessment. |
| **Completed some** | Learners part-way through. |
| **Completed none** | Learners who have not started any of them. |

This is the view that tells you whether a deadline is going to be met.

### Learner Completed Assessments

How many assessments each learner has completed, as a list. Use it to find the
individuals behind "completed some" — and to spot the learner who has quietly stopped.

### Completion Over Time

**Completed / In Progress / Not Started** as of each date. A line that flattens tells
you the week a cohort stalled, which is usually more useful than the final figure.

## Filtering

Narrow the report before reading it:

- **All quizzes** or one quiz.
- **All quiz types** or one type — see [Quiz types](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-settings).
- **All assignments** or one.

A programme with no quiz types shows that honestly rather than offering an empty filter.

## The per-learner table

The report also gives a row per learner with their scores, their completion and a
**Grand Total**, alongside **Name**, **Phone** and **Gender** where you hold them. It
is the view to export when someone asks for "the list".

## Completion is not a result

A learner can appear as **Completed all** and still have no grade. Completion means the
work was done; a result needs the work to have been **marked**. For a programme with
unmarked assignments sitting in the queue, the two views will disagree, and both are
right.

See [Grading quizzes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/grading-quizzes) for the marking
queue, and [Transcripts](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/transcripts) for results.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner is Not Started but they told me they sat the quiz.">
Check whether their attempt was submitted. An attempt left open is not completed. Check
too whether the quiz counts as done only on a pass — a learner who sat it and failed
reads as not completed under that rule. See
[Completion rules](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/completion-rules).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The totals do not match the learner list.">
The report counts learners in scope for the filtered assessments. Someone with
[partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access) who does not have
that content is not counted against it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see this for one batch only?">
Yes — open the report from the batch rather than from the course, or filter to it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is the same thing for lessons rather than assessments?">
That is progress, not assessment completion. See
[Progress tracking](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/progress-tracking) and the
programme's **Progress** tab.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
