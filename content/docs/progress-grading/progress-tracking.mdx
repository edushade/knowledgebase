title: "Progress Tracking"
description: "How a learner's percentage is worked out, where to see it, and why it sometimes moves when nobody did anything."
---

Progress is the share of a programme's content a learner has finished. It is a count of
ticks, not of marks — a learner can be 100% through a course and have no result yet,
because progress asks "did they do it?" and grading asks "how well?".

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## How the percentage is worked out

```
progress = items finished ÷ published items that count
```

Three rules decide what is in that denominator:

1. **Only published items count.** An unpublished lesson is not in the course as far as
   progress is concerned.
2. **What "finished" means comes from the item's completion rule.** Opened, watched to a
   point, sat, or passed. See
   [Completion rules](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/completion-rules).
3. **Attachments do not count.** A file hanging off a lesson is an extra; a file item in
   the outline is part of the course.

## Where to see it

| Screen | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| A programme's **Progress** tab | Every learner's progress through the course structure, item by item. |
| A programme's **Statistics** tab | The shape of it — average progress, completion rate, how learners are distributed. |
| A learner's **Overview** | One person: overall progress, lessons completed, assessments completed, time spent, active days. |
| <Path>Analytics > Progress</Path> | The same question across your whole academy. See [Analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/progress). |

The figures you will meet most often:

| Figure | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Overall Progress** | The learner's percentage through the programme. |
| **Average Progress** | The mean across everyone enrolled. |
| **Completion Rate** | The share of learners who have finished. |
| **Lessons Completed** / **Assessments Completed** | Counts rather than percentages. |
| **Est. Time Remaining** | What is left, from the durations you set on lessons. |
| **Total Active Days** / **Total Time Spent** | How much the learner has actually turned up. |

## Why progress moves on its own

Progress is a share, so changing the denominator moves everyone:

- **Unpublishing a finished item** — everyone who had finished it loses it from both
  sides of the sum, and percentages change.
- **Adding new content** — everyone's percentage drops, because there is more to do.
- **Changing a completion rule** — tightening a rule can un-finish an item people had
  already ticked.

None of these is a fault. But they explain the "my progress went down" message you will
get the day after you edit a live course.

## Partial access

A learner who bought one semester is measured against that semester, not the whole
programme. Content they do not have is left out of both sides. See
[Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access).

## Resetting and completing

From a learner's row you can:

- **Reset Progress** — clear their ticks for one item, or for the whole enrolment. It
  cannot be undone.
- **Complete Progress** — mark everything done and set the enrolment to completed.

Both are blunt instruments; see
[Resetting progress](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/resetting-progress) before
using them on someone with marks.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner is at 100% but has no certificate.">
Progress and certificates ask different questions. A certificate usually needs a
**pass**, not merely completion. See
[Certificate requirements](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-requirements).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner finished a video but it is not ticked.">
Check the lesson's **Duration** — progress is measured against the figure you typed, so
one that is longer than the real video can never be reached. See
[Lesson types](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/lesson-types).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The same lesson is in two courses. Does finishing it once count for both?">
Yes, where it is genuinely the same content — finishing it is recorded against each
enrolment that contains it. Marks, though, stay with the enrolment they were earned in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Progress sits at 99% and will not finish.">
Something small is unticked — usually a file item nobody opens, or a lesson with a
percentage rule set too high. Open the learner's **Progress** view for that programme
and look for the one row without a tick.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does time spent affect progress?">
Only where an item's completion rule is **Spend minimum time**. Otherwise time spent is
reported but changes nothing.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
