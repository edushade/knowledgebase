---
title: "Transcripts and Results"
description: "The document that reports a learner's result — what is on it, who may see it, and the four things that keep one hidden."
---

A transcript is the formal record of what a learner achieved: their subjects, their
marks, their semester results and their final result. It is produced from the marks
already recorded — there is nothing separate to fill in.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## What is on one

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **The learner and the programme** | Name, programme, and the batch with its dates. |
| **Subjects** | One row per course or subject, with its weight, marks and result. |
| **Assessments** | The quizzes and assignments inside each subject, with their marks. |
| **Semesters** | A result per term, with its GPA and how many subjects it holds. |
| **Overall** | The final percentage, the grade, and the CGPA. |
| **Progress** | How many assessments have been completed out of the total. |

If the programme has no grading scheme, you get an honest marks sheet instead — the
marks, with no invented GPA. See
[Grading schemes](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/grading-schemes).

## Getting one

From a programme's **Learner Grades** screen, open a learner and view or print their
transcript. Learners reach their own from their dashboard, when you have allowed it.

## The four gates

A transcript can be empty or hidden for four separate reasons, and they apply in this
order. Work down the list:

1. **Transcript is switched off for the programme.** **Enable Transcript** on the
   course's settings step. Off means learners see nothing — you can still view and print
   theirs. See [Course settings](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/course-settings).
2. **A release date has not arrived.** A programme can hold results until a date
   (**Transcript available from**), and so can each
   [term](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints).
3. **A term is not published.** An unpublished term keeps its results back.
4. **An assessment's result is being held.** A quiz whose result has not been released
   is left out, and the semester containing it reads as pending.

<Callout type="info">
A withheld semester still shows **how many subjects it contains**. "Semester 1 · 8 ·
Pending" tells a learner the truth; a blank or a zero would not.
</Callout>

## What a learner sees

With **Enable Transcript** on, a learner finds their result in their dashboard and can
print it. With it off, they see nothing at all — not a locked panel, nothing.

A learner with [partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access)
gets a transcript for what they bought, marked out of what they took.

## The look of the document

How a transcript is laid out — which columns, which headings, which logo — is a design
you control, and programmes pick one. See
[Transcript designs](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/transcript-designs).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says their transcript is blank.">
Go down the four gates above in order. The most common answer is the first: **Enable
Transcript** is off for that programme, so learners see nothing while you can see
everything.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The transcript shows a subject with no marks.">
Nobody has marked that subject's assessments yet. Whether that pulls the result down
depends on **Treat Ungraded Items as Zero** — see
[Course grading setup](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/course-grading-setup).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A quiz the learner sat is missing from the transcript.">
Either its result is being held, or it is a non-primary member of an alternative group
and the transcript is printing the member they actually took. See
[Alternative assessments](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/alternative-assessments).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can learners download their own transcript?">
Yes, when **Enable Transcript** is on for the programme and any release date has passed.
Otherwise it is an admin document.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The numbers do not match the assessment report.">
They measure different things. A transcript reports **results** — marks, weighted into a
grade. The [assessment report](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/assessment-report)
reports **completion** — who has done what. A learner can have completed everything and
still have no result, if nothing has been marked.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
