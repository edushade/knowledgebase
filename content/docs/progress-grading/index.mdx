This section covers everything that happens *after* a learner opens a lesson: how their
progress is tracked, when an activity counts as finished, and how their marks become a
grade or GPA.

You decide the rules. Your platform does the counting — automatically, in the background —
so learners always see an up-to-date progress bar and you always have an accurate grade
report when you need one.

## What you can set up here

- **Track progress** for every lesson, quiz, and assignment, and roll it up into a single
  course progress bar.
- **Decide what "done" means** for each kind of content (watched to the end? scrolled to
  the bottom? passed the quiz?).
- **Organize a long program into terms or periods** (like "Semester 1" or "Week 3") and
  get a grade for each one.
- **Choose how grades are calculated** — letter grades, GPAs, and how marks are weighted.
- **Generate a marksheet** (grade report) for any learner.
- **Motivate learners** with streaks and a learning-time summary.
