title: "Retakes and Replacement"
description: "When a learner sits the same assessment twice, which attempt counts — and what each of the three policies really does."
---

Letting a learner retake a quiz raises a question the system has to answer the same way
every time: which attempt is their mark? That answer is one setting on the programme's
**Grading** tab.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Turning it on

**Allow Grade Replacement** off means the latest attempt simply stands — the newest mark
is the mark.

On, you must choose a **Grade Replacement Policy**:

| Policy | Which attempt counts |
|---|---|
| **Replace (Use new grade only)** | The most recent attempt. |
| **Average (Average both attempts)** | The average of the attempts that have been marked. |
| **Best (Use higher grade)** | The highest attempt. |

## What each one is for

**Replace** suits a course where a retake is a second chance and the second attempt is
the real one. It is also what you get with grade replacement switched off.

**Average** discourages guessing. A learner who sits a quiz twice carries both results,
so a careless first attempt costs them. Note that only attempts which have actually
been **marked** are averaged — one still waiting for marking is left out, so an
unmarked attempt does not drag the average down to zero.

**Best** suits practice. A learner can sit a quiz as often as they like and keep their
highest score. Use it where the point is mastery rather than ranking.

<Callout type="info">
**Best, when an attempt is deleted.** If you delete the attempt that was a learner's
best, their next-best attempt takes its place automatically — their progress and grade
are both reworked. You do not have to recalculate by hand.
</Callout>

## How many retakes

The policy decides which attempt counts; **how many attempts are allowed** is a setting
on the quiz itself. The two are separate, and both have to line up:

- A quiz that allows one attempt will never produce a second, whatever the policy says.
- A quiz that allows unlimited attempts with **Replace** means the last try is the mark,
  however many times they go round.

See [Quiz settings](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-settings) and
[Attempts and retakes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/attempts-and-retakes).

## Where the policy is read from

The policy comes from the programme the assessment belongs to:

- A quiz sitting directly in the programme follows that programme's setup.
- A quiz inside a course that is itself inside a learning path follows **that course's**
  setup, if the course has one of its own.

This is why the same quiz can be marked differently in two programmes — and that is
intended. The quiz is shared; the rules it is marked by belong to the programme.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner retook a quiz and their old, lower mark is still showing.">
Check the policy. Under **Best**, a lower second attempt changes nothing — the higher
one stands, which is what Best means. Under **Replace**, the new one should be showing;
if it is not, the attempt may not have been marked yet.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner's mark briefly showed 0% after they submitted.">
Under **Replace**, the most recent attempt is the mark — and an attempt that has been
submitted but not yet marked has nothing in it. Once it is marked, the real figure
appears.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I changed the policy. Why have marks not moved?">
Results are stored as they are calculated. Run **Calculate All Grades** on the
programme's learner grades screen. See
[Course grading setup](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/course-grading-setup).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can one quiz in a course use Best and another use Replace?">
No. The policy belongs to the programme, not to the quiz. To mark two assessments by
different rules, they need to sit in different programmes — a nested course with its own
grading setup, for instance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner needs one more attempt but the quiz is closed.">
Give them a reschedule rather than reopening the quiz for everybody. See
[Reschedules](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/reschedules).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
