title: "Learning Streaks & Time"
description: "Streaks, an activity calendar, and learning-time stats that motivate learners to keep showing up."
---

Learning streaks and time tracking are a **motivation feature**. They reward learners for
showing up regularly and give them a simple picture of how much they have been studying.
There is nothing to configure — it works automatically as learners use your platform.

## What the learner sees

- **Current streak** — how many days in a row they have been active.
- **Best streak** — their longest run of consecutive active days, as a target to beat.
- **A 7-day activity calendar** — a quick at-a-glance view of which of the last seven days
  they studied.
- **Learning time** — how long they have spent studying, broken down by day, week, and
  month, and by the type of activity (for example, time spent on videos versus quizzes).

## Why it helps

- Streaks turn studying into a habit — learners do not want to "break the chain".
- The activity calendar makes it easy to see when they have slipped, and to get back on track.
- Time stats give learners a real sense of the effort they are putting in.

## Good to know

- A streak counts **active days** — days on which the learner did something on your platform.
- This is purely about encouragement; it does not affect grades or
  [completion](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/completion-rules).
- It pairs well with [certificates](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates) and progress bars as
  ways to keep learners engaged.
