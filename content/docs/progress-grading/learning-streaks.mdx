---
title: "Learning Streaks"
description: "A daily streak on the learner's dashboard — what it counts, and the one switch that turns it on or off."
---

A streak counts the days in a row a learner has done something. It appears on their
dashboard, and for some learners it is the only thing that gets them back tomorrow.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Turning it on

One switch, in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Gamification > Daily
Streak**: **Daily streak enabled**. It is on by default.

Switch it off and the streak disappears from learner dashboards. Switch it back on and
counting resumes — streaks run until you stop them.

## What a learner sees

Their current streak on the dashboard, with the days of the week marked for the days
they turned up. It is encouragement, not a measurement: a streak changes nothing about
progress, grades, certificates or access.

## Good to know

- **A streak is about turning up**, not about finishing anything. Opening a lesson
  counts; completing it is a separate idea.
- **A streak is not reportable.** There is no streak column in analytics or exports. If
  you want to know who is actually engaged, use
  [Progress analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/progress) and the **Total Active
  Days** figure on a learner's overview.
- **Streaks are academy-wide.** There is no per-course streak.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I reward a streak?">
Not automatically. There is no rule that issues anything at a streak length. You can
announce it and award something yourself — see
[Announcements](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/announcements).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner says their streak reset unfairly.">
A streak counts days of activity, and a day with no activity ends it. Check **Total
Active Days** and the learner's recent activity on their overview to see what the
system recorded.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a leaderboard for streaks?">
No. Leaderboards rank points from quizzes and assignments. See
[Leaderboards](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/leaderboards).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
