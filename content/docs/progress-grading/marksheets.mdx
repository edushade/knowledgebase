A **marksheet** (also called a transcript or grade report) is a full breakdown of one
learner's grades in one course. It is the document you hand over when someone needs proof
of how a learner performed.

<Callout type="warn">
A marksheet is an **admin tool**. You generate it for a learner. Learners do not download
their own transcript.
</Callout>

## What a marksheet shows

For one learner in one course (and, for a cohort program, optionally one cohort), the
report includes:

- **Every assessment** the learner attempted.
- **Marks obtained vs. total marks** for each assessment.
- The **percentage** for each assessment.
- The **letter grade and grade point** for each assessment.
- **Grades for each course** inside a larger program.
- An **overall final grade and GPA**.
- A **GPA for each term** in cohort programs (see
  [Terms & periods](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/terms-and-periods)).

## How the numbers are worked out

The marksheet follows the rules you already set up:

- It uses each assessment's **best score**.
- It applies your configured [weights](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/weighted-grading).
- It maps percentages to grades using your
  [grading scheme](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/grading-schemes).
- For a [makeup or alternative assessment](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/makeup-assessments),
  it shows **only the version the learner actually took**.

## Generating a marksheet

1. Pick the **learner** and the **course** you want a report for.
2. For a cohort program, choose the **cohort** if needed.
3. **Generate** the marksheet.

The report is built fresh from the learner's current grades, so it always reflects their
latest results.

## Good to know

- Because the marksheet uses best scores and your weights, it matches the final grade the
  learner sees in the course.
- For proof of *completion* rather than *grades*, use a
  [certificate](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates) instead.
- Grades come from [Assessments & Quizzes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments); the marksheet
  simply reports them.
