A marksheet is the simple form of a result: one line per subject, with what the learner
scored. It is the right document when your academy reports percentages rather than
grade points.

The fuller form — one line per assessment, with weights, grade points and a cumulative
result — is a [transcript](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/transcripts).

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## What a marksheet can show

How a marksheet reports results is set on its design, and there are three choices:

| Choice | Each subject row shows |
|---|---|
| **Percentage, each subject out of 100** | One percentage per subject. |
| **Marks, as each subject was marked** | The raw marks — 68, 45 — as given. |
| **Marks, with a percentage column** | Both, side by side. |

When the marksheet shows marks, you can also switch on **Show the Total Marks column**,
which prints what each subject was marked *out of* beside the marks obtained. Without
it, a 68 does not say whether it was out of 70 or out of 100.

The subject table and the semester summary both follow the same choice, so a marksheet
never reports one thing at the top and another at the bottom.

## Reading the totals

Each level is worked out from the rounded figure below it. That means the subject column
really does add up to the result printed beside it — the number shown is the number
used. If they disagree, the result was recorded under older settings and has not been
recalculated. See
[Rounding and precision](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/rounding-and-precision).

A **semester** line counts only the subjects that sit inside that term. A subject
sitting outside every term reaches the overall result and no semester line.

## When a row is empty

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| A subject with no marks | Nothing in it has been marked yet. |
| A semester reading "Pending" | Its results are held — by a date, by being unpublished, or by an assessment whose result has not been released. |
| A result with no grade letter | The programme has no grading scheme, or the result falls in a gap between two bands. |
| A blank marksheet for a learner | Usually **Enable Transcript** is off for the programme. |

## Where it comes from

Open a programme's **Learner Grades**, pick a learner, and view or print. The layout
comes from the design the programme uses — see
[Transcript designs](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/transcript-designs).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The column headings are wrong for our academy.">
They are the design's own text, not fixed labels. Change them in the design builder —
see [Transcript designs](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/transcript-designs).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I print marksheets for a whole batch at once?">
Open the batch's learner list and print from there, learner by learner. There is no
single button that produces the batch as one file.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A subject shows marks but the semester total ignores it.">
That subject is not inside the term. Only content under a breakpoint counts towards that
breakpoint. See [Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I use a marksheet or a transcript?">
A marksheet if you report percentages and one line per subject is enough. A transcript
if you report GPA, or if learners need to see each assessment. The kind is fixed when a
design is created — the two share no parts, so one cannot be switched into the other.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
