A **grading scheme** is the scale that turns a percentage into a letter grade and a grade
point. For example: 90–100% is an **A**, worth **4.0**. You set this up once and reuse it
across your courses.

## Building a grading scheme

A scheme is a list of bands. For each band you set:

- The **percentage range** (for example, 90–100%).
- The **letter or label** (for example, A, B+, Pass).
- The **grade point** (for example, 4.0) — the scheme is GPA-style.
- A **passing threshold**, so the system knows which grades count as a pass.

A simple example:

| Percentage | Grade | Grade point |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| 90–100% | A | 4.0 |
| 80–89% | B | 3.5 |
| 70–79% | C | 3.0 |
| 60–69% | D | 2.0 |
| Below 60% | F | 0.0 |

## Managing your schemes

You can:

- **Create** a new scheme.
- **Edit** an existing one.
- **Set a default** scheme that new courses use automatically.
- **Delete** a scheme you no longer need.

## How each course calculates grades

Separately from the scale itself, each course has its own **grading setup**. Here you
choose how marks are combined into a final grade:

| Setting | Choices | What it controls |
|---------|---------|------------------|
| **Calculation method** | Weighted · Simple average · Credit-based | How the individual marks are combined. |
| **Rounding & decimals** | Your choice | How precisely grades are shown (for example, one decimal place). |
| **Retake policy** | Best score · Average · Replace | Which mark counts when a learner retakes an assessment. |
| **Ungraded items** | Treat as zero (optional) | Whether work the learner never submitted counts as a zero. |

A quick guide to the calculation methods:

- **Weighted** — each assessment carries a weight you choose. See
  [Weighted grading](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/weighted-grading).
- **Simple average** — every assessment counts equally.
- **Credit-based** — grades are combined by credit value.

A quick guide to the retake policies:

- **Best score** — keep the learner's highest attempt.
- **Average** — average their attempts together.
- **Replace** — the latest attempt replaces the previous one.

<Callout type="info">
If you turn on **treat ungraded items as zero**, a learner who skips an assessment gets a
zero for it instead of having it ignored. Leave it off if you only want to grade what was
actually submitted.
</Callout>

## Good to know

- The scheme decides the *scale*; the course's grading setup decides the *maths*. You can
  reuse one scheme across many courses with different setups.
- When a learner submits a quiz, their grade updates automatically and the final grade
  recalculates — no manual recalculation needed.
- Grades are reported on a [marksheet](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/marksheets).
