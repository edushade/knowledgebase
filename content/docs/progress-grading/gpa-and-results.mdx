A result is not one calculation. It is four, stacked, each working from the one below.
Knowing the order is what lets you answer "why is this number what it is?" without
guessing.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The four levels

| Level | What it produces |
|---|---|
| **1. An answer** | What one question in a quiz scored. |
| **2. An assessment** | What the whole quiz or assignment scored. |
| **3. A subject** | What a course or batch scored, from its assessments and their weights. |
| **4. The programme** | The final result and CGPA, from its subjects and their weights. |

Each level is rounded before the level above uses it. That is why a marksheet's columns
add up to the percentage printed beside them — the number shown is the number used. See
[Rounding and precision](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/rounding-and-precision).

## Where GPA comes in

If the programme's grading scheme is a **GPA** scale, each band carries a grade point as
well as a letter. A result of 86% lands in the B band, and B is worth 3.5.

- A **subject** gets a grade point.
- A **semester** gets a **GPA** — the result for that term, converted through the scheme.
- The programme gets a **CGPA** — the result across everything.

If the scheme is a **Percentage** scale, the same calculations happen but no grade
points are shown. The points are still stored; Percentage just decides not to print
them. See [Grading schemes](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/grading-schemes).

## Semester results

When a batch is divided into [terms](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints),
each term produces its own result alongside the overall one.

**A semester's result counts only what sits inside that semester.** Its weights, its
subjects, its assessments. Content that sits outside every term belongs to the
programme but to no semester, so it reaches the final result and no semester result.

<Callout type="warn">
**A subject shared between two semesters is the usual cause of a strange semester
result.** If the same course sits under Semester 1 and Semester 2, each semester counts
only the part under it. Put a subject in one term, or split it.
</Callout>

## What stops a result appearing

A learner can have marks and still see nothing. Four gates, in this order:

1. **The programme has no grading scheme.** Then there is no scale to produce a grade,
   and what the learner gets is a marks sheet rather than a result — no invented GPA.
2. **Transcript is switched off** for the programme. Learners see no result at all;
   you can still view and print theirs. See
   [Course settings](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/course-settings).
3. **A release date has not arrived.** A programme, and each term, can hold results
   until a date.
4. **A quiz result is being held.** An assessment whose result has not been released is
   left out, and the semester holding it reads as pending — while still showing how many
   subjects it contains, so "Semester 1 · 8 · Pending" rather than a misleading zero.

## Nothing to grade yet

A learner with no marked work has no result — not a zero. "Ungraded" and "scored zero"
are different things, and the system keeps them apart. A zero is a mark somebody gave;
ungraded means nobody has marked anything yet.

Which of the two an unmarked assessment becomes is decided by **Treat Ungraded Items as
Zero**. See
[Course grading setup](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/course-grading-setup).

## Learners who bought part of a programme

A learner with [partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access) —
one semester rather than the whole diploma — is marked on what they bought. Content
they do not have is left out of both the top and the bottom of the calculation, so
their result is out of what they actually took.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The semester results do not add up to the final result.">
They are not meant to be added. The final result is calculated from the subjects and
their weights across the whole programme, not by averaging the semesters. A semester
with two subjects and one with eight do not count equally.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A subject shows a result but contributes nothing to the semester.">
It is probably not inside that term. Only content under a breakpoint counts towards that
breakpoint's result. See [Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner has a CGPA but one semester is blank.">
That semester is being withheld — either its release date has not arrived, or an
assessment inside it is holding its result. The subject count still shows, so you can
tell "pending" from "empty".
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Two learners with the same marks have different results.">
Check whether one of them has partial access, or a transfer behind them. A learner
marked on one semester is marked out of that semester. See
[Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access) and
[Transfers](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/transfers).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I see all of this for one learner?">
The programme's **Learner Grades** screen, and from there one learner's breakdown — the
subjects, the assessments inside them, and each semester. See
[Learner grades](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/learner-grades).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
