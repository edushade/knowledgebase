title: "Certificate Requirements"
description: "Why a learner has not been given a certificate — six gates, in the order the system checks them."
---

"My learner finished the course and there is no certificate" is the most common question
in this section. There are six gates, and the answer is always one of them.

Work down the list in order. Each one stops the process on its own.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The six gates

### 1. Is the certificate switched on?

**Enable Certificate** on the course's settings step, and a
[certificate type](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-types) assigned with at
least one requirement on it.

If either is missing, nothing is issued automatically — but you can still
[issue one by hand](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/issuing-certificates).

### 2. Do they already have one?

A learner who already holds a completion certificate for the programme is not given a
second.

### 3. Has the release date arrived?

**Certificate available from** on the course, or on the batch. With neither set, the
batch's end date is used; with no date at all, it is available immediately.

So a learner can have passed everything in March and still be waiting, because the batch
ends in June.

### 4. Do they hold the whole programme?

**A learner with [partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access)
never gets a completion certificate.** Somebody who bought two semesters of a four
semester diploma has not finished the diploma, whatever their marks say.

They must hold every part of the programme. This gate is deliberately strict: if the
system cannot establish full access, it refuses.

### 5. Are the requirements met?

The type's requirements, combined by its **Requirement mode**:

| Requirement | Met when |
|---|---|
| **On passing grade** | They pass, per the passing basis below. |
| **On enrollment completion** | Their enrolment is complete. |
| **On feedback form submission** | They have submitted the programme's feedback form. |
| **On approved review** | They have left a review that was approved. |

**ANY** means one is enough. **ALL** means every one you ticked.

<Callout type="info">
If a programme has reviews switched off, the **approved review** requirement is dropped
rather than counted as permanently unmet — otherwise an ALL-mode certificate would be
impossible to earn.
</Callout>

### 6. Does the grade actually pass?

Where a passing grade is required, the basis decides what passes:

| Basis | What is checked |
|---|---|
| **Overall program grade** | **Every gradable assessment must have a final result first**, and then the overall result must be in a passing band. |
| **All assessments pass** | Every assessment slot passes. |
| **Specific assessments** | Each assessment you named passes. |
| **Every counted subject passes** | Every subject the programme counts towards its result passes. |

<Callout type="warn">
**Why "overall" waits for everything to be marked.** With the default grading setup, a
learner who has sat one assessment and done well shows an overall "pass" — because the
unmarked work is left out. Issuing on that would hand out a certificate the first week.
So the overall basis waits until every gradable item has a final result.
</Callout>

**A held result also blocks it.** If any quiz result is being withheld, no certificate
is issued — awarding one would announce a result the learner cannot see yet. When the
result is released, the check runs again by itself.

## The quick diagnosis

| Symptom | Gate |
|---|---|
| Nobody on the course is getting one | 1 — switched off, or the type has no requirements |
| One learner is waiting, everyone else has theirs | 4, 5 or 6 |
| Everyone is waiting and the marks are fine | 3 — the release date |
| A learner who bought one semester is waiting | 4 — partial access |
| A learner who passed is waiting, with marking outstanding | 6 — the finality rule |
| A learner who passed is waiting, results not yet published | 6 — a held result |

## What a learner sees

A learner can see what they still need. Where a certificate is close, the requirements
are shown with what is outstanding — so "submit the feedback form" reaches them without
a support message.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner passed everything and still has nothing.">
Gates 3, 4 and 6 in that order: the release date, partial access, and whether every
gradable item has a final result. The third catches most of them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I issue one anyway?">
Yes. Issuing by hand bypasses the requirements entirely — see
[Issuing certificates](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/issuing-certificates). Use it
for the genuine exception, not as a routine.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner failed a subject but passed overall. Should they get one?">
That is your decision, and it is the **passing basis** that expresses it. **Overall
program grade** says yes; **Every counted subject passes** says no.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We require a feedback form and nobody is submitting it.">
Check the form is actually assigned to the programme, and that learners can find it.
The form binding is set on the programme, not on the type.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="If a learner's grade later drops, is the certificate taken back?">
No. An issued certificate stands. That is why the overall basis waits for everything to
be marked before issuing.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
