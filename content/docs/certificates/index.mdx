Three separate things make a certificate, and keeping them apart is most of
understanding this section:

| The thing | What it decides |
|---|---|
| **The type** | Who earns it, and on what basis. |
| **The template** | What the document looks like. |
| **The programme's settings** | Whether this course awards one, and when it is released. |

If a certificate has not appeared, go straight to
[Certificate requirements](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-requirements) —
it lists the six gates in the order they are checked.

## Two things to know before you start

- **A learner with partial access never gets a completion certificate.** Somebody who
  bought two semesters of a four-semester diploma has not finished the diploma.
- **There is no public verification page.** A third party cannot check a certificate
  themselves; you look the number up for them.
