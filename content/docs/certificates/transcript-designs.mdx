A transcript design decides what a learner's result looks like on paper. Programmes
choose one; a programme that chooses nothing uses your default.

Designs live in <Path>Settings > Transcript Design</Path>.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The two kinds

You choose the kind when you create the design, and **it cannot be changed afterwards** —
the two share no parts, so switching would discard the document.

| Kind | What it prints |
|---|---|
| **Simple percentage marksheet** | One line per subject, in a single percentage. |
| **Detailed GPA transcript** | One line per assessment, with weights, grade points and a cumulative result. |

Choose the marksheet if your academy reports percentages. Choose the GPA transcript if
you report grade points, or if learners need to see each assessment.

## What a marksheet can show

A **Simple percentage marksheet** has one more choice — **Results show**:

| Choice | Each subject row shows |
|---|---|
| **Percentage, each subject out of 100** | A percentage. |
| **Marks, as each subject was marked** | The raw marks — 68, 45. |
| **Marks, with a percentage column** | Both. |

When it shows marks, **Show the Total Marks column** prints what each subject was marked
out of beside the marks obtained. Without it, a 68 does not say whether it was out of
70 or 100.

The subject table and the semester summary both follow this choice, so the document
never reports one thing at the top and another at the bottom.

Column headings are the design's own text. Change them in the builder — they are not
fixed labels.

## Choosing which design a programme uses

| Setting | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Use as the default** | On the design | Every programme that has not chosen a design prints this one. |
| **Transcript design** | On a course's settings step | That programme's own choice. |
| **Active** | On the design | An archived design is never printed; programmes using it fall back to your default. |

<Callout type="info">
Archiving is the safe way to retire a design. Programmes still pointing at it quietly
fall back to your default, rather than failing to print anything.
</Callout>

## Writing the design

**Used for** is a note for your team — "which programmes should print this". It is worth
filling in: a year from now, four designs with similar names are impossible to tell
apart.

The document itself is drawn in the builder — see
[Designing a document](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/designing-a-document).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="I picked the wrong kind.">
Create a new design of the right kind. The kind is fixed once a design exists, because
the two kinds share no parts.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A programme is printing the wrong design.">
Check its **Transcript design** setting. A programme with none set uses your default,
and a programme pointing at an archived design also falls back to the default.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a batch have its own design?">
Designs are chosen on the programme. A batch follows its course.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Learners cannot see their transcript at all.">
That is a different setting — **Enable Transcript** on the course. See
[Transcripts and results](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/transcripts).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can one design serve both certificates and transcripts?">
No. A certificate design and a transcript design are different kinds of document.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
