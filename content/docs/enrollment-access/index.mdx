Two jobs live here. **Enrolment** is getting people into a programme. **Access** is
deciding what they can open once they are in — which turns out to be six separate
things, any one of which can hold content back.

If a learner says "I cannot see it", start with
[How access works together](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-access-works-together).
It is the checklist, in the order the answers usually turn out to be.

## Drip or eligibility?

The two are easy to confuse and behave differently:

| | **Drip schedule** | **Eligibility rule** |
|---|---|---|
| Asks | **When** does this open? | **Who** may see it? |
| A held item | Shows, locked, with its date | Does not appear at all |
| The learner is told | Yes | No |
