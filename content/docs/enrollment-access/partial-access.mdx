---
title: "Partial Access"
description: "Giving a learner one semester rather than the whole programme — how it is set, what it changes, and how it is marked."
---

A diploma runs four semesters and a learner pays for one. Partial access is how that
works: the learner is enrolled in the whole programme but holds only the part they
bought, and everything — content, progress, marks — follows what they hold.

The screens call each part a **scope**.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Where it comes from

Two ways:

- **They bought it.** A learner who buys one semester gets that semester. See
  [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
- **You gave it.** From an enrolment, **Assign Content Scope** — pick the parts they
  should hold.

For many learners at once, **Bulk edit enrollment scopes** does the same job across a
selection, and **Review scope changes** shows what is about to change before it does.

<Callout type="info">
The selection you bulk-edit is whatever your filters have narrowed the enrolment list
to. Filter first, check the count, then act.
</Callout>

## What partial access changes

**Content.** They see what they hold. Everything else simply is not there — no locked
panel, no explanation.

**Progress.** Measured against what they hold, not the whole programme. A learner with
one semester out of four can reach 100%.

**Marks.** Worked out from what they hold, on both sides of the sum. Their result is out
of what they actually took, not out of a diploma they have a quarter of.

**Certificates.** A certificate for the whole programme needs the whole programme. A
learner who holds one semester has not finished the diploma — see
[Certificate requirements](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-requirements).

## Seeing what someone holds

The enrolment list has a **Scopes** column, and an enrolment's own screen shows **This
learner can** — the parts they hold, in words.

Both are worth checking before you answer "why can this learner not see the final exam?"

## Growing access

A learner who buys the next semester, or is moved up by
[progression](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/progression), gains that part. They
keep what they already had — access is added, not swapped.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner says content is missing and I cannot see why.">
Check the **Scopes** column on their enrolment first. Restricted content does not appear
at all, so from the learner's side it looks identical to a course that was never built.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner's progress says 100% but they have done a quarter of the diploma.">
That is correct for partial access: they are measured against what they hold. Their
transcript shows which semesters they actually took.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why did a learner's result change after I gave them another semester?">
Because the denominator changed. They are now marked out of two semesters rather than
one, and the new semester's unmarked work is in the picture. See
[Course grading setup](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/course-grading-setup).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I give partial access on a self-paced course?">
Partial access works on the parts a programme is divided into, which means
[terms](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints) on a batch. A self-paced
course has no terms, so there is nothing to divide. Use
[eligibility rules](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/eligibility-rules) or separate
courses instead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner bought a semester but still cannot open it.">
Work down [How access works together](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-access-works-together).
The usual answers are a drip schedule holding the content until a date, or the semester
not being published yet.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
