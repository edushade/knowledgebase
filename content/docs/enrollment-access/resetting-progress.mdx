title: "Resetting Progress"
description: "Clearing what a learner has done — for one item, one enrolment, or a whole programme — and what each one destroys."
---

Sometimes a learner needs to start again. A reset clears their ticks so they can work
through the content a second time.

**None of it can be undone.** Read what each option takes with it before using one.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The three sizes

| Action | What it clears |
|---|---|
| **Reset Item Progress** | One lesson, quiz, assignment or file, for one learner. |
| **Reset Enrollment Progress** | Everything in one learner's enrolment — lessons, quizzes and assignments. |
| **Reset All Program Progress** | Every learner on the programme. |

Each asks before it acts, and the confirmation says what it covers. The programme-wide
one is the one to be careful with: it is a whole batch's work.

## The opposite: completing

**Complete Progress** marks everything done and sets the enrolment to completed. It is
the right tool for migrating learners who finished elsewhere, and for closing out a
batch that ran outside the platform.

It does not mark anything as *passed*. Completion and a result are different things, so
a learner completed this way has no marks unless you give them some.

## Shared content

Resetting a quiz that appears in more than one programme affects that learner wherever
they have taken it. The confirmation says which programmes are involved — read it.

See [Reusable content](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/reusable-content).

## What a reset does to marks

Clearing progress and clearing marks are not the same thing:

- **Resetting progress** clears the ticks.
- **Resetting an attempt or a submission** clears the work and the mark for that
  assessment.

After either, the programme's results need recalculating so the figures above agree —
run **Calculate All Grades**. See
[Learner grades](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/learner-grades).

## Learners resetting themselves

Your academy can let learners reset their own progress on a course. It is a setting,
and it is **off** by default. On, a learner gets a reset control in their own panel;
off, only you can do it.

Think before switching it on for a programme that awards a certificate.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Should I reset or transfer?">
Reset if they are repeating the same batch. Transfer if they are moving to a different
one — a transfer lets you choose whether marks travel, and leaves a trail. See
[Transfers](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/transfers).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I reset a learner and their grade is still there.">
Progress and marks are separate. Reset the attempt or submission as well, then
recalculate the programme's grades.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I undo a reset?">
No. Nothing is kept. For anything irreversible on a learner with real marks, export
their grades first.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner reset themselves by accident.">
Their ticks are gone and cannot be restored. If that is a risk for your academy, switch
the learner-reset setting off. See [Control Panel](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/control-panel).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a reset remove their certificate?">
No. A certificate already issued stands. See
[Issuing certificates](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/issuing-certificates).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
