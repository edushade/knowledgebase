Most learners enrol by buying. Sometimes you put them in yourself: a corporate group, a
learner who paid offline, a scholarship, a batch migrated from your old system.

## Enrolling people

Go to <Path>Enrollments</Path> and choose **Add Enrollment**, or reach the same screen
from a course, a learning path or a batch, where it is called **Assign Learners**.

The screen has two tabs — **Add learners**, and **Enrolled** for who is already in.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Choose a programme

Pick the **Program Type** — Course, Learning Path or Cohort — and then the programme
itself. Coming from a programme, this is already chosen, and **Change programme** swaps
it.

Each one in the picker shows what you need to decide with:

| On the card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Seats** | **No seat limit**, a number left, or **Full**. |
| **Starts** | Its first day, where it has one. |
| **Published** or **Scheduled** | Whether learners can see it yet. |
| **Runs in batches** | A cohort-based course — enrol into its batches, not into it. |

A cohort-based course with no batches yet says so: *"This programme has no batches yet.
Create one under Cohorts, then come back to enrol learners."*

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose the learners

Search by name, email or phone, and narrow the list with:

| Filter | Values |
|---|---|
| **Account** | **Active** or **Inactive**. |
| **Signed in** | **Has signed in** or **Never signed in**. |
| **Signed up** | A period — see below. |

Then either tick people by hand, or **select everyone who matches** — which is the
important one. It works on the server, so it covers **every page**, not just the one in
front of you. You can still untick individuals afterwards, and the dialog counts them
out for you.

#### The sign-up period

**Signed up** filters on when the account was created, with presets — **Today**,
**Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This month**, **Last month**, **This
year**, **Last year** — or **A whole month**, or a **Custom range** you pick on a calendar.
It is the same date filter used everywhere in the admin — see
[Date filters and your time zone](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/date-filters).

<Callout type="info">
**These are your academy's days, not the browser's.** "1 November" runs midnight to
midnight on your academy's own clock — the **Timezone** set under
<Path>Website > Settings > Localization</Path> — so an administrator working from another
country gets the same list you would.
</Callout>

Nothing matching gives you the reason rather than an empty box: *"No learners match. Try
a wider sign-up period, another search, or clear the filters."*

</Step>

<Step>

### Review enrolment

Nothing is enrolled until you confirm, and the confirmation is a summary rather than a
count:

| It tells you | Example |
|---|---|
| **Who** | "214 learners you picked", or "Everyone who matches, except 3 learners you unticked" — with your filters listed as chips beside it. |
| **Already enrolled** | How many are in it already and are left exactly as they are. |
| **Seats** | "40 of 200 left now, 26 after this" — or **No seat limit**. |
| **Notifications** | Each learner gets your academy's usual enrolment notification. |

**No filters: every learner in the academy** appears where you have set none. Read that
line before you confirm — it is the difference between enrolling a batch and enrolling
everybody.

</Step>

<Step>

### Decide what they get

**What they get** is on the same screen. Leave everything unticked and they get the
whole programme. Tick parts and they get only those — *"the way a learner who buys one
semester gets only that one"*.

The footer keeps score: **The whole course**, or **2 parts only**.

This is [partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access), set at
the moment of enrolling rather than fixed up afterwards.

</Step>

</Steps>

### When it will not let you

| It says | What to do |
|---|---|
| *"…is full: all 200 seats are taken."* | Raise its seat limit, or choose another batch. |
| *"Only 12 seats are left and 40 learners need one. Narrow the list by 28…"* | Enrol fewer, or raise the limit. |
| *"Nobody new to enrol"* | Everyone you picked is already in it. Not a failure. |

<Callout type="warn">
**Seat limits are enforced here.** You cannot quietly overfill a batch by enrolling
people yourself — the screen tells you how many seats are left and by how many you are
over.
</Callout>

**Enrolment being closed is different.** You are warned — *"Learners can't join it
themselves, but you can still enrol them here"* — and the button becomes **Enrol 40
learners anyway**. Closed stops learners, not you.

### Afterwards

One summary for the whole run, however many people it covered: **Enrolment finished**,
or **Enrolment finished, with some not enrolled** when part of it did not go through.

If the list could not be worked out at all, nothing is enrolled and it says so rather
than enrolling half of them.

## What enrolling someone yourself skips

| Skipped | What this means |
|---|---|
| **Payment** | No transaction is created. The enrolment shows no payment. |
| **The enrolment window** | You can add a learner to a batch whose enrolment has closed — with a warning. |

It does **not** skip seat limits, and it does **not** skip the one-batch-per-programme
rule: a learner already in another batch of the same course cannot be put into a second.

## Giving them part of a programme

The quickest way is at the moment of enrolling, under **What they get** above.

For people already enrolled, change it afterwards with **Assign Content Scope**, or
**Bulk edit enrollment scopes** for several at once. See
[Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access).

## The enrolment list

<Path>Enrollments</Path> is every enrolment in your academy, and it carries more than
most people expect:

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Active, Completed, Cancelled, Expired or Suspended. |
| **Progress** | How far through they are. |
| **Scopes** | Which parts of the programme they hold. |
| **Enrolled At** / **Enrolled By** / **Enrolled through** | When, by whom, and by which route. |
| **Expires** / **Access** / **Access ended** | Whether access is live, and when it stops. |
| **Payment**, **Payment Method**, **Payment Gateway**, **Payment Status** | How it was paid for. |
| **Certificate** / **Certificate Issued At** | Whether one has been awarded. |
| **Last Activity**, **Signed in** | Whether the learner has ever turned up. |

**Has signed in** and **Never signed in** is the most useful filter you are not using.
A batch where a quarter never signed in has an onboarding problem, not a teaching one.

The list exports, so the awkward question — "send me everyone who has not started" — is
a filter and a download.

## Removing someone

**Remove user** takes a learner off a programme. It asks first, because it takes their
enrolment with it.

If they are in the wrong batch, **transfer** them instead — it keeps their record and
leaves a trail. See [Transfers](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/transfers).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Nothing happened when I assigned learners.">
Everyone you picked was already enrolled — the screen says **Nobody new to enrol**. It
is not a failure.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Select all only took the people on screen.">
It does not. **Select everyone who matches** is worked out on the server and covers
every page of the filtered list. The confirmation says "Everyone who matches" and lists
your filters, so you can check before committing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I enrol more people than the batch has seats?">
No. The screen tells you how many seats are left and by how many you are over. Raise the
seat limit on the batch first, or enrol fewer people.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The batch's enrolment is closed. Can I still add someone?">
Yes. Closed stops learners enrolling themselves; it does not stop you. You are warned,
and the button reads **Enrol … anyway**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I cannot find the course in the list.">
Check the **Program Type**. A cohort-based course does not appear under Course; its
batches appear under Cohort.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I enrol learners from a spreadsheet?">
Not directly into a programme. Import the people first so they have accounts, then
select them here. See [Adding people](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/adding-people).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The learner is enrolled but sees no content.">
Work down [How access works together](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-access-works-together).
After a manual enrolment the usual answer is partial access, or content that is not
published.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does an enrolment I create count towards revenue?">
No. There is no transaction behind it. If money changed hands outside the platform,
record it as a manual payment so the figures are honest. See
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
