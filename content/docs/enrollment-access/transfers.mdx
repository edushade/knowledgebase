title: "Transfers"
description: "Moving a learner from one batch or programme to another, and deciding what comes with them."
---

A learner registered into the wrong batch, or needs to move to the evening group, or is
repeating with next year's intake. A transfer moves them without destroying their
record — and lets you decide whether their marks travel with them.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Transferring

From <Path>Enrollments > Transfers</Path>, or from a learner's enrolment, choose
**Transfer Learners**. You pick:

1. **Who** is moving — one learner or many.
2. **Where to** — the programme and batch they are moving into.
3. **Why** — a reason, kept on the transfer trail. "Registered into the wrong batch" is
   a real answer, and the one you will be glad of later.

Before it happens you get **Review transfer**: what is about to change, for whom. Read
it. A transfer is not something you want to discover was wrong a week later.

## What comes with them

This is the decision that matters, and it is made **per assessment**, not once for the
whole transfer.

Under **Marks they already have**, every assessment the learner has a mark for is listed
and **everything is ticked by default** — that is what a transfer has always done.

Untick one and the learner **starts it again in the new programme**:

- Their old mark stops counting there.
- It stays on record — nothing is deleted.
- They may sit it again **even if the assessment allows a single attempt**.

The list is grouped the way the programme is, so you can untick a whole semester with
one tick rather than hunting through it, and there is a search box — **Find an
assessment** — for a long curriculum. A **Start again:** line summarises what you have
unticked, so you can check it before committing.

<Callout type="warn">
**The default carries everything, including the failures.** A failed attempt that
travels with a learner counts in the new batch as the attempt it was. If somebody is
repeating a semester, untick that semester's assessments — otherwise their old failures
follow them into the fresh start you meant to give them.
</Callout>

| Situation | What to untick |
|---|---|
| Wrong batch at sign-up, same content | Nothing. Everything should follow. |
| Timetable clash, moving sideways mid-term | Nothing. |
| Repeating a semester they failed | That semester's assessments. |
| Moving to a different programme | Most of it — the content is not the same, so the marks are not comparable. |

## Keeping their access as it is

A learner who holds only part of a programme — one semester, say — keeps the equivalent
part in the new one, where there is an equivalent. If any learner's part has no match in
the target, the transfer **stops rather than moving them without it**, so nobody arrives
with less access than they left with and nobody notices three weeks later.

See [Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access).

## The transfer trail

Every transfer your academy has ever made is on one screen — <Path>Enrollments >
Transfers</Path>, titled **Transfer Trail**. A learner who has moved twice shows both
moves.

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/enrollment-access/transfer-trail.png"
  alt="The Transfer Trail, one row per transfer, showing where each learner moved from and to."
  caption="Learner names are blurred here. On your own screen they are readable — this is a record of real people."
/>

Each row carries:

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Learner** | Who moved. |
| **From → To** | The batch or course they left, and the one they joined. |
| **Scopes** | How much access went with them — `1 → 1 scopes`, or **Full access**. |
| **Payment** | Whether money had been taken, and through which gateway. |
| **Certificate** | **Issued** if one had already been given. |
| **Status** | Whether the transfer itself went through. |
| **Reason** | What you typed at the time. |
| **By** | Who did it. |

That record is the answer to "why is this learner in this batch?" and "why do their
marks start in week six?". The **Reason** column is the one that does that work, and it
is only as good as what you type — *"wrong section purchase"* tells the next person
everything; a blank tells them nothing.

You can filter the trail by **Status** and by **Program Type**, and search it by name.

## What a transfer does not do

- **It does not refund or re-charge.** Money is separate — see
  [Transactions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/transactions).
- **It does not re-issue a certificate.** A certificate already issued stands.
- **It does not change the learner's account.** Same person, same sign-in, different
  batch.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Should I transfer or just enrol them again?">
Transfer. Enrolling someone a second time leaves two enrolments, two progress records
and two places their marks could be — and the duplicate will confuse every report you
run afterwards.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I transferred someone and their old failed attempt is still counting.">
Carry forward was left on for that assessment — it is on by default. Untick it on the
transfer, or give them another attempt in the new batch. The old mark stays on record
either way.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner's marks appeared in a batch they never attended.">
They were carried forward in a transfer. Check the transfer trail on their enrolment to
see when, and from where.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I transfer between two different courses?">
You can move a learner to another programme, not only another batch of the same course.
Where the content differs, carrying marks forward makes little sense — reset instead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I undo a transfer?">
Not as a single action. Transfer them back, which adds a second entry to the trail
rather than erasing the first. That is the honest record of what happened.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to a learner who only bought one semester?">
Their partial access travels with them. Check afterwards that the new batch's semesters
line up with what they paid for. See
[Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
