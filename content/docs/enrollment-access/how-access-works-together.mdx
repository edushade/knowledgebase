title: "How Access Works Together"
description: "Six separate things decide whether a learner can open a piece of content. This is the order to check them in."
---

"Why can this learner not see the final exam?" has six possible answers. They are
independent of each other, and any one of them is enough on its own.

Work down this list in order. It is sorted by how often each one turns out to be the
answer.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The checklist

### 1. Is the item published?

The **Published** switch on the item, in the course builder. An unpublished item does
not exist for learners and counts for nothing.

*What the learner sees:* nothing at all.

### 2. Are they enrolled, and is the enrolment live?

An enrolment carries a status: **Active**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**, **Expired** or
**Suspended**. Only a live one gives access. Check the expiry date too — an enrolment
can lapse on a date.

*What the learner sees:* the programme, but not its content.

### 3. Do they hold this part of the programme?

[Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access). A learner who
bought one semester holds one semester. Look at the **Scopes** column on their
enrolment.

*What the learner sees:* nothing. The content is simply absent.

### 4. Is a drip schedule holding it?

[Drip scheduling](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling). Content can be
held until a date, until a period after enrolment, or until something else is finished.

*What the learner sees:* the item, **locked, with the date it opens**. This is the one
case where they are told.

### 5. Is a prerequisite unmet?

[Prerequisites](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/prerequisites). Content that waits
for something else to be completed.

*What the learner sees:* the item locked, with what they must finish first.

### 6. Does an eligibility rule exclude them?

[Eligibility rules](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/eligibility-rules). Rules about
*who* — country, enrolment elsewhere, profile details.

*What the learner sees:* nothing, and no explanation.

## A seventh, for paid programmes

A learner paying by instalments can have their access **paused** when a payment is
overdue past its grace period. Their enrolment looks live, but the content is closed.
Their instalment state says which: **on track**, **due date passed (in grace)**, **past
grace — access paused**, **defaulted**, or **paid off**. See
[Installments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments).

## Telling them apart quickly

| What the learner reports | Most likely |
|---|---|
| "The course is empty" | Not published, partial access, or an eligibility rule |
| "It says it unlocks on the 14th" | Drip schedule |
| "It says I must finish module 2" | Prerequisite |
| "I can see the course but nothing opens" | Enrolment not active, or access paused for payment |
| "It worked yesterday" | An expiry date, a lock date, or a payment that fell past grace |

<Callout type="info">
**Check it as a learner.** The builder's view is not the learner's view. If their
account can be impersonated for support, that answers the question in seconds — see
[Support login](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/support-login).
</Callout>

## Why "nothing at all" is so common

Four of the six produce no message. That is deliberate — a learner should not be able to
work out what other people are allowed to see. It is good for privacy and awkward for
support, so where a rule will exclude people who are likely to ask, say so somewhere
they can read it.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Two learners in the same batch see different content.">
Partial access or an eligibility rule. Compare the **Scopes** column on both enrolments
first; it is the faster of the two to check.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner lost access overnight.">
Three things happen on a clock: an enrolment expiry date, a drip lock date, and an
instalment falling past its grace period. Check their enrolment's **Expires** and
**Access** columns.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can see the content but the learner cannot.">
You are looking at it as an admin. Open it as a learner would before concluding
anything.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does unpublishing hide content from learners who already finished it?">
Yes — and it also removes it from their progress calculation, so everyone's percentage
moves. Use a drip lock if you only want to stop new learners reaching it.
</Accordion>
