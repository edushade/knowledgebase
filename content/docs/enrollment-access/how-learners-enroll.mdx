title: "How Learners Enroll"
description: "What a learner actually goes through from finding a course to opening the first lesson, and where it goes wrong."
---

Enrolment is the moment a stranger becomes your learner. It is worth knowing the path
exactly, because every step is somewhere they can fall out.

<WhoCan roles="Learner" />

## The path

<Steps>

<Step>

### They find the course

From your catalogue, from search, from a link you sent. The course page shows its
title, description, thumbnail, level, language, duration, **Learning Outcomes** and
**Requirements** — and any lesson you marked **Free Preview**.

A cohort-based course shows its batches instead of one **Enrol** button: each with its
start date and how many seats are left.

</Step>

<Step>

### They sign in or sign up

Buying requires an account. They can register with an email address or a phone number,
or sign in with Google or Facebook if you have switched those on.

Depending on your settings they may have to verify their email or phone, and complete
their profile, before they can go on. See
[Accounts and sign-in](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/accounts-and-sign-in).

</Step>

<Step>

### They pay

One item per checkout. They pick a payment method, enter a coupon code if they have
one, and pay. Where you offer it, they can pay in instalments instead of all at once.

See [Checkout](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/checkout) and
[Payment methods](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/payment-methods).

</Step>

<Step>

### They are enrolled

The enrolment is created as soon as the payment succeeds, and the course appears under
**My Course** on their dashboard.

What opens depends on what you set up: everything at once, or week one with the rest on
a [drip schedule](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling).

</Step>

</Steps>

## A free course

With a price of zero, there is nothing to pay and the learner is enrolled straight
away. They still need an account.

## When they are refused

Four rules can stop a purchase, in this order: the batch has ended, enrolment is
closed, the seats are full, or the batch has started and does not take late joiners.
A learner already in another batch of the same programme is also refused. See
[Enrolment windows](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/enrollment-windows).

Each refusal says which it is, so a learner who tells you "it would not let me in"
usually knows more than they are letting on — ask them what it said.

## Where learners actually fall out

In rough order of how often:

1. **The course has no price.** It is published, it looks ready, and there is nothing to
   buy. See [Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing).
2. **Verification is required and the email never arrived.** Check your sending setup
   before blaming the learner. See [Verification](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/verification).
3. **A cohort-based course with no batch.** Nothing to join.
4. **Profile completion is required** and they stopped at the form.
5. **The payment failed** and nobody followed up. Pending transactions are a list you
   can work. See [Transactions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/transactions).

## What you can do for them

- Enrol them yourself — see
  [Admin and bulk enrollment](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/admin-and-bulk-enrollment).
- Record a payment taken outside the platform — see
  [Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).
- Give them a scholarship or a coupon — see
  [Scholarships](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/scholarships) and
  [Coupons and offers](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/coupons-and-offers).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner paid but has no access.">
Find the transaction. If it is pending, the payment has not completed; if it succeeded,
check the enrolment exists and work down
[How access works together](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-access-works-together).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner buy two courses at once?">
No. Checkout takes one item per purchase. To sell several together, make a
[bundle](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/bundles), which is one item.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can somebody buy a course for someone else?">
Not as a gift purchase. Take the payment and enrol the recipient yourself.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner cannot see the Enrol button.">
Either the course has no price, or **Enrollment Open** is off, or an eligibility rule
excludes them — and the third gives them no message at all.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
