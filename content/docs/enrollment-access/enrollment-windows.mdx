---
title: "Enrolment Windows"
description: "When a batch or a semester is open to new learners — the four rules, in the order they are applied."
---

A batch is not simply open or closed. Four things decide whether somebody can join
today, and they are checked in order. Knowing the order is how you answer "why was this
learner refused?" without guessing.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The four rules, in order

### 1. Has the batch ended?

**The batch's end date is a hard bound.** Once a batch is over, nothing in it can be
joined — not the batch, not a single semester of it. The buyer is told the batch has
ended and to look for a newer one.

Nothing overrides this.

### 2. Is enrolment open?

**Enrollment Open** on the batch. Off refuses new learners while leaving the batch
visible and everyone already in it untouched.

### 3. Are there seats?

**Max Enrollment** on the batch. **0 means unlimited.** On a cohort-based course this is
the limit that counts — the course's own Maximum Enrollments does not apply to a batch.

### 4. Has teaching already started?

**Allow Late Enrollment** on the batch. Off, somebody arriving after the start date is
refused and told to look for the next batch. On, they may still join.

<Callout type="info">
**A semester overrides the late-entry rule.** If a learner is buying particular
semesters rather than the whole batch, the batch's late-entry answer is skipped and the
semester's own window decides. That is what lets a batch which is running with late
entry closed still sell Semester 3.
</Callout>

## A semester's own window

Each [term](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints) can carry
**Enrollment opens** and **Enrollment closes**. Leave both empty and the semester follows
its batch:

- On sale until the batch's end date, **or**
- when the batch does not allow late entry, only until that semester starts teaching.

Fill them in when one semester needs to sell on different dates from the rest — later
admissions for a semester that starts in the second half of the year, for instance.

## One batch per programme

A learner cannot be in two batches of the same programme at once. Somebody already in
Batch 1 is refused Batch 2 of the same course, whether they are buying the whole thing
or a single semester.

If they genuinely need to move,
[transfer](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/transfers) them rather than enrolling
them twice.

## Enrolling somebody anyway

These rules govern *purchase*. Enrolling a learner yourself is a different path — see
[Admin and bulk enrollment](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/admin-and-bulk-enrollment).
Use it for the agreed exception: the learner who paid offline, the late joiner you
decided to take.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner was told the batch has already started.">
**Allow Late Enrollment** is off and the start date has passed. Turn it on to accept
late joiners, or enrol this one learner yourself.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We want to sell Semester 3 of a batch that is already running.">
Give Semester 3 its own enrolment window. The batch's late-entry setting is skipped for
a purchase scoped to particular semesters, so the semester's window is what decides.
See [Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The batch shows seats left but nobody can join.">
Check the other three rules in order: the end date, **Enrollment Open**, and late entry.
Seats are only the third of four.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner says they are already enrolled, but they are not in this batch.">
They are in another batch of the same programme, which is what is refusing them.
Transfer them instead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I reopen a batch that has ended?">
Move its end date. Until you do, nothing in it sells — the end date is checked before
everything else.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
