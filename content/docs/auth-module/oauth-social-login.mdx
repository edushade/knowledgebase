# OAuth & Social Login

EduShade allows users to sign in or register using their existing social media accounts. This eliminates the need to remember another password and speeds up the onboarding process.

## Supported Providers

| Provider | Scopes Requested | Data Retrieved |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Google** | openid, email, profile | Name, Email, Profile Picture, Locale |
| **Facebook** | email, public_profile | Name, Email, Profile Picture |
| **GitHub** | user:email | Username, Email, Name, Avatar |
| **Apple** | — | Name, Email |

## How Social Login Works

### Step-by-Step Flow

1. **Click the provider button** — On the login or register page, click the Google, Facebook, or Apple button
2. **Redirect to provider** — You are redirected to the provider's authorization page
3. **Authorize access** — Grant EduShade permission to access your basic profile info
4. **Redirected back** — The provider redirects you back to EduShade with an authorization code
5. **Account resolution** — The system:
   - Checks if an account with your provider ID already exists → **Log in**
   - If not, checks if an account with your email exists → **Link provider and log in**
   - If neither, creates a new account → **Register and log in**
6. **Session created** — You receive access and refresh tokens, and are redirected to your dashboard

### What Gets Auto-Populated

When you register via social login, the following fields are automatically filled:

- **Name** — From your social profile
- **Email** — From your social profile (auto-verified if the provider confirms it)
- **Avatar** — Your profile picture URL
- **Provider ID** — Stored for future logins (e.g., `google_id`, `facebook_id`)

## Security

### State Parameter Protection

The OAuth flow uses a **secure state parameter** to prevent CSRF and replay attacks:

- The state contains an encrypted payload with: Tenant ID, Provider, Token, Expiry
- On callback, the state is validated for:
  - Correct tenant (prevents cross-tenant attacks)
  - Valid token (prevents tampering)
  - Not expired (prevents replay attacks)

### Email Auto-Verification

If the social provider confirms that the user's email is verified (e.g., Google), EduShade automatically marks the email as verified. This means you skip the email verification step.

## Linking Social Accounts

You can link multiple social accounts to your existing EduShade account.

### How to Link

1. Log in to your EduShade account
2. Go to **Account Settings**
3. Find the **Connected Accounts** or **Social Login** section
4. Click **Connect** next to the provider you want to link
5. Authorize the provider
6. Your social account is now linked

### How to Unlink

1. Go to **Account Settings**
2. Find the **Connected Accounts** section
3. Click **Disconnect** next to the provider you want to unlink
4. The provider is removed from your account

**Note:** You must have at least one login method available (password or another social account) before unlinking a provider.

## Admin-Initiated Linking

Administrators can link or unlink social accounts on behalf of users:

1. Go to **Admin → User Management**
2. Open the user's profile
3. Use the **Link OAuth** or **Unlink OAuth** action
4. Select the provider and provide the necessary details

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Authorization failed" | The provider denied access. Try again and ensure you click "Allow" |
| "Invalid state" | The OAuth session expired. Start the login process again |
| "Email already registered" | An account with this email exists but isn't linked. Log in with your password first, then link the social account |
| Provider button not showing | The provider may not be configured for your tenant. Contact your administrator |
| Profile picture not updating | Social profile pictures are fetched at registration time. Update your avatar manually in account settings |
