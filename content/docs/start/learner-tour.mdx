Everything you build has a learner's view. This is it, in order.

## Before they buy

Your website: the course page, with its description, outcomes, requirements, level,
language, instructor and price. Any lesson you marked **Free Preview** can be opened
without enrolling.

A cohort-based course shows its batches instead of one button — each with its start
date and seats left.

## Signing up and paying

They register with an email or a phone, verify it if you require that, and pay. One item
per checkout; a coupon code is typed in if they have one.

See [How learners enroll](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-learners-enroll).

## Their dashboard

A menu of twelve things: **Overview**, **My Course**, **Assignment**, **Exam**,
**Saved**, **My Calendar**, **Event**, **Notice Board**, **Notification**,
**Community**, **Payment History** and **Support**. **Community** appears once they are
in a programme with chat — see [Community](/docs/knowledgebase/learner/community).

**Overview** carries their figures — performance, courses ongoing and completed, time
spent, live classes attended, quizzes and assignments completed.

## Working through a course

The outline, with their place remembered. Each item ticks off when its completion rule
is met. From any lesson they can **Save** it, bookmark an exact moment, report a
problem, or ask a question about the lesson in the programme's Q&A.

What they can see of the outline is decided by six separate rules — see
[How access works together](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-access-works-together).

## Being assessed

Assignments and exams each have their own area, with **pending**, **previous** and
**published results**. A submitted assessment reads **Under Review** until it is marked
and released.

## Finishing

Their result, where you have enabled transcripts for the programme. Their certificate,
in **My Certificates**, once they have cleared all six gates.

## What they cannot do

| Not available |
|---|
| Buy two things at once |
| Pay for somebody else |
| Download a transcript, unless you enable it |
| Have a certificate verified on a public page |

## The quickest way to see it yourself

Two ways, and you should do both before launching:

1. **Register a fresh account** and go through the whole thing. Nothing else finds the
   broken verification email.
2. **[Support login](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/support-login)** into a real
   learner's account, read-only, when you need to answer a specific question.
