Everyone on your platform has an account, and what they can do comes from their
**role**. Deciding the roles before you add the team is far easier than untangling it
afterwards.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Two separate settings

| | Decides |
|---|---|
| **Learner / Instructor / Admin** marks | What kind of person they are. |
| **Role** | What they can actually open and change. |

Marking somebody as an instructor makes them eligible to teach. It does not let them do
anything.

## Roles a course business needs

| Role | Allowed to | Not allowed to |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner / Admin** | Everything. | — |
| **Instructor** | See and teach their own programmes, mark work. | Touch other programmes, people or money. |
| **Course builder** | Create and edit content. | See people or money. |
| **Registrar** | Enrolments, transfers, progression, certificates. | Edit content. |
| **Finance** | Billing, transactions, refunds. | Anything else. |
| **Support** | Read people and enrolments, use support login. | Change grades or money. |
| **Marketing** | The website, the blog, campaigns. | Learners' records. |

Start with the ones you need. Four roles well drawn beat ten.

## The rule that saves trouble

<Callout type="warn">
**Start narrow.** Widening a role when somebody asks takes a minute. Discovering that
everyone could delete courses, after one is deleted, takes a great deal longer.
</Callout>

Two more that follow from it:

- **One or two owners, no more.** An academy where eight people can change anything has
  no record of who did.
- **Limit instructors to their own programmes.** One switch on the role, applied to
  every permission in it at once.

## Checking a role before you use it

**Preview access** on a role shows which admin pages it can open, and names the
permissions missing where a page is only partly available.

Use it every time. A role that looks right and opens nothing is the normal first
attempt — most list pages need the permission to *list* as well as to *view*.

## Who should hold what

| Capability | Keep it to |
|---|---|
| Changing grades | Instructors on their own programmes, and registrars |
| Issuing certificates by hand | One or two people |
| Refunds | Finance |
| Support login into learner accounts | Support, read-only |
| Settings and roles | Owners |
| Deleting courses | Owners |
