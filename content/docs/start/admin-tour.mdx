The admin menu has six groups. Each answers one question, which is why things are where
they are.

## Insights — how is the academy doing?

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Where you land. The shape of your academy. |
| **Analytics** | Nine screens, nine questions. See [Analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics). |

## Teach — the content, and the sessions

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **My Programmes** | The programmes you personally teach. Only for instructors. |
| **Courses** | Everything you teach. |
| **Learning Paths** | Several courses as one programme. |
| **Quizzes** | Every quiz, reusable across courses. |
| **Reported Problems** | What learners have flagged as broken. |
| **Content Bank** | Reusable questions, lessons and assignments. |
| **Events** | Webinars, seminars, open days. |
| **Live Sessions** | Meeting schedules and the plans behind them. |

Batches are not here. They belong to their course — see
[Cohorts and batches](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/cohorts).

## People — who is on the platform

**All Users**, **Learners**, **Instructors**, **Admins & Staff**, and **Roles &
Permissions**. See [People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).

## Commerce — what is sold, and what was paid

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Enrollments** | Every enrolment, with more columns than you expect. |
| **Billing** | Pricing, transactions, offers, scholarships. |
| **Products** | Things that are not courses, and their taxonomies. |
| **Bundles** | Several things sold as one. |

## Engage — how we talk to learners

**Discussion**, **Noticeboard** and **Notifications**. See
[Communication](/docs/knowledgebase/communication) and
[Community](/docs/knowledgebase/community).

## Configure — set once, rarely revisited

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Website** | Your public site. |
| **Taxonomy** | Categories and tags. |
| **Grading** | Schemes, programme setups, learner grades. |
| **Settings** | Control Panel, Holidays, Integrations, Certificate Templates, Transcript Design. |
| **Audit** | Audit logs, consent records, activity logs. |

## What you see depends on your role

The menu is built from what your role allows. A colleague who cannot see an item does
not hold the permission for it — which is deliberate, so nobody clicks into a screen
that will refuse them.

If something described in this knowledge base is not in your menu, that is the first
thing to check. See
[People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).
