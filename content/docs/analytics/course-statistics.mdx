Academy-wide analytics tell you which programme to look at. This is where you look.
Every course, batch and learning path has a **Statistics** tab.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## What is on it

| Panel | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Curriculum Progress** | A breakdown of learner progress through the course structure. |
| **Learner Progress Distribution** | How learners are spread across the course. |
| **Content Completion** | Which items are being finished and which are not. |
| **Assessment Engagement** | Learners grouped by how many assessments they have attempted. |
| **Assessment Performance Distribution** | How scores are spread. |
| **Course Performance** | For a learning path, completion per course, ranked. |
| **Quiz Comparison** | Scores between two quizzes, where the programme has at least two. |
| **Completion Trend** | Completion over time. |

Alongside them the headline figures: **Total Learners**, **Active Learners**, **Average
Progress**, **Completion Rate**, **Avg Assessment Score**, **Total Time Spent**.

## The panel most people miss

**Content Completion** — how many learners have finished each quiz and assignment — is
where a course tells you what is wrong with it.

A lesson everyone finishes followed by one almost nobody does is the exact point the
course loses people. That might be a 40-minute video, a quiz that is too hard, or an
item with a completion rule nobody can meet.

Pair it with **Assessment Engagement**, which groups learners by how many assessments
they have attempted. A tall bar at zero means a group enrolled and never engaged at all.

## Quiz Comparison

Compares learners' scores between two quizzes — the mock and the final, the pre-test and
the post-test. It needs **at least two quizzes** in the programme; with one, the panel
says so.

It answers two questions at once: did the class improve, and which individuals went
backwards.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I see statistics for one batch?">
Yes — open the batch rather than the course, or use the batch filter.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Quiz Comparison says it needs two quizzes.">
The programme has one. Add another, or compare at the course level if the second quiz
lives in a different course of the same path.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Average assessment score looks high but completion is low.">
The learners who finish the assessments do well; most never reach them. **Assessment
Engagement** will show a tall bar at zero. The problem is earlier in the course than the
assessments.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Numbers here differ from the academy-wide screens.">
They are scoped differently. This counts the learners in this programme; the analytics
screens count across everything, unless you filter them.
</Accordion>
