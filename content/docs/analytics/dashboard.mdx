Two screens share this job. **Dashboard** is where you land when you sign in. **Analytics
Overview** is the fuller version.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The dashboard

<Screenshot
  src="/kb/analytics/admin-dashboard.png"
  alt="The admin dashboard, with four headline cards above Learning Performance, Completion Rate Over Time and Assessment Results."
  caption="Names are blurred here. The four quick actions sit under the greeting — the fastest way to start something new."
/>

The four cards at the top are the shape of your academy:

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Total Learners** | Everyone with a learner account. |
| **Active Courses** | Published courses, split into cohort-based and self-paced. |
| **Total Instructors** | People marked as instructors. |
| **Learning Paths** | Published paths, split the same way. |

Each carries a comparison against the previous 30 days, so the number is a direction as
well as a figure.

Below them:

| Panel | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Learning Performance** | Programmes ranked by average progress, with how many are enrolled and completed. |
| **Completion Rate Over Time** | Cumulative — of everyone enrolled by each week, the share who had completed. |
| **Assessment Results** | Passed against failed, across every learning item. |
| **Leaderboard** | The top learners by points. |
| **Notices** and **Recent Enrollments** | What has just happened. |

Across the top sit quick actions — **Course +**, **Assignment +**, **Quiz +**,
**Learning Path +** — which is the fastest way to start something new.

<Callout type="info">
**Learning Performance leaves small programmes out of its ranking.** Programmes with
very few learners are not ranked, because an average over three people says more about
those three than about the course. The note on the panel says so.
</Callout>

## Analytics Overview

The same question, with more room:

- **User Growth Trend** — accounts over time.
- **Top Performing Courses** — by completion.
- **Total Hours Watched** and **Time spent** — how much learning is actually happening.
- **Completion rate** — as a gauge.

Narrow it to one programme, or to a period, with the controls at the top.

## Reading the headline numbers honestly

**Total Learners counts accounts, not students.** Somebody who registered and never
returned is in that number. **Learners** analytics is where you find out how many are
real — see [Learners](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/learners).

**Completion rate depends on what counts as complete.** It is built from progress, which
is built from completion rules. A course whose rules are loose will show a flattering
completion rate. See
[Completion rules](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/completion-rules).

**A pass rate across every assessment mixes practice with exams.** Narrow to a programme,
or use the [assessment report](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/assessment-report).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The dashboard says we have 331 learners but only 40 are active.">
Both are true, and the gap is the thing worth working on. See
[Learners](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/learners) for who is actually turning up, and
filter the enrolment list by **Never signed in**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A course is missing from Learning Performance.">
Programmes with very few enrolled learners are not ranked.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the dashboard show the same thing to an instructor?">
It shows what their role allows. An instructor limited to their own programmes sees
those, not the academy.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does the completion rate chart only go up?">
It is cumulative: of everyone enrolled by each week, the share who had completed by
then. It is a different question from "how many completed this week".
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
