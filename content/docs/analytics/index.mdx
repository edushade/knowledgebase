Analytics sits under <Path>Insights > Analytics</Path>. Each screen answers one
question, which is why there are nine rather than one enormous dashboard.

Every screen can be narrowed to **All programmes** or one, and to a time period, so the
same question can be asked of the whole academy or of one batch. The period is the
admin's one date filter — **Today**, **Last 30 days**, **A whole month**, a **Custom
range** and the rest — and its days follow your academy's time zone. A screen shows
everything until you pick a period. See
[Date filters and your time zone](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/date-filters).

| Who is stuck, and who has stopped? | [Progress](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/progress) |
| Who is actually turning up? | [Learners](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/learners) |
| Are our results sensible? | [Grades](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/grades) |
| What have we awarded, and to whom? | [Certificates](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/certificates) |
| How are colleagues doing — and how fast do they mark? | [Instructors](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/instructors) |

## Two habits worth having

**Narrow before you read.** An academy-wide average across forty programmes tells you
almost nothing. The same figure for one batch tells you whether to act.

**Prefer the lists to the averages.** "Average progress 46%" is a number. "These eleven
learners have not opened anything in three weeks" is something you can do today.

<Callout type="info">
Most screens export, as a spreadsheet, built in your browser while you wait — it says
**Preparing…**, then **Export downloaded**. Long lists finish rather than timing out, so
"export the whole year" is a reasonable thing to ask for.

The awkward request — "send me everyone who has not started" — is
usually a filter and a download rather than a spreadsheet you build by hand.
</Callout>
