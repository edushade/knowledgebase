This is the screen that produces actions rather than numbers. It groups learners by how
far through their programme they are, and names the ones who have stopped.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The headline figures

| Figure | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Average progress** | The mean across learners still in progress. |
| **Not started** | Enrolled, nothing opened. |
| **In progress** | Somewhere in the middle. |
| **Completed** | Finished. |
| **Programmes past halfway** | How many programmes have their learners mostly beyond the midpoint. |

## The lists that matter

### How Far Learners Have Got

Learners still in progress, grouped by how much of their programme they have done. The
shape tells you where the course loses people — a bulge at 10% means they are falling
out in week one, which is a different problem from a bulge at 80%.

### Need attention

Learners **at risk** and **slipping**, most at risk first. These are the two groups
worth a message today:

- **Not started** learners are usually an onboarding problem — they never found the
  course, or never finished signing up.
- **Slipping** learners are usually a pacing problem — the week they stopped is often
  the same week for several of them, which points at the content.

### Progress by Programme

Average progress per programme, lowest first. A programme at the bottom of this list is
either badly paced or badly sold.

## What to do with it

1. **Filter to one programme.** Academy-wide averages hide everything.
2. **Look at the distribution, not the mean.** Two groups at 10% and 90% average to 50%
   and look healthy.
3. **Find the week people stop.** Open the course and look at what is there.
4. **Send something.** The at-risk list can become an audience for a campaign — see
   [Audiences](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/audiences).

## Where the numbers come from

Progress is the share of published content a learner has finished, and what counts as
finished comes from each item's completion rule. Two consequences:

- **Loose completion rules flatter you.** A course where everything counts "on view"
  will always look better than one that requires a pass.
- **Editing a live course moves everyone.** Unpublishing a finished item changes the
  share for every learner.

See [Progress tracking](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/progress-tracking).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Average progress dropped and nobody did anything.">
Content was added, or something was unpublished. Both change the denominator for
everyone.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner is at 100% but has not completed.">
Progress and enrolment status are different. Completion also depends on what the
programme counts — see
[Progress tracking](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/progress-tracking).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Learners with partial access look further ahead than they are.">
They are measured against what they hold, which is correct for them. Filter them out, or
read their figure as "of their semester" rather than "of the diploma".
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I message the at-risk learners?">
Build an audience from the same conditions and send a campaign. See
[Campaigns](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
