## The list

Newest first, searchable **by learner or certificate number**. That search is the one
you will use most: somebody sends you a number and asks whether it is genuine, and this
is where you answer. See
[Verifying certificates](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/verifying-certificates).

## Reading the trend

**Issued by Month** should follow your batches. A diploma that ends in June produces a
spike in June and July. If it does not, something in the chain is stuck.

A month with far fewer certificates than the batch that ended in it is worth
investigating — the usual causes are a release date nobody set, outstanding marking, or
learners with partial access who will never qualify. All six causes are on
[Certificate requirements](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-requirements).

## Comparing with results

Two numbers that should be close, and are not always:

- **At or above the pass mark**, from [Grades](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/grades).
- **Issued**, here.

Passing is only one of six gates a certificate has to clear. A large gap means one of
the other five is holding a group of learners back.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A batch finished and almost no certificates were issued.">
Go through the six gates. The usual answers, in order: the release date has not arrived,
some marking is outstanding so the overall basis is waiting, or those learners hold part
of the programme rather than all of it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What makes a certificate revoked?">
It has been withdrawn. An issued certificate is not withdrawn automatically when a grade
changes — a revoked one was revoked deliberately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see certificates for one programme only?">
Yes — the programme filter applies here as on the other screens.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Somebody has sent me a certificate number to check.">
Search for it here. A number that does not exist, or exists against another learner, is
your answer.
</Accordion>
