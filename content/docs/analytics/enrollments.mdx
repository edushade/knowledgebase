The commercial view: how many people are joining, when, and through what.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The figures

| Figure | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Total Enrollments** | Everyone, ever. |
| **Avg Enrollments a Month** | The steady rate. |
| **Peak Month Enrollments** | Your best month, and which one it was. |
| **Growth Rate (6m)** | The direction over six months. |

## The charts

| Chart | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Total Enrollments Over Time** | Are we growing? |
| **Enrollment Trend (6 Months)** | Have we grown recently? |
| **Enrolled and Completed by Month** | Are we delivering what we sell? |
| **Enrollment Trend by Platform** | Where do learners come from? |
| **Detailed Enrollment Data** | The rows behind the charts. |

Most trend charts cover the last 12 months whatever period is selected above, so the
shape of the year stays visible while the figures narrow.

## Reading it

**Peak month is a planning number.** If January is always your peak, that is when the
teaching load lands, when you need staff, and when the support queue grows. Plan for it
rather than discovering it.

**Enrolled against completed is the honest chart.** Two lines moving together is a
healthy academy. A widening gap means you are selling faster than you are delivering —
and the gap turns into refund requests about three months later.

**Platform tells you where to spend.** If enrolments come overwhelmingly from one place,
that is both your strength and your risk.

## A number this screen does not show

Enrolments include the ones you created yourself — bulk enrolments, migrations, staff
accounts. They are real enrolments and they are not sales.

For money, use [Revenue reports](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/revenue-reports).
For who actually turned up, use
[Learners](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/learners).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A huge spike that was not a campaign.">
Almost always a bulk enrolment or a migration. Check the enrolment list's **Enrolled
By** column for that period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Enrolments are flat but revenue grew.">
You are selling more expensive programmes, or fewer discounts went out. Both are good.
See [Revenue reports](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/revenue-reports).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see enrolments for one batch?">
Yes — filter to the programme, or open the batch's own learner list.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a cancelled enrolment still count?">
It is an enrolment with a cancelled status. Filter the enrolment list by status for the
figure you actually want.
</Accordion>
