Your learner count tells you how many people registered. This screen tells you how many
are real.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## The figures

| Figure | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active learners** | Learners active in the last 7 days. |
| **New learners** | Accounts created in the period. |
| **Hours learned** | Total time spent. |
| **Hours per active learner** | The honest version — total hours divided by the people who actually turned up. |
| **Days active** | How many days learners were present. |

**Hours per active learner** is the number to watch. Total hours grows simply because
you enrolled more people; hours per active learner grows only when the teaching is
holding attention.

## The charts

### Day by Day

Learners who studied each day, and the hours they spent. Use it to see whether a
campaign or a deadline actually moved anybody.

### Busiest Days of the Week

Average hours learned on each weekday. This is the practical one: it tells you when to
schedule a live session, and when to send an announcement so it is read.

### Most Active Learners

Ranked by hours learned in the period. Two uses — knowing who your advocates are, and
noticing who used to be on this list and is not any more.

<Callout type="info">
The screen says it itself: for learners who have **gone quiet**, this is not the list to
look at. Use [Progress](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/progress), which names the people
who are slipping rather than the ones who are doing well.
</Callout>

## The question this screen is really for

*How many of our learners are actually learning?*

Compare three numbers:

1. **Total learners** on the dashboard.
2. **Active learners** here.
3. **Never signed in**, from the enrolment list filter.

A large gap between the first and the second is normal. A large third number is a
problem you can fix — people who paid and never arrived, usually because of a
verification email or a confusing first step.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Hours learned looks far too low.">
Time is recorded while a learner is in the content. A learner who downloads everything
and reads offline registers almost nothing. Treat hours as a floor, not a measurement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Active learners dropped sharply.">
Check whether a batch finished. A cohort that ends takes its learners out of the active
count, and that is normal rather than a fault.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see one learner's detail?">
Yes — open the person and their overview shows progress, assessments completed, time
spent and active days.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does this include instructors?">
It is about learners. Colleagues' activity is on
[Instructors](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/instructors).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
