One row per programme, so you can see which are worth more of your attention and which
are quietly costing you.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## What is on it

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Enrollments by Programme** | Where your learners actually are. |
| **Completion Status Distribution** | Not started, in progress, completed — per programme. |
| **Completion and Rating Trend** | Whether a programme is getting better or worse over time. |
| **Revenue by Programme** | What each one earns. |
| **Enrollments vs Completions** | The gap between selling and delivering. |
| **Top Performing Courses** | Ranked by completion. |

Filter to **All programmes** or one, and to **Course**, **Cohort** or **Learning Path**.

## The four quadrants

Put enrolments against completion and you get four kinds of programme, each needing a
different response:

| | **High completion** | **Low completion** |
|---|---|---|
| **Many enrolments** | Your best course. Sell more of it. | The expensive problem — people are buying and not finishing. Fix this first. |
| **Few enrolments** | Good, unknown. A marketing problem, not a teaching one. | Retire it, or rebuild it. |

The top-right box is where the money is. A course that sells well and is not finished
produces refund requests, bad reviews and no certificates.

## Revenue beside completion

**Revenue by Programme** next to completion is the comparison worth making. A programme
earning well with poor completion is borrowing against its reputation; one completing
well with little revenue is probably underpriced or under-sold.

See [Revenue reports](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/revenue-reports) for the
money in detail.

## Ratings

**Completion and Rating Trend** tracks both together, which is more honest than either
alone. A course rated highly by the few who finish it is not a good course.

Ratings only exist where **Enable Rating** is on for the programme — see
[Reviews and ratings](/docs/knowledgebase/community/reviews-and-ratings).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A programme shows enrolments but no revenue.">
Those learners were enrolled by hand, or on a free price, or paid outside the platform
without the payment being recorded. See
[Manual payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/manual-payments).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cohort-based courses look worse than self-paced ones.">
Often a batch that has not finished yet — its learners are mid-course and count as in
progress. Compare batches that have ended.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A programme has no rating.">
Either **Enable Rating** is off, or nobody has reviewed it. Both are worth knowing, and
the screen cannot tell them apart — check the programme's settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I see one programme in detail?">
Open the programme and use its own Statistics tab. See
[Course statistics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/course-statistics).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
