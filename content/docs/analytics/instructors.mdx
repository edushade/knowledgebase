This screen compares colleagues: how many learners they carry, how their learners do,
and — the number nobody else reports — how long marking actually takes.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

<Callout type="info">
This screen is for academy-wide staff. Somebody whose access is limited to their own
programmes is refused it, because comparing colleagues needs the whole picture.
</Callout>

## The figures

| Figure | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Teaching staff** | How many people are teaching. |
| **Programmes without staff** | Programmes with nobody attached. Usually a mistake. |
| **Waiting to be marked** | Submissions sitting in the queue right now. |
| **Typical time to mark** | How long work usually waits. |
| **Longest wait** | The oldest thing still waiting. |
| **Marked** | How much has been marked in the period. |

## The two views

- **By Person** — each colleague, their programmes, their learners, their marking.
- **By Programme** — each programme, and who is on it.

**By Programme** is the one that catches problems: a programme with no staff has nobody
answering its learners, and nobody will tell you.

## Marking turnaround is the useful number

**Typical time to mark** and **Longest wait** are the two figures worth acting on.

A learner waiting three weeks for an essay has usually stopped working by the time it
comes back. If the longest wait is measured in weeks, that is not a person being slow —
it is a queue nobody owns.

Two practical responses:

1. **Share the load.** Add a second marker to the programme — see
   [Instructors and staff](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/instructors).
2. **Change the assessment.** An essay a week for forty learners is a workload decision
   disguised as a teaching decision.

## Reading colleague comparisons fairly

Resist the obvious conclusion. Two things make one instructor's figures look worse:

- **Harder subjects.** A maths course and an induction course do not produce the same
  grades.
- **More written assessment.** Marking essays takes longer than a queue of quizzes, and
  it is not the instructor's choice.

Compare an instructor against the same programme last term, not against a colleague
teaching something else.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="I cannot open this screen.">
It needs academy-wide access to people. Someone limited to their own programmes is
refused it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A programme shows no staff but somebody teaches it.">
They are not on its instructor or staff list. Being the person who built a course does
not attach them to it — see
[Instructors and staff](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/instructors).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Waiting to be marked looks wrong.">
It counts submissions needing a person. Auto-marked quizzes never appear, however many
have been sat.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I see what is actually waiting?">
<Path>Grading > Assignment Submissions</Path> — everything waiting, across every
programme. See
[Assignment submissions](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/assignment-submissions).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
