Everyone who uses your platform has an account. This page explains how people sign up,
the different ways they can sign in, and what to do about a forgotten or changed
password.

## Creating an account

To sign up, a person provides:

- A **name**
- An **email address and/or a phone number**
- A **password**

Once they sign up, their account starts in **pending verification** — it exists, but they
need to confirm their email or phone before they can sign in. See
[Verification](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/verification).

<Callout type="info">
When **you** create an account for someone from the admin area, it is **active right
away** — no verification step needed. See
[People & roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).
</Callout>

## Ways to sign in

People can sign in however suits them:

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Password** | Enter an email, username, or phone number, plus the password. |
| **One-time code (OTP)** | Get a short code by email or text and enter it — no password needed. |
| **Social sign-in** | Sign in with a **Google, Facebook, Apple, or GitHub** account. |

### Social sign-in

Signing in with Google, Facebook, Apple, or GitHub is fast and skips passwords. A few
things to know:

- The **first time** someone signs in this way, an account is created for them and it is
  **active immediately**.
- People can **link** more than one of these to the same account, so they can sign in
  with whichever they prefer.
- They can also **unlink** one later from their profile.

## Account statuses

Every account is in one of these states:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending verification** | Just signed up; email or phone not confirmed yet. |
| **Active** | Can sign in and use the platform normally. |
| **Inactive** | Deactivated by an admin; cannot sign in until reactivated. |
| **Suspended** | Temporarily blocked; access is paused. |
| **Deleted** | Removed and hidden, though the record is kept for a while before final cleanup. |

## Passwords

### Forgot password

If someone can't remember their password:

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email or phone on the account.
3. They get a reset link (or code) and choose a new password.

### Change password

To change a password while signed in:

1. Go to **Profile → Security** (or **Settings**).
2. Enter the **current password**, then the new one.
3. Click **Save**.

The new password must follow your platform's
[password rules](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/your-platform-settings#password-rules).

<Callout type="info">
Someone who only ever used social sign-in may not have a password yet. They can still
use **Forgot password** to set one, or simply keep using their Google, Facebook, Apple,
or GitHub sign-in.
</Callout>
