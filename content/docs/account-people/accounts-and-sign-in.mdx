Every sign-in setting lives in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Security &
Compliance**, and learners and staff are configured **separately**. That separation is
the useful part: you can be strict with the people who can change grades and gentle with
the people who are trying to watch a video on a phone.

## Registration

| Setting | What it decides |
|---|---|
| **Self-registration** | Whether people can sign themselves up at all. |
| **Learners can register with** | Which identifiers are accepted — email, phone. |
| **Require email verification** | Whether an email must be confirmed. |
| **Require phone verification** | Whether a phone must be confirmed. |

<Callout type="info">
**Allow one identifier, not both.** An academy that accepts email *and* phone will
accumulate [duplicate accounts](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/merging-accounts) —
the same learner, registered twice, with their work split across two records.
</Callout>

## Signing in

Set separately for **Learner sign-in** and **Staff sign-in**:

| Setting | What it decides |
|---|---|
| **Sign in with a password using** | Which identifiers work with a password. |
| **Sign in with a one-time code sent to** | Where a code can be sent. |
| **Sign in with** | Which social providers are offered. |
| **Password minimum length** | How long a password must be. |
| **Password complexity** | What it must contain. |
| **Two-factor authentication** | Whether a second step is required. |
| **Idle timeout (minutes)** | How long before an idle session ends. |
| **Maximum concurrent sessions** | How many devices at once. |

### A sensible starting point

| | **Learners** | **Staff** |
|---|---|---|
| Password length | 8 | 12 |
| Two-factor | Optional | **Required** |
| Idle timeout | Generous | Short |
| Concurrent sessions | 2–3 | 2 |

**Maximum concurrent sessions** is also your account-sharing control. One account on
five devices is usually five people.

## One-time codes

A code sent to an email address or a phone, instead of a password. In a market where
most people are on a phone and few keep a password manager, it removes the single
biggest reason people never come back: they forgot the password.

## Social sign-in

Google, Facebook, Apple and GitHub can be offered, once connected in
<Path>Settings > Integrations</Path>. See
[Integrations](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/integrations).

It raises completed registrations. It also means the learner's name comes from that
provider — which matters if names go on certificates. See **Locked Profile Fields** in
[the Control Panel](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/control-panel).

## What a learner manages themselves

From their profile settings: their **email**, **phone**, **password**, **two-factor**,
**devices** and **sessions**. See
[Profiles and devices](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/profiles-and-devices).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner cannot sign in.">
Check in order: is their account **Active**; is the identifier they are using one you
allow; is verification outstanding; and are they at the concurrent-session limit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Nobody can register.">
**Self-registration** is off, or the identifier they are trying is not allowed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Learners are being signed out while watching a video.">
The idle timeout is too short, or the concurrent-session limit is too low. Both are
learner-side settings you can relax without touching staff.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I require two-factor for learners?">
Rarely. It stops a meaningful number of people getting in at all. Require it for staff,
where the risk is real.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a learner change their email?">
Depending on **Locked Profile Fields**, and the change has to be verified. See
[Verification](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/verification).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
