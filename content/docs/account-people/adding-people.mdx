Everyone on your platform is a person with an account. How they got there is one of
three routes.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The three routes

| Route | When it fits |
|---|---|
| **They register themselves** | Public courses, open enrolment. |
| **You create the account** | A fixed intake, a corporate group, staff. |
| **They are migrated** | Moving from another system. |

Self-registration is a setting — **Self-registration** in
<Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Security & Compliance**. With it off,
every account is created by you.

## Creating one

<Path>People > All Users</Path>, then **Create User**.

### Who they are

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Their full name. |
| **Display name** | "The name as learners should read it." |
| **Username** | Optional. |
| **Email**, **Phone** | At least one — and it is the identifier they will sign in with. |
| **Password** | Their first one. |
| **Date of birth**, **Gender** | Where you record them. |
| **Avatar** | Their picture. |

### What they are

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Learner** | They can enrol and take courses. |
| **Instructor** | They can be put on a programme as an instructor, and they get **My Programmes**. |
| **Admin** | They are staff. |
| **Roles** | What they are actually allowed to do. See below. |

<Callout type="warn">
**Being an instructor and being allowed to do instructor things are two different
settings.** The **Instructor** mark makes somebody eligible to teach; their **role**
decides what they can open and change. Setting one without the other is the usual cause
of "they say they cannot see their course".
</Callout>

### Where and how they read

**Country**, **State**, **City**, **Address**, **Continent**, **Locale**, **Timezone**.
Locale and timezone are worth getting right: they decide what language the learner reads
and what time a deadline looks like to them.

### Their professional details

**Designation**, **Profession**, **Expertise**, **Bio**, **Professional summary**,
**Social links** — these appear where an instructor is introduced, so they matter most
for teaching staff.

### Their state

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Active, Inactive, Suspended, Pending Verification. |
| **Email verified** / **Phone verified** | Whether they have confirmed it. |

## Adding many people

There is no spreadsheet import on the people screen. For a batch of learners, the
practical route is to let them register — restricted to one identifier, with
verification on — and then
[enrol them in bulk](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/admin-and-bulk-enrollment).

For a migration from another system, that is a conversation with support rather than a
screen.

## Before you create an account

**Search first.** The people list searches name, email and phone. An academy that
creates accounts without checking accumulates
[duplicates](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/merging-accounts), and duplicates are
far harder to fix than to avoid.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Should I create accounts or let learners register?">
Let them register where the course is sold publicly — it is less work and the details
are theirs. Create accounts for a fixed intake, for staff, and wherever you already hold
the list.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A new instructor cannot see anything.">
Three things to check: are they marked as an **Instructor**, do they hold a role that
allows it, and are they actually on the programme's instructor or staff list. All three
are needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I create an account without an email address?">
With a phone number instead, where your academy allows registering with one. They need
something to sign in with.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does creating an account notify the person?">
Not by itself. Tell them, and tell them what to sign in with.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Inactive and Suspended?">
**Inactive** is dormant. **Suspended** is deliberately blocked. Both stop them using the
platform; the difference is what it says to the next person who reads the record.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
