title: "Support Login"
description: "Seeing the platform exactly as one of your learners does — the two modes, and which one to use."
---

A learner says a lesson will not open. You look at the course and it is fine. The only
way to settle it is to see what they see.

Support login does that: you view the platform as them, from their own account.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Starting

From the person's record, open **Impersonation** and start. Everything from then on is
their view — their courses, their progress, their locked content.

## The two modes

| Mode | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Read-only** | Look at everything. Change nothing. |
| **Full** | Act as them, including saving. |

<Callout type="warn">
**Use read-only.** It answers almost every support question, and it cannot damage
anything. Full mode does things to a learner's record in their name, with no way for
them to tell afterwards that it was not them.
</Callout>

In read-only mode, anything that would save is refused, and you are told: *"This action
is not allowed in impersonation mode — you are viewing this account as read-only. Stop
impersonation to make changes."*

## Stopping

Stop it as soon as you have the answer, and you are returned to your own session.

## What it is good for

| Question | Why support login settles it |
|---|---|
| "I cannot see the lesson" | You see exactly what they see, with every rule applied. |
| "The video will not play" | Their view, not your admin preview. |
| "My quiz score is wrong" | Their result page, not your grading screen. |
| "The certificate is not there" | What they actually have. |

It is particularly good for the four access rules that explain nothing — partial access,
eligibility rules, unpublished content and an inactive enrolment all look identical from
the learner's side. See
[How access works together](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/how-access-works-together).

## Using it responsibly

This is somebody's account. Three habits:

1. **Read-only unless you genuinely need to act.**
2. **Have a reason** — a support request, a fault you are chasing.
3. **Keep it short.** Get the answer and stop.

Support login is recorded, as admin actions are — see
[Activity and audit logs](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/activity-history). That is
a protection for your team as much as for the learner.

Who may do it at all is a permission. Keep that list short.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="I cannot save anything while impersonating.">
You are in read-only mode, which is the right default. Stop, and make the change from
your own account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I impersonate another admin?">
What you can do is governed by your role. In general, keep this for supporting learners.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the learner know?">
They are not notified. The action is recorded in your audit log.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I was signed out when I stopped.">
That happens when there is no admin session to return to. Sign in again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a safer way to check what a learner sees?">
Their record already shows their enrolments, scopes, progress and grades, which answers
many questions without entering their account at all. Try that first.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
