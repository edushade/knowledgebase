This is where you describe your platform's own identity and basics. These settings shape
what people see across your site, how they reach you, and the rules new accounts must
follow.

## What you can set

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Branding** | Your name, logo, and the basic look that ties your platform together. |
| **Contact details** | The email, phone, and address people use to reach you. |
| **Sign-in page customization** | An image shown on your sign-in, registration, and verification pages. |
| **Password rules** | How strong a password must be (see below). |

## Branding and contact details

Use branding to make the platform feel like yours — your name and logo appear where
people expect them. Contact details give learners a clear way to reach you for help.

1. Go to **Admin → Settings**.
2. Update your name, logo, and contact information.
3. Click **Save**.

<Callout type="info">
Your logo and branding also flow into other places, such as emails and certificates, so
keep them current.
</Callout>

## Sign-in page customization

You can add an image that appears on the pages where people sign in, register, and
confirm their email or phone. This is a simple way to make a strong first impression and
keep those pages on-brand.

1. Go to **Admin → Settings**.
2. Find the **Sign-in page** (or **Login page**) section.
3. Upload the image you want to show.
4. Click **Save**.

### What the learner sees

When someone opens your sign-in, registration, or verification page, they see your
chosen image alongside the form. It is the same friendly, branded experience whether they
are creating a new account or coming back.

## Password rules

You decide how strong passwords must be on your platform. Two settings are available:

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Minimum length** | The shortest a password is allowed to be. |
| **Complexity requirement** (optional) | When turned on, passwords must mix different character types (such as letters, numbers, and symbols). |

1. Go to **Admin → Settings**.
2. Open the **Password rules** section.
3. Set a minimum length, and turn the complexity requirement on or off.
4. Click **Save**.

These rules apply when anyone sets or changes a password — at sign-up, when resetting a
forgotten password, or when changing it from their profile.

<Callout type="info">
A longer minimum length is the single biggest improvement you can make to account
safety. Most teams use at least 8 characters.
</Callout>
