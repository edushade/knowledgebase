This is the part of your academy that learners see before they see anything else: its
name, its logo, its web address.

All of it is in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Platform Settings**.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Your academy

| Setting | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The browser tab, emails, certificates, everywhere. |
| **Description** | Search results and previews. |
| **Logos** | The top of every screen. Light and dark versions, so it works in both themes. |
| **Social Share Image** | The picture shown when somebody shares a link to your site. |

The social share image is the one everybody forgets, and it is the one a stranger sees
first when a learner shares your course in a group chat.

## Your address on the web

| Setting | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Subdomain** | Your address under the platform's own domain. |
| **Custom domain** | Your own domain, once it is pointed here. |

A custom domain is worth setting up before you start selling: links shared under the
old address keep working less well afterwards, and your learners' trust is attached to
the address they first saw.

## Your organisation

**Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Address**, **City**, **State**, **ZIP Code**,
**Country**.

These are the legal and contact details. They appear on documents and in emails, and the
email is what learners are told to write to when something goes wrong — so it has to be
an address somebody actually reads.

## Version number

**Show the version number** puts the build version in the corner. Useful while you are
testing, unnecessary once you are live.

## What makes a platform feel like yours

In rough order of what learners notice:

1. **The logo**, in both light and dark.
2. **Your own domain.**
3. **The academy name**, used consistently — on certificates as well as on screen.
4. **A social share image** that is not a default.
5. **Website colours and type** that match the rest of your brand — see
   [Templates and themes](/docs/knowledgebase/website/templates-and-themes).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="The logo looks wrong in dark mode.">
There are two logo slots. Upload a version for each — a dark logo on a dark background
disappears.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I changed the name but certificates still show the old one.">
Certificates already issued keep the design and details they were issued with. New ones
pick up the change.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use a custom domain?">
Yes, pointed at your platform. Set it up before you start selling.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do website colours and fonts live?">
In your website's theme, not here. See
[Templates and themes](/docs/knowledgebase/website/templates-and-themes).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the organisation address appear to learners?">
It appears where contact details belong — emails, documents, your site's footer. Keep it
accurate.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
