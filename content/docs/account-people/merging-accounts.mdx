---
title: "Duplicate Accounts"
description: "One learner, two accounts — how it happens, how to stop it, and what can be done once it has."
---

A learner registers with their phone number in January, forgets, and registers with
their email in June. Now they have two accounts, two sets of progress, and marks split
across both.

It is one of the most common data problems an academy has, and almost all of it is
preventable.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## How it happens

| Cause | What to do about it |
|---|---|
| They registered with email once and phone the next time | Decide on **one** identifier and allow only that. |
| They used Google sign-in once and a password the next | Same — pick one way in and say so. |
| Somebody enrolled them manually while they also signed up | Check before creating an account. |
| A typo in an email address | Verification catches this. |

## Preventing it

Four settings, all in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Security &
Compliance**:

1. **Learners can register with** — restrict it to one identifier. An academy that
   accepts both email and phone will accumulate duplicates, however careful everyone is.
2. **Require email verification** or **phone verification** — an unverified typo never
   becomes a working account.
3. **Sign in with** — fewer routes in means fewer identities.
4. **Self-registration** — off, and every account is created by you, which removes the
   problem entirely for an institution with a fixed intake.

Before you enrol anybody manually, search for them first. The people list searches name,
email and phone.

## Finding duplicates you already have

The people list is where you look:

- Sort by name and read for near-matches.
- Search a surname and see what comes back.
- Filter by **Never Logged In** — an abandoned duplicate usually never signed in.

Two accounts with the same name, one with enrolments and one without, is the common
shape.

## Once there are two

<Callout type="warn">
**There is no merge button in the admin screens.** Two accounts cannot be combined from
the people list, and deleting one deletes its progress, marks and certificates with it.
</Callout>

What you can do yourself:

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| The duplicate was never used | Delete it. Nothing is lost. |
| The duplicate has an enrolment but no real work | Enrol them on the account they actually use, then delete the duplicate. |
| Both accounts hold real progress and marks | Do not delete either. Decide which account they will keep using, enrol them there, and contact support about combining the two. |

For the third case, write down what you have before anybody does anything: the two
accounts, which is being kept, and what each holds. That list is what makes a clean
outcome possible.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I just move the enrolment to the other account?">
No. An enrolment belongs to a person. What you can do is enrol them again on the account
they are keeping — which starts them fresh unless the work is carried over.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner has two certificates, one on each account.">
They stand, on the accounts that earned them. Decide which account the learner keeps and
reissue on it if you need to.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change an account's email instead of merging?">
Yes, and it is often the simplest answer. Change the kept account's email to the one
they now use, and delete the unused duplicate — assuming it holds nothing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I stop this happening again?">
Allow one identifier, require verification, and search before you create. Those three
remove nearly all of it.
</Accordion>
