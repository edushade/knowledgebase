One idea underlies this whole section: **what somebody is** and **what they may do** are
two different settings.

Marking a colleague as an instructor makes them eligible to teach. Their **role** decides
what they can actually open and change. Setting one without the other is the commonest
reason a new team member says "I cannot see anything".

## Three decisions worth making early

| Decision | Why early |
|---|---|
| **One identifier for registration** | Allowing email *and* phone produces duplicate accounts you cannot merge from the admin. |
| **Lock the name field** | A learner who can edit their own name can edit the name on their certificate. |
| **Narrow roles** | Widening a role later is easy. Discovering everyone could delete courses is not. |
