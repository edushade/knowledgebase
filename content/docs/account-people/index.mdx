This section covers the people who use your platform and the settings that keep it
running smoothly. That includes how everyone signs in, the profiles they keep, the team
members who help you run things, and the records that let you see exactly what happened
and when.

## Who uses your platform

Anyone on your platform can hold one or more of three jobs at the same time:

| Person type | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Learner** (student) | Takes your courses, paths, and cohorts. |
| **Instructor** (teacher) | Creates content and teaches. |
| **Admin** | Manages the platform — people, settings, and everything else. |

One person can be more than one of these. For example, a popular instructor can also be a
learner in someone else's course, and an admin can teach too.
