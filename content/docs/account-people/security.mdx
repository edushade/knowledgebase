Your platform includes built-in protection to keep accounts safe, along with a clean way
to delete an account and a view of how your audience is growing.

## Protection against repeated failed sign-ins

To stop someone guessing a password, your platform watches for repeated wrong attempts:

- **Too many wrong tries temporarily locks the account.** The person simply waits and
  tries again later.
- **The wait grows** with each new round of failures, so guessing becomes slower and
  slower.
- **Abusive sources are blocked** for a while when they show clearly bad behavior.
- **A successful sign-in clears it** — once the right password (or code) goes in, things
  return to normal.

This all happens automatically. There's nothing to switch on.

<Callout type="info">
If a learner says they're locked out, the usual fix is simple: wait a little, then try
again — or use [Forgot password](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/accounts-and-sign-in#forgot-password)
to reset it. A correct sign-in clears the lock.
</Callout>

## Account deletion

A person can delete their own account whenever they choose.

- They'll be asked to **confirm with their password**. (If they only ever used social
  sign-in and never set a password, no password is needed.)
- **All of their signed-in devices are signed out immediately.**
- The account moves to a **deleted** state — hidden from view, with the record kept for a
  short while before final cleanup.

See the [account statuses](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/accounts-and-sign-in#account-statuses)
for how "deleted" fits with the other states.

<Callout type="warn">
Deletion ends access right away. If someone is unsure, suggest they step away first —
deactivating or simply not signing in is often enough, without losing their account.
</Callout>

## Growth stats

As an admin, you can see how your audience is growing — **totals** and **trends** over
time. It's a quick way to track how many people are joining and how that's changing.

You'll find these in your admin dashboards alongside your other reports.
