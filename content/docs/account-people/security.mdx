Security here is mostly four things: who can sign in, from how many places, with what,
and what you can see afterwards.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Two-factor authentication

A second step at sign-in. Set separately for learners and staff in
<Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Security & Compliance**.

**Require it for staff.** Anyone who can change a grade, issue a certificate or refund a
payment should have it. For learners it is usually more friction than it is worth.

If somebody loses their second factor, you can **reset two-factor authentication** from
their record. The screen asks you to confirm, because resetting it removes their
protection until they set it up again.

## Sessions

Each person's record lists their sessions: the **current session** — "the session you
are using now" — and the others, which can all be ended at once.

**Maximum concurrent sessions** limits how many devices an account can be on. It is
genuinely a security control and genuinely an account-sharing control: one login on
five devices is usually five people.

**Idle timeout** ends a session that has been left. Short for staff, generous for
learners.

## Failed sign-ins and locked accounts

A person's record shows whether they are **locked**, and how many failed sign-in
attempts they have had. The people list can be filtered by both — **Is Locked**,
**Min/Max Failed Login Attempts** — which turns "is somebody attacking us?" into a
filter.

A spread of failed attempts across many accounts is a different problem from one
account with forty.

## What is recorded

| Log | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Audit Logs** | Admin actions, with actor, result and IP address. |
| **Activity Logs** | What learners did. |
| **Data access logs** | Who looked at what, where you switch it on. |

See [Activity and audit logs](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/activity-history).

## Account states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Normal. |
| **Inactive** | Dormant. |
| **Suspended** | Deliberately blocked. |
| **Pending Verification** | Waiting to confirm an email or phone. |

Suspending is the right answer to a problem account. Deleting takes their work with it.

## A workable baseline

1. **Two-factor required for staff.**
2. **Staff passwords at 12 characters** with complexity; learners at 8.
3. **Short idle timeout for staff**, generous for learners.
4. **Concurrent sessions capped** for both.
5. **All three logs on**, with a retention period you have actually thought about.
6. **Roles kept narrow** — see [People and roles](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/people-and-roles).

The sixth does more for your security than the first five together. Most damage to an
academy's data is done by somebody who was allowed to do it.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A staff member is locked out.">
Check their account state and failed attempts on their record. If they have lost their
second factor, reset two-factor from there.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We think an account is shared.">
Look at its sessions and lower **Maximum concurrent sessions**. The sessions list shows
how many places it is signed in from.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Someone left the organisation.">
Suspend the account, remove their roles, and end their other sessions. Do not delete it
while it is attached to records you need.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I force everybody to sign in again?">
Per person, by ending their other sessions. There is no single academy-wide sign-out.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should learners have two-factor?">
Usually not required — offer it. The accounts worth protecting with it are the ones that
can change things.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
