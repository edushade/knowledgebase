Verification is the step where somebody proves the address or number they gave you is
theirs. It is a small thing that prevents a large one: an academy full of accounts
nobody can reach.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Turning it on

<Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Security & Compliance → Registration**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Require email verification** | An email must be confirmed. |
| **Require phone verification** | A phone must be confirmed. |

An account waiting on either reads **Pending Verification**.

## Why it is worth the friction

| Without verification | With it |
|---|---|
| A typo becomes a working account you can never reach | The typo never gets in |
| Certificates go to addresses that bounce | They reach somebody |
| Duplicate accounts from mistyped addresses | Far fewer |
| No way to reset a password | There is one |

The cost is real — some people stop at the verification step — but an unreachable
learner is worse than a lost one.

## When nothing arrives

The most common support request in any academy. Work down this list:

1. **Check your sending service is actually working.** Look at
   [Delivery and analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/delivery-and-analytics)
   and the delivery logs before blaming the learner. A whole academy's worth of
   "no email" is almost always your side.
2. **Spam folder.** Say so when you ask them to check.
3. **The address itself.** One wrong character is invisible to the person who typed it;
   read it back to them.
4. **Resend.**
5. **Verify them yourself.** From their record, where it is genuinely stuck.

<Callout type="warn">
Before an intake, send yourself a verification email from a fresh account. Discovering
on registration day that your sending service is misconfigured costs you the intake.
</Callout>

## Verifying somebody yourself

A person's record shows **Email verified** and **Phone verified**, and both can be set
by hand. Use it when you have confirmed their identity another way — not as a routine
way around a sending problem you have not fixed.

## Changing a verified address

A learner changing their email has to verify the new one. Whether they can change it at
all depends on **Locked Profile Fields** — see
[the Control Panel](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/control-panel).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Nobody is receiving verification emails.">
Your sending service, not your learners. Check the delivery logs — see
[Channels](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/channels).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch verification off?">
Yes, and you will collect unreachable accounts and duplicates. If you do, make sure you
have another way to confirm who somebody is.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner changed their phone number and cannot sign in.">
They need to verify the new one. If they cannot receive the code, update and verify it
from their record once you are satisfied it is them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does social sign-in need verification?">
The provider has already confirmed the address, so it arrives verified.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to an account stuck at Pending Verification?">
It depends on your settings — unused accounts can be removed on a schedule. See
[Privacy and consent](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/privacy-and-consent).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
