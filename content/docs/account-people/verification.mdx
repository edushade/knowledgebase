Verification is how a person proves an email or phone number is really theirs. It happens
right after sign-up and any time someone changes their email or phone.

## How it works

1. The person receives a short **one-time code** by email or text.
2. They enter the code on the verification page.
3. Once it matches, that email or phone is confirmed.

Either an **email or a phone number** can satisfy verification — a person doesn't have to
confirm both to get started.

## Good to know

| Detail | What to expect |
|---|---|
| **Code expiry** | A code is valid for about **15 minutes**. After that, request a new one. |
| **Attempts** | There are a **few tries** per code. Too many wrong entries and the person needs a fresh code. |
| **Resend** | If a code doesn't arrive or expires, click **Resend** to get a new one. |

<Callout type="info">
If a code isn't arriving by email, ask the person to check their spam or promotions
folder. For text messages, make sure the phone number was entered correctly, including
the country code.
</Callout>

## After verification

Once an email or phone is confirmed, a **pending** account becomes **active** and the
person can sign in normally. See
[Accounts & sign-in](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/accounts-and-sign-in).
