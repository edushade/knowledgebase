Everything here lives in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Security &
Compliance → Data Privacy**, with the records themselves under
<Path>Audit > Consent Records</Path>.

<Callout type="warn">
Several of these settings **delete data on a schedule**. Read what each one does before
switching it on — there is nothing to undo afterwards.
</Callout>

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The master switch

**GDPR compliance** turns on the stricter handling: consent prompts, the deletion rules,
the representative details. You do not have to be in Europe to want it — it is a
reasonable default for any academy holding learners' personal details.

## Keeping and deleting

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Keep personal data in logs for (days)** | How long personal details stay in your logs. |
| **Delete inactive learner accounts after (days)** | Accounts dormant for longer are removed. |
| **Remove accounts that were never used** | Clears accounts that signed up and never arrived. |
| **Notice before removal (days)** / **Reminder before removal (days)** | Warnings sent before an account goes. |
| **Email only addresses at well-known providers** | Only warn people whose address is at a provider likely to deliver it. |

**Delete inactive learner accounts** is the one to be careful with. A learner who
finished a diploma three years ago and comes back for their certificate is "inactive".
Set the window long enough for that, and make sure the notice and reminder give real
warning.

## Age

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Minimum age to sign up** | Below this, no account. |
| **Parent confirms sign-ups under (age)** | Between the two, a parent's email is asked for and their agreement collected. |

That second setting is what puts the date-of-birth and parent's email questions on
[checkout](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/checkout). The resulting state —
**Awaiting parent**, **Allowed** or **Declined** — is on the consent record.

## Consent

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ask about news and offers at sign-up** | A marketing consent question at registration. |
| **Cookie consent banner** | Shows a banner on your website. |
| **Who is asked** | Which visitors see it. |

The cookie banner governs the scripts in **Analytics & Tracking**. Without consent, they
do not run — which is the point, and also why your analytics may look thinner after you
switch it on.

## The legal pages

| Setting | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Terms of service page** | Where your terms live. |
| **Privacy policy page** | Where your privacy policy lives. |
| **Cookie policy page** | Where your cookie policy lives. |
| **Terms last changed** | A version marker. Change it when the terms change and people are asked to agree again. |

Write the pages on your website, then point these settings at them. See
[Pages and blocks](/docs/knowledgebase/website/pages-and-blocks).

## Who is responsible

| Setting | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Data protection officer** | Their contact details. |
| **Representative in the EU** / **Representative in the UK** | Where you need one. |

These appear where a learner or a regulator looks for them. Fill them in if you hold
data on people in those places.

## What you can do for one person

From a person's record:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Export their data** | Produces what you hold on them. |
| **Restrict use of their data** | Limits processing, with a note of why — for example, "disputes the address on file, asked by email on 6 October". |
| **Erase their data** | Closes the account and erases their personal data everywhere. It cannot be undone, and asks you to type **ERASE** to confirm. |

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Accounts are being deleted and we did not expect it.">
**Delete inactive learner accounts after (days)** or **Remove accounts that were never
used** is on. Switch it off, then decide on a window you are comfortable with.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does erasing somebody remove their certificates?">
It closes the account and erases their personal data. Think about what you need to keep
for your own records — and export it — before you erase.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner under the age limit cannot sign up.">
That is **Minimum age to sign up** working. Between the minimum and the parental-consent
age, they can sign up with a parent's agreement; below the minimum, not at all.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The parent never confirmed.">
Their consent record reads **Awaiting parent**. The learner's access depends on your
rules; the record tells you where they are.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Our analytics dropped after enabling the cookie banner.">
Expected. Visitors who decline are not tracked. Your learning analytics are unaffected —
those are not cookie-based.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
