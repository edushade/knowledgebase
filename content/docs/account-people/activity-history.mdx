title: "Activity History"
description: "See who did what and when across your platform, plus a separate record of sensitive changes with before-and-after values."
---

Your platform keeps a clear record of what happens, so you can answer questions like "who
changed this?" or "when did I last sign in?". There are two records, both shown as
**activity history**.

## Your activity history

This is a running list of actions on an account — for example:

- Signing in
- Viewing a course
- Submitting an assignment
- Running a search

Each entry shows **what happened**, the **time**, the **device or browser**, and a
**rough location**.

| Who | What they can see |
|---|---|
| **Learners** | Their own activity history, from their account. |
| **Admins** | Everyone's activity, with filters by **person, date, area, action, and status**. |

### What the learner sees

A learner can open their own activity history to review recent sign-ins and what they've
been doing. It's a reassuring way for them to confirm their account is only being used by
them.

## Change history

This is a separate, admin-only record of **sensitive changes** — for example, a price
change. For each change it shows the **before-and-after values** and **who made the
change**.

- **Admin-only** — learners and instructors don't see this.
- **Filterable** — narrow it down to find exactly the change you need.
- **Exportable** — download it for your records or a review.

<Callout type="info">
Change history is the place to look when you need accountability: it tells you not just
that something changed, but what it was before, what it became, and who did it.
</Callout>

## Support login on the record

Whenever an admin uses [support login](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/support-login)
to act as another person, that session is recorded here too — so helping someone is
always traceable.

## Automatic cleanup

Older records are tidied up automatically after a set period. You can choose how long to
keep them, balancing how far back you want to look against keeping things tidy.
