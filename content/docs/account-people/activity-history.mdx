title: "Activity and Audit Logs"
description: "Three records of what happened on your platform — who changed what, what learners did, and what consent was given."
---

Three logs sit under <Path>Audit</Path>, and they answer three different questions.

| Log | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Audit Logs** | What did my team change? |
| **Activity Logs** | What did this learner do? |
| **Consent Records** | What did this person agree to, and when? |

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Audit Logs

Changes made by people with access to the admin. Each entry carries the **Actor**, the
**Action**, the **Module**, the **Resource** it touched, the **Result**, the time, and
the **IP address**.

Entries are grouped by area so you can narrow to where you are looking:

Academy settings · Accounts and sign-in · Billing · Bookings · Categories, tags and
privacy · Reviews and reactions · Files · Imports · Learning · Notices ·
Notifications · Products · Quizzes · Website

This is the log you open when a grade changed and nobody remembers changing it, or when
a course's content is not what it was last week.

## Activity Logs

What learners did — signing in, opening content, submitting work. It is also on each
person's own record, which is usually where you want it: "what has this learner actually
been doing?" rather than "what happened on Tuesday?".

<Callout type="warn">
**Activity logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted.** The retention period is
yours to set in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Security & Compliance →
Audit Logs**, and once a day has passed out of that window it is gone. If an
investigation might need older activity, export it before it ages out.
</Callout>

## Consent Records

What each person agreed to and when — the terms, the privacy policy, marketing consent,
cookies. Where a young learner needs a parent's agreement, the record shows that too:
**Awaiting parent**, **Allowed** or **Declined**.

This is the log that answers a regulator or a complaint, which is why it is worth
checking the data is actually being captured before you need it. See
[Privacy and consent](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/privacy-and-consent).

## What is recorded, and for how long

Four settings in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **Security & Compliance →
Audit Logs**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Track admin actions** | Records what your team changes. |
| **User activity logs** | Records what learners do. |
| **Data access logs** | Records who looked at what. |
| **Keep admin audit logs for (days)** / **Keep user activity logs for (days)** | How long each is kept. |

Longer retention means more to search and more personal data held. Both matter; the
balance is yours.

## Using the logs well

- **Narrow by module first.** "Billing, last 7 days" finds a thing; the unfiltered log
  does not.
- **Follow the actor, not the resource**, when you are checking a person rather than an
  incident.
- **Export before you need it.** The log you wish you had is always the one that aged
  out last month.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A grade changed and nobody knows who changed it.">
Audit Logs, filtered to **Learning**, around the date. The actor is on the entry.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The log does not go back far enough.">
It has passed its retention period. Raise the retention setting for the future — nothing
brings back what has already been deleted.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner says they never agreed to marketing.">
Consent Records has what they agreed to and when.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can learners see their own activity?">
Yes — their own activity log is in their profile settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch logging off?">
Each of the three can be switched off. Think hard before you do: a log you did not keep
is an answer you cannot give.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
