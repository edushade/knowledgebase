As an admin, you have a single place to manage everyone on your platform — learners,
instructors, and fellow admins. You can also set up **roles** that control what each team
member is allowed to do.

## Managing people

Go to **Admin → Users** to see everyone on your platform. From there you can search,
filter, view, create, edit, and remove people.

### Finding people

A rich set of filters helps you find exactly who you're looking for:

| Filter by | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Active, pending, inactive, suspended. |
| **Type** | Learner, instructor, admin. |
| **Verification** | Whether email or phone is confirmed. |
| **Location** | Country, state, or city. |
| **Dates** | When they joined or were last active. |

### Creating and editing people

1. Go to **Admin → Users**.
2. Click **New** to add a person, or open someone and click **Edit**.
3. Fill in their details and **Save**.

<Callout type="info">
Accounts you create from the admin area are **active immediately** — the person can sign
in right away without a verification step.
</Callout>

### Friendly IDs

Every person gets an easy-to-read ID based on their type — for example **STU-001** for a
student or **INS-002** for an instructor. These make people simple to reference in
support, reports, and conversations.

### Bulk actions

When you have many people to handle at once:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Export to spreadsheet (CSV)** | Download a list of the selected (or filtered) people for your records or reporting. |
| **Bulk delete** | Remove many people at once. |

## Roles & permissions

Roles let you decide **what each team member is allowed to do**. Instead of setting this
person by person, you build a role once and assign it to anyone who should have that
level of access.

A few examples of roles you might create:

- **Course Admin** — can manage courses and content, but not billing.
- **Moderator** — can manage discussions and comments only.
- **Support** — can view people and help them, but not change settings.

### How roles work

1. **Create a role** and give it a clear name.
2. **Choose what it can do** — pick the allowed actions, neatly grouped by area (for
   example, people, courses, payments).
3. **Assign the role** to the right team members.

That's it — anyone with that role can do exactly what the role allows, and nothing more.

### Cloning a role

Need a role that's almost the same as one you already have? **Clone** it to copy all its
allowed actions, then adjust the copy. This saves setting everything up from scratch.

### Individual exceptions

Sometimes one person needs a little more (or less) than their role gives. You can grant a
**one-off exception** to a specific person — handy for a temporary need without creating a
whole new role.

<Callout type="info">
Keep it simple: most teams need only a handful of roles. Start with the few that match
how your team actually works, and add more only when a real need comes up.
</Callout>

## What people see

Team members only see the parts of the admin area their role allows. Buttons and menus
they can't use are simply hidden, so the platform stays clean and uncluttered for
everyone.
