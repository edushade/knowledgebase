Each person has a profile they can fill in, plus a list of the devices currently signed
in to their account. This page covers both.

## Editing a profile

Anyone can update their own profile from **Profile → Edit** (or **Settings**). A profile
can include:

- **Name** and **avatar** (profile picture)
- **Short bio** and a longer **summary**
- **Timezone** and **language/region** (locale)
- **Gender** and **date of birth**
- **Address**
- **Designation**, **profession**, and **expertise tags**
- **Social links**

The more complete a profile, the better — especially for instructors, whose profile
details can appear publicly next to their courses.

<Callout type="info">
Timezone matters more than it looks. It affects how dates, deadlines, and live session
times appear to that person, so encourage everyone to set it correctly.
</Callout>

## Changing an email or phone

A person can update the email address or phone number on their account. For safety, each
change is confirmed with a **one-time code** sent to the new email or phone — the change
only takes effect once that code is entered. See
[Verification](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/verification).

1. Go to **Profile → Security** (or **Settings**).
2. Enter the new email or phone.
3. Enter the code that arrives to confirm it.

## Signed-in devices

Everyone can see every device that's currently signed in to their account. For each one,
they'll see the **device or browser** and a **rough location**.

This is useful for spotting anything unexpected — for example, a sign-in from a place or
device they don't recognize.

### Ending a session

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **End this session** | Signs out the selected device. |
| **End all other sessions** | Signs out every device except the one being used right now. |

Ending all other sessions is the quickest fix if a phone or laptop is lost or stolen — it
locks everyone else out instantly while keeping the person signed in where they are.

1. Go to **Profile → Security** (or **Settings**) → **Devices** / **Sessions**.
2. Find the device in the list.
3. Click **End session**, or **End all other sessions** to clear them in one go.
