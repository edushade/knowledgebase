A profile is what your platform knows about a person. Some of it they fill in, some of
it you do, and some of it you should stop them changing.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Learner" />

## What a profile holds

| Group | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | Name, display name, username, email, phone, date of birth, gender, avatar. |
| **Location** | Country, state, city, address, continent, locale, timezone. |
| **Professional** | Designation, profession, expertise, bio, professional summary, social links. |
| **State** | Status, email verified, phone verified, unique ID. |

**Display name** is "the name as learners should read it" — which is not always the
legal name on the record.

**Locale** and **timezone** decide what language somebody reads and what time a deadline
looks like to them. Both are worth getting right for a learner in a different country.

## Locking fields

**Locked Profile Fields**, in <Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> under **User
Settings**, lists the fields a learner may not change themselves.

<Callout type="warn">
**Lock the name if you issue certificates.** A certificate prints the name on the
account. A learner who can edit their own name can edit the name on their certificate,
the evening before they download it.
</Callout>

Date of birth is worth locking too, where age rules apply.

## Profile completion

**Profile Completion**, in the same place, decides what a learner must fill in and when
they are asked. Used well it collects what you genuinely need; used badly it is a wall
between somebody who has paid and the course they paid for.

Ask for what you will actually use. Every extra required field loses learners at the
door.

## Devices and sessions

A person's record lists where their account is signed in: the **current session** and
the others, which can be ended together.

For a learner, the same list is in their own profile settings, so they can sign out a
phone they no longer have without contacting you.

**Maximum concurrent sessions** caps how many at once — see
[Security](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/security).

## What a learner manages themselves

From their profile settings:

| They can change | Notes |
|---|---|
| Their details | Except locked fields. |
| Email and phone | With verification. |
| Password | Their own. |
| Two-factor | Where offered. |
| Devices and sessions | Signing other devices out. |
| Notification preferences | What they hear about, and how. |
| Privacy | Their data. |
| Their account | Deletion, where you allow it. |

See [Profile and settings](/docs/knowledgebase/learner/profile-and-settings) for the
learner's own view of all this.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A learner's certificate has the wrong name.">
Correct the name on their account and reissue. Then lock the name field so it does not
happen again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A learner cannot change their own email.">
It is a locked profile field, or the change is waiting for verification.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Somebody is sharing their account.">
Look at their sessions, and lower the concurrent-session limit. The list shows how many
places the account is signed in from.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Profile completion is blocking people.">
Reduce what is required. Ask for the rest later, or not at all.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is expertise for?">
It describes an instructor — "Mathematics, Physics" — and appears where they are
introduced. It does nothing for learners.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
