<Path>Settings > Control Panel</Path> is where your academy's own rules live — its name,
how people sign in, what is kept and for how long, what learners can do to their own
accounts.

Twelve sections down the left. Most of them you will set once.

## Platform Settings

Who you are.

| Group | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Platform** | Your academy's **Name**, **Description**, logos, **Social Share Image**, **Subdomain** and **Custom domain**. |
| **Organization** | The legal details — name, email, phone, address, city, state, ZIP, country. These appear on documents and in emails. |
| **Version number** | Whether the build version shows in the corner. |

The **Organization** email is what learners are told to contact when something goes
wrong, so it needs to be an address somebody reads.

## Security & Compliance

The biggest section, and the one worth a careful read.

| Group | What it decides |
|---|---|
| **Registration** | Whether people may **self-register**; what they may register with; whether **email verification** and **phone verification** are required. |
| **Learner sign-in** / **Staff sign-in** | How each signs in — password, one-time code, social sign-in — plus password length and complexity, two-factor, idle timeout and maximum concurrent sessions. Learners and staff are set separately. |
| **Data Privacy** | Retention, deletion, legal pages, consent and age rules. See [Privacy and consent](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/privacy-and-consent). |
| **Audit Logs** | What is recorded, and for how long. |

<Callout type="warn">
**Staff sign-in should be stricter than learner sign-in.** They are separate settings for
exactly that reason — two-factor and a short idle timeout for the people who can change
grades, and something gentler for learners.
</Callout>

## Analytics & Tracking

Eleven services you can connect, each a switch and an ID: **Google Analytics**, **Google
Tag Manager**, **Facebook Pixel**, **Microsoft Clarity**, **Hotjar**, **TikTok Pixel**,
**Twitter/X Pixel**, **Plausible**, **Fathom**, **Mixpanel**, and **Custom Tracking
Scripts**.

These measure your *website*. They are nothing to do with the
[analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics) inside your academy, which measure learning.

If you ask for cookie consent, these are the scripts that consent governs.

## Media

Upload limits and media behaviour — including whether paid content may be downloaded.

## Gamification

| Group | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Leaderboard Settings** | How rankings behave. |
| **Daily Streak** | On or off. See [Learning streaks](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/learning-streaks). |

## Certificate

**Certificate Number Generation** — the pattern every certificate number is built from.
Set it before you issue anything. See
[Issuing certificates](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/issuing-certificates).

## Payments

Payment behaviour, including how long an unpaid renewal is tolerated before it counts as
lapsed.

## User Settings

What learners can do, and what is done to them.

| Group | What it decides |
|---|---|
| **Unique ID Generation Rules** | The pattern for student IDs. See [Student IDs](/docs/knowledgebase/account-people/student-ids). |
| **Deleted Account** | What happens when an account is deleted. |
| **Learner Progress Reset** | Whether learners may reset their own progress. **Off by default** — think before switching it on. |
| **Locked Profile Fields** | Which profile fields a learner may not change themselves. |
| **Learner Home** | What they land on. |
| **Reminders and Progress Reports** | The automatic nudges, and when they go out. |
| **Profile Completion** | What a learner must fill in, and when they are asked. |

### When the reminders go out

**Reminders and Progress Reports** no longer just switches the nudges on — it decides
when learners receive them.

| Setting | What it decides |
|---|---|
| **Send time** | The hour of day reminders and progress reports go out. |
| **Time zone** | The clock that send time is on. |
| **Weekly reports go out on** | The day of the week. Each report covers the 7 days before it. |
| **Language of reminders and reports** | Which language they are written in. |
| **Also send the reminder by SMS** | Sends the inactivity reminder by text as well as email. |

<Callout type="info">
**Pick a send time your learners are awake for.** A reminder that arrives at 3am is read
at 9am with forty other things, if at all. Early evening works for most academies, and
the day you choose for weekly reports sets what "the last 7 days" covers.
</Callout>

**Locked Profile Fields** matters for any academy that issues certificates: a learner who
can edit their own name can edit the name on their certificate.

## Email Editor

**Unlayer** — the editor used to design emails. See
[Message templates](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/message-templates).

## Mobile Apps

Settings for a mobile app: **Maintenance** mode, **Minimum supported version**, and
**Store review** prompts.

## Activity Completion

The academy-wide answer to "what counts as finished?" for each kind of lesson — a video
watched to some percentage, a page opened, a quiz passed. A programme can override it,
and so can a single item.

This is the default everything inherits, so it is worth getting right before you build
much content. See
[Completion rules](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/completion-rules).

## Video Playback

A default playback policy for every course and learning path — switched on with **Enable
a default playback policy**.

Left off, videos use the built-in behaviour: YouTube and Vimeo links play inside your own
player rather than sending the learner away. Switch it on when you want to decide things
like seeking and speed yourself. A programme can override this too. See
[Video playback](/docs/knowledgebase/files/video-playback).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="I changed a setting and nothing happened.">
Saves are per section — save the one you edited. Some settings also only affect new
activity: changing a retention period does not retroactively delete what is already
kept.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Learners cannot register.">
**Self-registration** is off, or the identifier they are trying to use is not among the
ones you allow.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where are payment gateways and calendar connections?">
Not here — they are in <Path>Settings > Integrations</Path>. See
[Integrations](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/integrations).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who can change these?">
Anyone whose role allows settings. It is worth keeping that list short: most of this
screen affects everybody.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can settings differ per course?">
A few can be overridden on a programme — video playback and certificate release, for
instance. The rest are academy-wide.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
