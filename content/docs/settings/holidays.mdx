title: "Holidays"
description: "Your academy's calendar of closures — what to record, and what recording one actually does."
---

A holiday is a date your academy is closed. They live in
<Path>Settings > Holidays</Path> and appear on calendars.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## Adding one

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What it is called — "Eid ul-Fitr", "Victory Day". |
| **Description** | A note. |
| **Type** | **Public**, **Religious** or **Company Specific**. |
| **Start Date** | When it begins. |
| **End Date** | Optional — leave it empty for a single day. |
| **Is Recurring** | Whether it happens every year. |
| **Status** | **Draft**, **Published** or **Archived**. |

**Company Specific** is for your own closures — a staff day, an anniversary — as against
national or religious holidays everyone observes.

## What a holiday does

It appears on calendars, so learners and staff can see the academy is closed.

<Callout type="warn">
**A holiday does not move anything.** It does not shift a drip schedule, extend a
deadline, or postpone a live session. If a deadline falls on a holiday, you move the
deadline yourself.
</Callout>

That is worth knowing before you plan a term around the calendar. Holidays are a notice,
not a rule.

## Finding one in the list

The list's **Date** filter looks forward, because holidays are planned ahead: **Next 7
days**, **Next 30 days**, **Next month**, **Next year**, a whole month, or a range you
pick. See [Date filters and your time zone](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/date-filters).

## Recurring holidays

**Is Recurring** marks a holiday that happens every year. Note that holidays following
the lunar calendar — Eid, for instance — fall on different dates each year, so check the
dates rather than assuming the recurrence has them right.

## Worth recording

- **National holidays**, so nobody schedules an exam on one.
- **Religious holidays** your learners observe. In a mixed academy this is a
  consideration as much as a calendar entry.
- **Your own closures**, so learners do not expect an answer.

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="A deadline fell on a holiday.">
Move the deadline. Holidays do not shift drip schedules or assignment lock dates. See
[Drip scheduling](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do learners get a notification?">
Not from the holiday itself. Post an [announcement](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/announcements)
if it matters that they know.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can holidays differ per batch?">
No. The holiday calendar is academy-wide.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does archiving a holiday do?">
Takes it off the calendar while keeping the record. Use it for a one-off that will not
happen again.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
