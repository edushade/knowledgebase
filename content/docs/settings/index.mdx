## The settings worth getting right on day one

| Setting | Why now |
|---|---|
| **Organization email** | It is what learners are told to contact. |
| **Certificate number pattern** | Certificates keep the number they were issued with. Change it later and you have two schemes. |
| **Staff sign-in** | Two-factor for the people who can change grades. |
| **Locked Profile Fields** | A learner who can edit their own name can edit the name on their certificate. |
| **Student ID pattern** | Easier to set before you have a thousand learners than after. |
