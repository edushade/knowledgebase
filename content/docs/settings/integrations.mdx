<Path>Settings > Integrations</Path> is one screen with five sections. Each connects
something outside your academy.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

## The five sections

| Section | What it connects |
|---|---|
| **Payments** | Online payment services, and direct payments into your own accounts. |
| **Sign-in Providers** | Google, Facebook, Apple, GitHub sign-in. |
| **Calendar** | A calendar for bookings. |
| **Conferencing** | Zoom, Google Meet and the like. |
| **Server-side tracking** | Sending conversions to tracking services. |

Each section is gated separately, so somebody allowed to manage payments may see that
section and not the others — what they cannot open shows a short notice rather than an
error.

## Payments

The first section, in three parts:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Online payment** | Services that take money on a secure page — learners get access at once. |
| **Direct payments** | Learners send money to your own bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, bank account or office, and your staff confirm each one. See [Direct payments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/direct-payments). |
| **At checkout** | Shown when both are on: which comes first, when direct payments are offered, and for which courses. |

Under **Online payment**, **Set up** a service and fill in its credentials, or **Add
payment gateway**. Each connection carries:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enabled** | On or off. **Turn off to pause without losing the setup.** |
| **Live mode** | Production credentials, or test. |
| **Gateway** | Which service. |
| Priority | Which is offered first. |

<Callout type="warn">
**Secrets are stored encrypted and never shown again.** Once saved, you cannot read a
key back — you can only replace it. Keep your own copy where your team keeps such
things.
</Callout>

A gateway that is removed takes its configuration with it. Disable rather than delete
when you are only pausing.

See [Payment methods](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/payment-methods) for which
gateways exist and what each needs.

## Sign-in Providers

Let people sign in with an account they already have: **Google**, **Facebook**,
**Apple**, **GitHub**.

Each takes a client ID and secret from that provider, and shows you the **authorized
redirect URI** to paste back into their console. Some values — the client ID — can only
be set at creation; to change one, remove the integration and set it up again.

Signing in through your own academy needs nothing set up at all.

<Callout type="info">
Social sign-in raises completed registrations, especially on phones. It also means a
learner's name and email come from that provider, which matters if those names go on
certificates — see **Locked Profile Fields** in
[the Control Panel](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/control-panel).
</Callout>

## Calendar and Conferencing

Connect a calendar so bookings appear on it, and a conferencing service so a meeting
link is created for you instead of pasted in by hand.

Once conferencing is connected, a **Live** lesson or a booking can create its own
meeting. See [Live sessions and bookings](/docs/knowledgebase/bookings/integrations).

## Server-side tracking

Sends conversions to tracking destinations from the server rather than the browser.
Each destination has its own setup and an **event log**, which is where you check
whether anything is actually arriving.

This is about measuring your marketing. The scripts that run in a visitor's browser are
in **Analytics & Tracking** on
[the Control Panel](/docs/knowledgebase/settings/control-panel).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="I cannot see one of the sections.">
Your role does not cover it. The sections are gated separately, and the one you cannot
open says so rather than failing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A gateway was working and stopped.">
Check **Enabled** and **Live mode** first, then whether the credentials were rotated at
the gateway's end. A key changed there has to be changed here.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Social sign-in returns an error about the redirect.">
The redirect URI in the provider's console must match the one shown here exactly,
including the protocol and any trailing slash.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I need to change a client ID.">
Remove the integration and set it up again. Some values are fixed at creation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Nothing is reaching my tracking destination.">
Open its **event log**. It will tell you whether events are being sent and refused, or
not sent at all — two different problems.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
