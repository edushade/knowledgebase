title: "Date Filters and Your Time Zone"
description: "The one date filter used across the admin, its choices, and why its days follow your academy's time zone."
---

Every admin list and report that can be narrowed by date uses the same filter. Learn it
once and it works the same on transactions, analytics, people lists and holidays.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

## Where you meet it

A button with a calendar icon at the top of the list, usually called **Date**. Some
screens give it a name that says which date it means:

| Screen | The filter is called |
|---|---|
| <Path>Billing > Transactions</Path> | **Date** |
| <Path>Analytics > Programmes</Path>, **Enrollments**, **Learners**, **Instructors** | **Date** |
| <Path>Analytics > Progress</Path> | **Enrolled** |
| <Path>Analytics > Grades</Path> | **Graded** |
| <Path>Analytics > Certificates</Path> | **Issued** |
| **User Growth Trend** and **Top Performing Courses** on the analytics overview | **Date** |
| People lists | **Created Date**, **Updated Date**, **Last Login Date**, **Enrollment Date**, **Last Learning Activity** |
| A person's **Communication** tab | **Sent** |
| Choosing learners to enrol | **Signed up** |
| <Path>Settings > Holidays</Path> | **Date**, looking forward (below) |

Once a period is applied, it shows beside the name — "This month", "Nov 2026" or
"Oct 3 – Nov 2, 2026" — so you can see what the list is narrowed to without opening it.

## The choices

| Choice | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Today. |
| **Yesterday** | Yesterday. |
| **Last 7 days** | Today and the six days before it. |
| **Last 30 days** | Today and the 29 days before it. |
| **This month** | From the 1st of this month to today. |
| **Last month** | The whole of last month. |
| **This year** | From 1 January to today. |
| **Last year** | The whole of last year. |
| **A whole month** | Pick a year with the arrows, then click a month. |
| **Custom range** | Pick a first day and a last day on the calendar, then press **Apply**. |

A preset or a month applies as soon as you click it. A custom range waits for **Apply**,
and both days you picked are included. Pick only one day and **Apply** uses that day.

**Clear** at the bottom removes the filter.

Analytics screens start with no period: they show everything until you choose one.

## Holidays look forward

Holidays are mostly planned ahead, so their filter offers days to come: **Today**,
**Next 7 days**, **Next 30 days**, **This month**, **Next month**, **This year**,
**Next year** and **Last year**. Future months and days can be picked.

## Filters for a single day

Two places ask for one day rather than a period:

- **Meeting Schedules**, under Live Sessions — **Yesterday**, **Today** or **Tomorrow**,
  or any day on the calendar. It applies as soon as you click.
- **Date of Birth**, in the people lists — a calendar with month and year lists, so you
  can reach a year long ago without clicking back month by month.

## Your academy's days, not your computer's

"Today" and "Yesterday" are worked out on your academy's clock. Someone checking
transactions from another country gets the same list you would, and a payment made at
11:30 at night lands on the day your learners would say it happened.

The bottom of the filter tells you which clock it is using — for example *"Days in
Asia/Dhaka time"*.

### Setting your academy's time zone

<Path>Website > Settings > Localization</Path>, under **Regional Settings**: the
**Timezone** list. Choose your academy's own city, for example **Asia/Dhaka**.

<Callout type="warn">
**If no time zone is set, the filter counts days in UTC.** For an academy in Bangladesh
that is six hours behind, so an evening payment can appear on the next day's list. The
footer of the filter says *"Days in UTC time"* when this is the case.
</Callout>

The same setting decides how dates and times appear on your website. See
[Languages](/docs/knowledgebase/website/languages).

## Common questions

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Why does a payment show under a different day than I expected?">
Check the line at the bottom of the filter. If it does not say your own time zone, set
it under <Path>Website > Settings > Localization</Path>.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I pick a day in the future?">
Only on Holidays, which look forward. Everywhere else the filter is about what has
already happened, so future days are greyed out.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Outstanding & at-risk card has a different selector.">
That card on the billing overview has its own short list — **Last 7 days**, **Last 30
days** and **Last 90 days**. It also ends on your academy's today.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I cleared the filter on User Growth Trend and the chart changed.">
That chart starts on the last month or so. Clearing removes the period altogether, so
the chart shows everything it has.
</Accordion>
