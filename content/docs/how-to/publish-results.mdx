---
title: "How to Publish Results"
description: "Marking, checking and releasing — in an order that cannot embarrass you."
---

Publishing results is the moment everything you set up is tested in public. This order
is designed so mistakes are found before learners see them.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

<Steps>

<Step>

### Mark everything

<Path>Grading > Assignment Submissions</Path> is everything waiting across your academy, and the
dashboard panel shows what is waiting now.

Mark question by question across learners rather than learner by learner — it keeps your
standard steady.

[Grading quizzes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/grading-quizzes) ·
[Assignment submissions](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/assignment-submissions)

</Step>

<Step>

### Check the questions before the people

Open the quiz analytics. A question almost everybody got wrong is usually a wrong answer
key, not a cohort that failed.

Fix it now, and correct the marks it already produced — see
[Fix a wrong answer key](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/fix-a-wrong-answer-key).

</Step>

<Step>

### Recalculate

**Calculate All Grades** on the programme. Results are stored as they are calculated, so
without this some learners carry old figures.

Run it after any correction, any weight change, and any change to **Treat Ungraded Items
as Zero**.

[Learner grades](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/learner-grades)

</Step>

<Step>

### Read the distribution

[Grade analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/grades), narrowed to this programme.

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A spike at zero | Check **Treat Ungraded Items as Zero** — those are probably unmarked, not failures. |
| Everybody above 90 | The assessments are not measuring anything. Note it for next time. |
| A hump just under the pass mark | Check the rounding. It may be the setting, not the cohort. |

</Step>

<Step>

### Check three learners by hand

The top, the bottom, and one borderline. Open their breakdown and read it the way a
learner will.

If a borderline learner's result does not make sense to you, it will not make sense to
them either — and they are the one who will write.

</Step>

<Step>

### Release

How depends on what you are releasing:

| Releasing | Where |
|---|---|
| One quiz's scores | The quiz's **Release scores on**, or turning immediate scores on |
| A semester's results | The term's **Transcript available from** |
| A programme's results | The course's **Transcript available from**, and **Enable Transcript** |

Release everybody at once. Results trickling out as you mark is how a cohort decides the
marking is arbitrary.

[Transcripts and results](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/transcripts)

</Step>

<Step>

### Tell them

A [campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns) to the batch, and a
[notice](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/announcements) on the board. Say what was
released, where to find it, and who to contact about a query.

Then watch the certificates: a passing cohort should produce certificates within
minutes. If it does not, see
[Certificate requirements](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-requirements).

</Step>

</Steps>

## If something goes wrong after release

**A mark was wrong.** Correct it, recalculate, and tell the learner directly. A quiet
correction that they discover themselves is worse than the original error.

**Everybody's result moved.** You changed a setting and recalculated. Say so, explain
why, and do not leave them guessing.

**Nobody can see their result.** **Enable Transcript** is off for the programme — they
see nothing while you see everything.
