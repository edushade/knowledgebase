title: "How to Run an Exam Day"
description: "Settings, window, invigilation and what to do on the day — including the check that saves you from publishing a wrong mark."
---

An exam is a quiz settled strictly. This is the whole day, from building it to releasing
the results.

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

<Steps>

<Step>

### Build the paper

A quiz, with a **Quiz Type** of "Exam" so you can report on exams later.

Write real **Instructions**: what is allowed, what to do if something breaks, who to
contact. Mention that **Report a problem** exists — a learner who hits a broken question
should flag it rather than lose ten minutes on it.

[Creating a quiz](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/creating-quizzes)

</Step>

<Step>

### Settle the settings

| Setting | For an exam |
|---|---|
| **Maximum Attempts** | 1 |
| **Time limit** | On, the real duration |
| **Allow Pause** | Off |
| **Shuffle questions** / **Shuffle answer options** | On |
| **Require All Questions** | On, if nothing may be skipped |
| **Pass Score** | Your pass mark |
| **Show answer after activity** | Off |
| **Show each learner's score as soon as they submit** | **Off** |
| **Release scores on** | After the window closes |

[Quiz settings](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-settings)

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the window

Add the quiz to the course and set its **Drip Schedule**: an **unlock** when the exam
opens and a **lock** when it closes.

Without a lock there is no window, and a learner sitting it two days later is
indistinguishable from one who sat it on the day.

[Quiz availability](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-availability)

</Step>

<Step>

### Give it its weight

On the programme's **Grading** tab. A final exam at 40% of the course is a decision made
once, in the weights editor.

[Weighted grading](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/weighted-grading)

</Step>

<Step>

### The day before

- Open the exam yourself as a learner and read it.
- Check the sending service — reminders are going out tonight.
- Check who is on the **Non-participants** list from last time, and chase them now.

</Step>

<Step>

### On the day

Expect reschedules. Somebody's connection will fail; somebody will be ill. Give them
their own window rather than reopening the exam for everybody.

[Reschedules](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/reschedules)

</Step>

<Step>

### Before you release anything

<Callout type="warn">
**Read the per-question analytics first.** A question almost everybody got "wrong" is
usually a bad answer key — and it is far cheaper to fix before anybody has seen a mark.
</Callout>

[Quiz analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-analytics) ·
[Fix a wrong answer key](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/fix-a-wrong-answer-key)

</Step>

<Step>

### Release

Mark the written questions, check the figures, then release everyone at once.

[Publish results](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/publish-results)

</Step>

</Steps>

## What you cannot do

There is no webcam proctoring, browser lock or screen recording. The honest list of what
is available — and a workable recipe for an unsupervised exam — is on
[Keeping an exam honest](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/proctoring).

For a high-stakes exam, that means invigilation in a room, with the **password** setting
used to hand out access there.

## If something goes wrong

**A learner was cut off.** Check whether it was the lock or the time limit. If the fault
was yours, reset the attempt and give them another.

**Several learners could not start.** Check the window, and whether they have access to
that content at all.

**The paper was too long.** The analytics show time taken against the limit. Fix it for
next time; for this time, decide what is fair and apply it to everybody.
