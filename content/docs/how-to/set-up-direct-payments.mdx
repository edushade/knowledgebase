---
title: "How to set up direct payments"
description: "Add your bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, bank or office details, decide what learners tell you, and switch it on."
---

At the end of this, learners can choose **Send the money yourself** at checkout and pay
into your own accounts.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

**Before you start** — have each number or account to hand, with the name exactly as it
shows when someone sends money to it. Decide how soon your team can answer: learners
are told.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the payments settings

Go to <Path>Settings > Integrations</Path>. **Payments** is the first item on the
page's own menu. Under **Direct payments**, press **Set up**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add the first account

Under **Where learners send money**, press **Add an account**.

1. **Pay by** — **bKash**, **Nagad**, **Rocket**, **Upay**, **Bank account** or **Cash
   at your office**.
2. For a mobile wallet, the **Account type**: **Personal** (learners use **Send
   Money**), **Merchant** (**Make Payment**) or **Agent** (**Cash Out**). Then the
   number, and the **Name on the account** as learners will see it when they send.
3. For a bank: **Bank**, **Account holder** and **Account number**, and the **Branch**
   and **Routing number** if you have them.
4. For your office: the **Address**, and **When to come** if it differs from your
   office hours.
5. **Tell learners to expect an answer within** — **1 hour**, **4 hours** or **1 working
   day**. Promise what you can keep.
6. **Largest amount (optional)** — leave it empty unless the account has a limit. A
   personal wallet usually does.

Check **Learners will see these steps** at the bottom of the dialog, then press **Add
account**.

Repeat for each account you want to offer.

</Step>

<Step>

### Decide what learners tell you

Under **What learners tell you**:

- The **Transaction ID** is always asked.
- **Number they sent from** — **Ask for it** or **Don't ask**. It is filled in from
  their profile, and makes finding the payment on a statement much quicker.
- **Screenshot of the payment** — **Optional**, **Required** or **Don't ask**.
  **Required** slows learners down; most send one anyway.

</Step>

<Step>

### Say when you check

Under **Checking payments**, write your **Office hours** — for example "Saturday to
Thursday, 10 am to 6 pm" — and press **Save**. Learners see it while they wait.

The staff who confirm payments are the ones whose role lets them change orders. They are
alerted by email and in the app each time a payment is reported.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add a note, if you need one

**A note for learners (optional)** is shown under the payment details. Use it for
anything the steps do not cover. Keep it short.

</Step>

<Step>

### Switch it on

Press **Back to Payments** and turn on **Take direct payments**.

If you also take payments online, the **At checkout** section appears. Choose which
comes first, whether to offer direct payments **On every order**, **Only after an
online payment fails** or **Only on orders above an amount**, and which courses take
them.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

Learners see **Send the money yourself** at checkout. Each payment they report waits in
<Path>Billing > Transactions > Pending Verification</Path>, and the **Billing** menu
shows how many are waiting. See
[Check a direct payment](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/check-a-direct-payment).

Buy a course yourself with a test account before you announce it, and read every step
as a learner would.

## If something goes wrong

- **The switch will not turn on.** Add at least one account first.
- **A learner cannot see one of your accounts.** The price is above that account's
  **Largest amount**, or the account's **Offer at checkout** is off.
- **Learners do not see Send the money yourself at all.** Check **Courses that take
  direct payments** and **Offer direct payments** under **At checkout**: the course may
  not be chosen, or the price may be below your **Smallest order**.
