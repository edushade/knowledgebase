---
title: "How to Move a Learner to Another Batch"
description: "Transferring somebody, and the one decision that decides whether it goes well."
---

A learner is in the wrong batch, needs the evening group, or is repeating with next
year's intake.

**Transfer them. Do not enrol them again.** A second enrolment leaves two records, two
sets of progress, and a duplicate that confuses every report afterwards.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

<Steps>

<Step>

### Decide whether their marks travel

This is the decision that matters, and it is worth making before you open the screen.

| Situation | Choose |
|---|---|
| Wrong batch at sign-up, timetable clash, moving sideways | **Carry forward** |
| Repeating the material, starting again | **Reset** |

<Callout type="warn">
**Carrying forward brings everything, including the failures.** A learner repeating a
semester with their old marks carried across is not repeating it. For a repeat, reset.
</Callout>

</Step>

<Step>

### Open the transfer

<Path>Enrollments > Transfers</Path>, or from the learner's enrolment, then **Transfer
Learners**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose who, and where to

The learner — or several at once — and the batch they are moving into.

</Step>

<Step>

### Write the reason

It is kept on the transfer trail. "Registered into the wrong batch" is a real answer and
the one you will be glad of in six months.

</Step>

<Step>

### Read the review

**Review transfer** shows what is about to change, for whom. Read it. A transfer is not
something to discover was wrong a week later.

</Step>

<Step>

### Check afterwards

Open their enrolment and check three things:

1. They are in the right batch.
2. Their **Scopes** are right — a learner who bought one semester should still hold one
   semester, and the new batch's terms need to line up with it.
3. Their marks are what you intended — carried or cleared.

Then run **Calculate All Grades** on the new batch so their result reflects where they
now are.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What a transfer does not do

| Not changed |
|---|
| Money — no refund, no re-charge |
| A certificate already issued |
| Their account, sign-in or details |

## The trail

Every transfer is recorded against the enrolment — where from, where to, when, by whom
and why, oldest first. A learner who has moved twice shows both moves.

It is the answer to "why is this learner in this batch?" and "why do their marks start
in week six?". Honest reasons make it do that job.

## If something goes wrong

**You transferred the wrong person.** Transfer them back. It adds a second entry rather
than erasing the first, which is the honest record.

**Marks came across that should not have.** Reset their progress in the new batch, and
recalculate. See
[Resetting progress](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/resetting-progress).

**Their semester access does not line up.** The new batch's terms are not the same as
the old one's. Set their scopes explicitly — see
[Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access).
