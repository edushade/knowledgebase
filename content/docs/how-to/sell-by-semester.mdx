---
title: "How to Sell a Programme One Semester at a Time"
description: "The whole arrangement — terms, scoped prices, windows, access and progression — in the order it has to be built."
---

A four-semester diploma that learners pay for a semester at a time is the most involved
thing Edushade does. It works, and it has to be built in order.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

**Before you start** — the programme must be **cohort-based**, because terms belong to
a batch. See [Self-paced or cohort-based](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/course-types).

<Steps>

<Step>

### Divide the batch into terms

When creating the batch, set **Breakpoint Interval** to **Custom Intervals** and give
the number of semesters. Check the **Breakpoints Preview** before saving.

Or add each **Breakpoint** by hand in the builder, when the terms are not evenly
spaced.

[Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints)

</Step>

<Step>

### Put every assessed item inside a term

This is the step that is quietly fatal if skipped.

Content outside every term reaches the overall result and **no semester result**. A
learner's Semester 1 mark will be missing the quiz that sits above the first breakpoint,
and nobody will know why.

[The course builder](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/curriculum-builder)

</Step>

<Step>

### Give each term its own enrolment window

On each term: **Enrollment opens** and **Enrollment closes**.

Leave both empty and the term follows its batch — on sale until the batch ends, or,
where the batch does not allow late entry, only until that term starts teaching.

Fill them in when a semester needs to sell on its own dates. A semester-scoped price is
governed by the term's window rather than the batch's late-entry rule, which is what
lets a running batch still sell Semester 3.

[Enrolment windows](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/enrollment-windows)

</Step>

<Step>

### Create a price per semester

In <Path>Billing > Pricing Management > Product Pricing</Path>, entity type **Cohort**,
and scope the price to one term.

Create one price per semester, and — usually — one for the whole programme at a
discount.

[Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing)

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the grading up

Weights per term, and a scheme with a clear pass line. A learner who bought one semester
is marked on what they hold, on both sides of the sum, so their result is out of what
they actually took.

[Set up grading for a programme](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/set-up-grading)

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the progression rule

On the weights page, beside each semester: **how many of its subjects a learner must
pass** to go on.

It counts subjects, not a percentage — because a learner can clear the overall pass mark
while failing most of their subjects.

[Progression](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/progression)

</Step>

<Step>

### Decide when results are released

Each term carries **Transcript available from**. Leave it empty and the term's own end
date is used — which is what lets Semester 1 results go out while Semester 2 is still
running.

[Transcripts and results](/docs/knowledgebase/progress-grading/transcripts)

</Step>

</Steps>

## Running it, term by term

At the end of each semester:

1. Mark everything, and check the analytics before releasing. See
   [Publish results](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/publish-results).
2. Open the **progression roster** and work the exceptions.
3. Give the next semester to the learners moving up — or let them buy it.
4. Record a decision, with a reason, for anybody treated differently from the rule.

## What learners experience

They hold one semester. The rest of the programme is simply not there — no locked
panel, no explanation. Their progress and their result are measured against what they
hold, so 100% means "this semester", not "the diploma".

A completion certificate for the whole programme needs the **whole** programme, so a
semester-by-semester learner qualifies only at the end.

[Partial access](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/partial-access) ·
[Certificate requirements](/docs/knowledgebase/certificates/certificate-requirements)

## If something goes wrong

**A semester's result is missing marks.** Content outside the term. Move it inside and
recalculate.

**Semester 3 will not sell on a running batch.** Give Semester 3 its own enrolment
window — the batch's late-entry rule is skipped for a semester-scoped price.

**A learner's result changed when they bought the next semester.** The denominator
changed. That is correct.

**A learner who passed has no certificate.** They hold part of the programme. A
completion certificate needs all of it.
