Nothing in your academy works without email. Verification, password resets, receipts,
certificates, reminders — all of it.

<Callout type="error">
An academy that discovers on registration day that its email is misconfigured loses that
intake. Do this before you need it.
</Callout>

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

<Steps>

<Step>

### Connect a service

<Path>Notifications > Settings</Path>. Choose an email service — SendGrid, Mailgun or
Cloudflare — and fill in its credentials.

Set it **Enabled**, and set its **rate limits** to what your plan actually allows.

[Channels](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/channels)

</Step>

<Step>

### Set who it comes from

**Default From Name**, **Default From Email**, and a **reply-to address**.

Use a real address somebody reads. A "no reply" address turns every question into a
support request sent some other way.

</Step>

<Step>

### Test the connection

Run the service's connection test. It checks the credentials without sending anything —
so a failure here is a credential problem, not a delivery problem.

</Step>

<Step>

### Send a real test

**Send Test Notification** to your own address. Then check:

- It arrived.
- It is not in spam.
- The sender name is right.
- Replying to it reaches somebody.

</Step>

<Step>

### Turn on the setting that prevents lockouts

**Exclude Transactional from Limits**, under the rate limits.

Without it, a marketing cap can block a verification email — and a learner who cannot
verify cannot get in at all.

[Notification settings](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/notification-preferences)

</Step>

<Step>

### Check the notifications that matter are on

| Notification | Why |
|---|---|
| **Account Verification** | Nobody gets in without it. |
| **Password Reset** | Nobody gets back in without it. |
| **Payment Successful** / **Failed** | Money. |
| **Instalment Due Soon** / **Overdue** | Money you are owed. |
| **Assignment Deadline Approaching** | The most effective nudge there is. |
| **Certificate Issued** | The moment learners tell other people about you. |

</Step>

<Step>

### Prove it end to end

Register a brand new account with an address you can read, and go through registration.

This is the only test that counts. It exercises the service, the template, the
notification setting and the rate limit at once — exactly as a learner will.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Before every intake

Four minutes, and it has saved more academies than any other check in this knowledge
base:

1. Send a test on each channel you use.
2. Open [Delivery and analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/delivery-and-analytics)
   and check nothing is sitting at zero.
3. Check your rate limits against what you are about to send.
4. Register a test account and go through verification.

## If something goes wrong

**Nothing is sent at all.** No service connected, or it is disabled. The connection test
tells you which.

**Sent but not delivered, many recipients.** Rate limits, or your sending reputation.

**Sent but not delivered, one recipient.** Their address.

**Delivered and they say otherwise.** Spam folder. Ask them to check, and tell them the
sender name to look for.
