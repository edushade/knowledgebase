---
title: "How to Launch a New Batch"
description: "From an empty cohort-based course to learners enrolled and the first lesson open — in the order that avoids redoing work."
---

A new intake touches eight screens. This is the order to work through them, with the
decisions that are awkward to change first.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

**Before you start** — the course must exist and be **cohort-based**. A self-paced
course has no batches. See
[Self-paced or cohort-based](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/course-types).

<Steps>

<Step>

### Create the batch

Open the course, go to step **2**, and **Create Cohort** — or **Re-use from Existing**
to copy last intake's setup, with or without its content.

Set the **Cohort Title**, the **Cohort Code**, the **Start** and **End** dates, and
**Max Enrollment**.

Then the decision that matters most: **Breakpoint Interval**. Pick **Custom Intervals**
with the number of semesters if this is a divided programme, or **No Breakpoints** if
it runs straight through. Check the **Breakpoints Preview** before you save.

[Cohorts and batches](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/cohorts)

</Step>

<Step>

### Decide the enrolment window

On the batch: **Enrollment Open**, and **Allow Late Enrollment**.

Late enrolment off means the batch closes to new learners the moment teaching starts.
That is usually right for a diploma and wrong for a short course.

[Enrolment windows](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/enrollment-windows)

</Step>

<Step>

### Build the batch's content

**Build Curriculum** on the batch card. A batch has its own content, so a change here
does not disturb the batch already running.

Put each item inside the term it belongs to. Anything outside every term reaches the
overall result and no semester result.

[The course builder](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/curriculum-builder) ·
[Terms and semesters](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/terms-and-breakpoints)

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the grading up

On the batch's **Grading** tab: the scheme, the weights, the rounding, and **Treat
Ungraded Items as Zero**.

Do it before learners have marks. Changing weights later means recalculating everyone.

[Set up grading for a programme](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/set-up-grading)

</Step>

<Step>

### Schedule the content

On the **Drip Schedule** tab, choose **Fixed Date** — a batch moves together, so
everybody should reach week three in week three.

Then set the exceptions: the exam on its date, the assignments with their lock dates.
An assignment with no lock date has no deadline.

[Drip scheduling](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/drip-scheduling)

</Step>

<Step>

### Price it

In <Path>Billing > Pricing Management > Product Pricing</Path>, with the entity type
**Cohort** — the batch, not the course.

If you are selling semesters separately, scope the prices to the terms. If you are
offering instalments, switch **Enable EMI payment** on and pick the plans.

[Pricing](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/pricing) ·
[Sell a programme one semester at a time](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/sell-by-semester)

</Step>

<Step>

### Put the staff on it

The batch's **Staff** screen: instructors, and the coordinators and assistants who will
answer learners.

A batch with nobody on it has nobody answering its discussion.

[Instructors and staff](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/instructors)

</Step>

<Step>

### Check it as a learner

Register a fresh account, buy a place, and open the first lesson.

This is the step people skip and the step that finds everything: the missing price, the
batch that refuses late joiners when it should not, the first week that is still
unpublished.

</Step>

<Step>

### Open it

Publish the batch, and announce it — a [notice](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/announcements)
on the board and a [campaign](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns) to reach
people who are not signed in.

</Step>

</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

**Nobody can enrol.** Four rules, in order: has the batch ended, is **Enrollment Open**
on, are there seats, and does it allow late joiners. See
[Enrolment windows](/docs/knowledgebase/enrollment-access/enrollment-windows).

**Learners see an empty course.** Nothing published, or the drip schedule is holding
everything. Open it as a learner.

**The dates on the terms are wrong.** Terms keep their own dates and do not follow a
change to the batch's. Correct each one.
