---
title: "How to Fix a Wrong Answer Key"
description: "A question marked the wrong answer correct. Fixing the question is the easy half — this is the other half."
---

A learner reports that a question is marked wrong, and they are right.

<Callout type="warn">
**Fixing the question does not re-mark the attempts already sat.** Every learner who
took that quiz still has the wrong mark, and nothing tells you so.
</Callout>

<WhoCan roles="Admin, Instructor" />

<Steps>

<Step>

### Confirm it is actually wrong

Open the quiz's [analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/quiz-analytics) and look at
that question.

A question almost everybody got "wrong" is a bad key. A question most people got right
is more likely one learner's misreading — still worth answering, not worth correcting.

</Step>

<Step>

### Fix the question

Correct the key, so nobody else meets it.

If the quiz is used in more than one course, it is the same quiz everywhere — the fix
reaches all of them. See
[Reusable content](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/reusable-content).

</Step>

<Step>

### Find everyone affected

The quiz's **Participants** list: everyone who sat it. The analytics tell you how many
answered that question.

</Step>

<Step>

### Override their scores

Open each affected attempt and override that question. Leave a note saying why — the
next person to look at that mark will want to know.

You can override any score, including auto-marked ones. See
[Grading quizzes](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/grading-quizzes).

</Step>

<Step>

### Recalculate

**Calculate All Grades** on the programme, so the subject, semester and final results
all catch up.

Without this, the corrected question sits under results that still reflect the old
mark.

</Step>

<Step>

### Tell them

Everyone affected, not just the learner who reported it. Say what was wrong, that it is
fixed, and that their mark has been corrected.

Then resolve the [report](/docs/knowledgebase/assessments/question-reports) with a note —
that note is what the learner who told you sees.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What not to do

| Instead of | Do |
|---|---|
| Deleting and re-running the quiz | Override the marks. Deleting loses the learners' work. |
| Fixing it quietly | Tell them. They will compare marks with each other. |
| Fixing only the learner who complained | Correct everybody. The others were marked wrongly too. |

## Preventing the next one

- **Read the per-question analytics before releasing results**, not after. A bad key
  found before anybody sees a mark costs nothing.
- **Mention Report a problem** in your quiz instructions. Most learners assume a wrong
  question is their own misunderstanding.
- **Keep good questions in the [Content Bank](/docs/knowledgebase/courses/content-bank)**
  once their analytics show they work.
