---
title: "How to check a direct payment"
description: "Find a payment a learner reported on your own statement, then give them access or tell them why not."
---

At the end of this, the learner either has access or knows exactly why not and what to
do next.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

**Before you start** — you need a role that can change orders, and access to the
statement for the account the learner paid into: your bKash or Nagad app, your bank
statement, or your cash receipts.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open Pending Verification

<Path>Billing > Transactions > Pending Verification</Path>. The number beside **Billing**
in the menu is how many are waiting.

The list shows the oldest first, so nobody waits longest. Switch to **Newest first** if
you prefer, or search by name, email, order or transaction ID.

</Step>

<Step>

### Read what the learner sent

Pick a payment. The right-hand side shows:

- **Sent to** — which of your accounts.
- **TrxID** — with **Copy**.
- **From** — the number they sent it from, if you ask for it.
- **Sent on** and **Reported**.
- **Changed** — an **Edited** badge if the learner fixed the details afterwards.
- **Checks** — **TrxID not used on any other order**, or a warning that it is on another
  order too.
- The screenshot, if they sent one, with **Open full size**.

Under it, a sentence tells you what to look for — for example *"In your bKash
statement, look for ৳4,500 from 01712-345678 with ID 9AB2C3D4 on 21 Nov."*

</Step>

<Step>

### Find it on your statement

Search your statement for the transaction ID, then check the amount and the sender.

</Step>

<Step>

### Decide

**Found it, give access** — the learner gets access at once and is told by email and in
their notifications.

**Couldn't find it** — choose why:

| Reason | What the learner reads |
|---|---|
| **No payment with this TrxID** | *"We couldn't find a payment with this transaction ID in our account. Please check the ID and send it again."* |
| **The amount is different** | *"The amount we received doesn't match the price."* |
| **Sent to a different account** | *"This payment reached a different account from the one you chose."* |
| **Something else** | Only your note, so a note is required. |

Add a note for the learner if it helps — what to check, or who to call. The dialog shows
exactly what they will read. Then press **Send to** and their name.

</Step>

</Steps>

You can make the same decision from the payment's own page: click its row in
<Path>Billing > Transactions</Path> and use the **Check this payment** card.

## What happens next

A confirmed payment opens the course and appears as **Paid**. A payment you could not
find shows as **Not accepted**; the learner sees your reason on the course page and
their order, and can **Send again** with corrected details. They are told they do not
need to pay twice.

## If something goes wrong

- **The transaction ID is on another order too.** Look at both before deciding. Two
  learners rarely share one payment; one of them has usually copied the wrong SMS.
- **The learner typed the ID wrong, and you found the payment anyway.** Open the payment,
  press **Edit**, correct **TrxID the learner sent**, and save. Then confirm it.
- **You confirmed the wrong payment.** Payments are not deleted. Refund it from
  <Path>Billing > Transactions</Path>; see [Refunds](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/refunds).
