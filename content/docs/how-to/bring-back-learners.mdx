---
title: "How to Bring Back Learners Who Stopped"
description: "Finding the people who have gone quiet, working out why, and sending something that actually works."
---

Every academy has them: learners who enrolled, started, and stopped. They are the
cheapest growth available, because they have already paid and already decided you were
worth it.

<WhoCan roles="Admin" />

<Steps>

<Step>

### Find them

Four lists, each a different kind of problem:

| List | Where | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Never signed in** | The enrolment list, **Signed in** filter | They paid and never arrived. |
| **Not started** | [Progress analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/analytics/progress) | Signed in, opened nothing. |
| **At risk** and **slipping** | The same screen, **Need attention** | Started and stopped. |
| **Non-participants** | A quiz's own screen | Missed a specific assessment. |

**Never signed in** is the one to work first. Somebody who paid and could not get in is
a refund request waiting to happen.

</Step>

<Step>

### Work out why before you write

Each list has a different cause, and the same message will not do:

| Group | Usual cause |
|---|---|
| Never signed in | A verification email that never arrived, or a confusing first step. |
| Not started | Nothing was open on day one, or they did not know where to begin. |
| Slipping | The course got hard, or long, in a particular week. |

For the third, look at **How Far Learners Have Got**. A bulge at the same point means
the course lost them there, not that they lost interest.

</Step>

<Step>

### Check it is not your side

Before messaging anybody who never signed in, check
[Delivery and analytics](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/delivery-and-analytics).

A whole cohort that never signed in is almost always a verification email that did not
arrive — and sending them another email is not going to help.

</Step>

<Step>

### Build the audience

An [audience](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/audiences) from the same conditions —
never seen, last seen more than 30 days ago, enrolled in a particular programme.

**Preview results** before sending. A campaign to nobody is a wasted afternoon.

</Step>

<Step>

### Send something specific

Generic re-engagement is ignored. What works:

| Group | Message |
|---|---|
| Never signed in | "Your course is ready — here is how to sign in", with the actual link. |
| Not started | "Start with lesson 1 — it is 8 minutes", linking straight to it. |
| Slipping | "You stopped at lesson 4. The next one is 10 minutes." |
| Missed an assessment | "You can still sit it — here is your new window." |

Three rules: name where they stopped, say how long the next step takes, and link
directly to it rather than to the dashboard.

[Campaigns](/docs/knowledgebase/communication/campaigns)

</Step>

<Step>

### Fix the cause

A campaign recovers some of this cohort. Fixing the cause stops the next one:

- **Open something on day one.** A course where nothing is available until week two
  loses people in week one.
- **Look at the week they stop** and shorten it.
- **Turn on deadline reminders.** The single most effective notification there is.
- **Check verification actually works**, before every intake.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Give them a way back in

For a learner whose access lapsed, or who is behind on instalments, the message is not
"come back" — it is "here is how". See
[Installments](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/installments) and
[Subscriptions](/docs/knowledgebase/selling-payments/subscriptions).

For one who genuinely cannot continue this term, a
[transfer](/docs/knowledgebase/how-to/move-a-learner) to the next batch keeps them
rather than refunding them.
