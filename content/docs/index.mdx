## Using this guide

- The **sidebar** browses by section.
- The **search** at the top (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`) jumps to any topic.
- Every page ends with **common questions** and links onward to the next thing.
- Every link is shareable — send a colleague straight to the page they need.
