academy. Every page is written in plain language, with no technical know-how needed.
Whether you're building your first course, taking payments, or sending your first
announcement, you'll find the *what*, the *why*, and the exact steps here.

## How to use these guides

- Use the **sidebar** on the left to browse by section.
- Use the **search bar** (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`) at the top to jump straight to any topic.
- Use the **list on the right** of each page to skim its sections.
- Every link is shareable — send a teammate straight to the page they need.
